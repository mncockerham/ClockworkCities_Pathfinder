## 1. CORE PREMISE
- Genre: steampunk-ish politics/intrigue + light brawling. Tone: status > individual. "Worth = who you represent" (house/guild/family). Individuals untrusted; group backing is everything.
- Default play: residents of **Ward 13 (Luxury Ward)** of a great spire city; mostly well-off middle class. [?] PC Intro names city "Abelanth"; detailed city = "New Avalon" (capital of Arclean).
- Physics: combustion unreliable in Neither → no cars, guns(gunpowder), combustion engines, telegraph/phone/radio. Power = "radiant power" harvested from the Zone; wireless lamps (~60W) in Neither.

## 2. ATMOSPHERIC LAYERS [CANON]
Standard terms (src normalized): **Aither** = clean air; **Neither** = corrupting medium; **Zone**; **Neithercrete** (spire material; old alias Natherium).
| Layer | Desc |
|---|---|
| Aither | clean air above Zone; rulers/nobles/king live here |
| Zone ("dead zone") | 20–150 ft (elsewhere 100–300 ft, +200 in storms) Aither/Neither mix; constant lightning storms; powers airships & cities. ~1% lightning risk on a "good day" on foot. Ground crossing via ≤20×20 ft tunnels up to 2 mi long |
| Upper/Light Neither | sun & moon visible; humans' natural home |
| Lower/Dark Neither | sun not locatable; most planet surface; orcs' home |
| Lightless Neither / Underground | caves, deep valleys; dwarves |
| The Deep / Mist | 5,000–12,000 ft underground, thick; deep ones |
- Neither Fog: periodic dense surges, lethal; reason humans live only in big settlements/caravans.
- Neither corrupts people/plants. Neither fungi feed off it, rot in any sunlight/electric light (harvested under special photosensitive light).
- Permanent structure reduces environmental exposure by 1 level. Hermetically sealed deep spires reduce exposure by 2 levels.
- Neither Storm Mitigation: Storms lose intensity as they penetrate below ground or through structures: –1 intensity per 100 ft depth in mines (at 300+ ft deep, storms have zero mechanical effect, only an audible howl); –1 for reinforced structures (spires/hamlets); –1 for native Orc physiology.
- Neither cycles ~5,000 yrs (full cover ↔ absent). Now ~1/6 down from an unusually strong peak.

## 3. HUMANITY: 6 TYPES (one species, environment-driven) [CANON]
Not separate species. Shifts ~1 type per ~3 gens of living in non-native layer (range 1–10+). Most people can pass as an adjacent type. Interbreeding: 1 shift viable (80% mother's type), 2 shifts rare, 3 essentially never. Child ≤1 shift from mother; parents must be ≤2 shifts apart (High Elf+Human ok, High Elf+Orc no). Same 6-form pattern applies to most other life.
| # | Name | Native layer | Traits / notes | Pop. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Elf / High Elf / Fair Folk | Aither, far N/S (Iceland), peaks | unadapted (= modern humans); deathly allergic to Neither; most intelligent; best tech (~1930s): steel, gunpowder/explosives, wood & steel buildings | 300k |
| 2 | ½ Elf | Aither 70%+/upper Neither | traders/diplomats for elves; superb elf-made gear; steel rusts & explosives unstable for them; build wood/cement. Legacy: +1 skill tree, Int max 16 | 200k |
| 3 | Human | Upper Neither | dominant civ; builds spires; needs some sun + uncorrupted food; Aither food beneficial; increased healing | 400M |
| 4 | Orc | Lower Neither | most numerous; widest range; master stonemasons; Aither affects them like Neither affects humans; increased healing; Neither-storm effects delayed 6 h; blur w/ humans visually | 600M |
| 5 | Dwarf | Lightless/underground (100–5,000 ft) | master diggers; direct sun = pain; low-light vision; heat/cold tolerance; kept Neither-tech alive through collapses; some live in spires (merchants/outcasts) or run mines | 10M |
| 6 | Deep One | The Deep (5k–12k ft), >100°F | Aither burns/poisons; fungus breeders—supply spire food/medicine strains (decay → constant demand); low-light vision, heat adapted, exceptional resolve (max 16) | 200k |
Sub-types (aberrant states):
- Goblin (3a/4c): human w/ long Neither exposure (or orc w/ Aither); mental+physical loss; breed true in high-flux zones. 90% of Warrens dwellers. ~200k [?] pop seems low vs Warrens size.
- Vampire (3b): human who ate human flesh; can't eat Aither food; decays unless drinking type 1–3 blood. Hidden in spires; killed/exiled if found. ~10k
- Beast (4a): orc that must eat type 3+ flesh to keep intellect/self-control. Origin unknown. ~10k
- Neanderthal (4b): orc deprived of Neither; physical degradation; descendants become humans. ~20k
- Troglodyte: orc living as dwarf / permanently changed goblins in the Tombs. ~10k

## 4. EXPOSURE & FOOD [LEGACY mechanics, CANON concept]
Environmental severity 0–7 per type × layer × weather (full table src L149–183). Scale: 0 natural; 1 uncomfortable/poor sleep; 2 slow heal; 3 –skill weekly; 4 –skill daily; 5 dmg hourly; 6 dmg per minute; 7 dmg in seconds.
Food classes (by origin): Aither/deep-water fish(150ft+) → Zone(airship-grown) → Upper Neither crops → Lower Neither/processed fungus/shallow fish → Underground raw fungus → Deep fungus. Each type has a tolerance % per class; exceeding → mental-stat loss (lighter food) or physical-stat loss/death (too "clean" for Neither-adapted).
Human-specific (Type 3):
- Sunlight/air: warrens/deep mines –3 phys; no sun but fresh air –2; limited sun –1; good sun 0. Onset ~1 month, –1/month; recovers 1 wk open air.
- Diet → mental penalty: Class0 deep fish/Aither = none; Class1 Neither crops; Class2 Mela/shallow fish/Neither livestock; Class3 raw Sycelium; Class4 other fungi. Variety mitigates.
- Examples: well-off Ward 13 = 0; poorer = –1; slums (85% Mela) = –2; Warrens (80% raw Sycelium) = –3.
- Staple: **Sycelium** mushroom, grows fast in dark; 10×10×10 ft feeds 15 raw / 10 processed. **Mela** = processed Sycelium, safest.
Elven tech instability: d20 per use; in Zone (+200 ft above) 20=explodes; in Neither 17–20 explodes. Elven items sometimes used in noble district.

## 5. HISTORY (timeline) [CANON, partly ?]
[?] Two incompatible framings: real-Earth timeline (1715 onward) vs "years ago" framing with radiant power 2,500 yrs, airships 900 yrs. Treat sequence as canon, dates soft.
1. ≤1715 = real history. 1715 Neither appears; 1790 covers tropics/sea level; radiant lights; **Neithercrete** → first spires; by 1940 near-total coverage.
2. First era: **5 Great Kingdoms** (English, German, Scandinavian, Western American, New Zealand). Abused Neither-dwellers. Air flights between northern kingdoms; ~1940s non-Neither tech.
3. First Age of Storms (~2250–2400): total cover; all cities fall; orc hordes (revenge for mistreatment) erase Great Kingdom knowledge; dwarves keep basic Neither tech.
4. ~50 successor kingdoms, each claiming Great Kingdom heirship, erasing rivals; ~1,500 static years.
5. Second great storm: total collapse; only orcs in a few spires + 2 Icelandic spires (elves) w/ history/tech.
6. ~1000 yrs ago: Neither drops; Icelanders improve Neithercrete (500 ft → 1+ mile). **Age of Spires** (~200 yrs).
7. Storm + **fungal collapse** (only ~2 food strains) → ~80% of humanity dies over 50 yrs. 200 yrs later: hundreds of fungal strains (dwarves/deep ones) prevent repeat; spire populations 10–100× higher.
8. ~700 yrs ago: **Airships** (ride the Zone, ~100 mi/day) → **Age of Conquest** (700–300 ya): drop troops on spire tops, starve cities; small arc cannons.
9. ~300 ya: **Great Arc Cannons** (Zone-powered; bigger = more range/dmg) → spires near-impregnable; 200+ yrs stability. Merchant houses appear (~300 ya).
10. ~100 ya: university professionals/professional guilds appear (2021 doc).
11. ~50 ya: long-range airships → new trade routes. ~10 ya: faster/longer; contact with lands months away that never saw airships.
12. **40 ya: Cartridge Weapons** — ~2× arc cannon range; fragile; ammo goes inert in months; rare materials from few spires → sieges viable again (outlast cartridge supply, then destroy cannons). **Last 20 yrs**: several cities fallen; small kingdoms merging; arms race; airship armadas. ← CURRENT TENSION
13. Ruins: "Atlantis" (last low-Neither valley civ, common ruins); older peak civ (very advanced, rare strange ruins).

## 6. WHERE HUMANS LIVE [CANON]
- Great cities/spires 87% (¼–⅓ of each city in Warrens). Sea level up to 4+ mi tall. Many never leave birth city.
- Hamlets 7.75%: noble-owned satellite settlements; need fresh food + rotation w/ spires or residents drift toward orc.
- Islands 3%: deep-water fish, isolated, own their land; less orc raiding; lose fish → shift to orc in a few gens.
- Highlands 1%: near Zone, harsh weather, sun & fresh food = healthiest/admired, but more primitive; raiding societies.
- Depths/mines 1%: serfs, debtors, prisoners; some miner-owned claims.
- Free Neither wanderers <0.25%: need filters/gear; distrusted.

## 7. SOCIAL STATUS & WEALTH [CANON core; numbers LEGACY]
Status = wealth = backing. Must spend at your level or lose status. Family/guild money matters more than personal coin.
- Permanent wealth: provided by house/guild (housing, food, gear); lifestyle = social level ±1 by standing. GM may track –99..+99 standing.
- Three-Layer Wealth Model: 1) Base Currency (weekly/monthly discretionary pocket money), 2) Max Currency (limit of liquid off-the-books cash hidable before audits/taxation), 3) Personal Wealth vs. House Assortment (gear owned outright vs. high-tier gear/weapons/airships issued by House/Crown and recallable upon disfavor).
- Hierarchy of Favors (Soft Social Currency): Bypasses bureaucracy without coin. Tiers: Friend (common/restricted knowledge), Boon (legal action not violating self-interest), Debt (legal action carrying personal/career risk), Obligation (willing to break law/battery/theft on your behalf), Life Debt (honor-bound to die or kill for you). High-risk favor calls can result in contact's discovery and execution.
Status ladder (legacy 0–10; 10 = royal):
| Track | Ranks (status) |
|---|---|
| Non-citizen (not for PCs) | Wanted –3 (kill on sight), Slave/Prisoner –2 (rare, disliked), Indentured –1, Unaligned 0 (≤7-day visit) |
| Citizen | Bum 0, Laborer 1, Foreman 2, Leader 3 — "most free" (no master) |
| Guild | Apprentice 1, Member 2, Master 3, Guild Leader 4 — must have sponsor (guild/merchant/royal) or drop to citizen |
| Merchant | Lesser 4, Merchant 4, Lesser House 6, House 6, Wall Merchant 8 — family controls money; liquid/fragile wealth (goods, ships, contracts) |
| Noble | Poor 5, Lesser 5, Noble 7, High 7, Greater 9 — wealth from grants/property; durable, crown helps rebuild |
| Military/Constable | Enlisted 2, Sergeant 3, Captain/Officer 4, General 5. Retired: revert or –1 status; subject to recall |
| University | Student 4, BA 4, Upper Grad 5, Adv. Degree 5, Professor 6; grads need employer or revert |
| Government (BA+) | Bureaucrat 4, Management 5, Minister 6 |
Arclean social classes (narrative, low→high) w/ local vote weight:
Fungal Farmers/"Nameless" (Warrens, no rights; escape via military) → Serfs & Debtors (crown law only; no vote; <5% serfs freed, 95% of those via 10 yrs military) → Foreigners → Freeman/woman (1/10 vote; incl. enlisted & constables) → Guild man/woman (master of guilded trade; also junior officers, students; ½ vote) → Merchant/Educated (BA or 3+ servants; mid officers, active King's Guard; 1 vote; lowest class nobles associate with) → Lesser Nobles (one city only; 1 vote) → Guild Masters/College ranks/Privy Council/Major Merchant Houses/top military (treated as nobles; Wall Merchants de-facto nobles) → Middle Nobility (estate in noble quarter) → Upper Nobility (multi-city) → **25 Great Houses** (5–6+ cities; only change in 200 yrs: House **Adler** banished for treason 20 ya, **Laeyer** got its holdings) → Royal Family → King.
Status modifiers: retired military –1; visiting another city (unofficial, below Great House) –1; with money, never below Freeman in home country (lesser nobles → merchant). King ennobles a few yearly (lowest nobility); ~every 5 yrs grants estate (middle/upper). Marriage = only route into Great Houses. Adult adoption used by houses to inject money. Multiple statuses possible (use relevant one).
- Nature of Prejudice (Civilization vs. Wild Raiders): While vulgar urban stereotypes claim humans look down on Orcs as "violent, uncontrollable brutes," true prejudice in the setting is **civilizational and institutional, not biological**. Spire citizens fear and despise the savage **raiding warbands** that prowl the surface ruins and lower valleys—regardless of whether those raiders are Orcs, Humans, or Dwarves. Conversely, an Orc born in the spire or serving honorably in a trade guild, military regiment, or constabulary is accepted as a fellow civilized citizen, judged strictly by their social class, uniform, and guild standing.
Society: formally sexist (women "fairer sex", constables 95% male), but Arclean prizes courage—defying norms is admired; in practice gender rarely limits action. Men in "female" jobs looked down on.
From 2021 doc (world facts only, mechanics discarded):
- Social class = single most important trait: decides how world reacts to you and which parts of world you touch. Not for PCs: slaves, outlaws, prisoners, enemy-nation citizens.
- Non-citizen ≠ allied foreigner; it means no place claims you.
- Guilds answer for members' actions → members must live in support of guild.
- Trader = lowest merchant tier (small shop or small land/sea route).
- Soldier/Constable/Gov't employee/Student status exists only while serving, or after 20+ yrs honorable service; leaving early reverts to prior class.
- Professionals = university grads in a professional guild; appeared ~100 ya.
- Merchant Houses work like noble houses but must keep earning or lose status; use adoption to keep money flowing. Wall Merchants own estates above the Aither line, control multiple ship routes.
- Greater Nobles = named families acknowledged by king; only king adds/removes members (rare); property in multiple spires. Royal Family ≈40 people (NPC); ex-royals marry easily into Greater houses. King = NPC.
- Holdings tied to class: noble land/industry grants, merchant trade routes, guild halls (Guild Master).
- Upbringing may differ from current class (raised noble, now guildsman etc.) → social friction scaled to gap; military or university paths not seen as negative.

## 8. MONEY & GEAR [coins CANON; prices/XP LEGACY]
Decimal coins: Pin (P) → Bit (10P) → Crown (CR,10B) → Imperial (I,10CR) → Silver Imperial (S,10I) → Gold Imperial (G,10S) → Silver Bar (10G) → Gold Bar (10SB). Bar = metal for 110 coins; raw metal worth ~90; flake ~60.
Armor: Cloth 40C, Leather 6I, Chain 9S, Plate 12G (tough). Shields: Buckler 1I, Small 3I, Large 7I, Tower 1S; cheap versions priced in crowns w/ limited soak (5/8/10).
Weapons era: arc cannons (Zone-powered, mounted), cartridge weapons (rare, unstable ammo), no gunpowder outside elves; melee/boxing common.
Legacy XP: stories 1–3 XP; skill 1, new skill chart 1–3, stat = cur×2, merit/flaw buyoff ×4.

## 9. ARCLEAN (home kingdom) [CANON]
- King **Macelus Wilheart**. 15 cities, ~1000 hamlets. Geography: Isle of Man, Orkney, N. Scotland; capital in N. Scotland. Orcs only in N. Scotland; dwarves on Isle of Man; Orkney human-only (some orc sailors). [?] spelled Arclean/Arclaen/Aclean.
- Rival: **Relocore** — largest kingdom/army known, across the Inner Sea.
- Other city: **Karfcana**, "City of Blimps" (blimps ferry cargo from skyships; city in highlands).
### Government
- King supreme. **Royal Council** (kingdom): 1 seat per Great House, 15 votes to pass; king may overrule/bypass; mostly rubber-stamps. 5 sub-councils (priority order): 1 Military (+King's Guard), 2 Foreign Trade, 3 Finance (mining, money, University), 4 Security (constables, low/mid justice, city posts), 5 Domestic Trade (hamlets, shires, internal routes).
- **Privy Council** (city): 15 ward reps + 2 University + 3 noble district + 5 king appointees = 25; 16 votes to pass; law auto-enacts if king silent 5 days.
### Law
- Laws: Royal (crown) law, Privy law, local ward law (can't conflict).
- **Constables**: per-ward, authority only in own ward (+its Warrens/servant quarters); foresters = field equivalent; can be lent/conscripted. Enforce crown law on all, privy law on non-nobles (never on nobles in practice).
- **King's Guard**: ≤1000, ≥1 per ward, authority everywhere, answer only to king; command constables & military; may deputize anyone; refusing aid = treason.
- Courts: Low & Mid Court (non-nobles); King's Court (nobles, noble district). Constables get KG to charge nobles; rarely refused.

## 10. NEW AVALON (capital spire) [CANON]
- Outer wall ~30 mi, 50 ft tall/thick, encloses ~50 sq mi of fields (livestock, crops, foundries, tanneries). Port ½ mi away via walled (100 ft) secured passage. Inner wall 150 ft, 50 bridges to Military Ward.
- Spire: 3 mi wide at base, ~14,000 ft tall; palace on top (1 mi wide). Built of Neithercrete ("Natherium", degrades in sun); top in stone & steel. [?] "perdium" also named as human building material.
- Structure: wards stacked; each ward = 3 **circles** (Low/Mid/High), 300–600 ft tall, ½–¾ mi deep. Behind wards: **Warrens** (½–1 mi in), then **Tombs**.
- Travel between wards ONLY via sanctioned routes; making new routes = high crime (culturally unthinkable). Warrens toll 12P–1CR.
  - 4 **Peasant Paths** (Ward 1↔13, low circles): S private carriages (≤10), N freight (100 t), E/W mass transit (200+ ppl, 10 t).
  - **Noble Path**: N carriage open (10× cost, reaches merchant/docks/sky docks/university, low circle stops); E noble-only (high circle stops, 50 t freight); W University (mid circle); S **Royal Road** crown only.
  - Trade Road: Merchant Ward ↔ Dock Ward ↔ Sky Docks ↔ Collegium. Freight elevators 7↔8, 10↔11. Walk from Noble Ward to palace.

## 11. RULES BASIS
- Game mechanics = **Pathfinder 2e Remaster (ORC)**, heavily modified. Rules live in `AISummary/` (index: 00_RulesFoundation.md); authoritative over any [LEGACY] mechanic here.
- Docs assume users own the books: cite `[Book p.#]`, never copy PF text; write only changes/new rules. Tags: Unchanged/Modified/Replaced/Removed/New.
- MAGIC = biggest change: supernatural magic does not exist. Traditional spellcasting classes (Wizard, Cleric, Druid, Witch, Bard, Sorcerer, Oracle) have been **completely removed from character creation** (no stripped-down casters, level caps, or multiclass-only restrictions). All traditional magical functions are redistributed into physical steampunk classes: healing & medicine -> Chirurgeon; area blasting & energy -> Mad Bomber & Clockwork Engineer; wilderness & subterranean survival -> Scout & Miner; inspiration & social buffs -> Merchant; investigation & debuffs -> Inspector & Chirurgeon. Detail: AISummary/00_RulesFoundation.md & AISummary/10_Magic.md.
- HEALING & WOUND THRESHOLDS (detail: AISummary/20_Healing.md): Total HP divided into 3 categories using Step = floor(Total HP / 4). **Step 1 (Mortal Wounds)**: 1 to Step HP, heals in weeks; imposes **major penalties** once Damage is depleted. **Step 2 (Critical Wounds)**: (Step+1) to 2×Step HP, heals in days; imposes **minor penalties** once Damage is depleted. **Remaining (Damage)**: (2×Step+1) to Max HP, heals per normal PF rules. Concoctions and Clockwork Tech can ONLY heal regular Damage (not Mortal/Critical wounds). Healing must progress from deeper levels first (Step 1 Mortal, then Step 2 Critical, then Damage), with one **PC Exception**: PCs can always heal up to their level in Damage regardless of existing wounds.
