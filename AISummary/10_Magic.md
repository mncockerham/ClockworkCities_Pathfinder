# Clockwork Cities — Magic Conversion
Status: **Draft**. Base rules: Spells & spellcasting [Player Core ch.7]. Precedence per 00_RulesFoundation.md.

## Core Principle — Replaced
There is no supernatural magic. "Magic" covers two key things. Spells stay in the game as **allowed / modified / removed** effects reflavored to fit them.

| Part | Name | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Clockwork Technology (Devices)** | Neither-functional mechanical and electro-physical devices that reproduce spell effects. |
| 2 | **Concoctions** | Biomedical mixes of fungi, plants, and chemicals that reproduce restorative, metabolic, or chemical spell effects. |

## Part 1 — Clockwork & Neither Technology (Devices)
Technology that **functions in the Neither**, commonly called *clockwork technology* or *Neither-tech*.
- **Devices**: Mechanical, pneumatic, spring-wound, electro-physical, and Neither-arc/pulse apparatuses.
- **Weapon Damage Types**: Neither energy weapons do not fire physical projectiles; they discharge either **Electricity** (arc projector) or **Force** (coherent pulse capacitor).
- Examples: *Fireball* = thrown incendiary grenade; *Air Bubble* = breather mask with pressurized canister; *Lightning Bolt* = focused Neither-arc discharge projector.

### Environmental Constraints
- **In the Neither:** Operates at **100% full capacity**.
- **In the Zone:** Power suffers heavy attenuation: **half damage, half range, or half duration** for standard Neither energy devices and weapons.
- **In the Aither:** Neither-tech devices and power cells are **completely inoperable** (capacitors bleed to zero, energy discharges fail to bridge, mechanisms lock or stall).

### Power Distribution & Energy Cartridges
1. **Municipal Radiant Power Grids:**
   - **Great Spires (Zone Interceptors):** Spires anchoring the continental network span across the Zone, absorbing immense atmospheric energy differentials. They broadcast broad-spectrum wireless radiant power shared through the Neither, supplying massive metropolitan zones.
   - **Dwarven & Deep One Grids:** Deep subterranean cities, mountain redoubts, and Deep One redoubts generate radiant power via heavy, heavily fortified geothermal dynamos and chemical Neither reactors. Coverage is strictly local.
2. **Standard / Civilian Storage Cartridges:**
   - Lower energy density (**~1/20th the capacity** of weapon cartridges by volume/weight).
   - Universal utility: can be safely recharged at standard municipal radiant charging stations found in Dwarven holds, Deep One settlements, and civilized Orc enclave hubs.
   - Bulky: chaining civilian cells to power military weapons requires cumbersome backpacks or heavy rigs, making traditional crossbows, bows, and firearms far more cost-effective for non-Spire factions.
3. **High-Density Military Cartridges (Weapon Cells):**
   - Compact, high-yield cells used for military energy weapons.
   - Three standardized classes: **Small (~200g, 3 standard / 9 rotary shots)**, **Medium (~400g, 3–6 rifle / 15 slide shots)**, **Large (~800g, 3 heavy discharges)**.
   - **Secret Biochemical Catalyst:** The immense energy density of weapon cartridges is unlocked by a secret bio-chemical compound produced exclusively in Spire cleanrooms by coordinated industrial teams.
   - **Exposure Vulnerability:** Once synthesized, the unshielded biochemical catalyst is extremely volatile: direct exposure to raw Neither triggers an explosive chain detonation; direct contact with Aither causes rapid, corrosive acidic dissolution.
   - Recharging military cells requires high-pressure Spire-grade injection and calibration tech; Orc raiders use captured cartridges but cannot recharge them once depleted.

### Instability & Field Tuning (The Role of "Casters")
- Clockwork devices and Neither-tech modules are **incredibly delicate and prone to drift or rapid feedback**.
- **Anyone can activate** a device once primed, stabilized, and handed off.
- Without active tuning (spell slots / spellcasting class features), field devices rapidly drift out of calibration, jamming, arcing out, or discharging into inert scrap.
- A "caster" (Clockwork Engineer, Mechanist, Saboteur) is trained to **calibrate, balance, and maintain** these devices on the fly. Daily slots represent the number of active, tuned devices an engineer can stabilize simultaneously.
- **Hand-off:** A primed device handed to an ally remains stable for the duration of the spell effect (e.g., an *Air Bubble* respirator operates for 1 minute before exhaust valves foul).

## Part 2 — Concoctions
Biomedical and chemical preparations synthesized from specialized Neither fungi (Sycelium derivatives, Deep strains), plants, mineral extracts, and reactive catalysts.
- **Form**: Ingested tonics, auto-injectors, aerosolized vapors, and topical dermal washes.
- Examples: *Heal* = cell-regenerative biomedical serum or injection; *Barkskin* = dermal hardening wash; *Stinking Cloud* = noxious fungal aerosol canister; *Neutralize Poison* = targeted chemical antitoxin.

### Industrial Production vs. Field Improvisation
- **Manufactured Institutional Science (Artisans, Scholars & Factory Teams):**
  - Truly miraculous, stable Neither creations—long-shelf-life clinical sera, high-yield biochemical weapon catalysts, stable civic preservation agents, and municipal antidote reserves—are **manufactured in controlled industrial cleanrooms**.
  - This requires coordinated specialization: professional **Artisans** forge precision sealed glass ampoules, lead-shielded casings, and micro-valves, while **Scholars** manage precise stoichiometric reactions, pH balancing, and catalytic stabilization under strict temperature controls.
- **Improvised Field Alchemy (The Genius of Chirurgeons & Mad Bombers):**
  - Chirurgeons and Mad Bombers are not defined by superior ivory-tower theory or spotless laboratory facilities; their true genius is **rapid, intuitive field improvisation**.
  - In muddy trench conditions, under enemy fire, or in polluted ruins, they evaluate scavenged fungal bile, adulterated chemical slag, dirty water, and improvised containers, intuitively altering chemical ratios on the fly to produce potent, temporarily stable minor concoctions and field explosives from random scrap.

### Volatility and Spoilage
- Active field concoctions rely on hyper-reactive enzymes and unstable biological catalysts that **separate, ferment, or oxidize into inert slush/toxic byproduct within minutes** if unmonitored.
- **Slots as Stabilization:** A Chirurgeon or Mad Bomber’s daily preparation slots reflect the cognitive load and chemical counter-reagents needed to keep a batch of field mixtures suspended and active for the day.
- **Hand-off & Administration:** Once unsealed and handed to an ally, a concoction must be consumed, injected, or thrown within moments, executing its standard effect and duration.

## Design Rules for Converting Spells
1. Every allowed spell must have a **physical explanation**: a mechanical/electrical device or a chemical/biomedical concoction.
2. Effects may be **exaggerated** but must fit the concept (pneumatic pistons, lenses, Neither-charged arc capacitors, fungal enzymes, reactive acids, cellular stimulants).
3. **Removed:** Effects with no physical or biochemical explanation, especially **large-scale transformation** (e.g., *Animal Form* and polymorph effects).
4. Range, area, and targeting must match the delivery method (thrown, fired, sprayed, injected, worn, drunk, applied).

### Conversion Examples
| Spell | Type | Status | Clockwork / Concoction Form |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fireball | Device | Modified | Thrown incendiary/Neither grenade |
| Air Bubble | Device | Modified | Breather mask + compressed small tank (1 min hand-off) |
| Shocking Grasp | Device | Modified | Spring-loaded arc discharge contact knuckles |
| Heal | Concoction | Modified | Fast-acting cellular stimulant tonic or auto-injector |
| Stinking Cloud | Concoction | Modified | Pressurized aerosol canister of caustic spores |
| Animal Form | — | Removed | Physical transformation not possible |

## Open Questions & System Alignments
- **Environmental Behavior (Resolved):** Neither-tech operates at 100% in Neither, 50% damage/duration in the Zone, and is completely inoperable in Aither. Biochemical catalysts explode on direct Neither contact and melt in Aither.
- **Instability Failures:** Exact mishap table for untrained characters attempting to hotwire uncalibrated Neither devices or unmonitored field concoctions.
- **Spell Traditions (Arcane, Divine, Occult, Primal):** Reflavoring traditions into engineering disciplines (Electro-mechanical, Pyrotechnic) vs. biochemical paths (Surgical, Spore-toxicology).
- **Cantrips and Focus Spells:** Standardizing permanent reusable tools (spark igniters, mechanical diagnostic meters) and baseline stabilization kits.
