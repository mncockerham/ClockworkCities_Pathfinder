# Clockwork Cities — Magic Conversion
Status: **Draft**. Base rules: Spells & spellcasting [Player Core ch.7]. Precedence per 00_RulesFoundation.md.

## Core Principle — Replaced
There is no supernatural magic. "Magic" covers two key things. Spells stay in the game as **allowed / modified / removed** effects reflavored to fit them.

| Part | Name | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Clockwork Technology (Devices)** | Neither-functional mechanical and electro-physical devices that reproduce spell effects. |
| 2 | **Concoctions** | Biomedical mixes of fungi, plants, and chemicals that reproduce restorative, metabolic, or chemical spell effects. |

## Part 1 — Clockwork & Neither Technology (Devices)
Technology that **functions in the Neither**, commonly called *clockwork technology* or *Neither-tech*.
- **Devices**: Mechanical, pneumatic, spring-wound, electro-physical, and Neither-arc/pulse apparatuses.
- **Weapon Damage Types**: Neither energy weapons do not fire physical projectiles; they discharge either **Electricity** (arc projector) or **Force** (coherent pulse capacitor).
- Examples: *Fireball* = thrown incendiary grenade; *Air Bubble* = breather mask with pressurized canister; *Lightning Bolt* = focused Neither-arc discharge projector.

### Environmental Constraints
- **In the Neither:** Operates at **100% full capacity**.
- **In the Zone:** Power suffers heavy attenuation: **half damage, half range, or half duration** for standard Neither energy devices and weapons.
- **In the Aither:** Neither-tech devices and power cells are **completely inoperable** (capacitors bleed to zero, energy discharges fail to bridge, mechanisms lock or stall).

### Power Distribution & Energy Cartridges
1. **Municipal Radiant Power Grids:**
   - **Great Spires (Zone Interceptors):** Spires anchoring the continental network span across the Zone, absorbing immense atmospheric energy differentials. They broadcast broad-spectrum wireless radiant power shared through the Neither, supplying massive metropolitan zones.
   - **Dwarven & Deep One Grids:** Deep subterranean cities, mountain redoubts, and Deep One redoubts generate radiant power via heavy, heavily fortified geothermal dynamos and chemical Neither reactors. Coverage is strictly local.
2. **Standard / Civilian Storage Cartridges:**
   - Lower energy density (**~1/20th the capacity** of weapon cartridges by volume/weight).
   - Universal utility: can be safely recharged at standard municipal radiant charging stations found in Dwarven holds, Deep One settlements, and civilized Orc enclave hubs.
   - Bulky: chaining civilian cells to power military weapons requires cumbersome backpacks or heavy rigs, making traditional crossbows, bows, and firearms far more cost-effective for non-Spire factions.
3. **High-Density Military Cartridges (Weapon Cells):**
   - Compact, high-yield cells used for military energy weapons.
   - Three standardized classes: **Small (~200g, 3 standard / 9 rotary shots)**, **Medium (~400g, 3–6 rifle / 15 slide shots)**, **Large (~800g, 3 heavy discharges)**.
   - **Secret Biochemical Catalyst:** The immense energy density of weapon cartridges is unlocked by a secret bio-chemical compound produced exclusively in Spire cleanrooms by coordinated industrial teams.
   - **Exposure Vulnerability:** Once synthesized, the unshielded biochemical catalyst is extremely volatile: direct exposure to raw Neither triggers an explosive chain detonation; direct contact with Aither causes rapid, corrosive acidic dissolution.
   - Recharging military cells requires high-pressure Spire-grade injection and calibration tech; Orc raiders use captured cartridges but cannot recharge them once depleted.

### Environmental Operating Tiers & Class Balance
NeitherTeck and Clockwork equipment draw upon ambient atmospheric resonance, radically altering combat balance depending on location:
1. **Tier 1: High-Power Radiant Zone (Spires & ~50 ft of Generators):** Spires piercing the Zone and areas **within ~50 feet of an active generator** broadcast saturated radiant power. **Artisans & Scholars dominate** with high-drain heavy grid weapons (full damage/range). In high-society wards where military NeitherTeck is banned, **Dandies and Pugilists** dominate.
2. **Tier 2: Moderate Radiant Field (Airships & 1×–5× Generator Distance):** On military airships or within **5 times the high-power radius (~250 ft from an outpost generator)**, energy density drops. Grid weapons remain operational with unlimited ammo, but run at reduced baseline power. Beyond 5× distance, induction fails.
3. **Tier 3: The Wild / Deep Outlands (Zero Radiant Power):** Outside generator perimeters, all devices require sealed, un-rechargeable cartridges. Once cartridges deplete, devices go inert; **Martials (Soldiers, Brutes, Scouts) dominate** with steel, bows, and athletics.

### Activation Points (AP) System & Operational Complexity
NeitherTeck and Clockwork devices are complex, delicate systems requiring constant round-to-round cognitive adjustments and tuning; any failure or exceeding of capacity causes them to cease functioning immediately. The total NeitherTeck gear a character can operate in a single turn equals their **Activation Point (AP) score**:
$$\text{AP per Round} = \text{INT Modifier} + \text{Class Base AP} + \text{Level Progression Bonus}$$
- **The Dual-System Resource Model:**
  1. **Passive Loadout Items (Committed Daily):** Continuous protective/environmental items (breathing masks, hazmat linings, assist harnesses) commit points against **Passive Capacity** ($\text{Key Attribute mod} + \lfloor \tfrac{\text{Level}}{2} \rfloor + \text{Class Base 4}$). Three class archetype tracks (Track A: Str/Con for heavy martials; Track B: Dex/Con for light martials/operatives; Track C: Int/Str/Con for technical/academic classes). Lighter armor grants discounts (Unarmored: –2 pts; Light: –1 pt; Heavy: +2 pts). Zero in-combat AP math.
  2. **Active Surge AP (Per-Action Cost):** Paid on **every individual Strike/use** or Reaction.
  - *Consumables & Injections:* Cost **1 to 2 AP** per use.
  - *Cartridge & Radiant Weapons (Base):* Cost **3 AP (1d6 pistols)**, **4 AP (1d8 rifles)**, and **5 AP (1d10/1d12 heavy weapons)**. Trained discipline tags grant a **–1 AP discount**.
  - *Enhancement Tier Escalation:* Enhanced weapons cost **+1 AP per tier** (Tier 1 Striking: +1 AP; Tier 2 Greater Striking: +2 AP; Tier 3 Major Striking: +3 AP).
- **Class Base & Scaling Progression:**
  - **Tier 3 (Technical Masters):** **+2 Base AP**; scales **+1 AP at levels 4, 8, 12, 16, 20**. *Scholar, Artisan, Clockwork Engineer.*
  - **Tier 2 (Technical Specialists):** **+1 Base AP**; scales **+1 AP at levels 5, 10, 15, 20**. *Inspector, Clockwork Rifleman* (reduces ranged NeitherTeck weapon AP cost by –1 at 1st, –2 at 6th, –3 at 12th, –4 at 18th level; min 1), *Chirurgeon, Mad Bomber* (personal class features and concoctions cost 0 AP).
  - **Tier 1 (Tactical Operatives):** **+0 Base AP**; scales **+1 AP at levels 6, 12, 18**. *Officer, Sky Captain, Survivor, Scout.*
  - **Tier 0 (Dedicated Martials):** **+0 Base AP**; scales **+1 AP at levels 10, 20**. *Soldier, Brute, Pugilist, Dandy.*
  - **Influence Category:** **+1 Base AP**; scales **+1 AP at levels 6, 12, 18**. *Merchant.*
- **Tactical Math & Firing Cadence:** 
  - Martials achieve **1 shot per turn** from levels 1–9, scaling to **2 shots at level 10+** with tagged weapons.
  - Technical classes achieve **2 shots at level 4** and **3 shots at level 8**, but lack weapon master proficiency and suffer standard **Multi-Attack Penalties (–0 / –5 / –10)**. Full rules in `ClockworkPathfinder/Rules/NeitherTeck/`.

## Part 2 — Concoctions
Biomedical and chemical preparations synthesized from specialized Neither fungi (Sycelium derivatives, Deep strains), plants, mineral extracts, and reactive catalysts.
- **Form**: Ingested tonics, auto-injectors, aerosolized vapors, and topical dermal washes.
- Examples: *Heal* = cell-regenerative biomedical serum or injection; *Barkskin* = dermal hardening wash; *Stinking Cloud* = noxious fungal aerosol canister; *Neutralize Poison* = targeted chemical antitoxin.

### Industrial Production vs. Field Improvisation
- **Manufactured Institutional Science (Artisans, Scholars & Factory Teams):**
  - Truly miraculous, stable Neither creations—long-shelf-life clinical sera, high-yield biochemical weapon catalysts, stable civic preservation agents, and municipal antidote reserves—are **manufactured in controlled industrial cleanrooms**.
  - This requires coordinated specialization: professional **Artisans** forge precision sealed glass ampoules, lead-shielded casings, and micro-valves, while **Scholars** manage precise stoichiometric reactions, pH balancing, and catalytic stabilization under strict temperature controls.
- **Improvised Field Alchemy (The Genius of Chirurgeons & Mad Bombers):**
  - Chirurgeons and Mad Bombers are not defined by superior ivory-tower theory or spotless laboratory facilities; their true genius is **rapid, intuitive field improvisation**.
  - In muddy trench conditions, under enemy fire, or in polluted ruins, they evaluate scavenged fungal bile, adulterated chemical slag, dirty water, and improvised containers, intuitively altering chemical ratios on the fly to produce potent, temporarily stable minor concoctions and field explosives from random scrap.

### Volatility and Spoilage
- Active field concoctions rely on hyper-reactive enzymes and unstable biological catalysts that **separate, ferment, or oxidize into inert slush/toxic byproduct within minutes** if unmonitored.
- **Slots as Stabilization:** A Chirurgeon or Mad Bomber’s daily preparation slots reflect the cognitive load and chemical counter-reagents needed to keep a batch of field mixtures suspended and active for the day.
- **Hand-off & Administration:** Once unsealed and handed to an ally, a concoction must be consumed, injected, or thrown within moments, executing its standard effect and duration.

## Design Rules for Converting Spells
1. Every allowed spell must have a **physical explanation**: a mechanical/electrical device or a chemical/biomedical concoction.
2. Effects may be **exaggerated** but must fit the concept (pneumatic pistons, lenses, Neither-charged arc capacitors, fungal enzymes, reactive acids, cellular stimulants).
3. **Mental Effects & Conditions Policy:**
   - **Allowed Physiological Mental Conditions:** Neuro-chemical toxins, paralytic venoms, soporific aerosols, acoustic shock, and blinding flash powder **can inflict mental conditions** such as **Sleep / Unconscious**, **Stunned**, **Confused / Delirious**, **Fascinated / Dazed**, **Stupefied**, **Frightened (via chemical panic / adrenaline disruptors)**, and **Slowed**.
   - **Removed (Telepathy & Mind Control Out):** Supernatural mental domination, telepathy, puppet mastery, and memory rewriting (e.g., *Dominate*, *Charm*, *Modify Memory*, *Suggestion*) are **strictly removed**. All mental impairment must operate via organic neuro-chemical delivery, auditory overload, or physiological trauma.
4. **Removed (Transformations Out):** Effects with no physical or biochemical explanation, especially **large-scale transformation** (e.g., *Animal Form* and polymorph effects), are completely removed.
5. Range, area, and targeting must match the delivery method (thrown, fired, sprayed, injected, worn, drunk, applied).

## Design Guidance: Expanding NeitherTeck & Magic Item Replacement
- **Dual-System Economy Replaces Attunement:** Traditional supernatural magic item advancement and PF2e/5e item investment/attunement limits are permanently replaced by a clean two-part system:
  1. **Passive Loadout Capacity (Defensive & Environmental Gear):** Governs continuous protective equipment (breathing units, hazard filtration suits, ballistic linothorax, powered exoskeletons). Calculated from three distinct class archetype tracks:
     $$\text{Capacity} = \text{Key Attribute Mod} + \lfloor \tfrac{\text{Level}}{2} \rfloor + 4$$
     - *Track A (Martial/Frontline):* Best of **STR or CON** (*Soldier, Brute, Pugilist, Officer*).
     - *Track B (Agile/Operative):* Best of **DEX or CON** (*Clockwork Rifleman, Scout, Survivor, Inspector, Dandy*).
     - *Track C (Technical/Scholastic):* Best of **INT, STR, or CON** (*Scholar, Artisan, Clockwork Engineer, Chirurgeon, Mad Bomber, Merchant*).
     - *Armor Weight Modifiers:* Unarmored (–2 loadout cost), Light (–1 loadout cost), Medium (+0), Heavy (+2 loadout cost). Allows low-strength characters to comfortably equip protective gear while keeping armor class choices impactful.
  2. **Active Surge AP (Actions, Triggers & Weapons):** Governs combat triggers, reactions, discharges, and concoctions. Always derived from:
     $$\text{AP per Round} = \text{INT Modifier} + \text{Class Base AP} + \text{Level Progression Bonus}$$
     Spent on Strike actions and Reactions. Base weapons cost 3–5 AP. Discipline tags (`[Military]`, `[Technical]`, etc.) reduce cost by **–1 AP** (or more on bespoke gear).
- **PC Power Targets & Firing Cadence:**
  - **Martial Classes (Tier 0/1):** Base AP +0. Achieve **1 shot per round** from Levels 1–9 with tagged weapons. Scale to **2 shots per round around Level 10+** (matching high martial weapon proficiency).
  - **Technical Classes (Tier 2/3):** Base AP +1 to +2. Achieve **2 shots per round at Level 4** and **3 shots per round at Level 8**, but operate under standard **Multi-Attack Penalty (MAP: –0/–5/–10)** and lower weapon proficiencies, maintaining tight parity with martials while representing rapid technological output.
  - **Weapon Tier Escalation:** Higher-powered fundamental runes increase operational drain (+1 AP for Tier 1 Striking, +2 AP for Tier 2 Greater Striking, +3 AP for Tier 3 Major Striking), ensuring high-damage strikes consume significant turn-budget.
- **Store Availability & The Level 12 Retail Ceiling:**
  - Spire commerce mirrors Victorian class and geography: Slum/Warrens stalls carry crude scrap (Levels 1–4, up to ~35 Nobels); Guild Municipal factorums supply standard military/service gear (Levels 5–8, 100–500 Nobels); High Spire noble armories supply masterwork `+2 Greater Striking` and `+2 Resilient` gear (Levels 9–12, 1,000–2,100 Nobels).
  - **Level 12 is the absolute hard ceiling for store retail.**
  - **Levels 13–20 (Heroic Custom Relics):** Sold completely outside the store system. Requires **Level 6+ Abyssal leviathan carapaces, pristine pre-collapse Era 1 cores, celestial sky-iron, and legendary guild artisans**. These cannot be bought at a corner shop with gold; they must be earned via deep-zone expeditions, royal patronage, or bespoke multi-session crafting quests.
- **Restoring Classic DM Agency via Bespoke Gear:** 
  The combination of Discipline Tags and out-of-store bespoke items gives the GM a surgical tool to bolster underperforming or thematic characters. Dropping a custom relic tailored with a struggling player's tag or reduced AP/passive costs boosts their impact directly without triggering an arms race among power-gamers.
- **NeitherTeck Weapons Absolute Dependency:** NeitherTeck weapons possess **no mundane backup functionality**. If outside a radiant grid (>5× generator distance), out of cartridges, or in pure Aither, **the weapon is completely dead and inert**. This hard failure threshold makes traditional martial classes (**Soldier**, **Brute**, **Scout**, **Pugilist**) indispensable, as they master conventional steel, bows, crossbows, and unarmed combat when high-tech gear fails.

## Open Questions & System Alignments
- **Environmental Behavior (Resolved):** Neither-tech operates at 100% in Neither, 50% damage/duration in the Zone, and is completely inoperable in Aither. Biochemical catalysts explode on direct Neither contact and melt in Aither.
- **Instability Failures:** Exact mishap table for untrained characters attempting to hotwire uncalibrated Neither devices or unmonitored field concoctions.
- **Spell Traditions (Arcane, Divine, Occult, Primal):** Reflavoring traditions into engineering disciplines (Electro-mechanical, Pyrotechnic) vs. biochemical paths (Surgical, Spore-toxicology).
- **Cantrips and Focus Spells:** Standardizing permanent reusable tools (spark igniters, mechanical diagnostic meters) and baseline stabilization kits.
