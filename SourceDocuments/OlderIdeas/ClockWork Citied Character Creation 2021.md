# Character Generation

Players start with 10 points to build their character and the first choice a character makes is what is their final social class (this is the social class one enters play with). The greater ones social class the more it will cost, this will determines base skills, optional skills as well as wealth. Players pay for their ending social class.

Social class is by far the most important characteristic determining how the world reacts with your character and defines what aspects of the world you character interacts with.

Slave/Outlaws/Prisoner/Citizen of a waring nation: Not allowed free will or the ability to interact with society. Players only will have these status in the most dire of situation and should not be chosen for character creation.

Serf/Convict/Non Citizen: The lowest standing players should have. Not Non Citizen does not apply if a character is a member of an allied culture or country, this is for those living without a place that claims them.

Laborer: The unskilled masses without anyone looking out for them. Strangely the only truly free person. They are beholden to no one, can make any decision they want and do what they wish. However they live outside of most of society and are untrusted, limited to what they can own and what protections they can expect. While the law supposedly stands for them they do not have anyone else backing them.

Guild Member: Similar to a laborer but are members of one or more guilds that look out for their interest. However each guild answers for what their members do so you must live your life supporting your guild.

Trader: The lowest level of the merchant class. Dealing with the masses and common goods. These people either own a small store or run a small land or sea based trading route.

Soldier/Constable/Government employee/Student at the University: These status only exist so long as a person is employed at that level or served 20 years or more years before honorably leaving. Leaving before this reverts a player to their previous social class.

Professional: Graduates of the university who have joined a professional Guild. These roles only started appearing about 100 years ago.

Merchant: Member of a mercantile federation specializing in trading 1 or more types of goods. These people will often have multiple traders working for them

Military/Constable Officers. Members or the kings Guard

Guild Officer: High ranking members of a Guild controlling aspects of the guild over a large area

Member of the college: High ranking professionals controlling how the group acts and behaves

Lesser Noble: The lowest rank of nobles, controlling small amounts of land/buildings they rent out.

Merchant House: The advent of great trading ships and airships resulted in merchants that wealth started to mirror to nobility. They actually work very similar to noble houses but they must retain wealth (nobles are granted domains that generate wealth) and failure to make money results in them loosing this status. These houses will often use adoption to insure that the money continues to flow.

Kings Guard Officers/ Commanders of the Military and Constable service

Nobles: These families own very profitable items, usually only 1 or two them but may own mines and property outside of the spires.

Wall Merchants: Merchants families that have made so much money to own estates above the Either and are some of the most important people in the cities. The families control multiple trade ships routes.

Greater Noble: Members of the named families, these families are acknowledged by the king and only he can add or remove members and these very seldom happens. These nobles will own property in multiple spires.

Royal Family(NPCs) the family of the king usually only about 40 or so people will be in this category. They have so much influence and power that even those who leave this status easily marry into the Greater Noble houses.

The Kings: Well the one truly free person (NPC)

# Paths

Paths represent what a player knows and they receive the Base Path and 3 of the optional paths, players can spend 1 edge and they were raised as a different class than what they ended up, if this is chosen players can select their 3 paths from anything in the other class or their current one. This includes the Base skill. Players will face in game effects of the varied social class, mostly related to how different the 2 are. Time in the Military or an approbriate rank or going to University are usually not considered negative. This represents either a move away from you families traditional roles, a unexpected change in social class or perhaps time spent in the university or military.

# Choose Stats

Players have nine stats in 3 catogories

Physical:

Strength Physical activity brute force

Agility Quickness and physical dexterity

Durability Endurance toughness

Mental:

Reason: The doing stat for mental activities

Intuition: The problem solving stat

Knowledge: Knowledge/education stat

Other:

Reaction Initiative how fast can you react used for piloting and shooting some weapons

Empathy: Pull and manipulation stat

Determination. Resist being provoked or other affects

## Stats Allocation

Players may choose any one of the following combinations to assign initial stats

1. Generalist 5,5,5 5,5,5 5,5,5
2. Favored 4,5,5 5,5,5 5,5,6
3. Skilled 4,4,5 5,5,5 5,6,6
4. Dominant 4,4,4 4,5,6 6,6,6
5. Prodigy 4,4,4 5,5,5 5,5,7
6. Savant 4,4,4 4,4,4 5,7,7

The assumption is PC have 5 in every stat and they can modify them as follows

Drop a stat to 4 for one point. Raising a stat to 6 costs 1 point raising a stat to 7 costs a total of 3 points (1 for 6 and 2 more to go to 7)

## Optional human type

Players are assumed to be type 3 humans, if the setting allows they may shift to another group, this affect both base stats (after character generation is complete) and how they react to sunlight and the neither.

Note on non Type 3 NPC characters. These types after character creation will receive +/- stat adjustment with stats between 2 and 9 but the actual range is the same for type 3 humans 1-10 and cannot be increased above the range or below 1. unless \* then the limit is 11. Even among orcs a very few have a resolve of 10.
