## 1. Overview of Weapon Categories

NeitherTeck weapons fall into **two distinct mechanical families**:

1. **Cartridge / Charged Weapons (Self-Powered & Universally Functional):**
   - **High-Burst Tactical Power (Grind Limited):** Cartridge weapons fire multiple shots without cycling (3 to 15 discharges per cartridge) and deal boosted energy damage (`1d8` to `2d10` base) at full standard firearm ranges (`30–150 ft`). While traditional bows (`100 ft`, `Reload 0`) and crossbows (`120 ft`) retain a slight range and endurance advantage, Cartridge weapons intentionally over-perform in active firefights because they are strictly limited by cartridge scarcity across the 70% attrition grind.
   - **Internal Power Source & Reload Scaling:** They carry their own contained energy in sealed biochemical cartridges. Reloading scales with cartridge size: **Small Cartridges = Reload 1** (1 Action), **Medium Cartridges = Reload 2** (2 Actions), and **Large Cartridges = Reload 3** (3 Actions / Full Turn).
   - **Universal Neither Utility:** Because they carry their own fuel/reaction mass, they **can operate anywhere within the Neither or Zone**, including deep outlands, wild caverns, and ruins completely detached from municipal power grids.
   - **Throughput Cost & Tag Full Firing Rate:** Cost **2 to 3 AP** per Strike with a matching Discipline Tag (`3 to 5 AP` untagged). Matching Discipline Tags also **waive the `+1/+2/+3` Enhancement Tier AP escalation** on weapons, ensuring trained operators can maintain their full firing rate (2+ attacks per round) as their weapons are upgraded to *Niracrest Striking* or *Mitheral Greater Striking*.

2. **Radiant Power Weapons (Generator-Coupled / Induction Grid):**
   - **Generator Requirement:** These weapons possess no internal battery and require an active external generator broadcasting wireless inductive energy through the Neither to charge the weapon's capacitors.
   - **Spire Converter Module Mandate & Regulation:** Within Spires, radiant weapons are **legally mandated to incorporate a detachable Converter Module** to interface with and harness the ambient radiant grid. Without this module, induction coupling is broken and the weapon is inert. Converter modules are **regulated identically to military cartridges** (banned from interior wards without King's Rite or emergency orders, locked in guardhouses, and checked out via gate depots).
   - **Prohibition on Non-Intended Receiver Usage & Illicit Weaponization:**
     - **Non-Intended Receiver Use is Illegal:** Under Crown and Municipal Law, **using any radiant power receiver (such as those in street lamps, domestic heaters, industrial winches, or artisan tools) for any purpose other than its licensed, intended function is strictly illegal**—and **even more so when diverted to power weapons**.
     - **Weaponizing Radiant Energy (Major Criminal Felony):** Any item, jury-rigged adapter, or modified receiver that **converts ambient radiant energy into a weaponized item or discharge outside of authorized legal usage is highly illegal and carries major criminal penalties** (prosecuted as grid sabotage, unlawful armament, and high felony/treason, resulting in severe prison/penal-mine sentences, asset confiscation, and family ruin).
   - **Grid Bleed vs. Dynamic Frequency Shifting:**
     - *Illicit Low-Power Converters:* Because radiant energy bleeds heavily through urban spires from streetlights and transit machinery, bootleg converter modules are physically easy for a skilled tinkerer to assemble illicitly, though doing so is a **major felony**. Even then, these crude modules can only siphon **low-power radiant energy** (operating at reduced low-power effectiveness).
     - *Dynamic Frequency Shifting:* Early NeitherTeck engineers instituted an essential security measure: municipal and fortress dynamos continuously modulate the **frequency and resonance pattern of broadcast radiant power**.
     - *Deliberate Bulk & Constant Monitoring:* Due to the extreme danger of unregulated high-energy radiant discharge, **High-Energy Radiant Converters are heavily regulated and intentionally manufactured to be extremely large, bulky, and visually conspicuous** (preventing easy concealment). Each unit is closely tracked, stamped with security hallmarks, and strictly monitored on checkout logs.
     - *Generator Energy Release Pattern Alterations:* Spires and stationary dynamos can alter their master pattern of energy release. When this occurs, **all high-radiant converters across the grid require manual/technical adjustments to synchronize with the new pattern**. Any converter not calibrated to the new release pattern is instantly decoupled from high-energy flow.
     - *High-Radiant Calibration & Skill Requirement:* Drawing maximum current for **High-Power Level operation** requires an official **calibrated converter module** that follows the generator's shifting frequency pattern, as well as an operator with the **technical skill** (e.g., trained in NeitherTeck/Arcana/Crafting) to synchronize and adjust the weapon. Without a calibrated module and operator skill, a radiant weapon cannot tap the saturated high-energy fields in spires or near dynamos.
   - **Two Operational Effectiveness Levels:** A radiant weapon can operate at two performance levels depending on proximity to the generator:
     - **High-Power Level (Spire Core & Outpost Generator Proximity):** Requires an active broadcast grid, a **calibrated converter module tracking dynamic frequencies, and operator skill**. Saturation allows the weapon to discharge at maximum capacity, dealing full listed damage and range with optimal capacitor cycling.
     - **Moderate / Low-Power Level (Airships, Extended Perimeters & Uncalibrated/Bootleg Converters):** On military airships, in perimeter zones (up to 5× distance), or when using bootleg/uncalibrated converter modules, radiant energy uptake is limited. Weapons operate at reduced baseline effectiveness (e.g., –1 damage die size, –10 to –20 ft range, or half fatal bonus) due to weaker capacitive step-up.
     - **Beyond 5× Distance:** Outside 5 times the high-power radius, radiant coupling collapses entirely and the weapon becomes completely inert.
   - **Throughput Cost:** Cost **3 to 6 AP** per Strike, strictly scaling with weapon caliber and capacitive draw.

## 4. Auxiliary Mundane Attachments & Melee Fallbacks

While NeitherTeck energy firing mechanisms are completely dead without power or cartridges, ranged weapons are physical assemblies of steel, hardwood, and brass that can be fitted with mundane close-quarters combat attachments `[Guns & Gears p.152]`:

- **Affixed Bayonet (Rifles & Longarms):**
  - Standard physical bayonet blade (or serrated spade) affixed beneath the barrel.
  - **Damage:** **1d6 Piercing** (or Slashing), Agile, Two-Handed.
  - **Mundane & Unpowered (0 AP):** Requires zero AP, zero cartridges, and zero power grids. If the rifle’s Neither capacitors die, deplete, or enter pure Aither, the weapon can still be wielded as an effective mundane spear/bayonet.
- **Reinforced Grip / Pistol Whipping (Pistols & Hand Emitters):**
  - Heavy brass, weighted steel pommels, or ribbed battery frames.
  - **Damage:** **1d4 Bludgeoning**, Agile, Finesse.
  - **Mundane & Unpowered (0 AP):** Allows any pistol or hand emitter to be swung in close quarters as a light club without spending AP, functioning even when completely inert.

## 5. Special Weapon Traits

### Cumbersome (Heavy Harness Bracing)
High-powered two-handed Neither weapons are heavy, top-heavy assemblies woven with thick capacitive conduits, grounding straps, and high-voltage stabilizing frames that require continuous, two-handed physical bracing and posture control.
- **Turn-Long Free-Hand Lockout:** If a character discharges, aims, or fires a weapon with the **Cumbersome** trait on their turn—or intends to do so during that turn—**they cannot use any action, reaction, or activity that requires a free hand for that entire turn (both before and after firing).**
- **Prohibited Actions on that Turn:** Grappling, Tripping, Shoving, Disarming, *Snagging Strike*, throwing an unarmed strike/punch, using *Battle Medicine*, drinking an elixir/serum, drawing an item/grenade from a belt, or manipulating tools with a free hand.
- **Permitted Actions:** Stride, Step, Take Cover, Aim (*Sniper's Aim*), use non-hand class directives (*Lucrative Incentive*, *Analytical Assessment*, *Demoralize*), or Interact to reload the heavy weapon using both hands.

### Radiant Induction
A weapon with this trait has no magazine or internal cartridge. It draws power wirelessly from an active municipal or Airship radiant dynamo.
- **Unlimited Ammo:** As long as you remain within an active radiant broadcast field (Tier 1 or Tier 2), ammunition is unlimited.
- **Capacitive Cycle (1 Round):** After firing, internal capacitors require 1 full round to recharge from the radiant field before discharging again.
- **Dead in Tier 3:** The weapon is completely non-functional outside radiant broadcast perimeters.

### Cartridge X & Poor-Quality Battlefield Weapons
Holds a sealed biochemical cartridge containing **X** shots. Firing individual shots from a loaded cartridge requires 0 reload actions between shots. Once all **X** shots are expended, replacing a spent cartridge requires Interact actions based on the cartridge size:
- **Small Cartridge:** **Reload 1** (1 Action)
- **Medium Cartridge:** **Reload 2** (2 Actions)
- **Large Cartridge:** **Reload 3** (3 Actions / Full Turn)

#### Shield & Off-Hand Reload Penalty (+1 Action)
Because replacing a chemical cartridge demands precision two-handed manipulation (unsealing pressurized valves, aligning contact prongs, and locking the casing), wielding a shield in your off-hand creates physical interference:
- **+1 Reload Action with a Shield:** **Wielding any shield adds +1 Interact Action to any cartridge reload** (e.g., reloading a Small Cartridge pistol with a shield strapped to your arm requires **2 Actions** instead of 1).
- **Unequipping Alternative:** A character can avoid this ongoing +1 action penalty only if they spend an action to unequip/drop the shield, perform the standard reload with unburdened hands, and spend an action to re-equip the shield.
- **Tactical Impact:** Gun-and-shield combatants (like Breacher Soldiers) lean heavily on high-capacity rotary cylinders (6 to 9 shots) or slide clips (15 pulses) to finish the firefight before ever having to cycle a fresh cell mid-combat.

#### Poor-Quality Cartridge Weapons (20% Reload Overheat / Burnout)
Standard Spire-grade energy weapons do not suffer random misfires. However, **poor-quality cartridge weapons** (battlefield salvage, cheap Warrens knockoffs, worn militia surplus, or Orc-captured rifles) often show up on the battlefield:
- **20% Burnout Chance on Reload:** Every time a poor-quality cartridge weapon is **reloaded**, there is a **20% chance (1–4 on 1d20)** that delicate internal components overheat or burn out from the fresh capacitor surge, rendering the energy weapon inoperable.
- **10-Minute Field Repair:** A **Clockwork Engineer, Artisan**, or character with the appropriate technical/Crafting skills can spend **10 minutes** to attempt a **Field Repair check**:
  - **Success:** The weapon is restored and can be used again.
  - **Failure:** The damage cannot be fixed in the field; repairing the weapon requires time and access to a **full Clockwork Workshop**.
