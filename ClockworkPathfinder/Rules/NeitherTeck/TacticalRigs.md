## 1. Overview of Tactical Rigs & Concoctions

Beyond direct energy weapons, NeitherTeck and Clockwork devices allow characters to deploy defensive fields, biomechanical enhancements, powered exoskeletons, and volatile chemical reactions. Equipment is split between **Passive Loadout items** (which draw from your Passive Capacity once per day) and **Active Surge items** (which consume Active Surge AP on Actions or Reactions).

---

## 2. Passive Tactical Rigs (Committed from Passive Loadout Capacity)

*These items provide continuous protection, environmental survival, or mechanical assistance. They are equipped during daily prep and consume your **Passive Capacity**. They do not consume or interact with your combat Active Surge AP pool.*

$$\mathbf{\text{Passive Loadout Capacity}} = \mathbf{\text{Key Attribute Modifier}} + \mathbf{\lfloor \tfrac{\text{Level}}{2} \rfloor} + \mathbf{\text{Class Base 4}}$$
*(Key Attribute: Track A uses STR/CON; Track B uses DEX/CON; Track C uses INT/STR/CON).*

### Base Armor-Weight Modifiers
Installing defensive systems into lighter garments requires less load-bearing support, reducing total Passive Point expenditure:
- **Unarmored / Explorer's Silks:** **–2 Passive Points** (minimum 1 point).
- **Light Armor (Padded Coat, Duster, Wire Shirt):** **–1 Passive Point** (minimum 1 point).
- **Medium Armor (Constable Vest, Cuirass, Hazmat Canvas):** **+0 (Standard Cost)**.
- **Heavy Armor (Full Plate Harness):** **+2 Passive Points**.
