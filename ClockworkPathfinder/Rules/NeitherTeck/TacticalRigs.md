## 1. Overview of Tactical Rigs & Concoctions
 
 Beyond direct energy weapons, NeitherTeck and Clockwork devices allow characters to deploy defensive fields, biomechanical enhancements, and volatile chemical reactions. Equipment is split between **Passive Loadout items** (which draw from your Passive Capacity once per day) and **Active Surge items** (which consume Active Surge AP on Actions or Reactions).
