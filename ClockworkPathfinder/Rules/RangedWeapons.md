# Ranged Weapons — Firearms & Cartridge Weapons

- **System:** Pathfinder 2e Remaster `[Player Core p.278–283, Guns & Gears p.150–154]`
- **Status:** **Modified** (Gunpowder weapons boosted by +1 damage die type; Neither weapons duplicated with 1/2 range and doubled magazine capacity via cartridges)
- **Precedence:** Rules in `AISummary/00_RulesFoundation.md` and world facts in `AISummary/WorldSummary.md`.

---

## 1. Setting Overview & Firearm Categories

In the Clockwork Cities setting, firearms fall into two distinct mechanical and environmental categories:

### A. Traditional Gunpowder Weapons (Aither / Elven Weapons)
- **Origin:** Manufactured predominantly by High Elves in the northern peaks and pure atmosphere of the **Aither**.
- **Physics:** Rely on traditional black powder combustion.
- **Damage Boost:** All traditional gunpowder weapons have their base damage die **boosted by 1 die size** (and their Fatal trait die increased accordingly), reflecting devastating ballistic lethality.
- **Environmental Hazard:** Combustion is wildly erratic and hazardous in the corrupting medium of the **Neither** and the **Zone**. When fired in the Neither or Zone, a critical failure or severe complication roll risks a catastrophic misfire or powder explosion.

### B. Neither Cartridge Weapons (Spire / Neither Weapons)
- **Origin:** Modern military technology developed approximately 40 years ago in Arclean and the Great Spires.
- **Physics:** Fire specialized chemical, pneumatic, or spark-primed sealed cartridges that function reliably in the damp, corrupting fog of the Neither.
- **Half Range:** Due to atmospheric drag in the heavy Neither and compact propellant charges, all Neither weapons have **half the range increment** of their gunpowder counterparts.
- **Double Magazine Capacity:** Because they feed from internal spring-loaded cartridge clips or multi-chamber magazines, Neither weapons can fire **double the number of shots before needing to be reloaded**.

## 5. Special Weapon Traits & Mechanics

### Cartridge X (Trait)
A weapon with the **Cartridge X** trait holds a sealed, spring-tensioned feed or multi-chamber clip containing **X** rounds. 
- You can fire the weapon **X times** before needing to reload.
- Once all **X shots** are fired, spending the weapon's listed **Reload action** ejects the spent casing/clip and slots in a fresh cartridge magazine.
- If a character has the **Slinger's Reload** class feature, their tactical reload action completely swaps and primes the cartridge clip.

### Ammunition & Cartridge Logistics
- **Standard Gunpowder Ammo (Aither):** Loose black powder and lead shot. Cheap, stable indefinitely if kept dry, but highly volatile/unreliable if used in the Neither.
- **Neither Cartridges:** Self-contained metal-and-lacquer casings with catalytic primers. 
  - **Perishability:** Neither cartridges remain stable for approximately **3 to 6 months** before the chemical primer degrades and goes inert.
  - **Moisture & Fog Resistance:** Fully sealed against Neither damp and fog corrosion during their operational shelf-life.
