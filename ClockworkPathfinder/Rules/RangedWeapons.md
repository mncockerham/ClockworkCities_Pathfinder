# Ranged Weapons — Firearms & Cartridge Weapons

- **System:** Pathfinder 2e Remaster `[Player Core p.278–283, Guns & Gears p.150–154]`
- **Status:** **Modified** (Gunpowder weapons boosted by +1 damage die type; Neither weapons duplicated with 1/2 range and doubled magazine capacity via cartridges)
- **Precedence:** Rules in `AISummary/00_RulesFoundation.md` and world facts in `AISummary/WorldSummary.md`.

---

## 1. Setting Overview & Firearm Categories

In the Clockwork Cities setting, firearms fall into two distinct mechanical and environmental categories:

### A. Traditional Gunpowder Weapons (Aither / Elven Weapons)
- **Origin & Manufacture:** Manufactured **exclusively by High Elves** in the northern peaks and clean atmosphere of the **Aither**. Only Elves possess the chemical knowledge and stable materials to synthesize and refine gunpowder.
- **Physics:** Rely on traditional black powder combustion, which operates reliably only within the pure air of the Aither.
- **Damage Boost:** All traditional gunpowder weapons have their base damage die **boosted by 1 die size** (and their Fatal trait die increased accordingly), reflecting devastating ballistic lethality in their intended element.
- **Extreme Environmental Hazard (Outside the Aither):** Outside the Aither (in the Zone and across all layers of the Neither), black powder combustion becomes violently unstable and suicidal to use. Every time a gunpowder weapon is fired outside the Aither, the shooter must roll on the **Gunpowder Combustion Volatility Table** (d10).
- **Spontaneous Ignition (20% Daily):** Gunpowder can travel safely through the Zone only within specially constructed, hermetically sealed insulated cases. Any exposed gunpowder, and **all gunpowder present in the Neither** (even in standard powder horns or paper cartridges), carries an unpreventable **20% daily chance of random spontaneous ignition** (flat roll 1–4 on a d20).
- **Elven Combustion Engines & Vehicles (~1900 Trains & Cars):** The High Elves also maintain a limited supply of circa-1900 internal combustion engines, operating motorcars and trains across their northern Aither territories (isolated populations may lack access to these resources). These engines share the exact same combustion vulnerability: outside the Aither, fuel combustion fails, causing engines to stall, backfire, or detonate. Reaching elven lands requires traversing the hazardous lightning squalls of the Zone, negotiating long fortified subterranean tunnels, or deploying amphibious airships capable of becoming sea-sailing vessels in northern waters. Between this technological exclusivity and bottlenecked routes, the Elves remain completely secure from invasion.

### B. Neither-Tech Energy Weapons (Cartridge vs. Radiant Weapons)
- **Origin & Nature:** Modern spire energy weapons developed approximately 40 years ago in Arclean and the Great Spires.
- **Non-Projectile Energy Projection:** These are **not traditional projectile or ballistic weapons**. They discharge directed beams or bolts of **Electricity (Arc)** or kinetic **Force (Pulse)**.
- **Environmental Operating Constraints (Neither vs. Zone vs. Aither):**
  - **Neither Environment (100% Operational):** Operates at full power in all layers of the Neither.
  - **The Zone (1/2 Damage or 1/2 Duration):** Atmospheric lightning flux disperses energy; damage and duration are **halved**.
  - **Pure Aither (Completely Inoperable):** Deprived of Neither particles, capacitive circuits discharge inertly with zero effect.
- **Two Distinct Mechanical Classes of Neither Weapons:**

  1. **Portable Cartridge Weapons (Expeditionary / Close-Quarters):**
     - Powered by self-contained, high-density **Neither Cartridges** utilizing a secret Spire biochemical catalyst.
     - **Half Range (15–35 ft):** Without an ambient broadcast field to collimate the beam, energy quickly disperses in the dense Neither fog.
     - **Tripled Capacity (3 to 15 shots):** High action economy before requiring a fresh cartridge.
     - **Strict Logistics:** Cartridges **cannot be field-recharged in the wild** and must be returned to a Spire or rare fortified military outpost. Once empty, they cannot fire.

  2. **Radiant Grid Weapons (Spire Defense & Zone Airships Only):**
     - Possess no internal battery cylinders. Instead, they feature inductive receptor antennae that draw continuous power wirelessly from an active **Radiant Power Grid**.
     - **Spire Mandate & Converter Modules:** Within civilized Spire jurisdictions, all radiant weapons are **legally mandated to incorporate a detachable Converter Module** to interface with and draw current from the municipal grid. Without an authorized converter module installed, the weapon's induction coupling is mechanically disabled and the weapon is inert.
     - **Ambient Bleed vs. Dynamic Frequency Shifting:** Due to ambient radiant bleed from spire utilities, simple bootleg converter modules can be easily crafted to siphon low-power current (powering civil tools or firing weapons at reduced low-power effectiveness). However, as an early NeitherTeck security measure, dynamos continuously modulate the **frequency and resonance pattern of stored/broadcast energy**.
     - **Deliberate Bulk & Constant Monitoring:** Due to the catastrophic risks of unregulated high-radiant energy, **High-Energy Radiant Converters are heavily regulated and intentionally manufactured to be extremely large, bulky, and visually conspicuous** to prevent illicit concealment. Even authorized units are closely monitored and tracked under strict guardhouse checkout logs.
     - **Generator Pattern Adjustments:** Spires and generator installations can alter their master pattern of energy release. Whenever this occurs, **all high-radiant converters require manual/technical adjustments to synchronize with the new pattern**; unadjusted or bootleg units decouple instantly.
     - **High Radiant Requirements (Calibrated Modules & Operator Skill):** Unlocking saturated **High-Power Radiant operation** requires an official **calibrated converter module** synchronized to follow the shifting frequency pattern, along with the **operator skill** to manage dynamic phase tuning. Without both, devices cannot access the higher energy density available in spires and near generators.
     - **Regulation Equivalent to Cartridges:** Because radiant weapons inside a Spire draw virtually unlimited ammunition from the ambient air, **converter modules are regulated under the exact same strict legal framework as military cartridges**. Possessing an active converter module in interior municipal wards without a sovereign warrant (King's Rite) or military dispatch is a severe crime (sabotage/treason). In peacetime, converter modules are locked down in guardhouses and checked in/out at gate depots or garrison armories under the same rules as cartridges.
     - **Full Standard Range (60–150 ft):** The ambient resonant field continuously sustains beam coherence across full ballistic distances.
     - **Unlimited Ammunition Supply:** Draws limitless energy straight from the air as long as an active converter module is engaged and the broadcast dynamo is operational.
     - **Capacitive Cycle Limitation (1 Round Recharge):** Because drawing wireless energy from ambient radiant fog takes time to step up voltage through the weapon's induction coils, standard radiant weapons **must cycle for 1 full round after firing to recharge internal capacitors before they can discharge again**. 
       - *Tactical Impact:* A gunner cannot fire every single turn without alternating weapons, coordinating firing lines (rank-and-file volley fire where Rank 1 shoots while Rank 2 cycles), or switching to secondary actions (taking cover, giving commands, aiming).
       - *Future Variants:* Experimental or lighter rapid-cycle radiant weapons can cycle faster by trading off damage output (e.g., smaller dice or shorter range).
     - **Zero Ground Mobility in the Wild:** Standard mobile surface dynamos do not exist—generating a radiant field requires massive municipal dynamos anchored to deep bedrock or volcanic vents. **The only mobile radiant generators that exist are carried aboard massive military Airships cruising directly inside the Zone**, tapping raw atmospheric storm energy.
     - **Geopolitical & Tactical Reality (Absolute Defense, No Conquest):** Radiant weapons grant Spire city-states formidable defense against barbarian hordes, Orc sieges, and surface incursions. However, **they cannot be used offensively to invade or conquer the wastes**, because the weapons instantly go dead the moment soldiers march beyond the perimeter of the Spire's broadcast grid. Out in the wild, Spires must rely on scarce cartridge weapons, bows, and steel.

## 5. Special Weapon Traits & Mechanics

### Gunpowder Combustion Volatility (Firing Outside the Aither)
Whenever a traditional gunpowder weapon is fired **outside the pure atmosphere of the Aither** (in the Zone, Upper Neither, Lower Neither, or subterranean depths), black powder reacts unpredictably with ambient atmospheric corruption. 

Immediately before resolving the attack roll, the shooter must **roll 1d10 on the Gunpowder Volatility Table**:

| d10 Roll | Combustion Outcome | Mechanical Effect |
|:---:|---|---|
| **1 – 3** | **Under-Fire (Squib / Fizzle)** | The powder detonates weakly with a hollow hiss. The force is so small that the bullet barely clears the barrel muzzle, dropping harmlessly to the ground. **The shot is completely wasted, no attack roll is made, and the ammunition is spent.** The weapon remains loaded with soot and must be cleared (1 Interact action) before reloading. |
| **4 – 6** | **Normal Fire** | The charge ignites within acceptable tolerances. Resolve the Strike and attack roll normally (utilizing the boosted damage die). |
| **7 – 10** | **Catastrophic Explosion** | The Neither causes the entire powder charge and breech to detonate instantaneously and violently. **The weapon is completely destroyed** (blown into useless scrap), the attack misses automatically, and the explosion **inflicts the weapon's normal boosted damage (including attribute modifier) directly to the person firing it** (Fortitude save DC 20 for half damage; no critical success possible). |

### Gunpowder Storage & Spontaneous Ignition
- **Exclusive Elven Knowledge:** The synthesis, purification, and balancing of black powder is a closely guarded secret possessed solely by the **High Elves**. Spires and human kingdoms cannot synthesize new gunpowder; all existing powder originates as Elven trade or diplomatic gifts.
- **Transporting Through the Zone:** Gunpowder can safely traverse the electrical squalls of the Zone **only if packed inside specially insulated, hermetically sealed brass transport cases**. Unsealed powder carried into the Zone immediately ignites on lightning static.
- **20% Daily Spontaneous Ignition in the Neither:** Even if packed in powder horns, cloth flasks, or paper cartridges, **all gunpowder present anywhere within the Neither has an unpreventable 20% daily chance of random spontaneous ignition** (GM or player rolls 1d20 once per 24 hours; on a roll of **1–4**, the powder explodes). An exploding horn or bandolier deals 3d6 fire and piercing damage per pound of powder carried to the bearer and adjacent creatures (DC 20 Reflex for half).
- **Tactical Reality:** Because of these devastating hazards, gunpowder firearms are virtually **never carried or fielded outside the highest noble circles of the Aither**. Across the spires, military regiments, constabularies, and explorers rely exclusively on modern **Neither Cartridge weapons**.

### Cartridge X (Trait)
A weapon with the **Cartridge X** trait holds a sealed, spring-tensioned feed or multi-chamber clip containing **X** rounds. 
- You can fire the weapon **X times** before needing to reload.
- Once all **X shots** are fired, spending the weapon's listed **Reload action** ejects the spent casing/clip and slots in a fresh cartridge magazine.
- If a character has the **Slinger's Reload** class feature, their tactical reload action completely swaps and primes the cartridge clip.

### Ammunition & Cartridge Logistics
- **Standard Gunpowder Ammo (Aither):** Loose black powder and lead shot synthesized by Elves. Stable indefinitely only if kept dry within the pure Aither; suicidal and prone to 20% daily spontaneous detonation in the Neither.
- **Two Tiers of Neither Energy Storage:**
  1. **Standard Civil & Domestic Cartridges (Low-Density):** Most non-weapon Neither technology (radiant lanterns, domestic winches, medical diagnostic tools, ventilation scrubbers) runs on standardized, low-density storage cells. These hold approximately **1/20th the energy density by size** of military-grade weapon cartridges.
     - *Advantage:* They can be recharged at **any standard stationary charging station**.
     - *Charging Station & Radiant Power Infrastructure:* Station dynamos are massive, heavily guarded industrial facilities operated in permanent settlements: the **Great Spires**, **Dwarven subterranean citadels**, and **Deep One enclaves**. 
       - Within these settlements, energy is distributed wirelessly through the Neither medium via **radiant power**.
       - **The Spire Advantage:** The **Great Spires that pierce through the Zone** are the **only places on the planet** where enough electrical energy is harvested directly from Zone lightning storms to broadcast radiant power across vast multi-mile urban sectors (powering public lighting, transit lifts, and charging stations simultaneously). Dwarven and Deep One generators rely on deep geothermal/chemical taps, covering much tighter municipal perimeters.
     - *Raider Vulnerability:* Mobile raiding warbands (whether Orc, Human, or outcast) do not possess mobile dynamos and cannot recharge in the field.
  2. **High-Density Military Weapon Cartridges (The Biochemical Secret):**
     - **More Than a Simple Battery:** High-capacity weapon cartridges are not merely electrical storage cells; their extreme energy density is unlocked by a **proprietary, highly classified biochemical compound** suspended in an internal catalytic matrix. This biochemical catalyst allows the cartridge to instantaneously concentrate and discharge massive bolts of coherent Neither energy (Arc and Pulse).
     - **Spire Production Monopoly:** The synthesis of this compound requires rare fungal enzymes, complex lab apparatuses, and specialized bio-chemists that exist **exclusively within the Great Spires**. Neither Dwarves, Deep Ones, Orcs, nor wilderness settlements possess the technology, resources, or trained Chirurgeon-alchemists to brew or stabilize this biochemical agent.
     - **Extreme Atmospheric Volatility (Direct Exposure Hazard):** Once synthesized, this volatile biochemical compound is **hyper-reactive to both Neither and Aither**. 
       - It remains stable only while hermetically sealed inside the insulated brass-and-lead cartridge casing.
       - If a cartridge casing is cracked, ruptured, or punctured and the liquid compound is **directly exposed to ambient Neither or pure Aither**, it undergoes instantaneous catalytic decomposition:
         - **Direct Neither Exposure:** Ignites in a violent chemical flash, triggering an immediate electrical short that detonates the cartridge (inflicting 2d6 electrical/acid damage in a 5 ft burst).
         - **Direct Aither Exposure:** Reacts violently with clean oxygen and solar radiation, foaming into a boiling, corrosive acidic sludge that dissolves metal linkages and permanently destroys the casing.
     - *Weapon Recharging Conversion:* While it is theoretically possible to daisy-chain and slow-charge a weapon cartridge from **twenty (20) standard low-density civil cartridges**, doing so only works if the internal biochemical substrate is intact. Once the biochemical fluid goes inert (after 3–6 months), civil dynamos cannot revitalize it—it must be returned to a Great Spire to be drained, cleaned, and refilled with fresh chemical compound.
     - *Tactical & Economic Reality:* Because weapon cartridges require massive infrastructure and secret spire chemistry to manufacture, **traditional Pathfinder weaponry (composite bows, crossbows, throwing axes, and spears) remains far cheaper, lighter, and vastly more practical** for wandering raiders, subterranean scouts, and outcasts.
- **Three Standard Weapon Cartridge Sizes & Weights:**
  - **Small Weapon Cartridge (~200 grams / Bulk L for 5):** Compact cells used in **pistols, pocket emitters, and light sidearms** (e.g., Neither Arc Pocket Emitter, Service Arc Pistol, Dueling Pistol). Holds **3 shots** (or **9 smaller charges** in a rotary cylinder).
  - **Medium Weapon Cartridge (~400 grams / Bulk L for 2, Bulk 1 for 5):** Elongated power cells used in **rifles, carbines, and medium tactical weapons** (e.g., Neither Service Arc Rifle, Twin-Arc Rifle, Slide Pulse Pistol). Holds **3 to 6 heavy rifle discharges** (or **15 rapid pulses** in a slide magazine).
  - **Large Weapon Cartridge (~800 grams / Bulk 1 for 2):** Heavy heavy-gauge industrial capacitors used in **arquebuses, cannons, and heavy breaker weapons** (e.g., Heavy Arc Arquebus, Force Breaker, Dragon Projector). Holds **3 massive high-output discharges**.
- **Spire Recharging Fees:** Municipal utility substations and guild armories recharge depleted military cells for standard fees: **1 sp per Small cell, 2 sp per Medium cell, 4 sp per Large cell**.
- **Operational Shelf-Life:** A charged Neither cartridge holds its capacitive charge reliably for **3 to 6 months** before the catalytic seal degrades and the energy harmlessly dissipates into ambient static.
