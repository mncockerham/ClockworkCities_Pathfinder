## 1. Historical Overview & The Neither Cycles

The history of the world is shaped by the great millennial oscillations of the **Neither**—a dense, corrupting, energy-bearing atmospheric medium that dramatically alters the laws of chemistry and physics. 

Before the advent of the Neither, human history aligned precisely with our own world. Over thousands of years, as the Neither expanded, crested into cataclysmic storms, and ebbed, humanity was forced to biologically adapt, engineer revolutionary materials (Neithercrete), harness wireless radiant power from the electrical squalls of the Zone, and build megalithic 14,000-foot spires to pierce into the clean Aither.

## 2. The Great Eras

### Era I: The Emergence & The Seven Great Kingdoms (1715 – 2110)

- **1715 — The First Mists:**
  - Up until approximately 1715, world history progressed identically to our own.
  - In 1715, the **Neither** first appeared. Initially dismissed as industrial coal smog or strange volcanic vapors, it spread steadily across low-lying terrain and oceans.
- **1730 — The Physics Breakdown (Combustion Instability):**
  - By 1730, scholars and engineers noticed that the fundamental laws of nature were warping in Neither-saturated zones.
  - **Unstable Combustion:** Black powder became violently erratic—sometimes detonating with 5 to 6 times its normal explosive force, and other times squibbing with only 1/6th of its expected power. 
  - Standard internal combustion engines, oil burners, and firearms became wildly unreliable and suicidal to operate.
- **1740 — The Zone Appears at the Equator:**
  - Around 1740, an impenetrable atmospheric boundary of perpetual electrical discharges—**The Zone**—formed along the equator. 
  - The Zone slowly migrated northward, tearing apart surface structures. Crossing the Zone on foot or horse was lethal: depending on transit speed, **5% to 20% of all crossing attempts ended in complete destruction by lightning arcs**.
  - Deep subterranean caves and stone tunnels were discovered to be the only safe means of crossing beneath the Zone without being incinerated.
- **1810–1890 — The Tropics Submerge & The Deep Neither:**
  - By 1810, the tropics lay entirely submerged beneath heavy Neither. Living within the early Neither caused chronic illness, respiratory inflammation, and biological strain (comparable to severe toxic smog).
  - By 1890, the **Deep Neither** began to pool in low valleys and ocean basins.
  - By 1910, the equator became completely impassable as the Zone and Deep Neither converged. Even moderate exposure to the Deep Neither proved rapidly fatal to unadapted baseline humans.
- **The Seven Great Kingdoms:**
  - In response to the encroaching miasma, world civilization consolidated into **Seven Great Kingdoms**: **English**, **German**, **Scandinavian**, **Western American**, **China**, and **India**.
  - For roughly 300 years, the wealthy elites lived lives of luxury in the extreme north or on pristine mountain summits in the clean air. The working masses were forced into the encroaching Neither, constantly pushed northward ahead of the rising Deep Neither.
- **The Dawn of Neither-Tech:**
  - During this era, humanity synthesized the very first Neither-tech: **Neithercrete** (incredibly tough solidified Neither stone), **radiant wireless energy grids**, and early stationary **energy arc weapons** powered directly by dynamos tapped into the Zone.
  - Rumors began to spread of fugitive populations surviving deep within the deadly Neither, escaping the imperial grasp of the Great Kingdoms.

### Era II: The First Age of Storms & Speciation (2110 – 2400)

- **2110–2400 — The Cataclysmic Surge:**
  - The Neither surged to unprecedented heights, covering nearly the entire globe. Catastrophic Neither storms battered the surface.
  - The Zone swept across mountain summits and pushed past Iceland, destroying coastal cities and forcing civilization into the highest mountain enclaves and polar zones.
- **Human Biological Adaptation (The Speciation of Humanity):**
  - Humanity proved extraordinarily adaptable. Over several generations, groups stranded in different atmospheric layers biologically diverged:
    - **Humans (Type 3):** Clung to the high spires and valleys directly beneath the Zone, requiring sunlight and uncorrupted food.
    - **Orcs (Type 4):** Those abandoned in the Lower and Deep Neither adapted dense bone structure, heavy muscle, and cellular immunity to fungal toxicity, thriving in conditions that poisoned spire dwellers. By 2400, humans and orcs recognized each other as distinct populations.
    - **Dwarves (Type 5):** Miners and quarrymen who took shelter in deep underground stone vaults (~2600) developed cat-like low-light reflective optics, intense sunlight glare sensitivity, and mineral resilience, becoming the masters of subterranean survival.
    - **Deep Ones (Type 6):** Deep miners living over a mile underground in super-heated geothermal depths adapted to boiling temperatures and high-spore atmospheres.
    - **Elves & Half-Elves (Types 1 & 2):** Isolated mountain and polar survivors who never touched the Neither preserved classical sciences and gunpowder chemistry (High Elves), while transition-zone aeronauts adapted to electrical squalls (Half-Elves).
- **Fungal Mutation:**
  - Plant life underwent parallel mutations: specialized fungal strains (led by **Sycelium**) learned to feed directly on ambient Neither particles, converting miasma into dense carbohydrates and proteins.

---

### Era III: The First Age of Proto-Spires & The Long Static Age (~2400 – 3900)

- **The Fall of the Storms & Proto-Spires:**
  - As the first storm age receded, the Zone dropped southward, uncovering mountain plateaus.
  - Humans built the **first proto-spires**—structures the size of modern 500-foot skyscrapers—using early dwarven masonry to live safely under the Zone rather than fighting it.
- **Technological Regression (~1580 Baseline):**
  - High pre-collapse sciences collapsed. Civilized technology regressed to roughly **circa-1580 standards** (wooden sailing ships, watermills, steel blades). Sailing vessels reconnected England, Northern Europe, and North America.
- **Orc Tribal Warbands:**
  - Vast nomadic tribes of Orcs dominated the low Neither valleys and plains, resisting the expansion of human proto-spires. Trade between Europe and Asia was virtually severed, relying on sporadic, dangerous caravan routes through hostile orc territory where news was years out of date.
- **1,500 Years of Static Warfare:**
  - For roughly 1,500 years, civilization stagnated across approximately **50 regional successor kingdoms**. Proto-spires were besieged, sacked by orc hordes, recaptured, and rebuilt in endless cycles.
  - With each cycle, ancient knowledge, manufacturing techniques, and historical records were erased. Eastern North America collapsed into total wilderness; Northern Europe lost contact with the wider world.
- **The Icelandic Enclave:**
  - The only exception was **Iceland**, where isolated human spires remained untouched by the brutal surface wars. In their cold, Neither-poor environment, the Icelanders preserved unbroken historical archives and high engineering theory.

---

### Era IV: The Second Great Neither Storm & Dwarven Boom (~3900 – 4100)

- **The Zone Moves North:**
  - A second mega-storm struck. The Zone swept north past Iceland, and the Neither pooled to suffocating depths.
  - Surface Orcs were driven northward out of the boiling deep valleys.
- **The Rise and Fall of Dwarven Citadels:**
  - Taking advantage of the dense Neither, Dwarven civilization reached its golden age, erecting massive subterranean citadels across the northern continent.
  - However, as the storm peaked and passed, massive hordes of Orcs swept south, devastating the dwarven cities and reclaiming the land. Iceland retreated into total hermetic isolation.

---

### Era V: The Great Neither Drop & The Age of Spires (~1500 – 1100 Years Ago)

- **The Neither Drop (~1500 Years Ago):**
  - Approximately 1,500 years ago, ambient Neither levels dropped dramatically across the globe.
- **The Neithercrete Engineering Revolution:**
  - Emerging from their isolation, Icelandic scholars allied with surviving Dwarven stone-engineers. Combining dwarven subterranean chemistry with Icelandic structural mathematics, they perfected **Neithercrete**.
  - Previously, proto-spires were limited to 500 feet. With advanced Neithercrete, spires could suddenly be engineered **miles high into the sky**, piercing straight through the Zone into the pure Aither.
- **The Golden Age of Spires (~200 Years of Megalithic Building):**
  - Over the next two centuries, the great spires of the modern world—including **New Avalon**—were raised.
  - Spires allowed enormous populations to concentrate in small geographic footprints, dominating surrounding lands. However, travel between spires through the surface Neither remained treacherous and slow, limiting kingdoms to compact spheres of control.

---

### Era VI: The Third Great Storm & Fungal Botanical Collapse (~1100 – 900 Years Ago)

- **The Fungal Collapse:**
  - Just as kingdoms stabilized, a catastrophic biological collapse struck: the handful of agricultural fungus strains cultivated in the spires suddenly succumbed to necrotic rot and died out.
  - Massive global famine ensued. Over an 80-year period, nearly **80% of human spire populations starved to death**.
- **The Deep Botanical Revolution:**
  - Desperate for survival, the Dwarves and subterranean **Deep Ones** bred and stabilized hundreds of resilient fungal species from deep geothermal shafts.
  - By crossbreeding wild strains, they established stable culinary and industrial fungi—most notably **Sycelium** (which processes into **Mela**)—ensuring that a single blight could never wipe out civilization again.
- **The Re-Emergence:**
  - As this third storm faded, the Icelandic enclaves and Half-Elven sky-traders began cautiously distributing agricultural strains and Neither-technologies to rebuild human dominance across the continents.

---

### Era VII: The Airship Revolution & The Age of Conquest (700 – 300 Years Ago)

- **700 Years Ago — The Invention of Airships:**
  - The single greatest geopolitical revolution in modern history: the development of **Zone-riding Airships**.
  - By harnessing the radiant electrical energy and atmospheric buoyancy planes of the Zone, airships bypassed surface orc warbands, jagged mountain ranges, and toxic Neither valleys.
  - Cruising at **twice the speed of ocean sailing ships (~100 miles per day)**, airships connected distant spires across continents without relying on waterways.
- **The Age of Conquest (700 to 300 Years Ago):**
  - Warfare transformed overnight. Armadas of airships sailed through the Zone to drop elite assault regiments directly onto the vulnerable summits of rival spires.
  - Invaders severed ground supply caravans and starving out spires until the desperate populace surrendered or degenerated into feral fungus eaters. Early low-caliber arc cannons were mounted on ship bows to conduct orbital bombardments.

---

### Era VIII: The Era of Great Arc Cannons & 200 Years of Peace (300 – 40 Years Ago)

- **300 Years Ago — The Perfection of Great Arc Cannons:**
  - Spire engineers perfected colossal **Zone-Powered Great Arc Cannons**. 
  - Mounted directly on Neithercrete spire parapets and powered by massive municipal dynamos tapping the Zone, these gargantuan weapons possessed devastating range and explosive power far exceeding any battery an airship could carry.
- **The Impregnable Fortress:**
  - Spires became virtually invulnerable to airship assault. Any approaching hostile fleet was obliterated miles before reaching docking distance.
  - For over 200 years, the civilized world enjoyed remarkable geopolitical stability. Spires occasionally fell to catastrophic internal revolts or massive subterranean orc breaches, but city-to-city conquest became obsolete.

---

### Era IX: The Modern Era — Cartridge Weapons & The New Arms Race (40 Years Ago – Present)

- **40 Years Ago — Neither Cartridge Weapons Developed:**
  - Engineers synthesized a classified biochemical catalyst, enabling high-density rechargeable **Neither Cartridges**.
  - Discharging concentrated pulse force and arc energy, these cartridge firearms fired at ranges **nearly twice that of traditional Great Arc Cannons**.
- **The Resurgence of Conquest (Last 20 Years):**
  - Cartridge weapons shattered the 200-year stalemate: attacking siege fleets could now bombard a defending spire's arc cannon batteries from outside defensive range.
  - However, weapon cartridges rely on rare chemicals and decay within 3 to 6 months. Attacking armies must outlast a defending city’s cartridge stockpiles, then storm defenses.
  - Over the past 20 years, several major spires have fallen to this tactic. Geopolitical boundaries shifted into a tense, tripartite cold war between the regional powers of the Isles and Channel:
    - **Arclean:** The industrial hegemon holding most of England and an embattled continental foothold along the Normandy coast (*Rouen Spire*). Its northern border is split evenly between Derdore (East) and the Highland/Western Orcs (West).
    - **Derdore:** Anchored in northeast Scotland (Edinburgh and Aberdeen) and the continental Dutch Twin Spires (*The Hague and Rotterdam*), maintaining an overseas North Sea pincer against Arclean while facing Orc pressure from the west.
    - **Feydial:** Commanding Ireland, Northern Wales (*Caernarfon* — the only direct touching point with Arclean), and a southwestern foothold in Plymouth/Cornwall (buffered from Arclean by the 2nd Orc Zone).
    - **The Isle of Man Spire:** Maintained as an independent sovereign city-state; if any single power attempts to seize it, the other two intervene immediately to preserve the balance of the Irish Sea.
    - **The Unspoken Doctrine of the Isles:** Though Arclean, Derdore, and Feydial wage endless covert warfare and privateering, all three fundamentally operate under an unwritten law: *"I against my brother, my brother and I against our cousins, our cousins and ourselves against the world."* Any continental empire foolish enough to attempt an amphibious or aerial conquest of the British Isles would face the immediate, unified, and devastating fury of all three powers.
    - **The European Proxy Web:** Coastal European kingdoms covertly sponsor privateers against Arclean in Normandy, while Lowland and Rhenish powers quietly support Arclean against Derdore's Dutch Spires.
    - **The Orc Strategic Ceiling:** Orc warbands in northern Scotland and the 2nd Orc Zone (Severn/South Wales) exert such lethal pressure that any inter-kingdom war beyond a 1v1 triggers catastrophic frontier breaches.
- **Recent Discoveries & Global Horizons (Long-Range Airship Breakthroughs):**
  - Within the last 50 and 10 years, innovations in airship engine efficiency extended flight ranges by months.
  - **Europe, Scandinavia & The Barcelona Limit:** Spires extend across Europe—from **Norway, Sweden, and Finland** (where small Elvish colonies also dwell in the high Norwegian mountain peaks) down to the **southernmost European Spire just south of Barcelona**, where higher mountain elevations and taller spires are required to pierce the deepening southern Neither.
  - **The East & The Americas:** East of the European kingdoms lies the vast **Land of the Warbands**, though exotic trade goods and rumors confirm that **farther East (Asia) another civilization with its own Great Spires exists** (with no direct communication yet). Meanwhile, although the **Elves and Half-Elves have never officially acknowledged it**, slips and rare glimpses indicate that **some form of civilization still survives in the Americas**, though even less is known about it than the East.

## 3. The Modern Demographics & Food Economy

### Urban Population Concentration
- **The Great Spires (~87% of Humanity):** The overwhelming majority of human civilization lives inside megalithic spires rising from sea level up to 14,000 feet tall.
- **The High-Low Divide:** The sovereign nobility and Great Houses live on open-air mountain summits in the clean Aither, while the impoverished masses labor in the sunless, damp lower circles and Warrens, harvesting fungi and never seeing natural sunlight.
- **Urban Immobility:** Due to the extreme expense of transit and the lethal Neither fog outside, the vast majority of spire citizens live and die within the single city of their birth.

### The Foundation of Life: Sycelium & Mela
- Without fungal agriculture, urban spire civilization would instantly collapse.
- **Sycelium:** The universal staple fungus. It grows with astonishing speed in complete darkness, nourished by municipal waste and Neither moisture.
- **Crop Yield Metrics:**
  - A compact **10 ft × 10 ft × 10 ft cultivation cube** produces enough raw Sycelium to feed **15 people indefinitely**.
  - However, eating raw Sycelium causes gradual digestive distress, mental fog, and biological decay in standard humans.
- **Mela (The Safe Standard):**
  - When raw Sycelium is dried, milled, and chemically processed to purge active Neither spores, it yields **Mela flour**.
  - A 10×10×10 ft cube yields enough processed Mela to safely feed **10 people**. Mela forms the universal staple bread, porridge, and rations consumed across all Great Spires.
