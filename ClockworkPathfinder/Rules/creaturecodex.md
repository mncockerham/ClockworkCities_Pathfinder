## 1. Encounter Philosophy & Design Principles

In the Clockwork Cities setting, creatures and adversaries are not random magical aberrations pulled from planar realms. Every creature encountered is the direct result of:
1. **The Biological Drift of the Humanity Spectrum:** The 6 environmental adaptations (High Elves, Half-Elves, Humans, Orcs, Dwarves, Deep Ones) and their tragic aberrant states (Goblins, Vampires, Beasts, Neanderthals, Troglodytes).
2. **Neither-Mutated Fauna & Apex Predators:** Wildlife exposed to dense Neither concentrations, Neither Storm surges, or subterranean breeding in the deep fungal beds.
3. **Clockwork & NeitherTeck Automatons:** Mechanical sentries, mining constructs, and defense colossi engineered during Era 2 or Era 3.
4. **Civil & Martial Factions:** Constabulary squads, ward enforcers, syndicate cutthroats, guild strike teams, and the dreaded King's Guard.

### The 15 / 70 / 15 Threat Distribution
Every encounter in Clockwork Cities aligns with the core tactical distribution:
- **15% — "We Are Heroes" (Showcase / Low Risk):** Lower-threat adversaries (slum pickpockets, solitary goblins, starving tunnel rats, lone sentries). Little to no risk of death; validates player build progression and allows martial classes to demonstrate combat mastery without burning finite cartridges or auto-injectors.
- **70% — "The Grind" (Attrition & Meaningful Peril):** Coordinated constable squads, orc surface raiders, Warren ghoul packs, wild Neither-wolves, and armed syndicate enforcers. Burns valuable resources (cartridges, shield Hit Points, medical supplies). Tactical mistakes push characters into **Critical (days of bed rest)** or **Mortal (weeks to recover)** wound thresholds.
- **15% — "The Crucible / Boss Fights" (Lethal Extremes):** Apex mutant horrors, deep-zone monstrosities, Clockwork Colossi, high-tier Vampire patriarchs, or King's Guard extermination cadres. High risk of permanent character death, limb loss, or equipment destruction.

### Tactical Realism: Self-Preservation vs. Fighting to the Death
In nature, predators and wild beasts do not throw their lives away recklessly against heavily armed humanoids:
- **The 50% Disengagement Rule (Survival Instinct):** Unless cornered, actively defending a litter/nest, or gripped by berserk rage, **most natural animals and uncorrupted beasts will not fight to the death**. Any non-raging creature that is reduced to **50% of its base Hit Points** (bloodied) will actively search for an escape route, break line of sight, and disengage to lick its wounds and hunt easier prey.
- **Cornered Beasts Fight to the Death:** If a wounded creature has its escape routes cut off, is trapped against a wall, in a pit, or pinned by weapons, its biological fight-or-flight triggers an all-out, desperate fight for survival. Cornered beasts fight savagely to the death with reckless fury.
- **Raging & Aberrant Predators:** Creatures possessing active rage states (*Feral Adrenaline Surge* from the Brute class), mind-breaking Neither-rot, or starvation madness (such as The Beasts or crazed Vampires) ignore self-preservation and fight to the death.

## 2. Aberrant Human Sub-Types (The Broken Spectrum)

When humanity lingers in incompatible atmospheric densities without proper diet or filtered air, the mutagenic stress of the Neither breaks their biological equilibrium, producing tragic, dangerous sub-species.

```
[Human Type 3]  ──(Severe Neither Saturation)──>  Goblin (Warrens/Slums)
[Human Type 3]  ──(Cannibalism + Neither Rot)──>  Vampire (Spire Shadows)
[Orc Type 4]    ──(Starved of Neither Diet)────>  Neanderthal (Cognitive Decay)
[Orc Type 4]    ──(Flesh Need / Feral Drift)───>  Beast (Feral Rage)
[Goblin/Orc]    ──(Millennia in Lightless)─────>  Troglodyte (Tombs/Abyss)
```

### A. Warren Goblins (Type 3a / 4c)
- **Origin & Habitat:** Humans permanently warped by generations of Neither exposure in the deepest Spire sumps, or Orcs damaged by prolonged Aither seepage. They make up **90% of the Warrens population** (~200,000 in New Avalon).
- **Physical Traits:** Stunted, wiry frames, mottled greenish-grey skin, enlarged light-sensitive eyes, sharp scavenger teeth.
- **Behavior & Tactics:** Cowardly alone, vicious in coordinated packs. They utilize ambush tunnels, scrap-iron shivs, crude blowguns firing Sycelium neurotoxins, and grease traps.
- **Rules Profile (Level -1 to Level 1 Skirmisher):**
  - **Traits:** Humanoid, Goblin, Aberrant.
  - **Low-Light Vision / Scent:** Scent (imprecise) 30 ft.
  - **Pack Tactics:** Deals an extra `1d4` precision damage against any flat-footed target engaged by an ally.
  - **Warren Scramble (1 Action):** Step up to 10 feet through difficult terrain or small crawlspaces without provoking reactions.
  - **Common Gear:** Crude scrap cleavers (`1d6` slashing), blowguns coated in Sump Rot toxin (DC 15 Fortitude or Sickened 1).

### B. Spire Vampires (Type 3b)
- **Origin & Habitat:** Humans who have lost the ability to metabolize Aither or standard food due to Neither-induced digestive decay. Triggered and maintained by consuming humanoid flesh and fresh type 1–3 blood. Hidden within high Spire society, merchant counting houses, and elite clubs (~5,000 worldwide).
- **Physical Traits:** Elegant, pale, outwardly indistinguishable from wealthy humans or half-elves until starved. Starvation causes rapid physical wasting, blackened veins, and uncontrolled predatory frenzies.
- **Behavior & Tactics:** Highly intelligent, sophisticated, and socially integrated. They rarely fight openly; they use wealth, blackmail, and charmed or coerced thralls. If cornered, they fight with supernatural speed and vicious claw strikes.
- **Rules Profile (Level 3 to Level 7 Infiltrator / Skirmisher):**
  - **Traits:** Humanoid, Vampire, Undead-Adjacent (Biologically Living Mutant).
  - **Blood Siphon (1 Action):** When grappling a humanoid, deals `2d6` piercing + `2d6` persistent bleed. The vampire regains HP equal to half the bleed damage dealt.
  - **Neither-Stoked Reflexes:** Quickened (gains 1 additional action per turn used only to Stride or Strike).
  - **Social Camouflage:** Gain a +4 circumstance bonus to Deception checks to pass as an unmutated noble or merchant.
  - **Weakness:** Cannot consume Aither food; starving for >7 days inflicts Drained 2 per day until consuming fresh blood.

### C. The Beasts (Type 4a)
- **Origin & Habitat:** Orc lineages whose neither-metabolism drifted, requiring the regular consumption of sentient humanoid flesh (Types 1–5) to retain their cognitive faculties and emotional self-control (~10,000 worldwide). Found in deep surface trenches, ruined hamlets, and dark Warren recesses.
- **Physical Traits:** Massive, hulking orcish brutes with elongated jaws, hunched spines, corded muscle, and bone-thickened brow ridges.
- **Behavior & Tactics:** When well-fed, they are cunning hunters and merciless tacticians. When starved, they devolve into mindless berserkers driven purely by hunting instinct.
- **Rules Profile (Level 2 to Level 5 Brute):**
  - **Traits:** Humanoid, Orc, Beast.
  - **Feral Mauling:** Bite (`1d10` piercing) and Claws (`1d8` slashing with Agile).
  - **Cognitive Frenzy:** When bloodied (below 50% HP), the Beast gains a +2 status bonus to attack rolls and Fortitude saves, but takes a -2 penalty to AC and cannot take manipulate actions.
  - **Apex Pounce (2 Actions):** Strides up to double its speed and makes a Strike; on a hit, the target is knocked Prone.

### D. Neanderthals (Type 4b)
- **Origin & Habitat:** Orc populations completely cut off from Neither-corrupted sustenance over multiple generations (~20,000 worldwide).
- **Physical Traits:** Robust, heavy-boned, suffering from neurological trauma and slowed cognition, but possessing immense physical endurance.
- **Behavior & Tactics:** Reclusive hunter-gatherer clans living on high mountain plateaus or sterile quarantine zones. Defensive and wary of outsiders.
- **Rules Profile (Level 1 to Level 3 Soldier):**
  - **Traits:** Humanoid, Orc.
  - **Unyielding Frame:** High physical Fortitude saves (+8 to +12); gains Temporary HP when entering combat.
  - **Crushing Blows:** Wields massive stone mauls and clubs (`1d10` bludgeoning + Shove).

### E. Troglodytes (The Tomb Dwellers)
- **Origin & Habitat:** Goblins or surface creatures trapped for centuries in the subterranean **Tombs (Levels 1–9)** beneath New Avalon and deep continent shafts (~10,000 worldwide).
- **Physical Traits:** Completely blind, translucent leathery hide, elongated sensory whiskers, deafening screeches, and toxic glandular musk.
- **Behavior & Tactics:** Lurk in total darkness; strike from ceiling fissures and air shafts. They drag victims into lightless drop-pits.
- **Rules Profile (Level 2 to Level 4 Lurker):**
  - **Traits:** Humanoid, Troglodyte, Aberrant.
  - **Echolocation & Tremorsense:** Precise tremorsense 60 ft; completely immune to visual effects, but vulnerable to sonic attacks.
  - **Stench Glands (Aura 10 ft):** Living non-troglodytes entering the aura must make a DC 18 Fortitude save or become Sickened 1 (Sickened 2 on critical failure).
  - **Wall-Crawler:** Climb speed equal to its base land speed (25 ft) without needing athletics checks on Neithercrete or stone.

## 3. Surface & Wasteland Fauna (Neither Mutants)

Outside the protective grounding shields of Great Spires and walled hamlets, the natural fauna has been aggressively reshaped by the ambient Neither medium and periodic Neither Storms:

### A. Sump Crawlers & Fungal Scorpions
- **Habitat:** Sewage outlets, agricultural mushroom beds, and Warren drainage basins.
- **Description:** Chitinous arthropods growing from 3 to 8 feet in length, their carapaces encrusted with bioluminescent moss and parasitic fungal blooms.
- **Combat Role:** Low-level skirmishers and environmental hazards (Level 0 to Level 2).
- **Key Abilities:** Caustic spit (acid damage), grasping pincers (Grab), and stinging tails that inject necrotic Neither-spores (Persistent Acid + Enfeebled 1).

### B. Neither-Whorl Wolves (Gildrod Stalkers)
- **Habitat:** Lower Neither wastelands, abandoned surface hamlets, and desolate rail corridors.
- **Description:** Canine predators whose muscle fibers are interwoven with dense, metallic neither-strands. Their hides are prized by tanners as **Gildrod Leather** (+2 Tier armor material).
- **Combat Role:** Mid-tier pack hunters (Level 3 to Level 5 Skirmisher).
- **Key Abilities:**
  - **Phase Dart (1 Action):** Short-range spatial hop through neither-currents (15 ft teleport that does not trigger reactions).
  - **Synchronized Pack Flank:** When two or more wolves threaten a target, the target cannot use reactions and suffers a -2 circumstance penalty to AC.
  - **Tearing Jaws:** `2d8+4` piercing + Knockdown.

### C. Neither Storm Drakes (Zone Skimmers)
- **Habitat:** The turbulent lower fringes of the Zone (Layer 2).
- **Description:** Leathery, serpentine winged reptiles with insulated dielectric scales that actively feed on atmospheric lightning. They flock around airships and spire lightning arresters.
- **Combat Role:** High-tier aerial predators (Level 6 to Level 9 Striker).
- **Key Abilities:**
  - **Lightning Absorption:** Immune to electrical damage; taking electrical damage grants the drake +10 ft fly speed and charges its breath weapon.
  - **Arc Discharge Breath (2 Actions):** 40-foot cone of lightning and concussive thunder (`6d12` electrical damage, DC 26 Reflex save for half). Recharges in `1d4` rounds.
  - **Wing Buffet & Disorient:** Forces aerial crew members to make Acrobatics checks or be blown off airship decks.

### D. Apex Mutant: The Neither Colossus (Grave Behemoth)
- **Habitat:** Deep surface chasms, dead spire ruins, and epicenter craters of ancient cataclysms.
- **Description:** Mountainous, quadrupedal horrors composed of calcified bone, fossilized Neithercrete, and boiling Neither sludge.
- **Combat Role:** Crucible / Boss Encounter (Level 12 to Level 16 Behemoth).
- **Key Abilities:**
  - **Atmospheric Dead Zone (Aura 60 ft):** Squeezes surrounding air; counts as **Hostile Air (Air Shift Level 3)** to all living creatures within the aura.
  - **Ground-Shaking Slam:** `3d12+12` bludgeoning with 15-foot reach + Greater Push.
  - **Cartridge Resonance Burst:** Any active NeitherTeck cartridge within 30 feet has a 25% chance per turn to violently discharge or jam.

## 4. Clockwork & NeitherTeck Automatons

Manufactured guardians, defense hulls, and legacy maintenance machines still prowling sealed vaults, Spire power shafts, and royal grounds:

### A. Spire Maintenance Drone (Clockwork Spider)
- **Role:** Utility & Municipal Repair (Level 0 to Level 1).
- **Armament:** Arc-welding torches (`1d6` fire + `1d4` electricity) and hydraulic cutting shears.
- **Traits:** Construct, Clockwork, Mindless.
- **Behavior:** Ignores citizens unless repairs are interrupted or municipal conduits are vandalized.

### B. Enforcer Clockwork Sentinel
- **Role:** Heavy Riot Suppression & Vault Defense (Level 4 to Level 6 Soldier).
- **Description:** Bipedal 8-foot brass-and-iron automaton powered by heavy clockwork spring-drums and internal Neither-steam flywheels.
- **Armament:** Heavy Steam Piston Cudgel (`2d10+6` bludgeoning) and forearm-mounted double-barrel Cartridge Carbine.
- **Mechanics:**
  - **Hardness 8 / HP 75:** Resists physical attacks; vulnerable to electrical disruption.
  - **Steam Vent (Reaction):** When struck in melee, releases a blinding cloud of boiling steam (10 ft emanation; `2d6` fire + Concealed).
  - **Wind-Up Dependency:** Requires key winding every 24 hours. A critical hit to the rear escapement gears can jam the automaton's drive spring (Stunned 1).

### C. Royal Clockwork Colossus (The Iron Wardens)
- **Role:** Crown Defensive Super-Constructs (Level 10 to Level 14 Brute/Tank).
- **Description:** 20-foot tall war machines guarding the King’s Citadel, high Aither lift gates, and royal bank vaults.
- **Armament:** Dual rotary radiant cannons and steam-powered crushers.
- **Mechanics:** Immune to non-magical mental effects, poison, disease, and standard bleeding. Armed with integrated High-Radiance emitters (`3d10` radiant force).

## 6. Environmental Hazards & Neither Anomalies

The environment itself is frequently as dangerous as any predatory creature:

- **Neither Gas Pockets (Sump Fumes):** Flammable and toxic. Any spark ignites a `4d6` explosion in a 20-foot radius. Inhaling fumes inflicts Sickened 2 and Drained 1.
- **Neither Static Arc (Zone Fringe):** Random ground arcing along high exposed catwalks. Deals `4d12` electrical damage (DC 22 Reflex save for half); metal armor inflicts a -2 penalty to the save.
- **Sycelium Spore Bloom:** Stepping on a mature spore pod releases a 15-foot cloud of choking, psychoactive spores. Living humanoids must make a DC 19 Fortitude save or suffer hallucinatory confusion (Confused condition for `1d4` rounds).
- **Neither Fog Surge:** A sudden rolling cloud of super-concentrated dark Neither. Triples environmental air penalty levels and deals `2d8` persistent void/acid damage per minute to unadapted flesh.

## 9. Modular Enhancement Templates (Scaling to Any Threat Level)

By applying one or more **Modular Enhancement Templates** (`ClockworkPathfinder/Classes/NPC/creature class/`), a Storyteller can instantly scale any unmodified animal up or down along eight biological and environmental vectors:

```
                          ┌──> [Brute (STR)]: +HP, flat dmg, knockback, resistance, fury
                          ├──> [Stalker (DEX)]: +AC, reflex, speed, stealth, pounce, precision & bleed
                          ├──> [Juggernaut (CON)]: Massive HP, fast healing, trample, immune to stun
                          ├──> [Venomous (Poison)]: Injected fangs, spitting neurotoxins, skunk musk cone
[Mundane Base Animal] ────┼──> [Biochemical (Acid/Blast)]: Caustic phlegm spit, acid splash, death detonation
                          ├──> [Galvanic (Static/Elec)]: Shock recoil vs metal, lightning absorption, arc flash
                          ├──> [Pack (Swarm Synergy)]: Synchronized tripping, mob flanking, harrying circles
                          └──> [Burrower (Subterranean)]: Burrow speed, tremorsense, sinkholes, drag-under
```

### Storyteller Guidelines: Flexibility, Adjustment & Lethality Cautions
- **All Changes are Optional:** The modifiers and abilities in these templates are flexible guidelines, not rigid shackles. Storytellers should feel completely free to add, remove, or decrease abilities, or boost specific traits to make the creature fit their exact narrative and tactical goals.
- **Creatures Weaker Than Expected Are Not a Problem:** If you err on the side of making a creature slightly too weak, it simply validates player capability and allows the heroes to feel powerful and competent.
- **Caution When Adding Stacking Buffs (Adjust CR / Level):** Be extremely careful when adding multiple stacked abilities, extra damage dice, or heavy physical resistances. An overloaded template can quickly turn a manageable encounter into an unintended Total Party Kill (TPK). Whenever you grant substantial extra capabilities or resistances, **adjust the creature's effective Level (CR)** upward to accurately reflect the increased threat and encounter budget.
- **The "Real Storyteller Out" (When in Doubt, Have It Flee):** If an encounter turns unexpectedly lethal or drags out, remember the golden GM safety valve: **just have the creature flee**. Ignore the technical "cornered" or "raging" restrictions entirely—act like you already quietly adjusted down the creature's remaining hit points, describe it suffering an agonizing wound or panicking from gunshot noise, and let it break through an opening and escape into the mist or shadows. The players win the encounter, survive, and keep their hard-won resources.
- **The "Act Like You Planned It" Rule (Rewarding Creative Logic):** While not something to hand out automatically for every encounter, **whenever players devise a genuinely clever plan based on biological logic, act like you had considered that exact vulnerability all along**:
  - *Example (The Flare Trick):* When players face an underground, lightless predator and say: *"Hey, this creature lives in total darkness and can't stand bright light—I'm striking a magnesium signaling flare right in its face!"* 
  - Instead of flipping through rules or arguing damage, nod knowingly and narrate the triumph: *"The beast recoils violently, screeching in pain and squinting through milky cataracts. It takes a –2 circumstance penalty to all attack rolls as long as that bright flare burns."*
  - This reinforces the illusion of deep systemic planning, empowers player agency, and strongly encourages players to **think creatively in-character rather than just rolling dice**.

### The 8 Modular Vectors at a Glance:
1. **The Brute (Strength / Hypertrophic Power):** Adds heavy HP, flat damage increases, physical resistance, and reckless charge feats. *(See `Brute.md`)*.
2. **The Stalker (Dexterity / Speed & Ambush):** Adds +1 to +3 AC, exceptional Reflex, +5 to +25 ft Speed, and stacking `+1d4` to `+4d6` precision damage and persistent bleed dice. *(See `Stalker.md`)*.
3. **The Juggernaut (Stamina / Dense Constitution):** Adds massive HP buffers (+20 to +225 HP), physical resistances, and Fast Healing 2 to 12 per round with Trample mechanics. *(See `Juggernaut.md`)*.
4. **The Venomous (Poison / Glandular Neurotoxins):** Adds multi-stage poisons (DC 16 to DC 31) delivering Clumsy, Enfeebled, Paralysis, or Blindness via bites, quills, or skunk-style musk sprays. *(See `Venomous.md`)*.
5. **The Biochemical (Acid / Volatile Combustion):** Mirrors Mad Bomber alchemical ordnance—pressurized caustic spit (30–70 ft), acid splash, armor-melting bile, and explosive death ruptures. *(See `Biochemical.md`)*.
6. **The Galvanic (Electrical & Dielectric Scales):** Bio-electric electrocytes delivering shock recoil against metal weapons, lightning absorption, static bursts, and cartridge-disrupting hums. *(See `Galvanic.md`)*.
7. **The Pack (Swarm & Tactical Coordination):** Pack flanking precision, coordinated takedowns, harrying circles preventing steps, and mob pins. *(See `Pack.md`)*.
8. **The Burrower (Subterranean & Fossorial Ambush):** Spade claws, burrowing speeds through soil and Neithercrete, precise tremorsense, collapsing sinkholes, and drag-under suffocation. *(See `Burrower.md`)*.

### Example Transformations:
- **Realistic Apex Grizzly Bear (Base PF2e Lv 3 + Brute 4 = Level 7 Boss):** 110 HP (+18 Temp HP), AC 18, Jaws +15 (`2d12+9`), Claws +15 (`2d8+9`), Resistance 3, *Sudden Charge* and *Knockback*.
- **Zone Static Drake (Base Eagle/Raptor Lv 3 + Galvanic 4 = Level 7 Aerial Menace):** 75 HP, AC 25, Fly 60 ft, Immune to electricity, Arc Discharge Cone (20 ft, `3d6` electricity), retaliatory shock against metal blades.
- **Warren Gutter-Hound (Base Hunting Dog Lv 0 + Pack 2 + Venomous 1 = Level 3 Pack Threat):** 38 HP, AC 17, Pack Flank (+`1d6` precision), tripping jaws coated in Stage 1 neurotoxin (DC 16 Fort save or Enfeebled 1).
- **Deep Sump Burrower (Base Boar Lv 2 + Burrower 3 = Level 5 Subterranean Horror):** 68 HP, AC 20, Burrow 20 ft, Precise Tremorsense 30 ft, erupts beneath targets with *Burst From Below* and collapses ground into sinkholes.
