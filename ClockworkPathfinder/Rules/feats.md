## 1. Core Feat Rules & Setting Constraints

1. **Secular & Physical Plausibility:** Supernatural magic, divine miracles, ki mysticism, and planar physics do not exist in Clockwork Cities. Any base Pathfinder feat that produces supernatural effects is either **Removed** or **Modified** into a mechanical, biochemical, or physical equivalent.
2. **Bonus Stacking Enforcement `[Player Core p.12]`:** Bonuses of the same type (**Circumstance**, **Item**, or **Status**) never stack; only the highest applies.
3. **Ancestry Feat Selection Rules (`[AncestriesAndHeritages.md]`):**
   - All six major populations share the same human species genome adapted across the 6 atmospheric strata (**Strata 1 High Elf** to **Strata 6 Deep One**).
   - Characters may select ancestry feats **only** from the **Universal Human Heritage** list and their **chosen Biological Heritage's** feat list.
   - *Blurred Heritage & 1-Shift Tolerance:* Characters who want mechanical access to adjacent-layer biological traits take the 1st-level *Expanded Adaptability (1-Shift Tolerance)* or *Blurred Heritage* feats on their heritage list.
4. **Calibration Feat Single-Purchase Cap:** Activation Point (AP) calibration feats (*NeitherTeck Tuning*, *Advanced Calibration*, *Master Calibration*, *Supreme Calibration*) can **only be purchased once** across a character's entire build (cannot be double-stacked via base class and archetype).
