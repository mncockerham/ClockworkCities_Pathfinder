## 1. What is the Neither? (Physics, Thermodynamics & Biology)

The **Neither** is a pervasive, heavy, semi-metaphysical atmospheric medium that blankets the world. It is not merely fog or industrial smog; it is an active physical, thermodynamic, and biological force that fundamentally alters chemistry, energy transfer, and the human genome:

### Thermodynamic Combustion Instability (The Energy Absorption & Surge Cycle)
- **Conservation of Energy vs. Temporal Surge:** In the Neither, fuel burns and theoretically releases its normal total energy, but **combustion output is temporally decoupled and violently unstable**:
  - As fuel burns (whether a simple wooden log, coal fire, or gunpowder charge), anywhere from **5% to 90% of the released thermal/kinetic energy is instantly absorbed into the surrounding Neither medium**.
  - This absorbed energy does not dissipate permanently; instead, it is **returned at a completely random, unpredictable rate back into the exact physical fire location over the subsequent 3 minutes**.
  - Consequently, an open flame or combustion chamber may suddenly dwindle to an anemic flicker as 90% of its heat is absorbed, only to violently detonate moments later when the Neither dumps all of the stored energy back in an instantaneous, catastrophic surge.
- **Why Standard Engines & Guns Fail:** This erratic energy absorption/surge cycle makes standard internal combustion engines and black powder firearms completely useless and suicidal outside pure Aither. Gunpowder weapons unpredictably squib or explode with 5 to 6 times their rated pressure.
- **Specialized Clockwork Steam Dampeners:** To harness steam power in the Neither, engineers design specialized **Clockwork Steam Engines** equipped with heavy mechanical kinetic flywheels, secondary expansion buffers, and spring-loaded pressure dampeners. These intricate mechanisms absorb, store, and mechanically smooth out the extreme, erratic spikes of Neither heat, maintaining a steady, even production of steam for factory winches, locomotive pumps, and industrial presses.

### Radiant Energy Conduction
- While hostile to instantaneous combustion, the Neither is an extraordinary conductor of steady electromagnetic energy. Radiant power harvested directly from the electrical squalls of the Zone broadcasts wirelessly across multi-mile municipal districts through the Neither medium, powering wireless streetlights (~60W) and industrial machinery without physical power lines.

### Cellular Mutagenesis & Rapid Speciation
- The Neither acts as an aggressive biological mutagen on any living creature. 
- Organisms living within a given Neither density undergo continuous physical, neurological, and cellular mutation **until their bloodline fully adapts to that specific atmospheric density**. 
- Once adapted, the lineage stabilizes into a distinct physiological type (e.g., Orcs in the Lower Neither, Dwarves in the lightless mines). However, migrating into an incompatible layer restarts the mutagenic stress cycle, resulting in systemic breakdown or aberrant drift (Goblins, Beasts, Neanderthals).

### Neither-Formed Substances & Solar/Aither Decay
- The Neither synthesizes and nourishes unique biochemical and mineral compounds—including **Neithercrete**, deep fungal cultures, Sycelium spores, and specialized catalytic reagents.
- **Vulnerability to Sunlight & Clean Air:** These substances are fundamentally bound to the Neither medium. When removed from the Neither and directly exposed to **natural unfiltered sunlight** or the pristine air of the **Aither**, their molecular matrices break down:
  - Neithercrete slowly crumbles, peels, and loses structural integrity over months of exposure to direct sunlight.
  - Highly reactive Neither biochemicals, pharmaceutical catalysts, and raw fungal extracts decay, dissolve, or oxidize into useless inert sludge within **a matter of seconds to days** when exposed to sunlight or pure Aither air unless preserved in hermetically sealed, light-proof vacuum canisters.

## 2. The Atmospheric Strata

The world is divided vertically and geographically into six distinct atmospheric environments:

```
[Pure Clean Air / High Summits]
  Layer 1: The Aither               (High Elves: Unadapted, pure oxygen, classical tech)
═══════════════════════════════════ (The Zone: Permanent lightning, static cloud sea)
  Layer 2: The Zone / Transition    (Half-Elves: High spire rims, aeronaut skyships)
───────────────────────────────────
  Layer 3: Upper (Light) Neither   (Humans: Sun & moon visible; great spires & hamlets)
───────────────────────────────────
  Layer 4: Lower (Dark) Neither    (Orcs: Sun obscured; surface valleys & wastelands)
───────────────────────────────────
  Layer 5: Lightless / Under-Earth  (Dwarves: Complete darkness; 100 to 5,000 ft deep)
───────────────────────────────────
  Layer 6: The Deep / The Mist      (Deep Ones: 5,000 to 12,000 ft deep; >100°F steam)
[Deep Subterranean / Boiling Core]
```

### Layer 1: The Aither (Clean Air / High Summits)
- **Altitude / Location:** Altitudes above the Zone (~12,000 to 14,000+ ft), high mountain peaks, and the uncorrupted far northern polar realms (Iceland).
- **Physical Environment:** Completely pristine, sunlit, clean oxygen. The Neither is completely absent.
- **Native Population:** **High Elves (Type 1)**. 
- **Environmental Realities:** Wireless radiant power completely fails here; lighting requires refined mushroom oil or rare wired circuits. Traditional gunpowder combustion functions with lethal, boosted precision (+1 die size).

### Layer 2: The Zone (The Dead Zone / High Spire Rims)
- **Altitude / Location:** The violent atmospheric transition boundary (20 to 150 ft thick, swelling to 300+ ft during storms) separating the Aither from the Neither.
- **Physical Environment:** A chaotic, glowing cloud sea of perpetual electrical discharges, static squalls, and severe barometric turbulence.
- **Native Population:** **Half-Elves (Type 2)** (Aeronauts, sky-captains, and spire-rim riggers).
- **Environmental Realities:** Provides the uniform buoyancy plane for airships (~100 mi/day) and powers the radiant grids of Great Spires. Neither-tech weapons operate at 1/2 damage or 1/2 duration. Crossing on foot carries a 5% to 20% risk of total destruction from lightning strikes.

### Layer 3: Upper (Light) Neither (Spire Civilization)
- **Altitude / Location:** From sea level up to ~12,000 ft in urban regions, encompassing the middle circles of Great Spires and agricultural hamlets.
- **Physical Environment:** Amber, golden, or grey atmospheric haze through which the sun and moon remain clearly locatable.
- **Native Population:** **Humans (Type 3)** (~87% of humanity).
- **Environmental Realities:** The baseline of civilized society. Wireless radiant power operates at full efficiency. Requires periodic sunlight exposure and uncorrupted crops (Mela flour) to stave off chronic lung rot and biological fatigue.

### Layer 4: Lower (Dark) Neither (The Surface Wastelands)
- **Altitude / Location:** Surface lowlands, sunken trenches, wild river basins, and valleys across the mainland.
- **Physical Environment:** A dense, suffocating purple-black haze where the sun is completely unlocatable and visibility drops to a few dozen yards.
- **Native Population:** **Orcs (Type 4)** (Surface clans, stonemason warbands).
- **Environmental Realities:** Highly toxic to unadapted humans without sealed breather masks. Rich in wild Sycelium mushrooms, subterranean rodents, and Neither-hardened beasts. Neither Storms strike with unmitigated surface fury.

### Layer 5: Lightless Neither / Subterranean Mines
- **Altitude / Location:** Underground shafts, foundational quarries, and deep caverns situated between 100 and 5,000 feet below the surface.
- **Physical Environment:** Total, pitch-black darkness saturated by heavy Neither condensation, mineral dust, and explosive sump gases. Direct unfiltered sunlight causes severe pain (*Dazzled*).
- **Native Population:** **Dwarves (Type 5)**.
- **Environmental Realities:** Low-light cat-optic vision operates perfectly off faint bioluminescent mosses. Neither Storms lose intensity underground (at 300+ ft depth, storms deal zero mechanical damage, reduced to an audible subterranean groan).

### Layer 6: The Deep / The Mist (The Boiling Depths)
- **Altitude / Location:** Deep geothermal trenches between 5,000 and 12,000 feet beneath the earth.
- **Physical Environment:** Super-heated subterranean atmosphere exceeding 100°F (often reaching 140°F), filled with thick sulfuric mists, boiling mineral sumps, and hyper-reactive fungal spores.
- **Native Population:** **Deep Ones (Type 6)**.
- **Environmental Realities:** Clean surface Aither air burns the respiratory tract of Deep Ones like acid. The source of the world's master pharmaceutical and agricultural fungal cultures.

## 3. Atmospheric Shift Distance (Air Penalty Levels)

Every character has a **Native Atmospheric Layer** determined by their biological Heritage. When operating in an atmospheric layer other than their native environment without an insulated breather apparatus or sealed cabin, they accumulate **Air Shift Levels**:

```
[Native Layer]  ──>  Adjacent (1 Level)  ──>  Distal (2 Levels)  ──>  Hostile (3 Levels)  ──>  Lethal (4+ Levels)
```

### Air Shift Distance Table

| Character Heritage | Layer 1: Aither | Layer 2: The Zone | Layer 3: Upper Neither | Layer 4: Lower Neither | Layer 5: Lightless | Layer 6: The Deep |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **High Elf** | **Native (0)** | 1 Level | 2 Levels | 3 Levels | 4 Levels | 5 Levels |
| **Half-Elf** | 1 Level | **Native (0)** | 1 Level | 2 Levels | 3 Levels | 4 Levels |
| **Human** | 2 Levels | 1 Level | **Native (0)** | 1 Level | 2 Levels | 3 Levels |
| **Orc** | 3 Levels | 2 Levels | 1 Level | **Native (0)** | 1 Level | 2 Levels |
| **Dwarf** | 4 Levels | 3 Levels | 2 Levels | 1 Level | **Native (0)** | 1 Level |
| **Deep One** | 5 Levels | 4 Levels | 3 Levels | 2 Levels | 1 Level | **Native (0)** |

### Air Shift Level Descriptions & Condition Penalties:

Unlike dietary exposure which acts over weeks, breathing foreign atmosphere directly assaults the respiratory tract and cellular respiration. Beginning at **Air Shift Level 2**, the atmosphere actively imposes the **Clumsy**, **Enfeebled**, and **Drained** status conditions starting at condition value **2** and **doubling in severity for every level thereafter (Levels 2 to 5)**:

| Air Shift Level | Environmental Severity | Status Conditions Applied (Airborne Penalty) | Clinical Symptoms & Exposure Effects |
|:---:|---|---|---|
| **Level 0** | **Native Air (Equilibrium)** | *None* | Perfect respiratory equilibrium. Normal stamina, clear focus, zero atmospheric stress. |
| **Level 1** | **Adjacent Air (Not Optimal)** | **Clumsy 1, Enfeebled 1, Drained 1** *(after 1 month)* | Mild chronic respiratory irritation, morning fatigue, and persistent throat dryness. Imposes a –1 penalty to physical checks, DCs, and max HP (–1 × level) after 1 full month of unmitigated exposure; does not worsen further. |
| **Level 2** | **Distal Air (Severe Strain)** | **Clumsy 2, Enfeebled 2, Drained 2** | Violent coughing fits, burning retinas, chronic muscle tremors, and acute hypoxia. Max HP is reduced by 2 × level, and physical checks/DCs take a –2 status penalty. |
| **Level 3** | **Hostile Air (Cellular Shock)** | **Clumsy 4, Enfeebled 4, Drained 4** | Agonizing respiratory spasms, bloody sputum, severe physical wasting, and extreme lethargy. Max HP is reduced by 4 × level, and physical checks/DCs take a –4 status penalty. |
| **Level 4** | **Lethal Air (Tissue Necrosis)** | **Clumsy 8, Enfeebled 8, Drained 8** | Acute cellular necrosis and respiratory blistering. Max HP is reduced by 8 × level; physical movement is severely paralyzed. Exposed lung tissue begins to liquefy without sealed life support. |
| **Level 5** | **Abyssal / Void Shock** | **Clumsy 16, Enfeebled 16, Drained 16** | Catastrophic biological incompatibility (e.g., a High Elf in the boiling Deep, or a Deep One in pure sunlit Aither). Immediate collapse, organ failure, and rapid death within minutes without sealed pressurized breathers. |

## 4. Dietary Shift Distance (Food Penalty Half-Points)

Because food metabolisms vary across the six heritages, edible foodstuffs are classified by their atmospheric origin and Neither saturation rather than relying on a human-centric baseline. Consuming food outside your biological baseline introduces digestive and cellular stress:

- **Strata 1 Food (Aither Fare):** Deep-water ocean fish (caught below 150 ft), spire-top palace greenhouse produce, high-mountain orchard fruit, sun-ripened crops. Pristine, uncorrupted organic compounds.
- **Strata 2 Food (Zone & High Spire Fare):** Airship-grown hydroponics, outer high-terrace vegetables, shallow-caught Aither fowl, high-altitude game.
- **Strata 3 Food (Mela & Spire Working Fare):** **Mela flour** (chemically processed, detoxified Sycelium), shallow Neither river fish, cellar greens, spire-grazed livestock. Standard civilized diet.
- **Strata 4 Food (Raw Sycelium & Sump Fare):** Fast-growing raw fungus harvested in darkness, cave lichen, shallow neither-crabs, mineral sump water.
- **Strata 5 Food (Deep Cavern Strains):** Heavy reactive subterranean fungi, thermal vent moss, high-spore cave broth, underground cave fauna.
- **Strata 6 Food (The Deep / Void Strains):** Hyper-reactive geothermal vent lichen, sulfurous broth, deep abyssal spore strains.

### The 0.5 to 5.0 Food Shift System (Mixed Diets)

Because citizens and adventurers rarely eat a 100% pure diet, **Food Shift is tracked in 0.5-point increments** from **0.5 up to 5.0 shifts**:
- If your diet over a week consists **entirely** of food one full strata away from your digestive baseline, you accrue **1.0 Food Shift**.
- If your diet is a **mixture of proper and improper food** (for example, a human eating 50% safe Mela bread and 50% raw Sycelium mushroom scraps from the Warrens), you accrue **0.5 Food Shift**.
- Consuming mixed scrap further away or surviving on heavily incompatible rations scales in 0.5 increments: **0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 2.5, 3.0, 3.5, 4.0, 4.5, or 5.0 Food Shift**.
- *Legacy Note:* Historical human records referred to food origins as "Class 0 through Class 4" (using humans as the zero baseline). In modern unified mechanics, foodstuffs correspond directly to **Strata 1 through 6 origins**, with digestive impact measured strictly by the **0.5 to 5.0 shift distance** from the character's biological heritage.

### Native Dietary Baseline by Heritage:

| Heritage | Native Strata Baseline | Compatible Tolerance (0 Shift) | Example Foreign Shift Diets (0.5 to 5.0 Shifts) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **High Elf** | Strata 1 (Aither) | Strata 1 (100% Pure) | Strata 2 = 1.0 Shift; Strata 3 (Mela) = 2.0 Shifts; Strata 4 (Raw Sycelium) = 3.0 Shifts (Toxic) |
| **Half-Elf** | Strata 2 (Zone) | Strata 1 & 2 | Strata 3 (Mela) = 1.0 Shift; Mixed Strata 3 / Strata 4 = 1.5 Shifts; Strata 5 = 3.0 Shifts |
| **Human** | Strata 3 (Upper Neither) | Strata 2 & 3 (Mela) | Mixed Mela + Raw Sycelium = **0.5 Shift**; Pure Raw Sycelium = **1.0 Shift**; Strata 5 Deep Fungi = **2.0 Shifts** |
| **Orc** | Strata 4 (Lower Neither) | Strata 3 & 4 (Raw Sycelium) | Deep Cavern Fungi = 1.0 Shift; Mixed Mela + Aither Fish = 0.5 Shift; Pure Aither Produce = 2.0 Shifts (Enzymatic starvation) |
| **Dwarf** | Strata 5 (Caverns) | Strata 4 & 5 | Strata 3 (Mela) = 1.0 Shift; Strata 2 = 2.0 Shifts; Pure Aither Food = 3.0 Shifts (Enzymatic rejection) |
| **Deep One** | Strata 6 (The Deep) | Strata 5 & 6 (Deep Spores) | Strata 4 = 1.0 Shift; Strata 3 = 2.0 Shifts; Pure Aither Food = 4.0–5.0 Shifts (Violent metabolic shock) |

### Food Shift Mental & Social Penalties (Stupefied & Cognitive Decay)

While breathing foreign air attacks physical muscles and stamina (Clumsy, Enfeebled, Drained), consuming foreign food floods the bloodstream with incompatible fungal enzymes or biochemical toxins that directly poison the brain and nervous system.

Starting at **Food Shift Level 2**, foreign food imposes the **Stupefied** condition and a direct status penalty to **ALL Intelligence and Charisma checks and DCs** starting at **–2** and **doubling in severity with every level thereafter (Levels 2 to 5)**:

| Food Shift Level | Severity | Status Conditions Applied | Penalty to All Int & Cha Checks / DCs | Clinical Symptoms & Mental Degradation |
|:---:|---|---|:---:|---|
| **Level 0 to 0.5** | **Equilibrium** | *None* | **0** | Clear mental clarity, sharp social wit, perfect enzymatic balance. |
| **Level 1.0 to 1.5** | **Not Optimal** | **Stupefied 1** *(after 1 month)* | **–1 Status Penalty** | Mild brain fog, sluggish recall, and persistent digestive fatigue. Imposes a –1 status penalty to all Intelligence and Charisma skill checks, Perception, and Recall Knowledge after 1 month; does not worsen further. |
| **Level 2.0 to 2.5** | **Moderate Shift** | **Stupefied 2** | **–2 Status Penalty** | Brain fog, delayed recall, irritable social disposition. –2 status penalty to all Intelligence and Charisma skill checks, Perception, Recall Knowledge, and spell DCs. |
| **Level 3.0 to 3.5** | **Severe Shift** | **Stupefied 4** | **–4 Status Penalty** | Severe apathy, memory lapses, slurred speech, acute cognitive confusion. –4 status penalty to all Int and Cha checks/DCs; cannot critically succeed at cognitive tasks. |
| **Level 4.0 to 4.5** | **Toxic Poisoning** | **Stupefied 8** | **–8 Status Penalty** | Severe neural degeneration, hallucinatory mania, or near-catatonic stupor. Inability to maintain coherent conversation or articulate complex thoughts. |
| **Level 5.0** | **Catastrophic Shock** | **Stupefied 16** | **–16 Status Penalty** | Complete enzymatic toxicity (e.g. Deep One consuming pure uncorrupted Aither fruit, or High Elf eating raw abyssal spores). Violent convulsions, total mental blackout, and brain death within days. |

### The Deep Water Fish Exception (Types 1 to 4)

A strange biological exception exists regarding aquatic life caught in deep water:
- **Universal Tolerance for Types 1 through 4:** Heritages belonging to **Type 1 (High Elf)**, **Type 2 (Half-Elf)**, **Type 3 (Human)**, and **Type 4 (Orc)** can all consume fish harvested from deep water—whether from **deep saltwater oceans** (caught at depths of 150 feet or greater) or **deep freshwater lakes and rivers**.
- **Counts as the Correct Zone (0 Food Shift):** When eaten by any member of Types 1 through 4, deep-water fish is metabolically neutral and **counts as their exact native dietary zone**, incurring **0 Food Shift** penalties regardless of the consumer's strata.
- **Economic & Expedition Significance:** This makes deep-water fishing fleets, deep river netting expeditions, and canned deep fish barrels among the most prized trade commodities in the world. An expedition crossing between the high Aither and the Lower Neither can sustain Elves, Half-Elves, Humans, and Orcs on a single shared food supply without risking digestive illness or exposure degradation.
- *(Note: Deep subterranean heritages—Dwarves and Deep Ones—whose digestive systems have completely speciated around subterranean fungal spores and heavy minerals, do not share this enzyme and still treat surface water fish as foreign).*

## 5. Combined Neither Exposure: Total Shift Score (Air + Food)

At the end of each travel week (or during extended downtime in the field), determine a character's **Total Environmental Shift Score**:

$$\mathbf{Total\ Shift\ Score} = \mathbf{Air\ Shift\ Levels} + \mathbf{Food\ Shift\ Levels}$$

*(For example: A standard Human [Strata 3] living in the Lower Neither without filtration [Air 1.0] who survives on a half-Mela, half-raw Sycelium ration [Food 0.5] has a Total Shift Score of **1.5**).*

The combined shift score governs **healing rates**, while individual shifts in **Air** and **Food** determine the onset timing and progression of physiological issues (Minor, Moderate, Critical, and Lethal).

---

### Total Shift Score Healing Table

Biological healing from natural rest, medical treatment, and downtime recuperation is directly suppressed by your Total Shift Score:

| Total Shift Score | Healing Rate & Daily Health Effects |
|:---:|---|
| **0.0 to 1.5** | **100% Normal Healing Rate.** Full recovery of Hit Points and wounds during long-term rest. No biological healing penalties. |
| **2.0 to 3.5** | **50% Normal Healing Rate.** All natural HP recovery and wound recovery steps take twice as long. |
| **4.0 to 5.0** | **25% Normal Healing Rate.** Sluggish, suppressed cellular recovery; resting restores only 1/4 normal HP. |
| **5.5 to 6.0** | **No Natural Healing + 1d8 Damage per Day.** Cellular necrosis sets in. Natural rest restores 0 HP, and the character suffers **1d8 damage each day at dawn** (cannot be reduced by physical resistance). |
| **6.5 or Higher** | **No Natural Healing + 3d8 Damage per Day.** Acute systemic collapse. Natural rest restores 0 HP, and the character suffers **3d8 damage each day at dawn**. Rapidly results in death without immediate evacuation or hermetic life support. |

### Exposure Progression & Time-to-Effect (The –1 Increment Rule)

Neither degradation does not strike all at once. Instead, **all penalties accumulate at –1 increments at set intervals until the maximum penalty for that shift level is reached**. 

Likewise, when a character returns to a safer atmospheric layer or diet, **penalties drop at –1 point at the exact same time rate** until they reach the new environment's baseline or fully dissipate.

---

#### 1. Air Shift Time Frame & Escalation Rate (Physical Conditions)
Breathing incompatible air imposes **Clumsy, Enfeebled, and Drained**. These conditions increase by **+1 condition value** (imposing a –1 penalty) at the following interval until reaching the level's maximum penalty:

| Air Shift Level | Time Frame per –1 Step | Maximum Condition / Penalty | Time to Reach Maximum Severity |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Level 0** | *None* | 0 | Immune / Native equilibrium. |
| **Level 1** | **Every 1 Month** | **–1** (Clumsy 1, Enfeebled 1, Drained 1) | 1 Month (Does not worsen) |
| **Level 2** | **Every 1 Week** | **–2** (Clumsy 2, Enfeebled 2, Drained 2) | 2 Weeks |
| **Level 3** | **Every 3 Days** | **–4** (Clumsy 4, Enfeebled 4, Drained 4) | 12 Days |
| **Level 4** | **Every 1 Day** | **–8** (Clumsy 8, Enfeebled 8, Drained 8) | 8 Days |
| **Level 5** | **Every 1 Hour** | **–16** (Clumsy 16, Enfeebled 16, Drained 16) | 16 Hours (Lethal shock within hours) |

*Recovery Rate:* When returning to clean native air or wearing a sealed respirator, condition values decrease by **–1 point per 1 week of clean air** (beginning after the 7-day stabilization period).

| Food Shift Level | Time Frame per –1 Step | Maximum Condition / Penalty | Time to Reach Maximum Severity |

#### 3. Shift-Workers, Commuters & The 28-Day Cumulative Exposure Window
Most citizens of the Clockwork Cities are not permanent hermits; miners, foundriers, wall guards, and couriers commute between layers daily or work weekly rotations. To prevent rapid cycling and accurately model shift labor, **exposure is calculated as the cumulative time spent in an environment over the previous 28 days (4 weeks)**:

- **Full Exposure (21+ days of the last 28 days in hostile layer):** The full environmental shift applies at normal progression speed.
- **Part-Time Shift Labor (7 to 20 days of the last 28 days):** Accumulates exposure more slowly based on total hours logged. A worker spending 12 hours a day, 6 days a week in a hostile layer logs roughly 14 days of equivalent exposure per month. This is sufficient to eventually trigger that layer's full conditions (such as Level 2 Clumsy 2, Enfeebled 2, Drained 2), but does so across weeks rather than days.
- **Brief Excursions (<7 days of the last 28 days):** Trace exposure only; never advances beyond Level 1 minor fatigue if unmitigated.

> **Example — The Human Miner:**
> A standard human miner (Strata 3) commutes down to deep Lightless quarries (Strata 5, Air Shift Level 2) for 12-hour shifts, 6 days a week, returning to middle-ward tenements to sleep. Over a month of cumulative breathing, the worker eventually develops the **Air Shift Level 2 conditions** (Clumsy 2, Enfeebled 2, Drained 2, representing chronic occupational "Miner's Lung"). 
> 
> However, because the mining company supplies decent subsidized spire Mela rations (Food Shift 0.0), the miner's **Total Combined Shift Score is only 2.0** ($2.0 \text{ Air} + 0.0 \text{ Food} = 2.0$). Their natural resting healing remains at **50% of the normal rate**, allowing them to sustain employment indefinitely without entering lethal cellular decline or organ failure. If their diet were instead upgraded to pristine Strata 1 fish, their combined score would remain safely manageable.

#### 4. The Unified Recovery Rule (1-Week Stabilization + 1 Week per Point)
When a character leaves a hostile atmospheric layer or foreign diet and returns to their native environment and clean rations, recovery follows a simple, universal downtime mechanic:
- **The 7-Day Stabilization Period:** The cellular matrix, respiratory mucosa, and digestive enzymes require **1 continuous week (7 days) of native rest to stabilize**. During this first week, penalties **halt and do not get worse**, but they **do not yet decrease**.
- **The Flat 1-Week Recovery Rate:** Once the 7-day stabilization period concludes, **both Air and Food penalties decrease at a flat rate of –1 point per full week of clean living**:
  - Each week of breathing native air heals **1 point of Air conditions** (reducing Clumsy, Enfeebled, and Drained by 1).
  - Each week of eating native/uncorrupted food heals **1 point of Food conditions** (reducing Stupefied and the Int/Cha check penalty by 1).
  - *Example:* A scout who returns from the deep ruins with Clumsy 2 / Enfeebled 2 / Drained 2 and Stupefied 2 rests in their home spire. Week 1 is stabilization (penalties remain at 2). At the end of Week 2, all conditions drop to 1. At the end of Week 3, all conditions drop to 0 (fully cleansed).

---

#### 5. Long-Term Aberrant Drift & Lethality
- **Lethal Necrosis:** If a character's combined **Total Shift Score reaches 5.5 or higher**, natural healing is completely suppressed and they take daily necrotic damage (**1d8/day at 5.5–6.0**; **3d8/day at 6.5+**).
- **Aberrant Speciation:** If a character remains at Level 4+ penalties for more than **3 consecutive months** without dying (such as through constant external healing or surgical life support), the body ceases fighting the foreign medium and permanently speciates into an aberrant form (Goblin, Neanderthal, or Troglodyte).

## 6. Environmental Mitigations & Medical Protection

Civilized survival in the Clockwork Cities relies on mitigating Air and Food scores:

### Structural & Equipment Mitigations
- **Permanent Neithercrete Architecture:** Being inside a standard residential ward, sealed stone hamlet, or enclosed vehicle reduces effective Air Shift Distance by **1 Level** (to a minimum of 0).
- **Hermetically Sealed Deep Chambers:** Operating inside airtight, filter-scrubbed vaults (such as the King's palace, luxury research labs, or medical cleanrooms) reduces effective Air Shift Distance by **2 Levels**.
- **Sealed Breather Apparatus (Airship / Gas Mask):** Wearing an oiled leather respirator fitted with catalytic Neither scrubbers reduces effective Air Shift Distance by **1 Level** for up to 8 hours before filters require replacement or cleaning.
- **Mine Depth Storm Mitigation:** For subterranean miners and Dwarves, every 100 feet of solid rock depth reduces external storm severity by **1 Level** (at 300+ feet, storm damage is completely negated).

### Medical Convalescence & Dietary Recovery
- **Natural Convalescence:** Resting in your native atmospheric layer and consuming native rations requires **1 week (7 days) to stabilize**, after which you naturally shed **1 point of Air penalties and 1 point of Food penalties per full week** of clean living.
- **Dietary Purges (The Mela Cleanse):** Undergoing an intensive 7-day medical regimen of 100% Strata 1 food or pure clinical Mela broth administered with Chirurgeon tonic purges bypasses the standard stabilization delay, completely resetting your Food Shift penalties back to 0.0.
- **Hyperbaric Aither Sanatoriums:** Wealthy spire clinics and noble estates maintain pressurized chambers flooded with pure Strata 1 Aither oxygen. Spending 72 continuous hours in a hyperbaric sanatorium bypasses the stabilization period and removes 1 full point of Air Shift penalties.
