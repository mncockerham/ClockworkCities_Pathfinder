# Character Creation Guide: Step-by-Step Procedure

- **Topic:** Step-by-Step Character Creation Order & Advice
- **Location:** `ClockworkPathfinder/Rules/createacharacter.md`
- **System Baseline:** Pathfinder 2e Remaster (ORC) `[Player Core pp. 18–31]`
- **Setting Foundation:** `AISummary/00_RulesFoundation.md`, `ClockworkPathfinder/Rules/edges.md`, `ClockworkPathfinder/Rules/SocialClasses.md`

---

## 1. Overview & Best Creation Order

Creating a character in Clockwork Cities follows the proven Pathfinder 2e Remaster structure, adjusted for the secular steampunk setting, the 10-Point Edge system, and institutional backing.

Follow these 7 steps in order for the smoothest character creation experience:

```
[1. Character Concept] ──> [2. Allocate 10 Edge Points] ──> [3. Choose Biological Heritage]
                                                                        │
[6. Starting Gear & Bulwark] <── [5. Attributes & Skills] <── [4. Choose Character Class]
          │
[7. Final Details & Background Story]
```

> [!WARNING]
> **The Class / Social Rank Prerequisite Warning:**
> The **Noble** and **Merchant** character classes depend directly on having the societal backing, estates, credit lines, and legal authority of their respective social tiers. 
> - If you play a **Noble**, you must spend the Edge Points to start at **Status 4 (Lesser Noble)** or higher to access your full class abilities.
> - If you play a **Merchant**, you must spend the Edge Points to start at **Status 3 (Merchant)** or higher (or Status 6 for Wall Merchant) to access your full commercial credit lines and contract abilities.
> - *All other character classes* (Soldier, Scout, Artisan, Scholar, Chirurgeon, Mad Bomber, etc.) can be played freely from any social rank from Status 0 up to Status 6.

---

## Step 1: Formulate Your Character Concept
Before opening menus, determine who your character is and where they fit in the Spire:
- Are you an indentured foundry hand or a subterranean tunnel digger?
- A skilled guild clockmaker or university medical student?
- A disgraced noble officer seeking redemption, or an alley-smart Warrens survivor?

---

## Step 2: Allocate Your 10 Edge Points (Social Rank & Personal Edges)
Every character starts with **10 Edge Points** (`[edges.md]`). Spend them now:

1. **Choose Your Starting Social Rank (1 pt per Status tier):**
   - **Status 0 (Warrens / Outcast):** 0 Points
   - **Status 1 (Laborer / Day-Worker):** 1 Point
   - **Status 2 (Artisan / Guild Apprentice / Military Regular):** 2 Points
   - **Status 3 (Standard Merchant / Professional):** 3 Points
   - **Status 4 (Lesser Noble / Officer / Scholar):** 4 Points
   - **Status 5 (Middle Noble / Collegium Chair):** 5 Points
   - **Status 6 (Wall Merchant or Great House Aristocrat):** 7 Points *(Base 6 + 1 for armed retainers)*
2. **Spend Remaining Points on Personal Edges (Single-Purchase Only):**
   - **Tactical Edges:** Armor Step-Up (3 pts, max Expert), AP Battery Surge (4 pts: +1 AP at Lv 1, 5, 15), Hardened Frame (+HP / Diehard, 2 pts).
   - **Heritage / Biological Edges:** Iron-Gut Poison Immunity (2 pts), Neither-Conditioned Lungs (3 rerolls vs Neither, 2 pts), Attribute Boost (+1 to STR/DEX/CON, 4 pts), Workhorse Bulk (1 pt), Half-Elf Static/Rigging traits (1–2 pts), Dwarf Stone Anchor/Sapper traits (1–3 pts).
   - **Practical Edges:** Extra Trained Skill (1 pt), Bonus 1st-Level Feat (2 pts), Jury-Rigger (2 pts), Street Anonymity (1 pt).

---

## Step 3: Choose Your Biological Heritage (Humanity Spectrum)
Select one of the six human environmental adaptations (`[AncestriesAndHeritages.md]`):
- **Human (Upper Neither):** The versatile center; adaptable everywhere, broad feat options.
- **Orc (Lower Neither):** Dense bone, muscle bulk, stonecrafting, delayed storm reactions.
- **Half-Elf (The Zone):** Slender, barometrically adapted to altitude, airships, and static lightning.
- **Dwarf (Lightless Neither):** Subterranean sappers, dense stature, reflective pupil flash recovery.
- **High Elf (The Aither) / Deep One (The Deep):** Specialized extreme-strata heritages (require life support or closed sanctuaries outside native environments).

*Heritage Details:* Record your base Hit Points (typically 8 HP for Human/Half-Elf, 10 HP for Orc/Dwarf), Size (Medium), Speed (25–30 ft), and initial Ancestry Feat.

---

## Step 4: Choose Your Character Class
Select your class (`ClockworkPathfinder/Classes/`):
- **Frontline Combatants:** Soldier, Pugilist (including Savage Combatants), Scout.
- **Marksmen & Demolitions:** Clockwork Rifleman, Mad Bomber.
- **Technical & Scientific:** Clockwork Engineer, Artisan, Chirurgeon, Scholar.
- **Investigators & Leaders:** Inspector, Officer, Sky Captain.
- **Influence & Social:** Dandy, Survivor, Black Marketeer, plus **Noble** and **Merchant** *(see warning above)*.

*Class Details:* Record your Key Attribute, class Hit Points per level, initial proficiencies (Perception, Saves, Attacks, Defenses), 1st-level Class Feat, and class features.

---

## Step 5: Assign Attributes & Starting Skills
Clockwork Cities uses standard Pathfinder 2e Remaster attribute boosts (`[Player Core p. 20]`):

1. **Calculate Attributes (STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA):**
   - Start with an initial baseline of +0 across all attributes.
   - Apply Ancestry boosts (typically two free boosts or heritage boosts).
   - Apply Background boosts (one from your trade/origin, one free).
   - Apply Class Key Attribute boost (+1 to your class key stat).
   - Apply 4 Free Attribute Boosts (each applied to a different attribute).
   - *(Optional: Apply the +1 Edge Attribute Boost if purchased in Step 2).*
   - *Cap:* No attribute can exceed +4 at 1st level.
2. **Assign Starting Skills & Social Skills (`Understanding`):**
   - Select your trained standard PF2e skills (Athletics, Stealth, Crafting, Medicine, etc.) based on your class baseline.
   - Choose your starting **Social Skills (`Understanding`)** per `[Skills.md]`: 
     - Choose your Broad Institutional Groups (Trade Guilds, Military, Underworld, Nobility, etc.).
     - Choose each entry's specificity tier: *Broad (+0), Sub-Group (+1), Specific Place (+2), or Specific Person (+3)*.

---

## Step 6: Select Starting Gear & Citizen Bulwark Slots
Unlike standard fantasy games with bags of random gold, gear is governed by your institutional backing (`[ResourcesAndWealth.md]`):

1. **Starting Personal Funds:**
   - Record your monthly spending allowance based on your Social Status tier (e.g., Status 1 = 2 Nobels, Status 3 = 20 Nobels, Status 5 = 100 Nobels).
2. **Select Your 3 Citizen Bulwark Slots (Player Choice):**
   - Every citizen has the recognized right to retain and maintain **any 3 core items of your choice** (weapons, armor, or NeitherTeck devices) that can never be permanently confiscated or stolen under ordinary Spire law.
   - Equip your basic class weapons, ammunition cartridges, and armor.
   - *(Note: Ensure your weapons comply with Spire carrying laws—pack weapons in cases when traveling through public wards, or carry a mundane steel dueling rapier if you hold noble/merchant status).*

---

## Step 7: Final Calculations & Background Story
Wrap up your character sheet:
- **Armor Class (AC):** $10 + \text{DEX mod (up to cap)} + \text{Armor Item Bonus} + \text{Proficiency Bonus}$.
- **Hit Points:** $\text{Heritage HP} + \text{Class HP} + \text{CON mod} + (\text{Hardened Frame Edge HP if purchased})$.
- **Active Power (AP):** Record your starting Active Surge pool (Tier 0 Martials = 0 AP; Tier 1 Operatives = 0 AP; Influence = 1 AP; Technical/Engineers = 2–3 AP; add +1 AP if you purchased the *AP Battery Surge Edge*).
- **Character Story (The Simple Personal Anecdote):** Write 1 or 2 simple sentences explaining how your character picked up their skills or unusual traits (no long essays required).

You are now ready to enter the Great Spires and the Neither wastelands!
