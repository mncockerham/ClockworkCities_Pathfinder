# Retainers & Hirelings Rules

- **Topic:** Retainers, Servants, Household Guards, and Subordinates in Clockwork Cities
- **Location:** `ClockworkPathfinder/Rules/retainers.md`
- **Status:** **Active Draft / Complete Rewrite**
- **Core Design Mandate:** Retainers are a baseline institutional benefit granted by Social Status or Class. They are **never the primary defense or offense of any player character or class**. They exist as simple, low-overhead subordinates without financial tracking or character wealth bookkeeping.

---

## 1. Sources of Retainers: Social Status vs. Class Option

Retainers come from two primary tracks: **Social Status (The Baseline Track)** and **The Dedicated Influence Class Track (Noble, Merchant, Grey Marketeer)**.
- **Superior Choice Governs:** Characters use whichever track provides the superior retainer entitlement. The dedicated class option significantly surpasses the baseline social status track.
- **Zero Financial Upkeep:** Retainers are supported entirely by the family house, merchant firm, or syndicate pipeline. There is **no cost to having them**, and they are **never tracked as part of character wealth or monthly spending limits**.


### Retainer Social Status (The -20 Reflected Status Rule)
In Spire society, full-time retainers (personal bodyguards, stewards, valets, carriage drivers, armed armsmen) do not command high status of their own right; rather, **their status is a direct reflection of who they represent**:
- **The -20 Formula:** A full-time retainer's operational Social Status is equal to their **Sponsoring Master's Status - 20**:
  - *Standard Merchant Retainer (Master Status 50):* Retainer operates at **Status 30** (treated with the standing of an established shopkeeper or clerk).
  - *Lesser Noble Retainer (Master Status 60):* Retainer operates at **Status 40** (treated with the standing of an innkeeper or senior estate steward).
  - *Standard Noble Retainer (Master Status 70):* Retainer operates at **Status 50** (treated with the deference of a court magistrate or university graduate).
  - *Wall Merchant Retainer (Master Status 85):* Retainer operates at **Status 65** (treated with the authority of landed barons; authorized to carry livery dueling sidearms under house rite).
  - *Great House Noble Retainer (Master Status 95):* Retainer operates at **Status 75** (commands immense civic deference across all wards, carrying Great House crests and authorized house weapons).
- **Reflected Standing, Not Personal Pedigree:** This status belongs to the **livery and crest they wear, not their personal bloodline**. If a Great House guard steps out of uniform or is dismissed, they instantly revert to their personal civilian baseline (often Status 11–20). While in uniform representing their master, ward constables and municipal clerks treat them according to their sponsor's reflected standing.

### Domestic Household Servants (The -30 Reflected Status Rule)

Domestic household servants (chambermaids, cooks, scullery staff, laundry maids, footmen, and household valets) can be hired by **anyone with the money to employ them** (merchants, academics, professionals, and nobles alike). 

Like retainers, their social standing reflects the household they serve:
- **The -30 Formula (Floor: Status 16):** A domestic household servant operates at **$	ext{Employer's Status} - 30$**, but their status **will never drop below Status 16 (Freeman / Freewoman Baseline)** so long as they are employed:
  - *Employed by a Freeman / Craftsman (Employer Status 25–40):* Operates at the **floor of Status 16** (respected free domestic worker).
  - *Employed by a Standard Merchant (Employer Status 50):* Operates at **Status 20** (recognized commercial household staff).
  - *Employed by a Lesser Noble (Employer Status 60):* Operates at **Status 30** (treated with the standing of a junior clerk or shopkeeper).
  - *Employed by a Standard Noble (Employer Status 70):* Operates at **Status 40** (treated with the standing of a respected estate manager or innkeeper).
  - *Employed by a Wall Merchant (Employer Status 85):* Operates at **Status 55** (treated with the standing and deference of an educated professional or magistrate's secretary).
  - *Employed by a Great House Noble (Employer Status 95):* Operates at **Status 65** (senior Great House chamberlains and head housekeepers command status rivaling landed barons in public wards).
- **Reflected Standing in the Markets:** When a Great House maid walks through the Ward 11 market wearing her family crest or badge, shopkeepers rush to serve her first and offer wholesale courtesy—knowing that insulting the maid insults the Great House that employs her.

### Track A: The Baseline Social Status Track
*For all characters who hold Noble or Merchant social status, but do not take levels in an Influence class.*
- **Base Nobles & Base Merchants:**
  - Receive **1 Retainer** at all levels (Levels 1–20).
- **Greater Nobles & Wall Merchants:**
  - Receive **1 Retainer** at Level 1.
  - Receive a **2nd Retainer** starting at Level 5.
- **Level Formula (Social Status Track):**  
  $$\text{Retainer Level} = \left\lceil \frac{\text{PC Level}}{4} \right\rceil \quad (\text{Minimum Level 1})$$

| PC Level | Social Status Retainer Level |
|:---:|:---:|
| **Levels 1–4** | Level 1 |
| **Levels 5–8** | Level 2 |
| **Levels 9–12** | Level 3 |
| **Levels 13–16** | Level 4 |
| **Levels 17–20** | Level 5 |

---

### Track B: The Dedicated Influence Class Track (Noble, Merchant, Grey Marketeer)
*Characters with levels in the **Noble**, **Merchant**, or **Grey Marketeer** classes receive superior retainer scaling representing active leadership and organizational networks.*

- **Starting Retainers:** They each start with **2 Retainers** at Level 1.
- **Retainer Progression (Scaling Up):** They gain **+1 additional Retainer** at **Levels 5, 10, 15, and 20**:
  - **Levels 1–4:** 2 Retainers
  - **Levels 5–9:** 3 Retainers
  - **Levels 10–14:** 4 Retainers
  - **Levels 15–19:** 5 Retainers
  - **Level 20:** 6 Retainers
- **Superior Level Formula (Half Level):** Retainers on this track are far more capable, scaling at **half the PC's level** rather than one-quarter:  
  $$\text{Retainer Level} = \left\lceil \frac{\text{PC Level}}{2} \right\rceil \quad (\text{Minimum Level 1})$$

| PC Level | Class Track Retainer Level | Total Retainers |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Levels 1–4** | Level 1–2 (Lv 1 at 1–2, Lv 2 at 3–4) | 2 |
| **Levels 5–9** | Level 3–5 | 3 |
| **Levels 10–14** | Level 5–7 | 4 |
| **Levels 15–19** | Level 8–10 | 5 |
| **Level 20** | Level 10 | 6 |

## 3. Alternative Slot Usage: The Part-Time PC Retainer Option

A player character with retainer slots may choose to expend **1 Retainer Slot** to bring fellow party members onto their house or business payroll as **Part-Time Retainers**:

### Benefits to the Sponsoring & Employed PCs:
- **1 Slot = 2 Part-Time PC Retainers:** Expending 1 Retainer slot covers **two (2) other player characters**.
- **Free Equipment Upgrade to Base Retainer Level:** The two employed PCs can immediately upgrade their baseline mundane gear and arms to the sponsoring PC's base retainer level **completely for free**, outfitted directly from the house or merchant firm's armory and stores.
- **Check-In & Check-Out Protocol for Extra Gear:** If specialized mission hardware, extra weapons, armor, or tactical tools are issued for an assignment, the employed PCs must **report in to the armory/quartermaster, formally check the gear out, and leave it behind when the shift or day is done**. The only items they keep and carry continuously are their **house livery** and their **assigned transportation chit**.
- **House Livery & Clear Social Backing:** They are issued official house or merchant uniforms/liveries that make it instantly and unmistakably clear to everyone in the Spire that they work for a person of the sponsoring PC's high status.
- **Legal Weapon Carry Rights (Sword Parity):** If the sponsoring PC's guards have legal authorization to openly carry short swords on duty (e.g. Nobles and Wall Merchants), **the two employed PCs gain that same legal right to carry swords** while wearing the livery.
- **Hassle-Free Spire Travel:** Whenever wearing the house livery, the employed PCs enjoy **hassle-free transit across Spire checkpoints and inter-ward lifts**, traveling with the full legal authority of being under active orders from that sponsoring noble or merchant.

### The Double-Edged Con: Dual Accountability
- **Judged by Both Institutions:** While wearing the livery, the employed PCs are not private individuals; their actions directly reflect on their employer's standing.
- **Double Punishment for Transgressions:** If an employed PC commits a crime, causes a public scandal, or violates Spire law, they are formally judged and held accountable by **both their own home group/guild AND the sponsoring PC's noble or merchant house**—potentially facing **double punishment** (fines, loss of standing, expulsion, or penal labor) for a single misdeed.

## 4. Retainer Loyalty, Accountability & The Realities of Pay

A crucial world truth of Clockwork Cities governs how retainers think and act:
- **Loyal to the House, Not the PC (Types 1 & 2):** Family retainers are assigned and paid by the **family house or commercial enterprise**, not out of the PC's personal coin. Consequently, **their ultimate loyalty is to the House, not the individual PC**.
- **Eyes and Ears of the Family:** Retainers report back what they see and hear to family elders, house matriarchs/patriarchs, or corporate directors. If a PC behaves disgracefully, violates house protocol, or flirts with criminal scandal, the retainer will report it—because if the PC gets exiled or demoted, the retainer intends to keep their job and position in the Spire.
- **Death of Family Retainers Severely Looked Down Upon (Types 1 & 2):** Family retainers are not disposable cannon fodder or expendable HP shields. If a PC gets their house retainers killed through recklessness, neglect, or treating them as meat-shields, the house views it with severe displeasure, threatening the PC's Social Standing, loss of future retainer privileges, or formal reprimand.
- **The Loyalty Reality of Types 2, 3, and 4 (They Do This for Pay):**
  - Retainers operating outside the safety of high-spire society (outland estate workers, Warrens toughs, and frontier Neither contractors) are **doing this strictly for pay**.
  - **You Can't Spend Coin If You're Dead:** These people know that life in the setting is brutal and unforgiving. It is not that they are cowards—they are willing to take real physical risks, face danger, and engage in serious conflict as part of the job. **However, they need to believe it is worth it.**
  - **Suicidal Orders & Disappearance:** If an assignment or combat situation looks suicidal, foolish, or hopelessly lethal, they will flatly refuse. If pushed, browbeaten, or threatened by a reckless PC, **they will simply walk away and disappear into the shadows or mist without waiting to collect their pay**—preferring to lose a contract's wages rather than forfeit their lives.

## 5. The Four Retainer Types by Social Class

Retainers come in **4 distinct types**, with options dictated by Social Class and operational environment:

### Type 1: Spire Servants & Bodyguards (Urban / Inside the Spire)

#### A. Noble Retainer Options (Safety & Status Enhancement)
Nobles surround themselves with people who keep them physically safe or actively elevate their social prestige:
- **Domestic & Status Servants:** Valets, personal maids, heraldic couriers, private coachmen. (Ensure immaculate presentation, handle Ward 13 protocol, carry calling cards).
- **Professional & Advisory Experts:** Scholar Doctors/Chirurgeons, Experts on Government & Civil Protocol, House Accountants, Crown Court Lawyers, genealogists. (Handle legal contracts, advise on Privy Council politics, treat sickness).
- **Noble Bodyguards:** Sworn household guards tasked with keeping the noble safe.
  - **Weapon Carry Rights:** While on duty, Noble Bodyguards are legally permitted to carry **short swords** (or other approved defensive sidearms) to protect the noble they attend.

#### B. Merchant Retainer Options (Safety & Wealth Generation)
Merchants focus on practical subordinates who either protect their person or actively help generate more wealth:
- **Commercial & Administrative Staff:** Executive secretaries, commercial couriers, junior clerks.
- **Business Operators:** Managers who oversee and run a specific shop, warehouse, taproom, or commercial enterprise on the merchant's behalf.
- **Topic & Trade Experts:** Specialists in a specific commodity or enterprise the merchant family trades in (e.g. bulk grain assayers, textile appraisers, NeitherTeck component evaluators, shipping navigators).
- **Merchant Guards:** Hired security and warehouse watchmen to protect merchant cargoes and person.
  - **Weapon Carry Limitations:**
    - **Wall Merchant Guards:** Enjoy elite livery privileges and **can legally carry short swords** while on active duty.
    - **Normal Merchant Guards:** Are restricted under municipal peace laws and **CANNOT carry short swords** (relying on simple wooden saps, truncheons, or heavy walking clubs).

#### C. Grey Marketeer Retainer Options (Underworld Operations & Warrens Network)
Grey Marketeers do not command noble valets or legitimate corporate accountants; they maintain an underworld retinue tailored to illicit logistics, enforcement, and street operations:
- **Inside the Spire Proper:**
  - **Former Constables:** Dishonorably discharged or retired watchmen who know municipal shift schedules, gate procedures, patrol gaps, and which active officers accept which scale of bribe.
  - **Survivors & Alley Lookouts:** Urban rogues, lock-breakers, pickpockets, and runners who monitor precinct movements, carry contraband dead-drops, and blend invisibly into lower-ward crowds.
  - **Fences & Pawnshop Factors:** Underworld appraisers who handle the rapid turnover of stolen or unrecorded goods.
- **Inside the Warrens & Low Sump Corridors:**
  - In addition to standard toughs, Grey Marketeers can add specialists of a decidedly less respectable nature:
    - **Back-Alley Chirurgeons:** Unlicensed sawbones who stitch bullet holes and knife wounds without notifying precinct coroners or filing Spire medical reports.
    - **Mad Bombers & Alchemical Brewers:** Volatile pipe-bomb fabricators, acid-siphoners, and smoke-grenade chemists operating illegal stills in abandoned drain junctions.
    - **Pugilists & Enforcers:** Pit-brawlers and bone-breakers maintaining gang order.
- **Willingness & Street Knowledge:**
  - Unlike the close-mouthed, sullen toughs hired by nobles or merchants (Type 3), a Grey Marketeer's retainers **are far more willing to help, guide, and actively share inside knowledge about the Warrens**—they respect the Marketeer's underworld standing and know that cooperating brings regular profit.
- **Consequences of Retainer Deaths:**
  - While high-society nobles and merchants can lose Type 3 Warrens muscle without anyone caring, a Grey Marketeer **operates directly within the underworld social ecosystem**.
  - Getting your crew killed through recklessness or using them as disposable meat-shields **causes serious street problems**: gang crews grow resentful, former constables refuse assignments, and word spreads among fences. Losing retainers triggers the same severe **Reputation and Quality Penalties** that other classes face in the outer Neither (forcing the Grey Marketeer to settle for lower-level or less trustworthy recruits).

---

### Type 2: The Civilized Wild (Areas Outside the Spire Proper)

These retainers operate in the satellite shires, walled farming perimeters, timber reserves, quarry estates, and outland holdings owned by or leased to the family outside the main Spire structure:
- **Family Ties & Firm Loyalty:** These are people associated directly with the family's outland holdings. Just like Spire servants, **they are more loyal to the family than to the PC**, and the house views their deaths with **extreme disfavor**. Getting rural family retainers killed damages estate productivity, alienates tenant loyalty, and provokes harsh family reprimands.
- **Professions Suited to Family Enterprise:** Both Noble and Merchant lists draw from practical outland trades that make sense for the family's rural investments, supply chains, or land grants:
  - **Guards & Perimeter Watchmen:** Defend family estate walls, storehouses, and rural toll gates against wild raiders and predators.
  - **Scouts & Outriders:** Pathfinders, track-readers, and dispatch couriers who monitor road safety and outland wildlife between satellite hamlets and the Spire.
  - **Farmers, Bailiffs & Husbandmen:** Manage family tenant farms, agricultural combines, mushroom caverns, or livestock paddocks.
  - **Woodsmen & Quarrymen:** Oversee family timber harvest tracts, fuel processing kilns, or stone quarries.

---

### Type 3: The Warrens (Underworld Toughs & Local Guides)

When entering the sunless, unpoliced Warrens behind the municipal wards, noble and merchant PCs hire local muscle:
- **No Family Ties & Mercenary Loyalty:** Unlike Types 1 and 2, **these people have zero ties to the PC's family or house**. They are toughs, bouncers, and brutes hired strictly to keep the client physically safe while navigating the Warrens.
- **Expendability (No One Cares If They Die):** While getting a family servant (Type 1) or outland tenant (Type 2) killed brings severe disgrace and reprimand from the house, **in the Warrens, no one cares if these people die in your service**. They are paid hired muscle; if they fall in an alley ambush or sump collapse, the client owes nothing to their family, and no house matriarch will ask questions.
- **Street Knowledge & Reluctance:** They are carefully chosen as people who genuinely know their way around the maze of tunnels, fungal conduits, and rookeries. However, they possess only limited practical understanding of broader Warrens politics, and **what knowledge they do have, they seldom share willingly** with their high-born clients.
- **Equipment & Ward Proximity Scaling:**
  - **Warrens Standards:** They are well-equipped for their level within the context of the Warrens, though their gear is noticeably rougher, patched, and crude compared to the fine armor and polished weapons carried by Spire guards outside.
  - **Economic Spillover from Adjacent Wards:** The wealth of the municipal ward directly outside a Warrens entrance dictates how much resource and currency flows inward. Consequently, **Warrens retainers hired near high-status wards (such as Ward 13 or Merchant Ward access points) are noticeably better equipped and fed** than desperate toughs hired out of the Lower Slum Warrens.

---

### Type 4: The Outer Neither (Away from Controlled Areas)

These retainers are frontier specialists hired when venturing into the unaligned, lawless, or wild Neither far beyond Spire-controlled shires:
- **Contractors, Not Family Members:** They are paid from house funds, but **are not part of the house**. Your family house does not personally care if they live or die to collect their payment.
- **The Reputation & Quality Penalty (Losing Hires Drops Retainer Level):**
  - While your house elders do not care about these contractors, **frontier mercenaries and wilderness guides certainly do**. 
  - If a PC develops a reputation for getting their outland hires killed, word spreads fast through frontier taverns and staging posts.
  - As a result, **it becomes difficult or impossible to hire quality personnel**. You are forced to take on less qualified retainers, meaning **subsequent hires are of a lower level** than your standard formula entitlement until your reputation recovers.
- **Available Professions & Archetypes:**
  - **Outland Scouts & Pathfinders:** Skilled in reading Neither currents, navigating toxic fog banks, and spotting beast tracks.
  - **Warriors & Mercenaries:** Rugged frontline fighters equipped with heavy weather leathers, axes, and cross-strata sidearms.
  - **Frontier Farmers & Foragers:** Specialists in finding edible uncorrupted fungi or wild game in lightless valleys.
  - **Chirurgeons:** Field surgeons and backwoods apothecaries experienced with Neither rot and caustic fog burns. *(Note: High-society Scholar Doctors are ivory-tower academics and are seldom or never found for hire in the rough outer Neither).*

## 6. Retainer Statistics, Builds & The Civilized Tag

All retainers possess an established **Level** ($\lceil \text{PC Level} / 4 \rceil$), standard class progressions, and pre-equipped gear appropriate to their station and purpose:
- **Standardized Builds with Menu Options:** Rather than forcing players or the Storyteller to build full characters from scratch, retainers utilize **standard class templates** (e.g. Soldier, Chirurgeon, Professional/Commoner, Scout, Pugilist) with simple plug-and-play choices for weapon loadout, primary skills, and purpose.
- **The Civilized Tag Distribution (`[NPCs.md]`):**
  - **Spire Intellectuals & Domestics (Civilized):** Certain interior Spire retainers—such as House Accountants, Lawyers, Valets, Secretaries, and high-society Scribes—possess the **`[Civilized]`** tag. They excel at specialized civilian and institutional knowledge, but suffer from combat hesitation and collapse into near-uselessness if they suffer Mortal damage.
  - **Bodyguards & Non-Spire Hires (NOT Civilized or Protected):**
    - **All Bodyguards inside the Spire** are professionally hardened combat personnel and are **NOT considered Civilized or Protected**.
    - **All retainers and hires outside the Spire proper** (Type 2 Civilized Wild, Type 3 Warrens toughs, and Type 4 Outer Neither contractors) are exposed to constant rough living, labor, or physical peril and are **NEVER considered Civilized or Protected**. They fight under standard Pathfinder 2e / Clockwork Cities wound threshold and combat rules without psychological shock collapse.

## 8. Standardized Retainer Builds (Plug-and-Play Examples)

All retainers possess standard class chassis appropriate to their level ($\lceil 	ext{PC Level}/4 
ceil$ for Social Status track, $\lceil 	ext{PC Level}/2 
ceil$ for Influence Class track). Their gear scales automatically with their level using **standard mundane materials** (fine wool, dark iron, steel, leather, canvas); they carry **no clockwork technology** unless their core class base explicitly requires it (such as a Mad Bomber's chemical vials or a Chirurgeon's biochemical reagents).

Each build focuses on **simple, dedicated skill packages** that directly serve their primary purpose:

---

### Category 1: Spire Servants & Bodyguards (Urban / Interior Spire)

#### 1. House Valet / Personal Maid (Domestic & Protocol)
- **Base Class / Archetype:** Professional / Commoner (`[Civilized]`)
- **Key Skills:** Society (High Spire Etiquette), Crafting (Tailoring & Maintenance), Diplomacy (Ward 13 Decorum), House Lore.
- **Purpose Feature (*Flawless Presentation*):** The employer gains a +1 circumstance bonus to initial Diplomacy and Society checks with peers when attending formal gatherings or salons while accompanied by the Valet.
- **Standard Gear:** Tailored wool servant livery, calling card case, brush & folding pressing kit, shoe polish, umbrella. Unarmed.

#### 2. House Accountant / Ledger Master (Finance & Contracts)
- **Base Class / Archetype:** Professional (`[Civilized]`)
- **Key Skills:** Accounting Lore, Society (Corporate & Tax Law), Perception (Sense Motive), Mathematics Lore.
- **Purpose Feature (*Audit & Discrepancy*):** Grants the PC a +2 circumstance bonus on checks to detect fraudulent contracts, audit commercial ledgers, or identify forged bills of exchange.
- **Standard Gear:** Fine dark frock coat, brass pocket calipers, ledger book, reservoir pen, abacus, magnifying loupe. Unarmed.

#### 3. Crown Court Barrister / Legal Advocate (Jurisprudence)
- **Base Class / Archetype:** Professional (`[Civilized]`)
- **Key Skills:** Legal Lore, Society (Spire Municipal Codes), Deception (Legalistic Obfuscation), Diplomacy.
- **Purpose Feature (*Legal Stay of Action*):** When the PC or an ally is questioned by Ward Constables over non-violent infractions, the Barrister can cite municipal charter statutes, granting a +2 circumstance bonus to avoid fines or detention.
- **Standard Gear:** Academic legal gown, embossed vellum brief-case, stamped court seals, pocket watch. Concealed cane.

#### 4. Scholar Doctor / Physician (High Medicine)
- **Base Class / Archetype:** Chirurgeon / Scholar (`[Civilized]`)
- **Key Skills:** Medicine, Academia Lore (Sanatoriums), Society, Crafting (Medical Solutions).
- **Purpose Feature (*Bedside Treatment*):** Can Treat Wounds and administer non-volatile restorative tonics during rests without the PC needing to invest in Medicine feats.
- **Standard Gear:** Clean white clinician coat, leather medical satchel, bone-handled scalpels, clean linen bandages, smelling salts, diagnostic stethoscope.

#### 5. Noble Household Bodyguard (Armed Security)
- **Base Class / Archetype:** Soldier (NOT Civilized or Protected)
- **Key Skills:** Athletics, Intimidation, Perception, Spire Patrol Lore.
- **Purpose Feature (*Bodyguard Interposition*):** Can be commanded with 1 action to grant the noble +2 circumstance AC against a triggering strike or provide flanking.
- **Standard Gear:** Fluted steel breastplate (scaled to level), noble heraldic tabard, **steel short sword**, buckler, whistle.

#### 6. Wall Merchant Security Guard (Asset Defense)
- **Base Class / Archetype:** Soldier / Brute (NOT Civilized or Protected)
- **Key Skills:** Athletics, Perception, Intimidation, Warehouse Lore.
- **Purpose Feature (*Cargo Bastion*):** +2 bonus to Grapple or Shove enemies attempting to rush past them to reach the Merchant or cargo.
- **Standard Gear:** Riveted studded leather coat, Wall Merchant family crest armband, **steel short sword**, heavy weighted sap.

#### 7. Base Merchant Watchman (Storefront Protection)
- **Base Class / Archetype:** Laborer / Guard (NOT Civilized or Protected)
- **Key Skills:** Athletics, Perception, Streetwise.
- **Purpose Feature (*Barricade Presence*):** Treats improvised storefront furniture or doors as heavy cover; sounds alarm whistles reliably.
- **Standard Gear:** Heavy padded canvas smock, brass municipal registration token, **heavy oak cudgel / truncheon** (no short sword allowed), bullseye lantern.

#### 8. Business Factor / Shop Manager (Enterprise Operations)
- **Base Class / Archetype:** Professional / Merchant (`[Civilized]`)
- **Key Skills:** Mercantile Lore, Diplomacy (Haggling), Deception, Trade Guild Lore.
- **Purpose Feature (*Off-Screen Management*):** Competently oversees a specific retail stall, warehouse, or taproom off-screen, maintaining steady house ledger accounts without PC micromanagement.
- **Standard Gear:** Decent tweed suit, ring of brass keys, merchant factor token, tally sheets, coin pouch. Unarmed.

---

### Category 2: The Civilized Wild (Areas Outside the Spire Proper)

#### 1. Estate Perimeter Guard (Holdings Security)
- **Base Class / Archetype:** Soldier (NOT Civilized or Protected)
- **Key Skills:** Athletics, Survival, Perception, Outland Lore.
- **Purpose Feature (*Wall & Gate Defense*):** Gains +2 circumstance bonus on Perception against sneaking raiders and +1 to hit against encroaching wild beasts.
- **Standard Gear:** Boiled leather armor, iron pot helm, heavy cross-handled hunting spear or bills, short bow, horn.

#### 2. Shire Scout & Outrider (Road & Transit Recon)
- **Base Class / Archetype:** Scout (NOT Civilized or Protected)
- **Key Skills:** Survival (Tracking), Stealth, Perception, Animal Handling / Ride.
- **Purpose Feature (*Route Pathfinder*):** Decreases overland travel delays between the Spire and family satellite hamlets by 25%; spots wild orc ambushes in advance.
- **Standard Gear:** Weathered oilskin duster, hunting recurve bow, 20 broadhead arrows, skinning knife, compass, binoculars.

#### 3. Estate Bailiff & Farm Overseer (Agricultural Production)
- **Base Class / Archetype:** Professional / Laborer (NOT Civilized or Protected)
- **Key Skills:** Agriculture Lore, Nature, Athletics, Diplomacy (Tenant Management).
- **Purpose Feature (*Crop & Herd Yield*):** Can evaluate blighted crops or livestock ailments, preventing spoilage and ensuring full tenant output for the family.
- **Standard Gear:** Heavy corduroy riding coat, tally ledger, leather crop, pocket pocketknife, sturdy work boots.

#### 4. Master Woodsman / Forester (Timber & Fuel)
- **Base Class / Archetype:** Scout / Laborer (NOT Civilized or Protected)
- **Key Skills:** Survival (Forestry), Athletics, Crafting (Carpentry & Charcoal), Perception.
- **Purpose Feature (*Heavy Felling & Clearing*):** Treats axes as martial weapons; ignores rough terrain caused by fallen timber, dense brush, or rocky scree.
- **Standard Gear:** Plaid wool shirt, reinforced hide trousers, two-handed felling axe, hand hatchet, sharpening stone, wedges.

#### 5. Quarry Foreman / Sapper (Stone & Excavation)
- **Base Class / Archetype:** Artisan / Laborer (NOT Civilized or Protected)
- **Key Skills:** Crafting (Masonry), Athletics, Subterranean Lore, Perception.
- **Purpose Feature (*Structural Sounding*):** Can sound rock walls and Neithercrete slabs to detect hollow spaces, impending cave-ins, or subterranean gas pockets.
- **Standard Gear:** Padded leather vest, hard leather mining cap, heavy iron mining pick, chalk markers, plumb bob.

#### 6. Outland Dispatch Rider / Courier (Rapid Communication)
- **Base Class / Archetype:** Scout / Commoner (NOT Civilized or Protected)
- **Key Skills:** Acrobatics, Survival, Perception, Athletics.
- **Purpose Feature (*Endurance Gallop*):** Can ride or sprint across rough terrain for twice normal duration without Fortitude saves vs. fatigue.
- **Standard Gear:** Sealed leather dispatch satchel with wax Crown seals, light leather jacket, riding boots, short hunting club.

---

### Category 3: The Warrens (Underworld Toughs & Guides)

#### 1. Warrens Bouncer / Heavy Muscle (Physical Protection)
- **Base Class / Archetype:** Brute / Pugilist (NOT Civilized or Protected)
- **Key Skills:** Athletics (Grapple & Shove), Intimidation, Warrens Lore.
- **Purpose Feature (*Alley Blockade*):** When adjacent to the client in a narrow tunnel (10 ft wide or less), enemies cannot step or tumble past them without rolling Athletics vs. Fortitude DC.
- **Standard Gear:** Scrap-plate riveted leather vest, lead pipe or iron wrench, spiked brass knuckle-duster, cheap clay pipe.
