## 1. The Evolutionary Paradox: Why Humans Shrink While Beasts Grow in the Neither

A foundational biological law of the Clockwork Cities world is the **inverse evolutionary response of Humanity versus Animals** when exposed to the atmospheric strata of the Neither (`[TheNeither.md]`):

1. **Humanity Tends to Become Smaller and More Compact with Deeper Neither Exposure:**
   - Humanity is one of the very few biological species on the planet that adapts to deeper Neither pressure and subterranean environments by **reducing body volume and stature**:
     - **Pure Aither (Strata 1 — High Elves):** Tall, slender, elongated frames (`6'2"–6'10"`).
     - **Zone & Light Neither (Strata 2–4 — Half-Elves, Baseline Humans, Orcs):** Medium stature; even **Orcs** (`Strata 4`) trade vertical height for extreme horizontal bone density, thick ribcages, and compact muscular leverage.
     - **Dark Neither & Lightless Mines (Strata 5 — Dwarves):** Short, broad, hyper-dense frames (`4'2"–4'8"`) built for low-ceiling tunnels and heavy air.
     - **Abyssal Dark Neither (Strata 6 — Deep Ones):** The smallest and most compact of all human heritages, conserving metabolic heat and minimizing surface exposure in lightless geothermal chasms.
2. **Animals & Beasts Undergo "Neither Hypertrophy" (Massive Growth & Lethality):**
   - In stark contrast to humans, non-human mammals, reptiles, and arthropods undergo **Neither Hypertrophy** when adapted to Light and Dark Neither strata.
   - Chronic exposure to dense mineral vapors, catalytic fungal spores, and heavy atmospheric pressure suppresses animal myostatin pathways while hyper-calcifying bone, horn, tusk, and chitin lattices.
   - **The Deeper the Stratum, the Deadlier the Beast:** While horses and hounds raised in the pure air of the Upper Spires or Aither plateaus remain at normal historical proportions, creatures native to **Light Neither (Strata 4)** double in vital mass and tearing power, and beasts of the **Dark Neither (Strata 5–6)** grow into armored, bone-crushing behemoths.

## 2. Atmospheric Mount Scaling Rules (Aither vs. Light Neither vs. Dark Neither)

All mounts and animal companions in Clockwork Cities are categorized into **three atmospheric biological tiers** based on their native stratum. These modifiers apply both to **standalone creature stat blocks** (`[Monster Core]`) and to **Bonded War-Mounts / Animal Companions** (`[WarRider.md]`):

| Atmospheric Beast Tier | Native Strata | Hit Point Multiplier | Natural Strike Damage Dice Boost | Biological & Visual Profile |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1: Aither, Zone & Spire Mounts**<br>*(Baseline)* | **Strata 1–3**<br>*(Pure Aither, Zone, Civilized Spire Wards)* | **$1.0\times$ Base HP**<br>*(Standard PF2e HP)* | **$+0$ Extra Dice**<br>*(Base `1d6` or `1d8`)* | Sleek, athletic, clean-coated horses, ponies, and mules raised on high-altitude Aither estates or aerated Spire stables. |
| **Tier 2: Light Neither Mounts**<br>*(Badlands & Outlands)* | **Strata 4**<br>*(Lower Neither Wastes, Orc Outlands, Spire Perimeter)* | **$2.0\times$ Base HP**<br>*(Double Hit Points!)* | **$+1$ Weapon Damage Die**<br>*(Base `1d8` $\rightarrow$ **`2d8`**)* | Thick-hided, heavy-boned, coarse-maned beasts with enlarged tusks, jaws, or iron-hard hooves adapted to toxic surface fog and thorny scrub. |
| **Tier 3: Dark Neither Mounts**<br>*(Subterranean & Abyssal)* | **Strata 5–6**<br>*(Lightless Mines, Deep Chasms, Heavy Neither Basins)* | **$2.4\times$ Base HP**<br>*(Light Neither HP $+20\%$!)* | **$+2$ Weapon Damage Dice**<br>*(Base `1d8` $\rightarrow$ **`3d8`**)* | Massive, hyper-calcified subterranean beasts with plated hides, bioluminescent or cat-optic eyes, and crushing jaws or battering horns that splinter stone. |

### How the Scaling & Core Systems Apply at the Table
1. **Hit Point Calculation:**
   - **Tier 1 (Aither / Spire):** $\text{Total HP} = \text{Standard PF2e HP}$.
   - **Tier 2 (Light Neither — Double HP):** $\text{Total HP} = \mathbf{2.0 \times \text{Standard PF2e HP}}$.
   - **Tier 3 (Dark Neither — Double HP + 20%):** $\text{Total HP} = \mathbf{2.4 \times \text{Standard PF2e HP}}$ (i.e., $1.20 \times \text{Light Neither HP}$, rounded down).
2. **Simple Mount HP Pools, Riding Skill, Morale (Bolting) & Healing (`[Skills.md]`, `[20_Healing.md]`):**
   - **Normal Single HP Pools Only:** To keep mounted play fast and simple at the table, all mounts use **normal single Hit Point pools for animals** (mounts do **not** track 3-Step Regular / Critical / Mortal wound thresholds).
   - **Riding Skill, Morale (Bolting) & 0 HP Rules (`[Skills.md]`):**
     - **Riding Checks & Proficiency Tiers:** Controlling a mount, performing special mounted maneuvers, and resisting being *Unseated* use the **Riding (`DEX / WIS`)** skill. Standard classes can advance *Riding* up to **Expert**; taking the *WarRider Dedication* unlocks **Master** (Lv 7+); the **[WarRider](file:///c:/Users/Mark/OneDrive/Documents/Git/ClockworkCities_Pathfinder-1/ClockworkPathfinder/Classes/WarRider.md)** class automatically advances *Riding* for free from **Trained (Lv 1) $\rightarrow$ Expert (Lv 3) $\rightarrow$ Master (Lv 7) $\rightarrow$ Legendary (Lv 15)**.
     - **Non-War-Trained Mounts (`DC 20 Riding Check` at $\ge 50\%$ Damage — 0 Actions):** Civilian riding horses, palfreys, draft horses, and pack mules are not conditioned for lethal trauma. Once a non-war-trained mount takes **50% or more damage** (reduced to $\le 50\%$ of its maximum HP), the rider must succeed at a **0-action DC 20 Riding check every round in combat** (never costs the PC an action or turn!) to keep the mount from bolting. On a failed check, the mount **bolts** (fleeing at full speed away from combat).
     - **War-Trained Mounts (All Bonded War-Mounts, Cavalry Chargers, and Neither War-Beasts):** **Will not bolt** under gunfire, explosions, or trauma (even below 50% HP), and **will fight to the death (0 HP)** without requiring Riding checks to prevent bolting.
     - **Clockwork Horses:** **Never bolt**, are completely immune to fear, and operate until reduced to **0 HP (at `0 HP`, a Clockwork Horse is totally destroyed—reduced to scrap—and cannot be repaired)**.
     - **When a Mount Drops to `0 HP` (Dismount Check — 0 Actions):** When any living or clockwork mount is reduced to `0 HP`, the rider immediately makes a **0-action DC 15 Riding (or Reflex) check**: on a **Success**, the rider lands standing in an adjacent square (no damage); on a **Failure**, the rider lands **Prone** in an adjacent square and takes **`1d6` bludgeoning damage**.
   - **Unified Riding & Maneuver Rules:** **Clockwork Mounts work identically to normal war-trained mounts** for all game rules governing riding, mounting/dismounting, commanding, and any special mounted maneuvers.
   - **Healing Living Mounts vs. Repairing Clockwork Mounts (`[20_Healing.md]`):**
     - **Living Biological Mounts:** Heal using **standard living creature rules** (`[20_Healing.md]`: natural rest recovery, *Treat Wounds*, and **Chirurgeon** field triage/sera).
     - **Clockwork Creatures & Clockwork Horses (Mechanical Repair Rules):** Clockwork mounts do not heal naturally and cannot be healed by biological medicine. Instead, they are repaired via **Clockwork Engineering (Crafting)** (see `[§8.B]`):
       - **Equipped Workshop Repair (`DC 15`):** In an equipped workshop (1 hour per check), roll a **DC 15 Crafting check** and restore Hit Points equal to **the amount by which the roll exceeds DC 15** ($\text{HP Healed} = \text{Roll Total} - 15$).
       - **Field Repair (`1 Hour`, `DC 20`, Max `10 HP / Hour`):** In the field, repairs take **1 hour**, require a **DC 20 Crafting check**, and restore Hit Points equal to $\text{Roll Total} - 20$, to a **maximum of 10 HP per hour**.
3. **Atmospheric & Fodder Exposure (`[TheNeither.md]`):**
   - Biological mounts suffer **Atmospheric Shift** and **Food Shift** (`Clumsy`, `Enfeebled`, `Drained`, and reduced healing) whenever they travel outside their native Stratum (`Strata 1–3` for Spire Equines, `Strata 4` for Light Neither mounts, `Strata 5–6` for Dark Neither mounts).
   - Fitting a biological mount with an **Equine / Beast Atmospheric Rebreather Hood** (`[§6.A]`) reduces its **Atmospheric Shift by 2.0 points**, and carrying native fodder (*Mela Oats* for Spire horses; *Sycelium Mash* or carrion for Neither beasts) eliminates **Food Shift**.
4. **Natural Strike Damage Dice Progression (`+1 Die` / `+2 Dice`):**
   - The bonus damage dice apply permanently to all of the mount's unarmed natural Strikes (*Hoof*, *Jaws*, *Tusk*, *Horn*, *Claw*, *Tail*) and stack with **Mature (Lv 4)** and **Savage/Specialized (Lv 14)** companion upgrades:

| Companion / Mount Growth Stage | Tier 1: Aither / Spire Mount | Tier 2: Light Neither Mount (`+1 Die`) | Tier 3: Dark Neither Mount (`+2 Dice`) |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Young War-Mount (Levels 1–3)**<br>*(or Base Creature Strike)* | `1d8` *(or `1d6`)* | **`2d8`** *(or `2d6`)* | **`3d8`** *(or `3d6`)* |
| **Mature War-Mount (Levels 4–13)** | `2d8` *(or `2d6`)* | **`3d8`** *(or `3d6`)* | **`4d8`** *(or `4d6`)* |
| **Specialized / Apex War-Mount (Levels 14–20)** | `3d8` *(or `3d6`)* | **`4d8`** *(or `4d6`)* | **`5d8`** *(or `5d6`)* |

> **Why Ride a Tier 1 Spire Horse if Neither Beasts Are Tougher?**
> 1. **Speed & Mobility:** Spire Warhorses and Aither Coursers are the **fastest surface mounts** (`Speed 45–50 ft` with *Gallop*), outrunning heavier Neither beasts in open maneuvers.
> 2. **Atmospheric Adaptation (`[TheNeither.md]`):** Light and Dark Neither beasts suffer **Upward Decompression Sickness** (`Enfeebled`, `Sickened`, and rapid Environmental Shift) if brought up into the clean air of the Upper Spire Wards or High Aither without pressurized rebreather harnesses!
> 3. **Social Status & Legal Access:** Inside civilized Spire wards, riding a pedigreed horse is a badge of high **Social Status**, whereas bringing an un-quarantined Dire Boar or Abyssal Lizard into a middle- or upper-class ward triggers an immediate armed response from the Ward Constabulary.

## 3. Horses & Social Status in the Great Spires

In a vertical Spire city housing millions of citizens (`[NewAvalon.md]`, `[ResourcesAndWealth.md]`), open ground is precious, pasture exists only on elevated sky-terraces or royal country estates, and every bale of hay must be hoisted by freight elevator or dirigible. Consequently, **the breed, grooming, and tack of a character's horse is one of the most visible markers of Social Status in civilized society**.

### A. The Five Equine Status Grades in Spire Society

Just like weapons, clothing, and meals (`[ResourcesAndWealth.md §1]`), equine mounts in the Spire visually broadcast their rider's **Social Status Tier** and the **Group / Organization** that issued them:

| Equine Status Grade | Typical Breed & Role | Issuing Group & Social Status Tier | Social & Mechanical Impact in the Spires |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Grade 1: Underclass / Pit Beast**<br>*(Status 0–1: Slums & Mines)* | **Blind Pit-Pony, Cull-Nag, or Scavenged Mule**<br>*(Swaybacked, scarred, fed on fungal mash)* | **Slum / Mine Labor Gangs**<br>*(Status 0–1)* | • Used for hauling slag carts in Worker/Slum wards.<br>• **Social Stigma:** Riding a cull-nag in Ward 13 or any middle/upper ward inflicts a **–2 circumstance penalty to Society and Diplomacy (*Make an Impression*)** and draws immediate suspicion from constables. |
| **Grade 2: Working Guild Beast**<br>*(Status 2: Worker Wards 7–9)* | **Heavy Draft Percheron, Shire Dray, or Pack Mule**<br>*(Thick-legged, iron-shod, braided work harness)* | **Trade, Teamster & Freight Guilds**<br>*(Status 2)* | • Respectable working animals issued by Teamster, Brewer, and Freight Guilds.<br>• **Traffic Restriction:** Permitted on freight avenues and cargo lifts, but barred from Upper Ward passenger promenades unless pulling a licensed guild dray. |
| **Grade 3: Military & Service Charger**<br>*(Status 3: Military Ward & Ward 13)* | **Crown Dragoon Warhorse, Constable Steed, or Frontier Mustang**<br>*(Brand-marked on flank, steel bit, service saddle)* | **Military Regiments, Ward Constabulary & Scout Corps**<br>*(Status 3)* | • Standard issue for **Officers**, **Cavaliers**, **Mounted Constables**, and **Scout Outriders**.<br>• **Service Authority:** Grants a **+1 circumstance bonus to Intimidation (*Demoralize* / *Coerce*)** against pedestrian civilians and rioters while mounted.<br>• Permitted on all Military, Ward 13, and municipal thoroughfares. |
| **Grade 4: High-Society Hackney & Palfrey**<br>*(Status 4: Merchant Ward & Lesser Nobles)* | **Spire Park Palfrey, High-Stepping Hackney, or Matched Carriage Pair**<br>*(Curried coat, silvered stirrups, velvet shabrack)* | **Merchant Consortiums, University & Lesser Houses**<br>*(Status 4)* | • Ridden for leisure and business across Ward 13 parks, University quadrangles, and Merchant Ward avenues.<br>• **Genteel Prestige:** Grants a **+1 status bonus to Society and Diplomacy (*Make an Impression*)** with Status 3–4 citizens, merchants, and officials. |
| **Grade 5: Royal & Great House Courser**<br>*(Status 5–6: The 25 Great Houses & Crown)* | **Purebred Aither Courser or Royal Destrier**<br>*(Raised on Strata 1–2 Aither estates; tattooed ear-pedigree, gilded barding)* | **The 25 Great Houses & Royal Household**<br>*(Status 5–6)* | • The pinnacle of equestrian breeding; bloodlines registered in the Royal Studbook since the Great Kingdom.<br>• **Aristocratic Awe:** Grants a **+2 status bonus to Society, Diplomacy (*Make an Impression*), and Command/Leadership checks** across all Spire wards.<br>• Ward Constables automatically salute and clear checkpoint lanes for a rider mounted on a Grade 5 Courser. |

### B. Issued Mounts & Zero Upkeep Costs (`[ResourcesAndWealth.md]`)
1. **Zero Upkeep Costs (100% Covered by the Issuing Group):**
   - There are **no purchase or upkeep costs tracked for mounts**. Just like weapons, armor, and class gear (`[ResourcesAndWealth.md]`), **both living mounts and Clockwork Mounts** (along with their saddles, barding, clockwork battle gear, modules, stabling, fodder, and replacement parts) are **issued and all costs are 100% covered by the group, guild, warband, or organization issuing them**.
2. **Spire Equine & Beast Ordinances (World Law):**
   - **Spire Street Tack:** Inside paved Spire wards (Wards 1–13), horses are issued rubber-cushioned or brass-rimmed *Cobble-Shoes* (preventing sparks near gas/arc lines) and a leather *Catch-Apron*.
   - **Neither-Beast Quarantine Writs:** **Tier 2 (Light Neither)** and **Tier 3 (Dark Neither)** beasts—such as *Dire Boars*, *Dire-Wolves*, *Cave-Rams*, and *Abyssal Monitor Lizards*—are classified as **Hazardous Fauna** inside civilized Spire walls. They are freely ridden in the Outlands, frontier towns, subterranean mines, and Perimeter Military Yards, but bringing one inside Upper Spire Wards requires a stamped **Military/University Quarantine Writ** and a heavy steel muzzle when off-duty.

## 8. Clockwork Horses (Mechanical Steeds, `4 / 6 / 8 AP` Maintenance & Modular Components)

For **Clockwork Engineers**, **Clockwork Riflemen**, and mechanized Spire expeditions venturing into the toxic Lower Neither or deep caverns where living Spire horses choke on the air, master machinists construct **Clockwork Horses**—quadrupedal brass, spring-steel, and pneumatic steeds powered by Neither-catalytic regulators (`[ClockworkEngineer.md]`, `[ClockworkRifleman.md]`).

### A. Daily AP Maintenance Requirement, 15 AP Maximum Cap & 24-Hour Shutdown Rule
1. **Daily Synchronization Threshold & 15 AP Maximum Mount Value:**
   - Every Clockwork Horse has a **Total Mount AP Value** (`Chassis Base AP [4, 6, or 8] + Installed Module AP Costs`) and a **Required Daily Maintenance AP**:
     $$\mathbf{\text{Total Mount AP Value}} = \text{Chassis Base AP } [4, 6, \text{ or } 8] + \text{Installed Module AP Costs} \quad (\mathbf{\text{Maximum Cap: } 15\text{ AP}})$$
     $$\mathbf{\text{Required Maintenance AP}} = \text{Total Mount AP Value } (\text{max } 15) - \text{Class Discount}$$
   - **Hard 15 AP Mount Value Ceiling:** No Clockwork Horse can be configured with a **Total Mount AP Value** (Chassis Base AP + all installed Passive and Active Modules combined, before Class Discounts) exceeding **15 AP** (e.g., a Mark III `8 AP` Destrier can hold at most `+7 AP` of modules; a Mark II `6 AP` Courser at most `+9 AP`; a Mark I `4 AP` Cob at most `+11 AP`).
   - During daily preparations (10 minutes per mount), the maintaining character must possess a **Base Active Surge AP Capacity (`INT Mod + Class Base AP + Level Scaling`)** equal to or greater than the mount's **Required Maintenance AP**.
   - *Note:* Daily maintenance checks your **total AP capacity** during morning calibration; it does **not** lock out or reduce your per-round Active Surge AP pool during combat!
   - **24-Hour Shutdown:** If a Clockwork Horse is not calibrated during daily preparations by an operator who meets its Required Maintenance AP, its escapements de-synchronize after 24 hours and **it shuts down completely**, becoming an immobile statue until 10 minutes of qualified maintenance is performed.
2. **Progressive Class Maintenance Discounts:**
   - **Clockwork Engineer (Up to `–4 AP` Discount — Earned at Levels `3, 6, 9, 12`):**
     - **Levels 1–2:** `0` Discount.
     - **Level 3:** **`–1 AP` Discount**.
     - **Level 6:** **`–2 AP` Discount**.
     - **Level 9:** **`–3 AP` Discount**.
     - **Level 12+:** **`–4 AP` Full Discount** (minimum 0 AP).
   - **Clockwork Rifleman (Up to `–2 AP` Discount — Earned at Levels `4 and 8`):**
     - **Levels 1–3:** `0` Discount.
     - **Level 4:** **`–1 AP` Discount**.
     - **Level 8+:** **`–2 AP` Full Discount** (minimum 1 AP).
   - **All Other Classes (`0` Discount):** Must meet the full un-discounted Maintenance AP threshold, or rely on a **Clockwork Engineer** to maintain the mount for them.
3. **Clockwork Engineer Squadron Maintenance (`+1 Extra Mount per 3 Levels`):**
   - In addition to maintaining their **own** Clockwork Horse, a **Clockwork Engineer** can maintain **1 extra Clockwork Horse at their current discounted AP capacity for every 3 Clockwork Engineer levels they possess**:
     - **Levels 1–2 (`0` Discount):** **1** Clockwork Mount (Personal).
     - **Level 3+ (`–1 AP` Discount):** **2** Clockwork Mounts (Personal + **1** Ally).
     - **Level 6+ (`–2 AP` Discount):** **3** Clockwork Mounts (Personal + **2** Allies).
     - **Level 9+ (`–3 AP` Discount):** **4** Clockwork Mounts (Personal + **3** Allies — *a full 4-PC party!*).
     - **Level 12+ (`–4 AP` Discount):** **5** Clockwork Mounts (Personal + **4** Allies).
     - **Level 15+ (`–4 AP` Discount):** **6** Clockwork Mounts (Personal + **5** Allies).
     - **Level 18+ (`–4 AP` Discount):** **7** Clockwork Mounts (Personal + **6** Allies — *a full mechanized cavalry squad!*).
   - When an Engineer maintains a Clockwork Horse for an ally, the Engineer synchronizes the saddle controls to that ally for 24 hours—allowing a **Soldier**, **WarRider**, **Officer**, or **Noble** to ride and Command the Clockwork Horse with **0 AP required from the rider**!
4. **Unified Riding, Maneuver & Morale Rules for Clockwork Horses:**
   - **Identical to Normal War-Trained Mounts:** Clockwork Horses use the **exact same game rules as normal living mounts** (`[PC1 p.429–430]`, `[WarRider.md §3]`) for riding, mounting and dismounting (`[one-action]` Move), *Command an Animal* (`[one-action]` for 2 mount actions; automatic with the *Ride* feat or as a WarRider's Bonded War-Mount), and all special mounted maneuvers.
   - **Clockwork Saddle Synchrony (Engineer & Rifleman Feat 2):** Both the **Clockwork Engineer** and **Clockwork Rifleman** can take the 2nd-level class feat **Clockwork Saddle Synchrony** (`[ClockworkEngineer.md §3]`, `[ClockworkRifleman.md §3]`), which makes them **Trained in Riding** (or if they already have Riding, **increases their Riding proficiency by 1 step up to a maximum of Master**) and grants a **+2 circumstance bonus to all Riding checks** (**+3 at Lv 8+**) whenever riding a **Clockwork Horse**.
   - **Never Bolts & Simple HP Pool:** Like all mounts, Clockwork Horses use a **normal single Hit Point pool** (no 3-Step Wound Thresholds). Clockwork Horses **never bolt** under any circumstances and fight/operate until reduced to 0 HP.
   - **Multiple Attack Penalty (Piston Hooves vs. Active `[one-action]` Modules):**
     - A Clockwork Horse's heavy **Piston Hoof / Ram Strike (`1d8` to `5d8`)** works like any normal mount's natural Strike (`[PC1 p.429]`): **it shares a Multiple Attack Penalty with the rider** (unless the rider has the WarRider's *Uncoupled First Bite/Gore* feature).
     - Conversely, an installed **Active `[one-action]` Combat Module (`[§8.D]`)** fires from an independent mechanical gimbal and **never suffers from or increases the rider's MAP (0 MAP interaction, max 1/round)**.

### B. The Three Clockwork Horse Chassis Tiers (`4 AP`, `6 AP`, and `8 AP`) & Repair Rules

All three chassis share **Construct Immunities**: immune to **Bleed, Poison, Disease, Mental/Fear, Paralyzed, Fatigue/Exhaustion, and Atmospheric/Food Exposure Shift**.
- **Clockwork Creature Repair & Total Destruction Rules (Workshop vs. Field):**
  - **0 HP = Total Destruction (Scrap):** If a Clockwork Horse is reduced to **`0 HP`**, its core mechanisms shatter into **unrepairable scrap** (it cannot be repaired and must be replaced by the issuing group, just like a slain biological mount).
  - **Repairing Damaged Clockwork Mounts (`> 0 HP`):** Unlike biological mounts (which heal via `[20_Healing.md]` standard living creature rules), damaged Clockwork Horses do not heal naturally and are repaired using **Clockwork Engineering (Crafting)**:
    1. **Equipped Workshop Repair (`DC 15`, Uncapped):** Working in an equipped shop (1 hour per check), roll a **DC 15 Crafting check**; the Clockwork Creature heals Hit Points equal to **the number by which your roll exceeds 15** ($\mathbf{\text{HP Restored} = \text{Roll Total} - 15}$).
    2. **Field Repair (`1 Hour`, `DC 20`, Max `10 HP / Hour`):** Working in the field without a shop takes **1 hour** and requires a **DC 20 Crafting check**; the Clockwork Creature heals Hit Points equal to $\text{Roll Total} - 20$, **up to a maximum of 10 HP per hour**!
- **Environmental Physics (`[10_Magic.md]`):** Operates at **100% in Neither and Spire Wards (Strata 3–6)**, **1/2 efficiency in the Zone (Strata 2)** (–10 ft Speed unless fed 1 Military Cartridge/hour), and **shuts down completely in Pure Aither (Strata 1)**.
- **Issued Equipment & Spire Social Status (`[mounts.md §3]`):** Like living mounts, Clockwork Horses and their modular components are **issued and their costs/upkeep are 100% covered by the issuing group**. Treated as **Grade 4–5 Prestige Hardware** (`+1` to `+2` status bonus to Society/Intimidation in the Spires).

### C. Modular Component Upgrades (`+1 AP` Standard vs. `+2 AP` Heavy — Gated by Item Level, Max 15 AP Total Mount Value)

Just like weapon *Potency*, *Striking*, and armor *Resilient* runes (`[00_RulesFoundation.md]`), Clockwork Horse **Chassis Marks** and **Modular Components** are gated by **Item Level & Crafting Rank**:
1. **Standard `+1 AP` Modules (Item Levels 2–4, Trained in Crafting):** Available in Spire Guild shops and military quartermasters during early tiers (Levels 2–4).
2. **Heavy / Overclocked `+2 AP` Modules (Item Levels 8–10, Expert in Crafting):** Available starting at Levels 8–10.
3. **The Level 12 Spire Retail Ceiling (`12 AP` Retail Store Cap vs. `15 AP` Masterwork Cap):**
   - **Levels 1–12 (Retail & Requisition Limit — Max `12 AP` Value):** Standard Spire commerce and military requisitions supply chassis and modules up to a **Total Mount AP Value of `12 AP`** (e.g., a Mark III `8 AP` Destrier with `+4 AP` of modules).
   - **Levels 13–20 (Masterwork & Era 1 Vault Limit — `13 to 15 AP` Value):** Pushing a Clockwork Horse from `12 AP` to the **`15 AP` Maximum Cap** requires custom fabrication by a **Master Clockwork Engineer (Level 12+)** or salvaging **Era 1 Vault / Royal Masterwork** components.

### D. Active `[one-action]` Combat Modules (Auxiliary Mount Attacks — $\left\lceil \frac{3}{4} \times \text{Player Level} \right\rceil$ Scaling)

In addition to passive chassis upgrades, a maintainer can install **Active Combat Modules** (`+1 AP` Low-Damage or `+2 AP` Moderate-Damage added to the mount's Daily Maintenance AP).
- **Rider Activation (`[one-action]`, 1/Round):** While mounted, the rider can spend **1 Action `[one-action]`** (pulling a saddle lever or depressing a stirrup pedal; **0 in-combat AP cost**) to trigger an installed Active Combat Module.
- **Tactical Role (Bypasses the –5 Second-Attack Penalty Without Outshining the PC):**
  - **Uncoupled from MAP:** Because the attack originates from the Clockwork Horse's independent mechanism, **it neither suffers from nor increases the rider's Multiple Attack Penalty (MAP)**! This gives a mounted Rifleman, Engineer, or Cavalier a reliable third-action or follow-up attack instead of taking a –5 / –10 MAP penalty.
  - **Functional Level = $\left\lceil \frac{3}{4} \times \text{Player Level} \right\rceil$ at Expert Proficiency (`+4`):** To ensure the horse's auxiliary module is never as accurate as the PC's primary attack, the Clockwork Horse calculates its Module Attack Bonus and Save DC using **Expert Proficiency (`+4`)**, its **Chassis Attribute Modifier** (`+3` at Lv 1–7, `+4` at Lv 8–15, `+5` at Lv 16+), and **$\left\lceil \frac{3}{4} \times \text{Player Level} \right\rceil$ (rounded up)**:
    $$\mathbf{\text{Horse Module Attack Bonus}} = \mathbf{4\text{ (Expert)} + \text{Mount Mod } (+3\text{ to }+5) + \left\lceil \tfrac{3}{4} \times \text{Player Level} \right\rceil}$$
    $$\mathbf{\text{Horse Module Save DC}} = \mathbf{14\text{ (10 + Expert)} + \text{Mount Mod } (+3\text{ to }+5) + \left\lceil \tfrac{3}{4} \times \text{Player Level} \right\rceil}$$
