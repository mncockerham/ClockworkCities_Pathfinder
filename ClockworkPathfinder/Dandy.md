# Dandy

- **Base Class:** Swashbuckler `[Player Core 2 p.176]`
- **Status:** **Modified** (1-to-1 baseline with Pathfinder 2e Remaster Swashbuckler; tailored to high-society duelists and courtly rakes)
- **Availability:** **Primary PC Class** (Full 1–20 progression)
- **Discipline / Trait:** Martial / Skill (Finesse & Social Dueling)

---

## 1. Setting Overview
*The glamorous, sharp-tongued duelists and salon dandies of the upper spire circles.*

- **In-World Identity:** Scions of minor noble houses, third- or fourth-born children of great merchant dynasties, or wealthy dilettantes with coin to spare and no direct estate to manage. They turn high-society boredom into an art form, mastering the rapier, cane-sword, ballroom etiquette, and the cutthroat politics of Ward 13 (Luxury Ward) and the Noble District.
- **Typical Social Class & Backing:** Lesser Noble (Status 5), Noble (Status 7), or children of Merchant Houses and Wall Merchants (Status 6–8). They enjoy substantial family allowances, elite dress, and legal deference, backed by the prestige of their family name.
- **Physical / Scientific Explanation:** Exceptional agility, salon dueling pedagogy, lightning-fast footwork, and psychological gamesmanship (taunts, bravado, fencing feints) rather than heavy armor or crude brute force.

## 5. Multiclass Archetype (Dedication)
*Characters of other classes can gain training in the Dandy (Swashbuckler) archetype [Player Core 2 p.220].*

- **Dandy Dedication (Feat 2):** `[Player Core 2 p.220]`  
  - **Prerequisites:** Dexterity 14 (+2), Charisma 14 (+2).
  - **Benefits:** Trained in Acrobatics and your chosen style skill; gain Panache class feature and access to basic Panache generation actions.
- **Basic Finisher (Feat 4):** Gain the Confident Finisher action `[Player Core 2 p.220]`.
- **Basic Flair (Feat 4):** Gain a 1st- or 2nd-level Dandy feat `[Player Core 2 p.220]`.
- **Dandy's Resiliency (Feat 4):** Gain 3 additional HP for each Dandy archetype feat taken `[Player Core 2 p.220]`.
- **Advanced Flair (Feat 6):** Gain a Dandy feat up to half your character level `[Player Core 2 p.220]`.
- **Vivacious Stepper (Feat 8):** Gain Vivacious Speed bonus (+5 ft, or +10 ft with Panache) `[Player Core 2 p.220]`.
- **Evasive Swashbuckler (Feat 12):** Gain the Evasion class feature (Reflex to Master) `[Player Core 2 p.220]`.
