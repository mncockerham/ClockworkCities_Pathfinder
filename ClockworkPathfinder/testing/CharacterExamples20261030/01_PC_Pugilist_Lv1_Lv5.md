## 1. Character Creation Edge Allocation (10 Points)
*Marcus grew up fighting in the illegal subterranean pits of Ward 13. By starting at Status 0, he retains all 10 Edge points to become an unyielding physical specimen:*
- **Starting Social Status (Status 0):** **0 Points** (10 remaining).
- **Armor / Weapon Step-Up (3 Points — 1-Time Purchase, Max Expert):** Gains training in **Light Armor** (padded/leather jerkins for street scraps) scaling to Expert.
- **Hardened Combat Frame (2 Points):** Gains **+1 HP per character level** and the *Toughness* feat.
- **Iron-Gut & Natural Poison Immunity (2 Points):** Treating all saving throws against natural poisons, venoms, or toxic fungal spores as a **Critical Success on a normal Success**.
- **Neither-Conditioned Lungs & Exposure Resilience (2 Points):** Gains **3 Fortune Rerolls between full rests** on saves against Neither sickness, atmospheric shock, or suffocating coal dust.
- **Workhorse Bulk & Industrial Stamina (1 Point):** Treats Strength as +2 higher for Bulk; successes on forced march / heavy labor Fortitude saves become **Critical Successes**.

## 2. Level 1 Build & Mechanics

### Attributes & Defenses
- **Attributes:** STR +3 (16), DEX +2 (14), CON +3 (16), INT +0 (10), WIS +1 (12), CHA –1 (8)
- **Hit Points:** **25 HP** (Human 8 + Pugilist 10 + Con 3 + Toughness 1 + Hardened Frame Edge Lv 1 3)
- **Wound Thresholds (Step $\lfloor 25/4 \rfloor = 6$):**
  - **Mortal Wounds:** 1 to 6 HP (Weeks recovery)
  - **Critical Wounds:** 7 to 12 HP (Days recovery)
  - **Regular Damage:** 13 to 25 HP (13 HP band; heals normal rest/field kits)
- **Armor Class:** **17** (10 + Expert Unarmored 4 + Dex 2 + Level 1) — *Can fight bare-chested in a parlor without gear penalty!*
- **Saving Throws:** Fortitude +8 (Expert), Reflex +7 (Expert), Will +6 (Expert)
- **Perception:** +4 (Trained)
- **Speed:** 30 feet (Base 25 + Pugilist unarmored +5 ft)

### Social Skills (`Understanding`) & Active AP
- **Active Surge AP:** **0 AP / round** (Tier 0 Martial). Operates purely on biological stamina and kinetic muscle; zero reliance on radiant dynamos or cartridges.
- **Starting Social Skills (`Understanding`):**
  - **1. Criminal Underworld [Ward 13 Warren Pits]** (Specific Place, `+2`) — Knows Johnny Ralen's pit bookies, safehouses, and referee payoffs.
  - **2. Labor Organizations [Dockside Stevedores & Coal Shovelers]** (Singular Sub-Group, `+1`) — Backed by dock brawlers; can seek refuge in coal silos.

### Combat Strikes & Actions
- **Bare-Knuckle Heavy Strike:** +6 to hit, **1d6+3 B** (Agile, Finesse, Nonlethal, Unarmed).
- **Flurry of Blows ([one-action]):** Make two unarmed strikes against a target for 1 action. Multi-attack penalty applies normally after both attacks.
- **Street Clinch (Athletics +6):** Trip or Grapple without requiring a free hand or weapon.

### Level 1 Equipment Loadout & Citizen Bulwark Slots
* **3 Citizen Bulwark Slots (Player Choice — Never Confiscated):**
  1. *Weighted Canvas Hand-Wraps* with leaded knuckles (Primary Unarmed Weapon).
  2. *Standard Neither Breather Mask* (Brass nose cup with Sycelium filter).
  3. *Reinforced Leather Tool Vest* (Padded defense, Bulwark retention).
* **Other Gear:** Rough corduroy trousers, wool flat cap, bone-handled folding jackknife, tin chicory flask, greasy pouch with 12 Slivers and 4 Pins.

## 3. Level 5 Progression & Upgrades

### Attributes & Defenses
- **Attribute Boosts (Lv 5):** STR +4 (18), DEX +3 (16), CON +4 (18), WIS +2 (14)
- **Hit Points:** **79 HP** (Human 8 + Pugilist 50 + Con 20 + Toughness 5 + Hardened Frame Edge 5)
- **Wound Thresholds (Step $\lfloor 79/4 \rfloor = 19$):**
  - **Mortal Wounds:** 1 to 19 HP
  - **Critical Wounds:** 20 to 38 HP
  - **Regular Damage:** 39 to 79 HP (41 HP buffer)
- **Armor Class:** **22** (10 + Expert Unarmored 4 + Dex 3 + Level 5)
- **Saving Throws:** Fortitude +13 (Master / Juggernaut), Reflex +12 (Expert), Will +11 (Expert)
  - *Edge Bonuses Active:* 3 Rerolls/day vs Neither sickness; poison/spore successes become Critical Successes.
- **Speed:** 40 feet (Base 25 + Pugilist unarmored +15 ft)

### Combat Strikes & Feats
- **Master's Heavy Boxing Strike (+1 Striking Brass Knuckles):** +13 to hit, **2d6+4 B** (Agile, Finesse).
- **Savage Combatant (Feat Lv 2 — 1 Action):** Enters adrenaline fury: gains +6 Temp HP, +1 melee damage (+2 two-handed), –1 AC.
- **Relentless Savagery (Feat Lv 4):** Increases melee damage bonus to +2 (+3 two-handed); grants +1 status to Athletics (Grapple/Trip); free 10-ft Stride when activating Savage state.
- **Whirling Throw ([one-action]):** Hurl a grappled opponent up to 20 feet into a wall or table, dealing `2d6+4 B` and knocking them Prone.
