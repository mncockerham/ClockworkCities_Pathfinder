# Character Profile: Rowan Thorne
## Category 4: Neither & Outland Specialist (Scout) — Levels 1 & 5

- **Class:** Scout (Ranger baseline: Surface Outrider & Deep Delver) `[Scout.md]`
- **Ancestry / Heritage:** Orc (Native Lower Neither / Thick Epidermis) `[AncestriesAndHeritages.md]`
- **Social Class:** Tier 2: Free Citizens & Laborers (Status 1: Free Outland Guide) `[SocialClasses.md]`
- **Specialty:** Wilderness tracking, composite longbow marksmanship, zero-grid survival

## 1. Character Creation Edge Allocation (10 Points)
*Rowan was raised on the surface valley lowlands guiding caravans through the dark Neither. Starting as a Status 1 Freeman, he pays 1 Edge point for status and allocates his remaining 9 points to maximize his outland durability and archery lethality:*
- **Starting Social Status (Status 1 — Free Outrider):** **1 Point** (9 remaining).
  - *Benefits:* Legally recognized independent citizen, 2 Nobels monthly spending allowance, right to carry hunting bows and tools on Peasant Paths.
- **Armor / Weapon Step-Up (3 Points — 1-Time Purchase, Max Expert):**
  - Steps up armor proficiency from native baseline into **Heavy Armor** (cured beast-bone and iron plate coats), scaling to Expert.
- **Neither-Conditioned Lungs & Exposure Resilience (2 Points — Orc Trait):**
  - Gains **3 Fortune Rerolls between full rests** on saving throws against Neither sickness, spore clouds, or toxic sump vapors.
- **Iron-Gut & Natural Poison Immunity (2 Points — Orc Trait):**
  - Any successful saving throw against natural poisons, venoms, or raw Sycelium rot counts as a **Critical Success**.
- **Workhorse Bulk & Industrial Stamina (1 Point):**
  - Strength counts as +2 higher for Bulk; treats forced march / survival endurance successes as **Critical Successes**.
- **Extra Trained Skill (1 Point):**
  - Gains training in **Crafting: Trapping & Leatherwork**.

## 2. Level 1 Build & Mechanics

### Attributes & Defenses
- **Attributes:** STR +3 (16), DEX +3 (16), CON +2 (14), INT +0 (10), WIS +2 (14), CHA –1 (8)
- **Hit Points:** **22 HP** (Orc 10 + Scout 10 + Con 2)
- **Wound Thresholds (Step $\lfloor 22/4 \rfloor = 5$):**
  - **Mortal Wounds:** 1 to 5 HP (Weeks recovery)
  - **Critical Wounds:** 6 to 10 HP (Days recovery)
  - **Regular Damage:** 11 to 22 HP (12 HP buffer)
- **Armor Class:** **18** (10 + Trained Heavy Armor 3 + Dex 1 + Cured Hide & Iron Splint Coat +4) — *Heavy Armor Edge active!*
- **Saving Throws:** Fortitude +7 (Expert), Reflex +8 (Expert), Will +5 (Trained)
  - *Orc Edge Active:* 3 Rerolls/day vs Neither exposure; poison successes become Critical Successes.
- **Perception:** +7 (Expert / Low-Light Vision & Scent)
- **Speed:** 25 feet

### Active Surge AP & Combat Operations
- **Active Surge AP Pool:** **0 AP / round** (Tier 1 Operative).
- **Zero-Grid Independence:** Rowan fires mechanical composite bows and swings cold-forged hatchets. He requires zero radiant grid power, zero dynamos, and zero cartridges, operating at peak lethal capability deep in dead wastelands where technology stalls.

### Combat Strikes & Actions
- **Composite Longbow Strike (Range 100 ft, 2-Handed):** +6 to hit, **1d8+1 P** (Deadly d10, Propulsive, Volley 30 ft).
- **Hunted Prey ([one-action]):** Designate a target within 100 ft; gain circumstance bonuses to Seek/Track, and reduce multiple attack penalties (Flurry edge: –3 / –6 instead of –5 / –10).
- **Twin Takedown ([one-action]):** When dual-wielding hand axes in melee, make two Strikes for 1 action against your hunted prey.

### Level 1 Equipment Loadout & Citizen Bulwark Slots
* **3 Citizen Bulwark Slots (Player Choice — Never Confiscated):**
  1. *Laminated Composite Longbow* (Heavy horn and ash bow).
  2. *Cured Wolfhide & Iron Splint Coat* (Heavy armor, +4 AC, Dex cap +1).
  3. *Paired Forged Camp Hatchets* (Matched melee implements, 1d6 S, Agile).
* **Other Carried Gear:** Quiver of 20 bodkin arrows, 50-ft braided Neither-grass rope, iron snare kit, water bladder, salt-pork rations, 2 Nobels in silver.

## 3. Level 5 Progression & Upgrades

### Attributes & Defenses
- **Attribute Boosts (Lv 5):** STR +4 (18), DEX +4 (18), CON +3 (16), WIS +3 (16)
- **Hit Points:** **68 HP** (Orc 10 + Scout 50 + Con 15)
- **Wound Thresholds (Step $\lfloor 68/4 \rfloor = 17$):**
  - **Mortal Wounds:** 1 to 17 HP
  - **Critical Wounds:** 18 to 34 HP
  - **Regular Damage:** 35 to 68 HP (34 HP buffer)
- **Armor Class:** **23** (10 + Expert Heavy Armor 4 + Dex 1 + Level 5 + +1 Niracrest Splint Coat +5)
- **Saving Throws:** Fortitude +12 (Expert), Reflex +13 (Master), Will +10 (Trained)
  - *Orc Edge Active:* 3 Rerolls/day; poison immunity; delayed Neither storm onset (Orc physiology).
- **Perception:** +12 (Master / Danger Sense)
- **Speed:** 25 feet

### Combat Actions & Feats
- **Master's +1 Striking Composite Longbow:** +12 to hit, **2d8+2 P** (Deadly d10, Propulsive).
- **Orc Ferocity ([reaction]):** When reduced to 0 HP, Rowan remains standing at 1 HP instead of falling unconscious.
- **Skirmish Strike ([one-action]):** Stride up to half speed and make a Strike, either before or after moving.
