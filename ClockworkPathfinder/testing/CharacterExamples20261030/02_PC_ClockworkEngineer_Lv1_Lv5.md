## 1. Character Creation Edge Allocation (10 Points)
*Theron belongs to the Machine-Wrights & Clockmakers Guild in Ward 11 (Craft). As a Status 2 Artisan, he pays 2 Edge points for his guild standing and uses his remaining 8 points to supercharge his technical and armor capacities:*
- **Starting Social Status (Status 2 — Guild Member):** **2 Points** (8 remaining).
  - *Benefits:* Permanent workshop flat in Ward 11, half-cost guild scrap access, 1/2 council vote, 5 Nobels monthly spending allowance.
- **Armor / Weapon Step-Up (3 Points — 1-Time Purchase, Max Expert):**
  - Steps up from Light Armor baseline to **Medium Armor** (wears fitted Niracrest mail vests and riveted mechanic breastplates).
- **AP Battery Surge (4 Points):**
  - Gains **+1 Base AP at Level 1**, plus **+1 AP at Level 5** and Level 15. This allows him to sustain rapid-fire radiant discharges and run auxiliary gadget barriers simultaneously.
- **Jury-Rigger / Scrap Savant (1 Point [adjusted]):**
  - Can field-repair broken or burned-out NeitherTeck in 10 minutes instead of hours, using half the normal scrap cost.

## 2. Level 1 Build & Mechanics

### Attributes & Defenses
- **Attributes:** STR +1 (12), DEX +2 (14), CON +2 (14), INT +4 (18), WIS +0 (10), CHA +0 (10)
- **Hit Points:** **18 HP** (Human 8 + Engineer 8 + Con 2)
- **Wound Thresholds (Step $\lfloor 18/4 \rfloor = 4$):**
  - **Mortal Wounds:** 1 to 4 HP (Weeks recovery)
  - **Critical Wounds:** 5 to 8 HP (Days recovery)
  - **Regular Damage:** 9 to 18 HP (10 HP band)
- **Armor Class:** **17** (10 + Trained Medium Armor 3 + Dex 1 + Armored Coat/Breastplate +3) — *Medium Armor Edge active!*
- **Saving Throws:** Fortitude +7 (Expert), Reflex +5 (Trained), Will +5 (Expert)
- **Perception:** +3 (Trained)
- **Speed:** 25 feet

### Active Surge AP & Radiant Cadence
- **Active Surge AP Pool:** $\text{INT (+4)} + \text{Base (+2 Tier 3)} + \mathbf{\text{Edge (+1 AP)}} = \mathbf{7 \text{ AP / round}}$.
- **Firing Cadence (Inductive Arc Projector):**
  - Base AP cost: 4 AP. Trained `[Technical]` discipline tag reduces cost to **3 AP per shot**.
  - In a Radiant grid (or with charged battery pack), Theron can fire **2 full radiant shots per round (3 AP + 3 AP = 6 AP)** and still have **1 AP remaining** to deploy a kinetic gadget shield or prime an auto-injector.

### Combat Strikes & Actions
- **Inductive Arc Projector (2-Handed Radiant Weapon, Range 60 ft):** +5 to hit, **1d8+1 E** (Electricity/Radiant Force, Fatal d12; 3 AP with technical tag).
- **Overdrive ([one-action], Crafting DC 15):** Supercharge internal coils. On success, adds +2 circumstance damage (or +4 on critical) to all NeitherTeck strikes for 1 minute.
- **Tamper ([one-action]):** Interfere with an adjacent enemy's clockwork weapon or NeitherTeck device (Crafting vs Reflex DC), inflicting Clumsy 1 or weapon misfires.

### Level 1 Equipment Loadout & Citizen Bulwark Slots
* **3 Citizen Bulwark Slots (Player Choice — Never Confiscated):**
  1. *Prototype Inductive Arc Projector* (Primary Weapon Innovation).
  2. *Riveted Niracrest Mechanic's Vest* (Medium armor breastplate, Bulwark retention).
  3. *Clockwork Multi-Meter & Calibrator Case* (Masterwork technical tool).
* **Other Gear:** Heavy oiled canvas apron, insulated rubberized gloves, standard Neither Breather (Brass nose cup), 2 backup civilian rechargeable battery cells, 5 Nobels in Guild scrip.

### Active Surge AP & Combat Modifications
- **Active Surge AP Pool:** $\text{INT (+4)} + \text{Base (+2)} + \text{Class Lv 4 (+1)} + \mathbf{\text{Edge Lv 5 (+2)}} = \mathbf{9 \text{ AP / round}}$.

- **Megavolt ([two-actions]):** Release a 60-foot line of crackling arc energy dealing `3d12` electricity damage (Reflex save DC 22).
