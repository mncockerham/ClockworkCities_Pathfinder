## 1. Character Creation Edge Allocation (10 Points)
*Aldous is the junior heir of the Sterling Maritime & Airship Import Syndicate in Ward 14. To wield the full corporate influence, legal presumption, and armed retainers of a true Wall Merchant, he pays 7 Edge points for status, entering the game with significant social leverage but constrained personal combat edges:*
- **Starting Social Status (Status 6 — Wall Merchant):** **7 Points** (Base 6 + 1 for Armed Retainers; **3 points remaining**).
  - *Benefits:* Permanent luxury estate along the Ward 14 Spire Wall, 150 Nobels monthly spending allowance, legal right to open-carry dueling rapiers with "Golden Child" legal presumption, corporate carriage pass, armed retainers.
- **Armor / Weapon Step-Up (3 Points — 1-Time Purchase, Max Expert):**
  - Steps up weapon training from Simple Weapons to **Martial Weapons** (trained in masterwork Spire Dueling Rapiers and dueling smallswords), scaling to Expert.
- *(Remaining Edge Points: 0 Points)*. Aldous relies on his wealth, contracted bodyguards, and legal clout rather than biological mutations or heavy armor.

## 2. Level 1 Build & Mechanics

### Attributes & Defenses
- **Attributes:** STR +0 (10), DEX +2 (14), CON +1 (12), INT +3 (16), WIS +1 (12), CHA +3 (16)
- **Hit Points:** **17 HP** (Human 8 + Merchant 8 + Con 1)
- **Wound Thresholds (Step $\lfloor 17/4 \rfloor = 4$):**
  - **Mortal Wounds:** 1 to 4 HP (Weeks recovery)
  - **Critical Wounds:** 5 to 8 HP (Days recovery)
  - **Regular Damage:** 9 to 17 HP (9 HP buffer)
- **Armor Class:** **16** (10 + Trained Light Armor 3 + Dex 2 + Level 1 + Tailored Frock Coat +1)
- **Saving Throws:** Fortitude +4 (Trained), Reflex +7 (Expert), Will +6 (Expert)
- **Perception:** +6 (Expert / Eye for Counterfeits & Liars)
- **Speed:** 25 feet

### Active Surge AP & Influence Directives
- **Active Surge AP Pool:** $\text{INT (+3)} + \text{Base (+1 Influence)} = \mathbf{4 \text{ AP / round}}$.
- **Expenditure Focus:** While Aldous cannot fabricate NeitherTeck, his 4 AP allows him to prime and trigger high-end disposable clockwork devices, alchemical flash canisters, or personal force-converters bought from master artisans.

### Combat Strikes & Actions
- **Spire Dueling Rapier Strike (Fair Steel Mandate):** +7 to hit (+2 Dex + Trained Martial 4 + Level 1), **1d6 P** (Deadly d8, Disarm, Finesse).
  - *Fair Steel Legal Mandate:* Made of fine mundane steel (Hardness 9 masterwork at 50 Nobels; zero clockwork cheats, completely legal in all upper salons).
- **Commercial Incentive ([one-action], Auditory):** Grant an ally within 30 feet a +1 status bonus to attack rolls or skill checks for 1 round by promising a financial bounty or tactical bonus.
- **Contract Bodyguard Interpose ([reaction]):** When targeted by a melee Strike, command a contracted apprentice or bodyguard within 5 feet to interpose, granting Aldous Standard Cover (+2 AC).

### Level 1 Equipment Loadout & Citizen Bulwark Slots
* **3 Citizen Bulwark Slots (Player Choice — Never Confiscated):**
  1. *Spire Dueling Rapier* (50 Nobels masterwork steel, Hardness 9, Fair Steel Mandate).
  2. *Tailored Silk & Wool Ballistic Frock Coat* (Light armor, +1 AC, Dex cap +3).
  3. *Imported Elven Battery Lantern* (Ward 11 charged, polished silver casing).
* **Other Carried Gear (High Liquid Wealth Advantage):** Gilded pocket watch, vellum commercial ledger, Ward 14 West Carriage mid-circle transit pass, 2 Chirurgeon Cellular Vigor tonics, 45 Nobels in gold sovereigns and banknotes.
