# Character Profile: Corbin Hall
## Category 5: Broad Generalist (Soldier) — Levels 1 & 5

- **Class:** Soldier (Fighter baseline: Line Regular / Shield Vanguard) `[Soldier.md]`
- **Ancestry / Heritage:** Dwarf (Lightless Neither / Subterranean Sapper lineage) `[AncestriesAndHeritages.md]`
- **Social Class:** Tier 4: Military & Constabulary (Status 2: Garrison Shield Regular) `[SocialClasses.md]`
- **Specialty:** Heavy formation defense, weapon master proficiency (+2 attack bonus advantage), shield block bastion

---

## 1. Character Creation Edge Allocation (10 Points)
*Corbin is an unyielding Dwarven frontline infantryman who grew up quarrying Neithercrete foundations before taking the garrison oath. Spending 2 points on Status 2, he commits his remaining 8 points to achieve supreme melee and defensive mastery:*
- **Starting Social Status (Status 2 — Military Regular):** **2 Points** (8 remaining).
  - *Benefits:* Barracks bunk in Ward 2, military gear maintenance discounts, 5 Nobels monthly allowance.
- **Master Mastery Upgrade (5 Points — Max Master, Requires Native Expert):**
  - Because Soldier natively grants **Expert** weapon proficiency at Level 1, Corbin spends 5 points to allow his sword proficiency to scale to **Master** earlier and cap at absolute mechanical perfection.
- **Dense Stature & Stone Anchor (2 Points — Dwarf Trait):**
  - Saves/DCs against **Shove, Trip, or Reposition** treat any **Success as a Critical Success**; +4 circumstance bonus against forced movement on stone.
- **Workhorse Bulk & Industrial Stamina (1 Point):**
  - Strength counts as +2 higher for carrying Bulk; immune to encumbrance from heavy armor; forced march / heavy labor successes become **Critical Successes**.

## 2. Level 1 Build & Mechanics

### Attributes & Defenses
- **Attributes:** STR +4 (18), DEX +1 (12), CON +3 (16), INT +0 (10), WIS +1 (12), CHA +0 (10)
- **Hit Points:** **23 HP** (Dwarf 10 + Soldier 10 + Con 3)
- **Wound Thresholds (Step $\lfloor 23/4 \rfloor = 5$):**
  - **Mortal Wounds:** 1 to 5 HP (Weeks recovery)
  - **Critical Wounds:** 6 to 10 HP (Days recovery)
  - **Regular Damage:** 11 to 23 HP (13 HP buffer)
- **Armor Class:** **18** (10 + Trained Heavy Armor 3 + Level 1 + Splint Mail +4) / **20 with Heavy Shield Raised**
- **Saving Throws:** Fortitude +8 (Expert), Reflex +6 (Expert), Will +4 (Trained)
  - *Stone Anchor Active:* Trip/Shove successes become Critical Successes; unmovable on stone.
- **Perception:** +6 (Expert)
- **Speed:** 20 feet (Unburdened Iron negates –5 ft heavy armor penalty)

### Active Surge AP & Combat Operations
- **Active Surge AP Pool:** **0 AP / round** (Tier 0 Dedicated Martial). Corbin relies purely on cold steel, leverage, and martial discipline; completely immune to dead grids, lightning stalls, or cartridge misfires.

### Combat Strikes & Actions
- **Military Broadsword Strike (+2 Master Attack Advantage):** **+9 to hit** (+4 Str + 4 Expert + Level 1), **1d8+4 S** (Versatile P).
- **Reactive Strike ([reaction]):** Make a melee Strike against any enemy that moves, manipulates, or fires a ranged weapon within reach.
- **Shield Block ([reaction]):** Reduce incoming physical damage by the Hardness of his reinforced steel shield (Hardness 5, 20 HP).
- *Shield Reload Penalty Note:* When Corbin wields his heavy shield, any cartridge reload action takes **+1 Action** unless he drops/unequips the shield. Corbin fights with a broadsword (no reload required), completely bypassing this penalty!

### Level 1 Equipment Loadout & Citizen Bulwark Slots
* **3 Citizen Bulwark Slots (Player Choice — Never Confiscated):**
  1. *Forged Steel Military Broadsword* (Hardness 7, 1d8+4 S/P).
  2. *Heavy Arclean Splint Mail* (Heavy armor, +4 AC).
  3. *Reinforced Steel Pavise Shield* (Hardness 5, +2 AC when raised).
* **Other Carried Gear:** Standard Neither Breather (brass nose cup), oilstone, spare scabbard, whetstone, water flask, 5 Nobels in military pay scrip.
