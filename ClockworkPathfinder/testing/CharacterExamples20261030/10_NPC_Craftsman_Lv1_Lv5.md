# NPC Profile: Craftsman (Blacksmith Apprentice & Guild Journeyman)
## Non-Player Character Class — Levels 1 & 5

- **Class:** Craftsman (Guild Registered Artisan Baseline)
- **Role:** Ward 11 blacksmith, armory repairer, carpenter, tannery hand
- **Social Class:** Tier 3: Guild Members & Artisans (Status 1 to 2)
- **Edge System Notes:** Craftsmen belong to registered city guilds. At Status 2 (2 Edge points in PC builds), they possess guild protection, commercial shopfront rights, wholesale trade access, and legal authority to maintain active forge fires and industrial steam conduits.

---

## 1. Level 1 Craftsman Stat Block

### Attributes & Defenses
- **Attributes:** STR +2 (14), DEX +1 (12), CON +2 (14), INT +2 (14), WIS +0 (10), CHA –1 (8)
- **Hit Points:** 18 HP (Human 8 + Craftsman 8 + Con 2)
- **Trauma Thresholds:** Mortal Band $\le 4$ HP ($\lfloor 18/4 \rfloor$); Critical Band $5\text{--}9$ HP ($\lfloor 18/2 \rfloor$)
- **Armor Class:** **15** (10 + Trained Light Armor 3 + Dex 1 + Heavy Cowhide Blacksmith Apron +1)
- **Saving Throws:** Fortitude +7 (Expert), Reflex +4 (Trained), Will +3 (Trained)
- **Perception:** +3 (Trained)
- **Speed:** 25 feet

### Citizen Bulwark Slots (Level 1)
1. **Primary Tool/Weapon:** Forged Steel Ball-Peen / Shop Hammer (1d6+2 B, 1 Bulk).
2. **Defensive Garb:** Heavy Cowhide Blacksmith Apron & Heat-Resistant Gauntlets (Light Armor, +1 AC, fire resistance 1).
3. **Artisan Registration:** Guild Registration Medallion (Ward 11) & Precision Calipers / Flux Belt.

### Core NPC Abilities
- **Guild Crafting & Maintenance:** Trained in Crafting (+5) and *Blacksmithing Lore* (+5). Repairs mundane weapons, armor, and tools without risk of critical failure on ordinary materials.
- **Workshop Tool AP Discount:** –1 AP cost on hydraulic presses, forge blowers, and pneumatic riveters.
- **Sturdy Repose:** Fire resistance 1 (conditioned by forge hearths and molten metal).

### Attacks & Actions
- **Blacksmith's Ball-Peen Hammer:** +5 to hit, **1d6+2 B**.
- **Forge Tongs Strike:** +5 to hit, **1d4+2 B** (Disarm trait).
- **Tanner's Lye Splash ([one-action]):** Fling a ladle of burning chemical lye; target within 10 ft must make DC 14 Reflex save or take 1d6 acid damage and become *Dazzled* for 1 round.

### Gear (Level 1)
- Heavy cowhide blacksmith apron, heat-resistant leather gauntlets (+1 AC, 2 N).
- Forged steel shop hammer (1d6 B, 1 N).
- Belt ring with steel calipers, wire brush, and flux paste (1 N).
- Guild registration medallion of Ward 11, 6 Nobels in silver shillings.

## 2. Level 5 Craftsman (Journeyman Armorer / Master Smith)

### Attributes & Defenses
- **Attributes:** STR +3 (16), DEX +2 (14), CON +3 (16), INT +3 (16), WIS +1 (12), CHA +0 (10)
- **Hit Points:** 58 HP (Human 8 + Craftsman 40 + Con 15)
- **Trauma Thresholds:** Mortal Band $\le 14$ HP ($\lfloor 58/4 \rfloor$); Critical Band $15\text{--}29$ HP ($\lfloor 58/2 \rfloor$)
- **Armor Class:** **19** (10 + Trained Light Armor 7 + Dex 2) — *Hardened boiled leather shop armor (+2 AC, Dex cap +3)*
- **Saving Throws:** Fortitude +12 (Expert), Reflex +9 (Trained), Will +8 (Trained)
- **Perception:** +8 (Trained)
- **Speed:** 25 feet

### Citizen Bulwark Slots (Level 5)
1. **Primary Tool/Weapon:** Master's Forged High-Tensile Steel Sledgehammer (+1 to hit, 1d8+3 B, Shove, 2 Bulk).
2. **Defensive Garb:** Hardened Boiled Leather Shop Cuirass & Riveted Chaps (+2 AC, fire resistance 2).
3. **Journeyman's Instrument:** Master Craftsman Guild Signet Stamp & Pneumatic Hand Riveter (1 AP).

### Core NPC Upgrades
- **Quick Field Patchwork:** During a 10-minute rest, restores 5 HP to broken shields or dented armor using rivets and wire.
- **Stonemason / Smith Hardness Bypass:** Ignores 5 points of object Hardness when striking stone, brick, or metal mechanisms.

### Attacks & Actions
- **Master's Forge Sledge (+1 Heavy Hammer):** +11 to hit, **1d8+3 B** (+1 circumstance damage against constructs or armor).
- **Sparks of the Anvil ([two-actions]):** Strike an iron anvil or metal shield with thunderous force; creatures in a 15-foot cone must make DC 19 Reflex save or take 2d6 fire/sonic damage.

### Gear (Level 5)
- Hardened boiled leather shop armor (+2 AC, 5 Nobels).
- Forged high-tensile steel Sledgehammer (+1 to hit, 35 Nobels).
- Pneumatic hand riveter (uses 1 AP, 5 Nobels).
- Repair kit with brazing torch, solder wire, and 28 Nobels in guild scrip.
