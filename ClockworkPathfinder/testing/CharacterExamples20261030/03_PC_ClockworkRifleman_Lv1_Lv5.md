- **Class:** Clockwork Rifleman (Gunslinger baseline: Way of the Sniper / Airship Marine) `[ClockworkRifleman.md]`

- **Specialty:** Long-range precision fire, capacitive low-radiant cycling, airship gunwales

## 1. Character Creation Edge Allocation (10 Points)
*Kroll is an enlisted marine veteran hailing from high-altitude airship patrols in the Zone. Starting at Status 2, he spends 2 points on military standing and allocates his remaining 8 points for shooting precision and zone survivability:*
- **Starting Social Status (Status 2 — Military Regular):** **2 Points** (8 remaining).
  - *Benefits:* Regimental bunk in Ward 2 (Military), military cartridge checkout rights, 5 Nobels monthly allowance.
- **AP Battery Surge (4 Points):**
  - Gains **+1 Base AP at Level 1**, plus **+1 AP at Level 5** and Level 15. Lets him fire his heavy cartridge rifle and prime specialized tactical cartridges without starving his round.
- **Zone Static Attunement & Dielectric Grounding (2 Points — Half-Elf Trait):**
  - Gains **Electricity Resistance equal to $\max(2, \text{CON mod})$** (Resistance 2).
  - Reflex successes against lightning or electrical attacks become **Critical Successes** and give +10 ft Speed for 1 round.
- **Rigging Reflexes & Catwalk Agility (2 Points — Half-Elf Trait):**
  - Acrobatics balance checks on airship rigging and spire catwalks treat a **Success as a Critical Success**; treats falls as 20 feet shorter.

## 2. Level 1 Build & Mechanics

### Attributes & Defenses
- **Attributes:** STR +1 (12), DEX +4 (18), CON +2 (14), INT +1 (12), WIS +1 (12), CHA +0 (10)
- **Hit Points:** **20 HP** (Half-Elf 8 + Rifleman 8 + Con 2 + Military conditioning 2)
- **Wound Thresholds (Step $\lfloor 20/4 \rfloor = 5$):**
  - **Mortal Wounds:** 1 to 5 HP (Weeks recovery)
  - **Critical Wounds:** 6 to 10 HP (Days recovery)
  - **Regular Damage:** 11 to 20 HP (10 HP buffer)
- **Armor Class:** **18** (10 + Trained Light Armor 3 + Dex 4 + Level 1 + Quilted Ballistic Coat +1)
- **Saving Throws:** Fortitude +7 (Expert), Reflex +9 (Master!), Will +4 (Trained)
  - *Dielectric Grounding Active:* Resistance 2 to electricity; Reflex success vs electricity becomes Critical Success.
- **Perception:** +6 (Expert)
- **Speed:** 25 feet

### Active Surge AP & Cartridge Shooting Cadence
- **Active Surge AP Pool:** $\text{INT (+1)} + \text{Base (+1 Tier 2)} + \mathbf{\text{Edge (+1 AP)}} = \mathbf{3 \text{ AP / round}}$.
- **Firing Cadence (Neither Service Rifle):**
  - Base AP cost: 4 AP. `[Military]` Tag discount (–1) + Rifleman innate discount (–1) $\rightarrow$ **2 AP per shot**.
  - At 3 AP, Kroll fires **1 full precision shot (2 AP)** and still has **1 AP remaining** to activate a tactical scope, prime a smoke round, or trigger an airship grappling winch.
  - *Shield Reload Rule Note:* If wielding a buckler/shield, reloading requires +1 Action unless unequipped. Kroll wields his 2-handed rifle with both hands (no shield), keeping reloads at standard 1 Action!

### Combat Strikes & Actions
- **Neither Service Rifle Strike (Range 60 ft, 2-Handed):** +9 to hit (Dex 4 + Master Proficiency 4 + Level 1), **1d8+1 P** (Fatal d12, Concussive, 2 AP).
- **Sniper's Aim ([two-actions]):** Take careful sight; gain a +2 circumstance bonus to the attack roll and ignore circumstance penalties from lesser cover.
- **Covering Reload ([one-action]):** Reload rifle while taking the Take Cover or Hide action behind an airship bulwark or sandbag parapet.

### Level 1 Equipment Loadout & Citizen Bulwark Slots
* **3 Citizen Bulwark Slots (Player Choice — Never Confiscated):**
  1. *Neither Service Rifle* (Primary Weapon with 2-round internal cartridge chamber).
  2. *Quilted Marine Ballistic Coat* (Light armor, +1 AC, Dex cap +4).
  3. *Airship Rigging Goggles & Breather Unit* (Protects against Zone wind and Neither haze).
* **Other Carried Gear:** Marine bayonet (1d6 P), 4 sealed 2-round cartridge clips (8 rounds total, checkout log), brass signal flare, service compass, 5 Nobels in military scrip.

  - **Regular Damage:** 31 to 63 HP (33 HP buffer)
- **Armor Class:** **23** (10 + Expert Light Armor 6 + Dex 4 + Level 5 + +1 Niracrest Weave Coat +2)
- **Saving Throws:** Fortitude +12 (Expert), Reflex +14 (Master), Will +9 (Trained)
  - *Half-Elf Traits:* Electricity Resistance 3; falls treated as 20 ft shorter.
- **Perception:** +12 (Master / Battlefield Senses)
- **Speed:** 25 feet

### Active Surge AP & Master Rifle Marksmanship
- **Active Surge AP Pool:** $\text{INT (+2)} + \text{Base (+1)} + \text{Class Lv 5 (+1)} + \mathbf{\text{Edge Lv 5 (+2)}} = \mathbf{6 \text{ AP / round}}$.
- **+1 Striking Masterwork Service Rifle:** +15 to hit, **2d8+2 P** (Fatal d12!).
  - *On Critical Hit:* Deals **`(2d12+2) × 2 + 1d12` fatal bonus die**, obliterating priority targets instantly.
- **Combat Cadence at 6 AP:** Can fire **2 shots per round (3 AP + 3 AP = 6 AP)** with a +1 Striking rifle, or fire 1 shot and execute a **Vital Aim** (+1d6 precision).
- **Running Reload ([one-action]):** Stride, Step, or Sneak while Interacting to reload the rifle during movement.
