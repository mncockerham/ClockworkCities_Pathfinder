# Civilian-Template Level 5 PCs (Comparative Analysis)
## Testing the 6 Functional PC Categories with Civilian Background Templates

This document takes the 6 functional Level 5 PC heroes from our benchmark suite and applies the **Civilian Background / Protected Status Template**:
- **Why this test matters:** In Clockwork Cities, player characters are extraordinary individuals who often arise from ordinary civilian trades (laborers, artisans, clerks, tavern keepers). When a character retains civilian roots or operates with civilian standing rather than military commission, their mechanics shift to reflect everyday trade skills, social standing perks, and slight adjustments in HP and passive gear loadouts.
- **Rule Alignment (2026-10-30):** Incorporates the 10-Point Edge System (`edges.md`), single-purchase limits, Citizen Bulwark Slots, and active AP structures.

## 2. Detailed Civilian Adjustments per Character

### Character 1: Marcus "Ironjaw" Flint (Pugilist — Foundry Laborer Background)
- **Civilian Roots:** Marcus never enlisted in the guard or joined a noble retinue; he worked a 12-hour shift shoveling iron ore at the Ward 1 foundry before fighting in underground pit bouts at night.
- **Edge System Synergy:** Marcus invested his 10 Edge points into physical resilience (Hardened Frame, Iron-Gut, Neither Lungs, Light Armor proficiency, Workhorse Bulk). His civilian laborer background provides native familiarity with industrial hazards.
- **Adjusted Skills:** Gains *Foundry Lore* (Expert) and Athletics (Expert).
- **Civilian Trait:** Treats all heavy wrenches, crowbars, and sledgehammers as unarmed brawling weapons (qualifying for Flurry of Blows).
- **Citizen Bulwark Slots:** (1) Heavy Cast-Iron Shop Sledge, (2) Reinforced Padded Foundry Apron, (3) Union Work Registry Medallion.
- **Equipment:** Carries forged steel calipers, foundry fire goggles, and a heavy brass worker's canteen alongside his +1 Striking brass knuckles.

---

### Character 2: Theron Vane (Clockwork Engineer — Registered Guild Craftsman)
- **Civilian Roots:** Rather than a renegade inventor, Theron is a card-carrying member of the Ward 11 Machinists' Guild who operates a commercial clockwork repair shop.
- **Edge System Synergy:** Paid 2 Edge points for Status 2 (Guild Artisan), 3 points for Medium Armor, 3 points for AP Battery Surge (+2 Max AP), and 2 points for Jury-Rigger.
- **Adjusted Skills:** Gains *Clockmaker Lore* (Master), Crafting (Master), and *Society* (Trained).
- **Civilian Trait:** **Guild Wholesale Discount:** Can purchase clockwork parts and common metals at an 80% discount (20% off retail) from guild supply houses.
- **Citizen Bulwark Slots:** (1) Arcane-Tuned Spanner Wrench, (2) Riveted Leather Artisan Apron, (3) Official Guild Seal & Multi-Gauge Screwdriver Set.
- **Equipment:** Wears a clean suede shop apron, carries patent inspection seals, and maintains an official guild shop receipt book.

---

### Character 3: Sergeant Donald Kroll (Rifleman — Municipal Watch Bailiff)
- **Civilian Roots:** Retired from frontline military airships; now serves as a civilian ward bailiff protecting middle-circle market squares.
- **Edge System Synergy:** Half-Elf Heritage. Paid 2 Edge points for Status 2 (Military Regular / Civil Bailiff), 3 points for AP Battery Surge, 3 points for Zone Static Attunement, and 2 points for Rigging Reflexes.
- **Adjusted Skills:** Gains *Ward 12 Lore* (Expert) and Intimidation (Expert).
- **Civilian Trait:** **Peace-Bond Exemption:** Legally allowed to carry his Neither Service Rifle uncased within his designated home ward while wearing his brass bailiff's gorget.
- **Citizen Bulwark Slots:** (1) Regulated Service Rifle (Bulwark-registered), (2) Stiffened Serge Constabulary Greatcoat, (3) Brass Bailiff's Gorget & Key of Office.
- **Equipment:** Wears a constable's dark wool uniform jacket with polished brass buttons and a municipal patrol whistle.

---

### Character 4: Rowan Thorne (Scout — Peat Harvester & Surface Freeman)
- **Civilian Roots:** Rowan is an independent orcish contractor licensed by the municipal agricultural board to dig fuel peat from the damp lower Neither trenches.
- **Edge System Synergy:** Orc Heritage. Paid 1 Edge point for Status 1 (Free Outrider / Harvester), 3 points for Heavy Armor, 2 points for Neither Lungs, 2 points for Iron-Gut, and 2 points for Workhorse Bulk.
- **Adjusted Skills:** Gains *Surface Peat Lore* (Expert) and Survival (Master).
- **Civilian Trait:** **Peat Forager:** Can locate dry, combustible Neither-sponge peat in wild areas that burns clean and produces heat without attracting wild predators.
- **Citizen Bulwark Slots:** (1) Balanced Composite Longbow, (2) Quilted Surface Parka, (3) Licensed Peat Digger's Harvester Token & Compass.
- **Equipment:** Heavy bog-shoes, trenching shovel strapped to his backpack, and a bundle of dried Sycelium fire-starter cakes.

---

### Character 5: Corbin Hall (Soldier — Brewery Drayman / Gatekeeper)
- **Civilian Roots:** A towering working-class citizen who escorts heavy wooden beer and grain dray-carts through the freight elevators between Ward 1 and Ward 13.
- **Edge System Synergy:** Dwarf Heritage. Paid 2 Edge points for Status 2 (Military Veteran / Senior Dray Guard), 5 points for Weapon Mastery (Master Broadsword at Level 5), 2 points for Stone Anchor, and 1 point for Workhorse Bulk.
- **Adjusted Skills:** Gains *Brewing Lore* (Trained) and Athletics (Expert).
- **Civilian Trait:** **Heavy Dray Shielding:** When taking cover behind large cargo crates, barrels, or wagons, gains +4 circumstance AC (Greater Cover) rather than +2.
- **Citizen Bulwark Slots:** (1) High-Tensile Steel Broadsword, (2) Fluted Half-Plate Cuirass, (3) Master Drayman's Brass Gatepass & Crowbar.
- **Equipment:** Heavy leather drayman's apron worn over his fluted steel half-plate; carries a cooper's iron crowbar alongside his masterwork broadsword.

---

### Character 6: Aldous Sterling (Merchant — Burgher Publican & Market Landlord)
- **Civilian Roots:** Owns three popular middle-circle taverns and four boarding tenements in Ward 11, drawing his wealth directly from rent, ale sales, and local gossip.
- **Edge System Synergy:** Paid 7 Edge points for Status 6 (Wall Merchant + armed retainers), and 3 Edge points for Martial Weapons proficiency (Expert Fair Steel Rapier).
- **Adjusted Skills:** Gains *Tavern Lore* (Master), Diplomacy (Master), and *Accounting Lore* (Expert).
- **Civilian Trait:** **Street Ear:** Whenever Aldous visits a market or tavern in his home district, the GM provides 1 relevant local rumor or piece of criminal gossip without requiring a skill check.
- **Citizen Bulwark Slots:** (1) Fair Steel Spire Dueling Rapier, (2) Tailored Niracrest Silk Morning Coat, (3) Great Seal Ring of the Sterling Merchant Exchange.
- **Equipment:** Bespoke tweed morning suit, gold pocket watch, iron ring holding 24 tenement room keys, and a ledger of tenant rent payments.
