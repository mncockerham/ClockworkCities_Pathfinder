# Character Profile: Theron Vane
## Category 2: High Radiant Specialist (Clockwork Engineer) — Levels 1 & 5

- **Class:** Clockwork Engineer (Inventor baseline: Apparatus / Arc Innovator)
- **Ancestry / Heritage:** Human (Spire Artisan lineage)
- **Social Class:** Tier 3: Guild Members & Artisans (Status 2)
- **Innovation / Specialty:** Weapon Innovation (Inductive Arc Arquebus / Coherent Pulse Projector)

---

## 1. Level 1 Build & Mechanics

### Attributes & Defenses
- **Attributes:** STR +1 (12), DEX +2 (14), CON +2 (14), INT +4 (18), WIS +0 (10), CHA +0 (10)
- **Hit Points:** 18 HP (Human 8 + Engineer 8 + Con 2)
- **Wound Thresholds (Step $\lfloor 18/4 \rfloor = 4$):**
  - **Mortal Wounds:** 1 to 4 HP
  - **Critical Wounds:** 5 to 8 HP
  - **Regular Damage:** 9 to 18 HP (10 HP band)
- **Armor Class:** **16** (10 + Trained Light Armor 3 + Dex 2 + Level 1) — *Wears reinforced leather work coat (+1 AC, Dex cap +3)*
- **Saving Throws:** Fortitude +7 (Expert), Reflex +5 (Trained), Will +5 (Expert)
- **Perception:** +3 (Trained)
- **Speed:** 25 feet

### NeitherTeck Dual-System Pool
- **Passive Loadout Track C (Int/Str/Con):** $\text{Key Stat (+4 INT)} + \lfloor 1/2 \rfloor + 4 = \mathbf{8 \text{ Points}}$.
  - *Armor Penalty:* Light armor discount grants **–1 loadout cost**. Effective capacity: **9 Points**.
  - *Committed Gear:* Standard Breather (1 pt), Insulated Copper Grounding Harness (2 pts), Tool Bandolier (1 pt). Remaining: 5 pts.
- **Active Surge AP:** $\text{INT (+4)} + \text{Base (+2 Tier 3)} + \text{Progression (+0)} = \mathbf{6 \text{ AP/round}}$.
  - *Firing Capacity:* In High Radiant zones, radiant induction powers the heavy inductive coil.
  - *Inductive Arc Rifle:* Base cost 4 AP. Trained `[Technical]` discipline tag reduces cost by **–1 AP** $\rightarrow$ **3 AP per shot**.
  - *Cadence:* At 6 AP, Theron can comfortably execute **2 shots per round** inside a High Radiant grid (3 AP + 3 AP = 6 AP).

### Combat Actions
- **Inductive Neither Arc Projector (2-Handed, Range 60 ft):** +5 to hit, **1d8+1 E** (Electricity / Arc, Fatal d12, 3 AP with tag).
- **Overdrive ([one-action], Crafting DC 15):** Attempt to supercharge the arc coils. On a success, add +2 circumstance damage (or +4 on critical) to all NeitherTeck strikes for 1 minute.
- **Explosive Leap ([two-actions]):** Discharge a concussive steam/Neither blast to leap up to 30 feet in any direction.

### Level 1 Equipment Loadout
- Heavy oiled leather apron and insulated gloves (+1 AC, 2 N).
- Custom Prototype Arc Arquebus (Weapon Innovation).
- 2 Standard Civilian energy cells (backup for low-power operation outside grid, 4 N).
- Masterwork brass calipers, screwdriver kit, and volt-meter (5 N).
- Standard Neither Breather Mask (2 N).

## 2. Level 5 Progression & Upgrades

### Attributes & Defenses
- **Attribute Boosts (Lv 5):** STR +2 (14), DEX +3 (16), CON +3 (16), INT +4.5 (19 $\rightarrow$ Boosted to +4.5 / 19, or +4 Int / +3 Dex)
- **Hit Points:** 58 HP (Human 8 + Engineer 40 + Con 15)
- **Wound Thresholds (Step $\lfloor 58/4 \rfloor = 14$):**
  - **Mortal Wounds:** 1 to 14 HP
  - **Critical Wounds:** 15 to 28 HP
  - **Regular Damage:** 29 to 58 HP (30 HP band)
- **Armor Class:** **21** (10 + Trained Light Armor 7 + Dex 3 + Level 5 + 1 Item)
- **Saving Throws:** Fortitude +12 (Expert), Reflex +10 (Trained), Will +11 (Expert)
- **Speed:** 25 feet

### NeitherTeck Dual-System Pool
- **Passive Loadout Track C (Int/Str/Con):** $\text{Key Stat (+4 INT)} + \lfloor 5/2 \rfloor + 4 = \mathbf{10 \text{ Points}}$ (+1 Light Armor discount = **11 Effective Points**).
  - *Equipped Gear:* Sealed Hazmat Breather (2 pts), Powered Assist Exo-Braces (+1 Str Athletics, 3 pts), Inductive Static Barrier Shield (3 pts). Remaining: 3 pts.
- **Active Surge AP:** $\text{INT (+4)} + \text{Base (+2 Tier 3)} + \text{Progression (+1 at Lv 4)} = \mathbf{7 \text{ AP/round}}$.
  - *Cadence:* With a +1 Striking Arc Rifle (Base 4 + 1 Striking – 1 Tag = **4 AP**), Theron can fire 1 boosted shot (4 AP) and activate an electric pulse gadget (2 AP) or prime an auto-injector (1 AP).

### Combat Actions & Modifications
- **+1 Striking Masterwork Arc Arquebus:** +11 to hit (+4 Int Innovation / Dex + 5 Trained + 1 Item + 1), **2d8+2 E + Overdrive (+3 damage)** (Fatal d12).
- **Megavolt ([two-actions]):** Release a 60-foot line of crackling electrical discharge dealing 3d12 electricity damage (Reflex save DC 21).
- **Clockwork Barrier Projector:** Deploys a portable static shield providing +2 circumstance AC and absorbing 10 kinetic damage before collapsing.

### Level 5 Equipment Loadout (~210 Nobels total value)
- Fine Niracrest-reinforced leather duster (+2 AC, 35 Nobels).
- Prototype +1 Striking Arc Arquebus with focus lenses (100 Nobels).
- 4 Sealed Military Cartridges for unpowered outland excursions (8 Nobels).
- Chirurgeon Emergency Auto-Injectors (2 Minor Vigor sera, 8 Nobels).
- Precision clockwork multi-tool watch with integrated wire-stripper and gauge (15 Nobels).
