# Character Profile: Rowan Thorne
## Category 4: Neither & Outland Specialist (Scout) — Levels 1 & 5

- **Class:** Scout (Ranger baseline: Surface Outrider & Deep Delver)
- **Ancestry / Heritage:** Orc (Native Lower Neither / Thick Epidermis)
- **Social Class:** Tier 2: Free Citizens & Outlanders (Status 1)
- **Specialty:** Wilderness navigation, tracking, heavy composite longbow, zero-grid survival

---

## 1. Level 1 Build & Mechanics

### Attributes & Defenses
- **Attributes:** STR +3 (16), DEX +3 (16), CON +2 (14), INT +0 (10), WIS +2 (14), CHA –1 (8)
- **Hit Points:** 22 HP (Orc 10 + Scout 10 + Con 2)
- **Wound Thresholds (Step $\lfloor 22/4 \rfloor = 5$):**
  - **Mortal Wounds:** 1 to 5 HP
  - **Critical Wounds:** 6 to 10 HP
  - **Regular Damage:** 11 to 22 HP (12 HP band)
- **Armor Class:** **17** (10 + Trained Medium Armor 3 + Dex 2 + Level 1 + Hide Armor +1) — *Wears thick cured wolfhide armor (+3 AC, Dex cap +2)*
- **Saving Throws:** Fortitude +7 (Expert), Reflex +8 (Expert), Will +5 (Trained)
- **Perception:** +7 (Expert / Low-Light Vision & Scent)
- **Speed:** 25 feet

### NeitherTeck Dual-System Pool
- **Passive Loadout Track A (Str/Con):** $\text{Key Stat (+3 STR)} + \lfloor 1/2 \rfloor + 4 = \mathbf{7 \text{ Points}}$.
  - *Armor Penalty:* Medium armor cost is **+0** (neutral). Available capacity: **7 Points**.
  - *Committed Gear:* Neither Filter Goggles (1 pt), Camouflage Outland Cloak (1 pt), Heavy Peat Boots (1 pt). Remaining: 4 pts.
- **Active Surge AP:** $\text{INT (+0)} + \text{Base (+0 Tier 1)} + \text{Progression (+0)} = \mathbf{0 \text{ AP/round}}$.
  - *Combat Independence:* Rowan uses mechanical tension (composite bow) and cold-forged steel. Requires zero radiant power, zero dynamos, and zero cartridges. Operates at peak effectiveness in dead wastes.

### Combat Actions
- **Composite Longbow Strike (Range 100 ft):** +6 to hit, **1d8+1 P** (Deadly d10, Propulsive, Volley 30 ft).
- **Hunted Prey ([one-action]):** Designate a creature within 100 feet; gain bonuses to Seek, Track, and reduce multiple attack penalties (Flurry edge: –3 / –6 instead of –5 / –10).
- **Twin Takedown ([one-action]):** When dual-wielding hatchets, make two melee strikes for 1 action against your hunted prey.

### Level 1 Equipment Loadout
- Cured thick hide armor and heavy peat-cutting boots (+3 AC, 2 N).
- Composite Longbow and quiver of 20 steel-tipped arrows (14 N + 2 sp).
- Paired heavy camp hatchets (1d6 S, Agile, 2 N).
- Trapper's iron snare kit, 50-foot braided hemp rope, and flint/steel (2 N).
- Canvas bedroll, water bladder, and dried salt-meat rations for 7 days.
