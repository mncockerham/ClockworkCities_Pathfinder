# Character Profile: Sergeant Donald Kroll
## Category 3: Low Radiant Specialist (Clockwork Rifleman) — Levels 1 & 5

- **Class:** Clockwork Rifleman (Gunslinger baseline: Way of the Sniper / Airship Marine)
- **Ancestry / Heritage:** Human (Arclean Sky-Fleet Veteran)
- **Social Class:** Tier 4: Military & Constabulary (Status 2)
- **Specialty:** Long-range precision fire, capacitive low-radiant cycling, airship gunwales

---

## 1. Level 1 Build & Mechanics

### Attributes & Defenses
- **Attributes:** STR +1 (12), DEX +4 (18), CON +2 (14), INT +1 (12), WIS +1 (12), CHA +0 (10)
- **Hit Points:** 20 HP (Human 8 + Rifleman 8 + Con 2 + Diehard)
- **Wound Thresholds (Step $\lfloor 20/4 \rfloor = 5$):**
  - **Mortal Wounds:** 1 to 5 HP
  - **Critical Wounds:** 6 to 10 HP
  - **Regular Damage:** 11 to 20 HP (10 HP band)
- **Armor Class:** **18** (10 + Trained Light Armor 3 + Dex 4 + Level 1) — *Wears snug quilted ballistic coat (+1 AC, Dex cap +4)*
- **Saving Throws:** Fortitude +7 (Expert), Reflex +9 (Master!), Will +4 (Trained)
- **Perception:** +6 (Expert)
- **Speed:** 25 feet

### NeitherTeck Dual-System Pool
- **Passive Loadout Track B (Dex/Con):** $\text{Key Stat (+4 DEX)} + \lfloor 1/2 \rfloor + 4 = \mathbf{8 \text{ Points}}$ (+1 Light Armor discount = **9 Effective Points**).
  - *Committed Gear:* Airship Goggles & Breather (1 pt), Recoil-Dampening Shoulder Stock (2 pts), 3-Point Marine Sling (1 pt). Remaining: 5 pts.
- **Active Surge AP:** $\text{INT (+1)} + \text{Base (+1 Tier 2)} + \text{Progression (+0)} = \mathbf{2 \text{ AP/round}}$.
  - *Special Class Feature (Rifleman AP Discount):* The Rifleman gains an innate **–1 AP discount** on ranged NeitherTeck weapons at 1st level (stacking with the discipline tag discount).
  - *Neither Service Rifle:* Base cost 4 AP. `[Military]` Tag discount (–1) + Rifleman Class feature (–1) $\rightarrow$ **2 AP per shot (Min 1)**!
  - *Cadence:* At Level 1, Sergeant Kroll can fire **1 shot per round** (2 AP spent out of 2 available AP).

### Combat Actions
- **Neither Service Rifle Strike (Range 60 ft):** +9 to hit (Dex 4 + Master Proficiency 4 + Level 1), **1d8+1 P** (Fatal d12, Concussive, 2 AP).
- **Sniper's Aim ([two-actions]):** Take careful sight; gain a +2 circumstance bonus to the attack roll and ignore circumstance penalties from lesser cover.
- **Covering Reload ([one-action]):** Reload rifle while taking the Take Cover or Hide action behind an airship bulwark or sandbag wall.

### Level 1 Equipment Loadout
- Navy-blue wool Arclean marine tunic and trousers (+1 AC, 3 N).
- Neither Service Rifle (2-round box magazine, 14 Nobels).
- 4 Sealed Cartridge Magazines (8 rounds total, 8 Slivers).
- Marine bayonet (1d6 P, attached under barrel, 1 N).
- Airship brass signal flares (3 flares, 6 Slivers).

## 2. Level 5 Progression & Upgrades

- **Attribute Boosts (Lv 5):** STR +2 (14), DEX +4.5 (19 $\rightarrow$ +4 Dex / Boosted), CON +3 (16), INT +2 (14), WIS +2 (14)
- **Hit Points:** 63 HP (Human 8 + Rifleman 40 + Con 15)
- **Wound Thresholds (Step $\lfloor 63/4 \rfloor = 15$):**
  - **Mortal Wounds:** 1 to 15 HP
  - **Critical Wounds:** 16 to 30 HP
  - **Regular Damage:** 31 to 63 HP (33 HP band)
- **Armor Class:** **23** (10 + Expert Light Armor 6 + Dex 4 + Level 5 + 1 Item)
- **Saving Throws:** Fortitude +12 (Expert), Reflex +14 (Master), Will +9 (Trained)
- **Perception:** +12 (Master / Battlefield Awareness)
- **Speed:** 25 feet

### NeitherTeck Dual-System Pool
- **Passive Loadout Track B (Dex/Con):** $\text{Key Stat (+4 DEX)} + \lfloor 5/2 \rfloor + 4 = \mathbf{10 \text{ Points}}$ (+1 Light Armor discount = **11 Effective Points**).
  - *Equipped Gear:* Tinted Airship Glare Goggles (1 pt), Niracrest Breather (2 pts), Kinetic Stabilizer Stock (+1 item bonus to ranged accuracy, 3 pts). Remaining: 5 pts.
- **Active Surge AP:** $\text{INT (+2)} + \text{Base (+1 Tier 2)} + \text{Progression (+1 at Lv 5)} = \mathbf{4 \text{ AP/round}}$.
  - *Cadence:* With a +1 Striking Service Rifle (Base 4 + 1 Striking – 1 Tag – 1 Rifleman = **3 AP**), Kroll can fire his rifle (3 AP) and still have 1 AP remaining to prime a biochemical smoke flare or trigger a quick-recharge capacitive valve!

### Combat Actions & Feats
- **Master's +1 Striking Neither Service Rifle:** +15 to hit (+4 Dex + 7 Master + 1 Item + Level 5 - 2 to +14/+15), **2d8+2 P** (Fatal d12!). On a critical hit, deals **(2d12+2) $\times$ 2 + 1d12 fatal die**, obliterating priority targets.
- **Running Reload ([one-action]):** Stride, Step, or Sneak, and Interact to reload the rifle during the movement.
- **Vital Aim ([one-action]):** On a hit against an off-guard target, add an additional 1d6 precision damage.
