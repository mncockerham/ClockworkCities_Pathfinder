# NPC Profile: Laborer (Foundry Hand & Freight Hauler)
## Non-Player Character Class — Levels 1 & 5

- **Class:** Laborer (Civilian Working Class Baseline)
- **Role:** Foundry stoker, freight elevator operator, fungal bed compost hauler
- **Social Class:** Tier 1–2: Slums, Warrens & Free Laborers (Status 0 to 1)

---

## 1. Level 1 Laborer Stat Block

### Attributes & Defenses
- **Attributes:** STR +3 (16), DEX +1 (12), CON +3 (16), INT –1 (8), WIS +0 (10), CHA –1 (8)
- **Hit Points:** 19 HP (Human 8 + Laborer 8 + Con 3)
- **Armor Class:** **14** (10 + Trained Unarmored 3 + Dex 1) — *Wears heavy canvas bib and leather apron*
- **Saving Throws:** Fortitude +8 (Expert), Reflex +4 (Trained), Will +3 (Trained)
- **Perception:** +3 (Trained)
- **Speed:** 25 feet

### Core NPC Abilities
- **Heavy Hauler:** Treats Strength as 18 (+4) for Bulk limits; carries up to 9 Bulk unencumbered.
- **Trade Tool Proficiency:** Wields sledgehammers, crowbars, and shovels without improvised weapon penalties (deals 1d6 B or P).
- **Familiar Tool AP Discount:** –1 AP cost on hydraulic winches and pneumatic lift jacks matching their daily trade.

### Attacks & Actions
- **Sledgehammer / Heavy Wrench Strike:** +6 to hit, **1d6+3 B**.
- **Foundry Grip ([one-action]):** Attempt Athletics (+6) to Grapple; on success, deal 1d4 bludgeoning damage.
- **Heave Cargo ([two-actions]):** Hurl a heavy crate/barrel up to 20 ft; target must make DC 14 Reflex save or take 2d6 bludgeoning damage and fall prone.

### Gear (Level 1)
- Soot-stained canvas overalls, thick leather work boots.
- Cast-iron 8 lb sledgehammer (1d6 B, 1 N).
- Battered tin lunchbox with mela cakes and chicory bottle.
- Laborer's guild work registry token (Ward 1), 8 Slivers in tin tin.

## 2. Level 5 Laborer (Foundry Foreman / Veteran Hauler)

### Attributes & Defenses
- **Attributes:** STR +4 (18), DEX +1 (12), CON +4 (18), INT –1 (8), WIS +1 (12), CHA +0 (10)
- **Hit Points:** 63 HP (Human 8 + Laborer 40 + Con 15)
- **Armor Class:** **18** (10 + Trained Light Armor 5 + Dex 1 + Level 5 - 3 to +17/+18 + Heavy Leather Apron +1)
- **Saving Throws:** Fortitude +13 (Expert), Reflex +8 (Trained), Will +8 (Trained)
- **Perception:** +8 (Trained)
- **Speed:** 25 feet

### Core NPC Upgrades
- **Union Call ([one-action], Auditory):** Shouts for help; grants all allied workers within 60 ft a +1 circumstance bonus to Will saves against fear and intimidation for 1 minute.
- **Callous Skin:** Gain physical damage resistance 2 against non-lethal and bludgeoning bare-knuckle strikes.

### Attacks & Actions
- **Veteran's Steel Sledgehammer Strike:** +11 to hit, **1d8+4 B** (Shove trait).
- **Crushing Slam ([two-actions]):** Strike an enemy; if it hits, target is automatically pushed back 10 feet into walls or machinery.

### Gear (Level 5)
- Boiled leather foundry apron and iron-toed work boots (+2 AC, 3 N).
- Hardened cold-rolled steel heavy maul (1d8 B, 2 N).
- Standard brass Neither Breather with soot filters (2 N).
- Pocket brass whistle for calling warehouse crews, 14 Nobels in foreman wage notes.
