# NPC Profile: Professional (Barrister & City Accountant)
## Non-Player Character Class — Levels 1 & 5

- **Class:** Professional (Educated Civil Non-Combatant)
- **Role:** Ward legal advocate, trade house accountant, university lecturer
- **Social Class:** Tier 5: Academia & Professional Bourgeoisie (Status 3 to 4)

---

## 1. Level 1 Professional Stat Block

### Attributes & Defenses
- **Attributes:** STR –1 (8), DEX +1 (12), CON +0 (10), INT +3 (16), WIS +2 (14), CHA +2 (14)
- **Hit Points:** 15 HP (Human 8 + Professional 6 + Con 0 + Civilized 1)
- **Armor Class:** **14** (10 + Trained Unarmored 3 + Dex 1) — *Unarmored, wears tailored woolen frock coat*
- **Saving Throws:** Fortitude +3 (Trained), Reflex +4 (Trained), Will +7 (Expert)
- **Perception:** +7 (Expert / Keen eye for forgeries and fraud)
- **Speed:** 25 feet

### Core NPC Abilities
- **Institutional Lore Mastery:** Trained in *Legal Lore* (+6), *Accounting Lore* (+6), and *Society* (+6). +2 circumstance bonus on guild charter and contract disputes.
- **Professional Instrument AP Discount:** –1 AP cost on diagnostic optical lenses, mechanical arithmometers, and speaking tubes.
- **Bureaucratic Shield ([one-action], Mental):** Quote legal statutes or municipal code at an attacker within 30 ft. Target must make DC 15 Will save or take a –1 circumstance penalty to attack rolls against the Professional for 1 round.

### Attacks & Actions
- **Dueling Walking Cane:** +4 to hit, **1d4–1 B** (Nonlethal, Agile).
- **Panic & Cower ([reaction]):** When shot at or attacked in melee, immediately drop Prone and take cover, gaining Lesser Cover (+1 AC/Reflex).

### Gear (Level 1)
- Pressed wool trousers, linen waistcoat, dark necktie, spectacles.
- Varnished ash walking stick with brass ferrule (1 N).
- Leather briefcase with court deeds, fountain pen, and blotting paper (2 N).
- Calling card case, 5 Nobels and 20 Slivers in coin purse.

## 2. Level 5 Professional (Senior Municipal Advocate / High Auditor)

### Attributes & Defenses
- **Attributes:** STR –1 (8), DEX +1 (12), CON +1 (12), INT +4 (18), WIS +3 (16), CHA +3 (16)
- **Hit Points:** 43 HP (Human 8 + Professional 30 + Con 5)
- **Armor Class:** **18** (10 + Trained Unarmored 5 + Dex 1 + Level 5 - 3 to +17/+18)
- **Saving Throws:** Fortitude +8 (Trained), Reflex +8 (Trained), Will +14 (Master!)
- **Perception:** +12 (Master / Uncanny Forensic Deduction)
- **Speed:** 25 feet

### Core NPC Upgrades
- **Courtroom Authority:** +2 circumstance bonus to Diplomacy and Deception in any legal tribunal or before the Privy Council. Can issue a legal stay to delay an arrest warrant for 24 hours.
- **Diplomatic Privilege:** Striking this NPC inside civil wards is a Class B misdemeanor resulting in immediate constable arrest.

### Attacks & Actions
- **Concealed Stiletto Cane Strike:** +8 to hit, **1d4–1 P** (Agile, Concealable, Finesse).
- **Command Decorum ([one-action], Auditory):** Demand silence; all creatures in a 20-foot room must make a DC 21 Will save or lose 1 action on their turn attempting to compose themselves.

### Gear (Level 5)
- Tailored Niracrest-threaded velvet morning suit (+1 item bonus to AC, 20 Nobels).
- Ebony Sword Cane with hidden silver stiletto blade (3 Nobels).
- Mechanical pocket Arithmometer for rapid debt calculations (10 Nobels).
- Royal audit seal of the High Spire Ministry of Finance, 45 Nobels in letters of credit.
