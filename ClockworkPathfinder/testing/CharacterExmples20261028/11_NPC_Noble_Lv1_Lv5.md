# NPC Profile: Noble Civilian (Estate Heir & Salon Dilettante)
## Non-Player Character Class — Levels 1 & 5

- **Class:** Noble (Civilian Aristocratic Non-Combatant)
- **Role:** Great House scion, royal council page, high-society patron
- **Social Class:** Tier 7: Nobility & Great Houses (Status 6 to 7)

---

## 1. Level 1 Noble Civilian Stat Block

### Attributes & Defenses
- **Attributes:** STR +0 (10), DEX +2 (14), CON +0 (10), INT +1 (12), WIS +1 (12), CHA +3 (16)
- **Hit Points:** 15 HP (Human 8 + Noble 6 + Con 0 + Protected 1)
- **Armor Class:** **15** (10 + Trained Unarmored 3 + Dex 2) — *Unarmored, wears tailored silk morning coat*
- **Saving Throws:** Fortitude +3 (Trained), Reflex +5 (Trained), Will +6 (Expert)
- **Perception:** +6 (Expert / Trained in courtly etiquette and social deception)
- **Speed:** 25 feet

### Core NPC Abilities
- **Hereditary Privilege:** Immune from summary arrest by municipal constables; constables must petition the High Magistrate or King's Guard.
- **Salon Fencing Privilege:** Legally authorized to openly carry a dueling sword within all civil Spire wards.
- **Retinue Call ([two-actions], Auditory):** Call out for his liveried servants or bodyguards. 1d2 armed retainers (using Laborer or Soldier stats) arrive in 1d4 rounds to protect him.

### Attacks & Actions
- **Salon Masterwork Rapier Strike:** +5 to hit, **1d6 P** (Deadly d8, Disarm, Finesse).
- **Outrage & Demand ([one-action], Mental):** Berates an attacker with social authority; target must make DC 16 Will save or become *Fascinated* or hesitate.

### Gear (Level 1)
- Fine velvet and silk morning coat with gold piping and crest (10 N).
- Masterwork engraved dueling rapier (10 N).
- Enamelled gold pocket watch (quarter-repeater, 15 N).
- Scented handkerchief, mother-of-pearl calling card case, 50 Nobels in gold sovereigns.

## 2. Level 5 Noble Civilian (Privy Council Attaché / Salon Master)

### Attributes & Defenses
- **Attributes:** STR +0 (10), DEX +3 (16), CON +1 (12), INT +2 (14), WIS +2 (14), CHA +4 (18)
- **Hit Points:** 43 HP (Human 8 + Noble 30 + Con 5)
- **Armor Class:** **20** (10 + Trained Light Armor 5 + Dex 3 + Level 5 - 3 to +17/+18 + Niracrest-Weave Coat +2)
- **Saving Throws:** Fortitude +8 (Trained), Reflex +10 (Trained), Will +13 (Master!)
- **Perception:** +11 (Expert / Court Schemer)
- **Speed:** 25 feet

### Core NPC Upgrades
- **Great House Sanction:** Commands a permanent retinue of 4 armed retainers and 1 carriage driver.
- **Pure Aither Essence:** Carries a small silver vial of pure Strata 1 essence that resets any minor Neither food/air exposure degradation back to 0.0.

### Attacks & Actions
- **Ancestral +1 Niracrest Dueling Rapier:** +11 to hit, **1d6+1 P** (Deadly d8, Disarm, Finesse).
- **Aristocratic Command ([one-action]):** Direct an armed retainer or ally to make an immediate melee Strike.

### Gear (Level 5)
- Custom Niracrest-weave velvet riding coat (+2 AC, 35 Nobels).
- Heirloom +1 Striking Salon Rapier with engraved ancestral crest (100 Nobels).
- Imported northern Elven Aither dueling pistol (1d8 P, Fatal d12, 25 Nobels).
- Platinum signet ring with Great House seal, carriage deed, 150 Nobels in royal gold.
