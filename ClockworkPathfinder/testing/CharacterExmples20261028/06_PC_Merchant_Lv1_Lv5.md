# Character Profile: Aldous Sterling
## Category 6: Influence Class (Merchant) — Levels 1 & 5

- **Class:** Merchant (Replaced Bard baseline: Commerce, Trade Guilds & Disposable Ordnance)
- **Ancestry / Heritage:** Human (Ward 14 Wall Merchant Kin)
- **Social Class:** Tier 6: Merchant Houses & Wall Merchants (Status 4)
- **Specialty:** 70/30 Soft power, contract retainers, liquid wealth expenditure, tactical items

## 1. Level 1 Build & Mechanics

### Attributes & Defenses
- **Attributes:** STR +0 (10), DEX +2 (14), CON +1 (12), INT +3 (16), WIS +1 (12), CHA +3 (16)
- **Hit Points:** 17 HP (Human 8 + Merchant 8 + Con 1)
- **Wound Thresholds (Step $\lfloor 17/4 \rfloor = 4$):**
  - **Mortal Wounds:** 1 to 4 HP
  - **Critical Wounds:** 5 to 8 HP
  - **Regular Damage:** 9 to 17 HP (9 HP band)
- **Armor Class:** **16** (10 + Trained Light Armor 3 + Dex 2 + Level 1 + Quilted Frock Coat +1) — *Wears tailored armored wool frock coat (+1 AC, Dex cap +3)*
- **Saving Throws:** Fortitude +4 (Trained), Reflex +7 (Expert), Will +6 (Expert)
- **Perception:** +6 (Expert / Sharp Eye for Counterfeits)
- **Speed:** 25 feet

### NeitherTeck Dual-System Pool
- **Passive Loadout Track C (Int/Str/Con):** $\text{Key Stat (+3 INT)} + \lfloor 1/2 \rfloor + 4 = \mathbf{7 \text{ Points}}$ (+1 Light Armor discount = **8 Effective Points**).
  - *Committed Gear:* Silk-Lined Neither Breather (1 pt), Concealed Kevlar-Weave Vest (2 pts), Mechanical Coin Sorter & Ledger (1 pt). Remaining: 4 pts.
- **Active Surge AP:** $\text{INT (+3)} + \text{Base (+1 Influence)} + \text{Progression (+0)} = \mathbf{4 \text{ AP/round}}$.
  - *Expenditure Focus:* While Aldous does not invent tech, his 4 AP allows him to prime and trigger high-end disposable clockwork devices, spark grenades, and biochemical injectors bought from guild masters!

### Combat Actions & Influence Features
- **Concealed Sword Cane Strike:** +5 to hit (+2 Dex + Trained 3), **1d6 P** (Agile, Concealable, Finesse).
- **Commercial Incentive ([one-action], Auditory):** Grant an ally within 30 feet a +1 status bonus to attack rolls or skill checks for 1 round by promising a financial bounty or tactical payout.
- **Throw Mad Bomber Chemical Grenade:** +5 to hit (Range 20 ft), deals 1d8 Fire + 1 persistent fire (Alchemist's Fire, 1 AP to prime).

### Level 1 Equipment Loadout (High Liquid Wealth Advantage)
- Tailored fine wool merchant frock coat lined with ballistic mesh (+1 AC, 5 N).
- Fine polished ebony Sword Cane with silver pommel (3 N).
- 2 Mad Bomber Acid Flasks and 2 Alchemist's Fire flasks (12 N total).
- 2 Chirurgeon Cellular Vigor tonics (Minor, 8 N).
- Gilded pocket watch, parchment ledger, letter of credit worth 50 Nobels in Ward 14 bank.

- **Perception:** +11 (Expert / Lie Detector)

### NeitherTeck Dual-System Pool

- **Direct Dividend ([two-actions]):** Command an allied Soldier or Pugilist to immediately make a Strike as a free reaction.
