# NPC Profile: Commoner / Burgher (Tavern Host & Shopkeeper)
## Non-Player Character Class — Levels 1 & 5

- **Class:** Commoner (Civilian Urban Commercial Baseline)
- **Role:** Neighborhood publican, Ward 11 dry-goods storekeeper, boarding house manager
- **Social Class:** Tier 2: Free Citizens & Burghers (Status 1 to 2)

---

## 1. Level 1 Commoner Stat Block

### Attributes & Defenses
- **Attributes:** STR +1 (12), DEX +1 (12), CON +1 (12), INT +0 (10), WIS +2 (14), CHA +2 (14)
- **Hit Points:** 16 HP (Human 8 + Commoner 6 + Con 1 + Civilized 1)
- **Armor Class:** **14** (10 + Trained Unarmored 3 + Dex 1) — *Unarmored, wears linen apron and waistcoat*
- **Saving Throws:** Fortitude +4 (Trained), Reflex +4 (Trained), Will +7 (Expert)
- **Perception:** +7 (Expert / Sharp street ear for trouble)
- **Speed:** 25 feet

### Core NPC Abilities
- **Ward Rumor Network:** +2 circumstance bonus on Diplomacy checks to Gather Information regarding local gossip, crimes, or merchant disputes in home ward.
- **Behind the Counter Cover:** While standing behind a bar, counter, or desk, treats Standard Cover as Greater Cover (+4 circumstance AC / Reflex).
- **Domestic Tool AP Discount:** –1 AP cost on tavern lighting inductors and beer-cooling coils.

### Attacks & Actions
- **Heavy Oak Club / Skillet Strike:** +4 to hit, **1d6+1 B**.
- **Whistle for the Watch ([one-action]):** Blows emergency brass ward whistle; a constable patrol arrives in 2d4 rounds.
- **Settle Down ([one-action]):** Attempt Diplomacy (+5) against highest Will DC in an unruly crowd to de-escalate tavern shouting matches.

### Gear (Level 1)
- Stout canvas apron, flannel shirt, tweed trousers.
- Heavy oak bung-starter club (1d6 B, 1 sp).
- Heavy brass ward whistle on leather neck cord.
- Cash drawer with 4 Nobels, 35 Slivers, and 50 Warrens Pins.

## 2. Level 5 Commoner (Master Innkeeper / Market Burgher)

### Attributes & Defenses
- **Attributes:** STR +1 (12), DEX +1 (12), CON +2 (14), INT +1 (12), WIS +3 (16), CHA +3 (16)
- **Hit Points:** 48 HP (Human 8 + Commoner 30 + Con 10)
- **Armor Class:** **18** (10 + Trained Unarmored 5 + Dex 1 + Level 5 - 3 to +17/+18)
- **Saving Throws:** Fortitude +9 (Trained), Reflex +8 (Trained), Will +14 (Master!)
- **Perception:** +12 (Master / Neighborhood Fixer)
- **Speed:** 25 feet

### Core NPC Upgrades
- **Tavernkeeper's Bouncer Call:** Once per encounter, command 2 off-duty laborers or patrons to take a Shove or Grapple action on an unruly target.
- **Sharp Assayer:** Automatically identifies counterfeit coins, forged bank credit notes, or shaved silver on Perception DC 15.

### Attacks & Actions
- **Tavern Bludgeon (+1 Club):** +9 to hit, **1d6+2 B** (Disarm trait).
- **Bar-Room Shove ([one-action]):** Attempt Athletics (+8) to push a patron backward over a table or through a window.

### Gear (Level 5)
- Good quality wool morning coat, silk cravat, heavy apron.
- Weighted lead-lined bar club (+1 to hit, 35 Nobels).
- Masterwork brass ward whistle with acoustic megaphone horn (2 Nobels).
- Key ring to 12 tenement apartments, strongbox deed to the tavern, 35 Nobels in trade silver.
