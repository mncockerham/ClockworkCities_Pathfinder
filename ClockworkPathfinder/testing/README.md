# Clockwork Pathfinder — Playtesting & Mechanical Validation Suite

Welcome to the **Testing & Simulation** workspace for Clockwork Cities Pathfinder.

## Purpose
This folder is dedicated to sandbox testing, scenario validation, balance stress-testing, and running mathematical simulations of new rules before finalizing them into core rulebooks:

1. **NeitherTeck Dual-System Balance Simulations:**
   - Active Surge AP budgets, firing rates, Multi-Attack Penalty (MAP) effects, and class damage curves across levels 1 to 20.
   - Passive Loadout Capacity stress tests across armor weights (Unarmored, Light, Medium, Heavy) and environmental hazard suits.

2. **Combat Encounters & Environment Scenarios:**
   - Civil Spire Disarmed Parlor Brawls (Pugilists, Dandies, Survivors, Brutes).
   - High-Radiant Grid defense engagements (Scholars, Artisans, Clockwork Engineers).
   - Low-Radiant Airship skirmishes and outer perimeter engagements (Clockwork Riflemen, Sky Captains).
   - Unpowered Wilderness expeditions (Pure martials, bows, conventional steel).

3. **Wound Threshold & Trauma Testing:**
   - Lethal vs. Nonlethal damage splits, Critical/Mortal condition application, and rest/recovery pacing.

4. **Economic & Equipment Progression Audits:**
   - Validating the Level 12 store ceiling and wealth progression in Nobels/Slivers.
