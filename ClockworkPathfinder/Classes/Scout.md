# Scout

- **Base Class:** Ranger `[Player Core p.152]`
- **Status:** **Modified** (1-to-1 baseline with Pathfinder 2e Remaster Ranger; tailored for Neither wasteland navigators, caravan outriders, and spire perimeter guards)
- **Availability:** **Primary PC Class** (Full 1–20 progression)
- **Discipline / Trait:** Martial / Skill (Tracking, Neither Navigation & Ranged Combat)

---

## 1. Setting Overview
*The hardened outriders, caravan pathfinders, and surface trackers who dare to navigate the Neither.*

- **In-World Identity:** Caravan outriders guiding armored transports along the Peasant Paths, perimeter foresters stationed along the great outer walls, sky-dock lookouts, or lone Neither wanderers who know how to read the lethal surges of Neither fog, navigate lightning-swept Zone borders, and track mutated fauna and orc raiding bands.
- **Typical Social Class & Backing:** Freeman / Outrider (Status 1), Forester / Rural Constable (Status 2), Merchant House Caravan Scout (Status 2–3), or Free Neither Wanderer (Status 0–1).
- **Physical / Scientific Explanation:** Exceptional environmental awareness, mastery of filtration breather gear, long-range marksmanship (heavy crossbows, spring-guns, bows), and acute sensory tracking in low-visibility Neither fog.

## 4. Spells & Apparatus (If Applicable)
*Warden Spells:* Any base Ranger Warden Spells (primal focus spells) taken via feats are reflavored as specialized field apparatus:
- *Gravity Bow / Heal Companion:* Pneumatic tension clips, biological soothing salves, or chemical coagulants.
- They follow the Concoction and Clockwork Device rules from `AISummary/10_Magic.md` and cannot produce supernatural planar transformations.

## 5. Multiclass Archetype (Dedication)
*Characters of other classes can gain training in the Scout (Ranger) archetype [Player Core p.220].*

- **Scout Dedication (Feat 2):** `[Player Core p.220]`  
  - **Prerequisites:** Dexterity 14 (+2) or Strength 14 (+2).
  - **Benefits:** Trained in Survival (or another skill if already trained); trained in Scout class DC. Gain the Hunt Prey action.
- **Basic Hunter's Trick (Feat 4):** Gain a 1st- or 2nd-level Scout feat `[Player Core p.220]`.
- **Scout's Resiliency (Feat 4):** Gain 3 additional HP for each Scout archetype feat taken `[Player Core p.220]`.
- **Hunter's Edge (Feat 6):** Gain the benefits of your chosen Hunter's Edge against your hunted prey `[Player Core p.220]`.
- **Advanced Hunter's Trick (Feat 6):** Gain a Scout feat up to half your character level `[Player Core p.220]`.
- **Master Spotter (Feat 12):** Perception increases to Master `[Player Core p.220]`.
