# Marksman

- **Base Class:** Composite of Fighter (Archer) `[Player Core p.136]`, Gunslinger (Crossbow) `[Guns & Gears p.106]`, and Archer Archetype `[Player Core 2 p.196]`
- **Status:** **New** (Dedicated **Tier 0 / Lowest AP** non-magical, non-NeitherTeck Bow & Crossbow Martial Class)
- **Availability:** **Primary PC Class** (Full 1–20 progression)
- **Discipline / Trait:** Martial (Bows, Crossbows, Mechanical Tension & All-Strata Ballistics)
- **Active Surge AP Tier:** **Tier 0 (Dedicated Martials — Lowest AP: +0 Base AP at Lv 1, +1 AP at Levels 10 & 20)**

---

## 1. Setting Overview
*The silent roof-stalkers, frontier longbowmen, and heavy arbalest sharpshooters who need neither sparks nor cartridges to kill from three hundred yards.*

- **In-World Identity:** 
  - **Outland & Frontier Marksmen:** Longbowmen and heavy crossbow hunters patrolling the 50-mile Spire perimeters, escorting wagon trains across the Tier 3 Neither wastes where recharging dynamos do not exist, or holding frontier palisades against Orc warbands and mutated mega-fauna.
  - **Warrens & Rooftop Arbalists:** Silent syndicate sharpshooters, bounty hunters, and night-watchmen operating in the cramped catwalks of the Warrens or urban rooftops where the thunderclap of a firearm or arc-rifle would summon an entire precinct of Ward Constables within seconds.
  - **Zone & All-Strata Escorts:** Airship boarding marksmen and polar expedition guards whose mechanical bows and steel-prod arbalests never lose half their power in the Zone, never explode in the Neither, and never go dead in the pure Aither.
- **Typical Social Class & Backing:** Outland Yeoman / Forester (Status 10–20), Military Sharpshooter / Wall Watch (Status 26–50), Merchant Caravan Escort (Status 20–40), or Warrens Bounty Hunter / Syndicate Enforcer (Status 0–25).
- **Physical / Scientific Explanation & The 0-AP Advantage:**
  - **100% Independent of NeitherTeck (0 AP):** Unlike the **Clockwork Rifleman** (who relies on Active Surge AP and Spire-synthesized biochemical cartridges), the **Marksman** is a **Tier 0 Dedicated Martial** alongside the **Soldier**. Every Marksman class feature, reload, stance, and trick shot costs **0 AP** and relies purely on leaf-spring steel prods, compound pulley cams, ratchet windlasses, and supreme human hand-eye coordination.
  - **Unmatched Endurance in the 70% Grind:** While energy gunners ration scarce cartridges and worry about 20% component burnouts or 2- to 3-action battery swaps, the Marksman fires round after round at peak accuracy (`Expert` $\rightarrow$ `Master` $\rightarrow$ `Legendary`) in every atmospheric layer on the planet.

### A. The Three Marksman's Creeds (Choose One at 1st Level)

#### 1. Creed of the Longbowman (Volley & Rapid Draw Specialist)
*You master high-draw yew, horn, and pulley-assisted compound bows, sending a hail of heavy shafts downrange faster than any rifleman can cycle a bolt.*
- **1st Level — Point-Blank & Rapid Nocking:** You gain the *Point-Blank Shot* stance `[Player Core p.140]` as a bonus feat (while in the stance, you do not take the –2 *Volley* penalty with longbows inside 30 feet, and you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to damage with shortbows and crossbows within their first range increment, replacing the +1 from *Tension & Draw Mastery* while within that range). In addition, when making multiple Strikes on your turn with a `Reload 0` Bow, your Multiple Attack Penalty is reduced from –5 / –10 to **–4 / –8**.
- **9th Level — Elastic Cam Momentum:** When you hit a target with a Bow Strike, your next Bow Strike against the same target before the end of your turn ignores Lesser and Standard Cover and reduces its Multiple Attack Penalty by an additional 1 (to **–3 / –6**).
- **15th Level — Unbroken Hail:** When using *Double Shot* or *Triple Shot* with a Bow, you do not take the feat's –2 penalty to your attack rolls.

#### 2. Creed of the Arbalist (Heavy Crossbow & Ratchet Crank Specialist)
*You wield steel-prod crossbows, repeating magazine crossbows, and heavy siege arbalests capable of punching clean through boiler-plate armor.*
- **1st Level — Heavy Prod & Ratchet Crank Reload `[one-action]`:**
  - **Upgraded Bolt Die:** Any simple or martial Crossbow you wield increases its weapon damage die by **one step** (`1d4` $\rightarrow$ `1d6`, `1d6` $\rightarrow$ `1d8`, `1d8` $\rightarrow$ `1d10`, `1d10` $\rightarrow$ `1d12`), and gains the **Propulsive** trait (adding half your Strength modifier, if positive, to damage from heavy mechanical bracing).
  - **Ratchet Crank Reload `[one-action]`:** In a single action, you perform **1 Interact action to reload** your crossbow AND either **Stride up to half your Speed**, **Step**, or **Take Cover**. If you are wielding a `Reload 2` Heavy Crossbow/Arbalest, spending **2 Actions** completes the entire 2-action reload *plus* lets you Step or Take Cover!
- **9th Level — Armor-Punching Windlass:** Your Crossbow Strikes ignore the first **5 points of physical Resistance or Shield Hardness** on the target, and on a critical hit, the target is knocked **5 feet directly backward** (or knocked *Prone* if backed against a wall).
- **15th Level — Split-Second Ratchet:** You treat all `Reload 2` Heavy Crossbows as **`Reload 1`**, and once per round you can perform a 1-action Crossbow reload as a **free action `[free-action]`** at the start of your turn.

#### 3. Creed of the Warrens Skirmisher (Silent Ambush & Trick-Shot Specialist)
*You operate in the vertical catwalks of the Warrens, smoky alleyways, and dense Neither fog—striking unseen with takedown bows, hand crossbows, and grappling bolts.*
- **1st Level — Silent Loose & Skirmisher's Reload `[one-action]`:**
  - **Ambush Precision:** Whenever you hit an *Off-Guard* target with a Bow or Crossbow within its first two range increments, you deal an extra **1d6 precision damage** (increasing to **2d6 at 9th level** and **3d6 at 15th level**).
  - **Skirmisher's Reload `[one-action]`:** Interact to reload a crossbow (or draw a specialty arrow for a bow) and either **Hide**, **Sneak**, or **Create a Diversion** as a single action. When you make a Bow or Crossbow Strike while Hidden, the flat DC or Stealth penalty to remain Hidden after firing is reduced by half.
- **9th Level — Catwalk & Rubble Stalker:** You are never *Off-Guard* while Balancing or Climbing, can fire a two-handed Bow or Crossbow while clinging to a ladder, rope, or wall, and gain a +10-foot status bonus to Speed during the first round of combat.
- **15th Level — Phantom Fletcher:** Making a Bow or Crossbow Strike while Hidden or Undetected no longer automatically reveals your location; observers must succeed at an active Seek check against your Stealth DC to pinpoint where the shaft came from.

### B. Fletcher's Craft — Daily Mechanical Trick Ammunition (0 AP)

Each morning during daily preparations, you craft **4 + $\lfloor \text{Level}/2 \rfloor$** specialty arrows or bolts (usable only by you; expire at next daily prep). Firing a Trick Arrow/Bolt requires no extra actions and **0 AP**—simply declare which shaft you are nocking when you make a Strike:

| Trick Arrow / Bolt | Mechanical Effect on a Hit |
|---|---|
| **1. Serrated Broadhead** | Deals **1d4 persistent bleed damage** on a regular hit (**2d4 persistent bleed** on a critical hit, or **1d6/2d6** at Level 9+). |
| **2. Tempered Bodkin (AP)** | Ignores **3 points** of the target's physical Resistance or object/shield Hardness (**6 points** at Level 7+, **10 points** at Level 15+). |
| **3. Barbed Pinning Shaft** | Tangled barbs or split-bolt forks snag clothing and tendons: target takes a **–10-foot circumstance penalty to all Speeds** for 1 round (or is **Immobilized** until it spends 1 Interact action, Athletics DC = your Marksman Class DC, on a critical hit). |
| **4. Weighted Blunt / Blackjack Bolt** | Converts the shot's damage to **Bludgeoning** with the **Nonlethal** trait. On a critical hit, the target is **Stunned 1** (ideal for taking bounties alive or avoiding murder charges in Spire wards). |
| **5. Grappling-Spool Bolt / Arrow** | Trails 50 feet of braided high-tensile wire anchored to your belt or crossbow winch. Can be fired at masonry, timber, or rigging (AC 15) to instantly establish a climbing/zipline cable, or fired at a creature to prevent it from moving more than 30 feet away from you unless it succeeds at an Athletics check vs. your Marksman Class DC. |
| **6. Whistling / Flare-Tip Shaft** | Emits a piercing shriek or magnesium spark trail up to 300 feet. Illuminates a 20-ft radius for 1 minute, removes *Concealed* from fog/darkness in a 10-ft burst for 1 round, or signals allies (+1 circumstance bonus to allies' next attack roll against a struck target). |

## 5. Multiclass Archetype (Marksman Dedication)

*Characters of other classes (such as Soldiers, Scouts, Survivors, or Scholars) can train in the Marksman archetype (`[Player Core 2 p.196]` Archer Archetype + Marksman features).*

- **Marksman Dedication — Feat 2 `[New / PC2 p.196]`:**
  - **Prerequisites:** Dexterity +2.
  - **Benefits:** You become Trained in all simple, martial, and advanced **Bows and Crossbows**. Whenever you gain a class feature that grants Expert or higher proficiency in any weapon, you also gain that proficiency in all Bows and Crossbows. In addition, you gain the **Point-Blank Shot** stance OR the **Ratchet Crank Reload `[one-action]`** action (without the die size step-up).
- **Basic Marksman's Trick — Feat 4:** Gain a 1st- or 2nd-level Marksman feat.
- **Marksman's Resiliency — Feat 4:** Gain 3 additional Hit Points for each Marksman archetype feat you have.
- **Fletcher's Pouch — Feat 4:** Gain the *Fletcher's Craft* class feature, allowing you to prepare **3 Mechanical Trick Arrows/Bolts** during daily preparations at 0 AP.
- **Advanced Marksman's Trick — Feat 6:** Gain a Marksman feat of up to half your character level.
