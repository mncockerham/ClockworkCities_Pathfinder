## Soldier
- **Base Chassis:** Fighter [Player Core p.136] (Player Core [p.136-149])
- **Squad Role:** Frontline Anchor / Chokepoint Defender
- **Key Attribute:** Strength or Dexterity | **Hit Points:** 10 + CON mod per level
- **Perception:** Expert (Master at Lv 9) | **Saving Throws:** Fortitude: Expert, Reflex: Expert, Will: Trained (Expert at Lv 3)
- **Armor:** Trained in all armor and shields. Expert at Lv 11. | **Weapons:** Expert in simple and martial weapons. Master at Lv 5.
- **Skills:** Trained in Athletics or Acrobatics, plus 3 + INT mod additional skills.
- **Social Advancement:** Tier 4 Track (5 advancements). Base: 2 Auto + 1 Class + CHA.
