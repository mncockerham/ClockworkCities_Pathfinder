# Mad Bomber

- **Base Class:** Alchemist (Bomber) `[Player Core 2 p.44]`
- **Status:** **Modified** (Split from Alchemist; specialized exclusively in explosive ordnance, volatile bombs, demolition charges, acids, and poisons; mutagen and familiar feats removed)
- **Availability:** **Primary PC Class** (Full 1–20 progression)
- **Discipline / Trait:** Clockwork Technology (Devices & Munitions) / Chemical Ordnance

---

## 1. Setting Overview
*The trench demolitionists, siege engineers, and volatile munitions artisans of the Great Spires.*

- **In-World Identity:** Ordnance technicians from the Military Ward (Ward 2), sappers in the sky docks, demolition miners excavating Neithercrete foundations, or wild Warrens scavengers assembling unstable blast grenades from scavenged chemical capacitors.
- **Typical Social Class & Backing:** Military track (Sergeant / Officer, Status 3–4), Craft Guild Munitionist (Status 2–3), or Warrens mercenary demolitions expert (Status 0–1).
- **Physical / Scientific Explanation:** Mastery of volatile reactive minerals, pressurized gas flasks, Neither-reactive chemicals, spring-loaded shrapnel cores, caustic acid ampoules, and noxious toxin canisters. Since traditional gunpowder and combustion engines are inert or unreliable in the Neither, they utilize hyper-reactive catalytic charges and pneumatic detonators.

## 5. Multiclass Archetype (Dedication)
*Characters of other classes can gain training in the Mad Bomber archetype.*

- **Mad Bomber Dedication (Feat 2):** `[Player Core 2 p.218]`  
  - **Prerequisites:** Intelligence 14 (+2), Dexterity 14 (+2).
  - **Benefits:** Trained in Mad Bomber class DC; gain a modest pool of Versatile Vials each day. You can craft temporary infused items with the **Bomb**, **Weapon**, **Acid**, or **Poison** traits only. Gain the Demolitions splash protection benefit.
- **Basic Munitions (Feat 4):** Gain a 1st- or 2nd-level Mad Bomber feat (e.g., Quick Bomber, Far Lobber, Demolition Charge).
- **Bomber's Resiliency (Feat 4):** Gain 3 additional HP for each Mad Bomber archetype feat taken.
- **Advanced Munitions (Feat 6):** Gain a Mad Bomber feat up to half your character level.
- **Expert Demolitionist (Feat 12):** Expert proficiency in Mad Bomber class DC.
