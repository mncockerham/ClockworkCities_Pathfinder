# Black Marketeer

- **Base Class:** Bard `[Player Core p.94]` (Spellcasting removed; supplemented with non-combat, non-stealth skill progression from Rogue `[Player Core p.168]`)
- **Status:** **Influence Class** (Magic completely removed; re-engineered into an underworld kingpin, contraband smuggler, and syndicate broker balancing cutthroat directives, illicit stashes, and criminal leverage)
- **Availability:** **Primary PC Class** (Full 1–20 progression)
- **Discipline / Trait:** Social / Influence (Underworld, Smuggling, Fences & Warrens Syndicates)

---

## 1. Setting Overview
*The shadow brokers, fence masters, bootleg converter syndicates, and Warrens bosses who control what the law forbids.*

- **In-World Identity:** Power brokers of Ward 13's Warrens, the lower labor docks, unpoliced sumps, and forgotten tomb networks. While constables police the open corridors and nobles govern from high spires, the Black Marketeer commands the **underground pipelines of illegal Neither cartridges, bootleg low-power converters, stolen industrial blueprints, forged gate passes, and illicit Mela rations**.
- **Typical Social Class & Backing:** Slums Resident (Status 0), Warrens Kingpin (Status 0–3), or Shadow Guildmaster. While lacking noble blood or merchant house legitimacy, they command intense loyalty from street gangs, corrupt ward gatekeepers, desperate smugglers, and illicit artisans.
- **The 70/30 Contact Architecture:**
  - **70% Dedicated Sphere (Underworld, Warrens & Criminal Factions):** Fences, bootleggers, safehouse keepers, cutthroats, gang lieutenants, corrupt gate constables, and slum fixers.
  - **30% Cross-Sphere Options:** Laundering coin through front shops and legitimate shipping guilds (reaching Merchants) or selling sensitive intelligence and deniable services to corrupt nobles (reaching Nobles).
- **Physical / Scientific Explanation:** Intimate knowledge of urban sprawl, secret transit chutes, contraband concealment, black-market pricing, and criminal intimidation. In confrontations, their underhanded directives, street signals, and cutthroat leverage coordinate dirty-fighting ambushes and terrorize adversaries.

## 4. Multiclass Archetype (Dedication)
*Characters of other classes can gain training in the Black Marketeer archetype.*

- **Black Marketeer Dedication (Feat 2):**  
  - **Prerequisites:** Charisma 14 (+2) or Intelligence 14 (+2).
  - **Benefits:** Trained in Deception and Thievery (or another skill if already trained); trained in Black Marketeer class DC. Gain access to the *Cutthroat Incentive* directive and basic Contraband Cache.
- **Basic Directives (Feat 4):** Gain a 1st- or 2nd-level Black Marketeer feat.
- **Scoundrel's Resiliency (Feat 4):** Gain 3 additional HP for each Black Marketeer archetype feat taken.
- **Underworld Connections (Feat 6):** Gain an additional skill increase and skill feat.
- **Advanced Directives (Feat 6):** Gain a Black Marketeer feat up to half your character level.
- **Warrens Kingpin (Feat 12):** Increase Deception or Thievery to Master, and class DC to Expert.
