# Noble

- **Base Class:** Bard `[Player Core p.94]` (Spellcasting removed; supplemented with non-combat, non-stealth skill progression from Rogue `[Player Core p.168]`)
- **Status:** **Influence Class** (Magic completely removed; re-engineered into an aristocratic, political, and cultural powerhouse wielding high-society prestige, royal warrants, academic patronage, and sovereign directives)
- **Availability:** **Primary PC Class** (Full 1–20 progression)
- **Discipline / Trait:** Social / Influence (Aristocracy, Governance, High Arts & Academic Patronage)

---

## 1. Setting Overview
*The scions of the Great Houses, Privy Council regents, royal archivists, and cultural patrons who dictate the laws, tastes, and philosophy of the Spires.*

- **In-World Identity:** Landed gentry with ancestral estates above the Zone in the high Aither, senior magistrates of the Privy and Royal Councils, deans and benefactors of the University Collegium, or high-caste dilettantes patronizing the grand theaters. Where the Merchant moves coin and the Black Marketeer moves contraband, the Noble moves **sovereign decrees, institutional policy, academic discovery, and social legitimacy**.
- **Typical Social Class & Backing:** Lesser Noble (Status 5), Noble (Status 7), or Great House Magnate (Status 8+). They command hereditary deference, private estate retinues, exclusive audience rites, and immunity from common constabulary detention.
- **The 70/30 Contact Architecture:**
  - **70% Dedicated Sphere (High Society, Governance & High Culture):** Lords, ladies, court ministers, privy councilors, university chancellors, master artists, and royal judges.
  - **30% Cross-Sphere Options:** Expanding downward into commercial monopolies and trade charters (reaching Merchants) or hiring deniable street fixers and cutouts for court espionage (reaching Black Marketeers).
- **Status Dependency & Disgrace (The 3/4 Connection Loss Rule):**
  - While most adventurer classes (Soldiers, Scouts, Pugilists, Alchemists) can easily fluctuate across social classes without losing their operational abilities outside routine social checks, the **Noble is fundamentally tied to institutional status**.
  - A character with levels in the **Noble class must retain active legal and social Status as a Noble (Status 5+)**—whether via hereditary standing, Privy Council appointment, or active royal patent.
  - If a Noble is stripped of their title, disowned by their Great House, excommunicated from court, or falls into bankruptcy/penal degradation, their combat directives and baseline skill features remain functional, but they **immediately lose 3/4 (75%) of all primary domain social connections, patrons, and high-society favors**.
  - **Player's Choice of Retained Contacts:** The player specifically **chooses which 1/4 (25%) of their contacts and favors remain loyal** through the disgrace (such as a devoted family servant, a loyal childhood friend in the ministry, or a sympathetic university fellow). The rest disavow, shun, or refuse audience with the fallen aristocrat until royal status or formal pardon is restored.
- **Physical / Scientific Explanation:** Comprehensive classical education in royal etiquette, rhetoric, high jurisprudence, genealogical heraldry, and command psychology. In confrontations, their sovereign presence, unshakeable poise, and high-status warrants inspire loyal retainers and paralyze adversaries with the threat of state retaliation.

## 4. Multiclass Archetype (Dedication)
*Characters of other classes can gain training in the Noble archetype.*

- **Noble Dedication (Feat 2):**  
  - **Prerequisites:** Charisma 14 (+2) or Intelligence 14 (+2).
  - **Benefits:** Trained in Society and Diplomacy (or another skill if already trained); trained in Noble class DC. Gain access to the *Imperial Poise* Sovereign Directive and basic Sovereign Writs.
- **Basic Directives (Feat 4):** Gain a 1st- or 2nd-level Noble feat.
- **Noble's Resiliency (Feat 4):** Gain 3 additional HP for each Noble archetype feat taken.
- **High-Society Peerage (Feat 6):** Gain an additional skill increase and skill feat.
- **Advanced Directives (Feat 6):** Gain a Noble feat up to half your character level.
- **Privy Councilor (Feat 12):** Increase Society or Diplomacy to Master, and Noble class DC to Expert.
