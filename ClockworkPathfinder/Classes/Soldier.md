# Soldier

- **Base Class:** Fighter `[Player Core p.136]`
- **Status:** **Modified** (1-to-1 baseline with Pathfinder 2e Remaster Fighter; ready for setting customization)
- **Availability:** **Primary PC Class** (Full 1–20 progression)
- **Discipline / Trait:** Martial

---

## 1. Setting Overview
*The disciplined backbone of spire garrisons, ward constabularies, merchant escorts, and the King's Guard.*

- **In-World Identity:** Enlisted regulars, seasoned sergeants, trench line infantry, ward constables, and elite King's Guardsmen trained in formation combat, tactical defense, and close-quarters melee.
- **Typical Social Class & Backing:** Military track (Enlisted: Status 2, Sergeant: Status 3, Officer: Status 4) or Citizen/Laborer prior to service. Many serfs and debtors enter military service as their primary avenue to earn free status.
- **Physical / Scientific Explanation:** Rigorous physical conditioning, weapon mastery, shield drill, and mechanical armor familiarity adapted to urban choke points, spire ramparts, and close-quarters fighting in the Neither.

## 5. Multiclass Archetype (Dedication)
*Characters of other classes can gain training in the Soldier archetype [Player Core p.219].*

- **Soldier Dedication (Feat 2):** `[Player Core p.219]`  
  - **Prerequisites:** Strength 14 (+2) or Dexterity 14 (+2).
  - **Benefits:** Trained in simple and martial weapons; trained in Athletics or Acrobatics (or another skill if already trained); trained in Soldier class DC.
- **Basic Soldier Maneuver (Feat 4):** Gain a 1st- or 2nd-level Soldier feat `[Player Core p.219]`.
- **Soldier's Resiliency (Feat 4):** Gain 3 additional HP for each Soldier archetype feat taken `[Player Core p.219]`.
- **Advanced Soldier Maneuver (Feat 6):** Gain a Soldier feat up to half your character level `[Player Core p.219]`.
- **Diverse Weapon Expert (Feat 12):** Expert proficiency in simple and martial weapons `[Player Core p.219]`.
