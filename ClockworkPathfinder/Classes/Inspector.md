# Inspector

- **Base Class:** Investigator `[Player Core 2 p.102]`
- **Status:** **Modified** (1-to-1 baseline with Pathfinder 2e Remaster Investigator; all item crafting and alchemical concoction features removed; specialized purely on deduction, forensic analysis, and evidence gathering)
- **Availability:** **Primary PC Class** (Full 1–20 progression)
- **Discipline / Trait:** Skill / Mental (Deduction, Forensics & Strategic Strike)

---

## 1. Setting Overview
*The sharp-eyed constabulary detectives, court inquisitors, and forensic investigators of the Spires.*

- **In-World Identity:** Senior investigators in the Ward Constabularies (especially in Ward 13's Luxury Ward and the Trade Wards), special inquisitors answering to the King's Court or Privy Council, private eyes retained by Wall Merchants to unmask corporate sabotage, or forensic scholars from the University Faculty of Law.
- **Typical Social Class & Backing:** Constable Officer / Inspector (Status 3–4), University Graduate (Status 4), Government Bureaucrat (Status 4–5), or Merchant House intelligence agent (Status 4–6).
- **Physical / Scientific Explanation:** Rigorous logical deduction, crime scene reconstruction, ballistics and chemical residue analysis, encyclopedic legal and social knowledge, and calculated tactical strikes targeting exposed vulnerabilities.

## 4. Spells & Apparatus (If Applicable)
*Not applicable.* The Inspector does not cast spells or prepare concoction/device slots. Their abilities represent sharp cognitive faculties, forensic science, and deductive reasoning.

## 5. Multiclass Archetype (Dedication)
*Characters of other classes can gain training in the Inspector (Investigator) archetype [Player Core 2 p.220].*

- **Inspector Dedication (Feat 2):** `[Player Core 2 p.220]`  
  - **Prerequisites:** Intelligence 14 (+2).
  - **Benefits:** Trained in Society or your methodology skill; trained in Inspector class DC. Gain the On the Case action (Pursue a Lead, Clue In) and the Devise a Stratagem action.
- **Basic Deduction (Feat 4):** Gain a 1st- or 2nd-level Inspector feat `[Player Core 2 p.220]`.
- **Inspector's Resiliency (Feat 4):** Gain 3 additional HP for each Inspector archetype feat taken `[Player Core 2 p.220]`.
- **Investigator's Stratagem (Feat 4):** Gain the Strategic Strike feature, dealing 1d6 precision damage `[Player Core 2 p.220]`.
- **Advanced Deduction (Feat 6):** Gain an Inspector feat up to half your character level `[Player Core 2 p.220]`.
- **Keen Intuition (Feat 12):** Perception increases to Master `[Player Core 2 p.220]`.
