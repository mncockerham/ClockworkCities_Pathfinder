## Consolidation Overview

Rather than maintaining a standalone 1–20 Barbarian (`Brute`) class—which overlapped with the **Pugilist** (street brawlers/pit fighters) and **Soldier** (heavy melee/shock infantry) while introducing supernatural Instincts that did not fit the secular Victorian setting—the archetype of the reckless, adrenaline-fueled brawler is now a **3-step class feat option (Levels 2, 4, and 8)** available to both **[`Soldier`](file:///c:/Users/mncoc/OneDrive/Documents/Git/ClockworkCities_Pathfinder/ClockworkPathfinder/Classes/Soldier.md)** and **[`Pugilist`](file:///c:/Users/mncoc/OneDrive/Documents/Git/ClockworkCities_Pathfinder/ClockworkPathfinder/Classes/Pugilist.md)**:

1. **Level 2 — Savage Combatant (1 Action):**
   - Modeled after Rage at a grounded, lower mechanical impact: grants **temporary HP equal to $\lfloor \text{Half Level} \rfloor + \text{CON mod}$**, **+1 melee/unarmed damage** (**+2** two-handed melee), and **–1 AC** for 1 minute.
   - **Strict Weapon Restriction:** Cannot make **Ranged Weapon Strikes** (bows, crossbows, firearms, or energy emitters) while in a Savage state, and cannot use Concentrate actions except *Seek* and *Intimidate*.
   - **Social Interaction Penalty:** While in a Savage state and for **10 minutes afterward**, you take a **–2 status penalty to all social interaction checks and Understanding checks** (*Diplomacy, Deception, Performance, Society, and calling polite Favors*), **except Intimidation**, which gains a **+1 status bonus**.
2. **Level 4 — Relentless Savagery:**
   - Increases melee/unarmed damage bonus to **+2** (**+3** two-handed melee), grants **+1 status bonus to Athletics** (*Grapple, Shove, Reposition, Trip*) while Savage, and lets you **Stride (half Speed) or Step as a free action** when activating *Savage Combatant*.
3. **Level 8 — Savage Onslaught:**
   - Increases temporary HP to **$\text{Full Level} + \text{CON mod}$**, grants **physical Resistance equal to CON mod (min 1) against Bludgeoning and all Nonlethal damage**, scales melee damage bonus to **+3** (**+4** two-handed), and increases the **Intimidation** bonus to **+2** while Savage.
