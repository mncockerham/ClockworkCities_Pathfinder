# Brute

- **Base Class:** Barbarian `[Player Core 2 p.78]`
- **Status:** **Modified** (1-to-1 baseline with Pathfinder 2e Remaster Barbarian; reflavored for pit fighters, industrial heavy-laborers, and warrens brawlers)
- **Availability:** **Primary PC Class** (Full 1–20 progression)
- **Discipline / Trait:** Martial (Heavy Melee & Unbridled Might)

---

## 1. Setting Overview
*The towering pit fighters, quarry breachers, dockyard heavies, and Warrens enforcers.*

- **In-World Identity:** Born to heavy labor in the deep mines, foundry floors of Ward 1 (Labor), the sprawling commercial docks, or underground fighting pits of the Warrens. They survive through sheer muscular density, pain tolerance, and explosive surges of raw kinetic violence.
- **Typical Social Class & Backing:** Laborer / Farm Hand (Status 1), Warrens "Nameless" (Status 0), or Serf / Indentured Debtor (Status –1). Frequently hired as heavy muscle by criminal syndicates (like Johnny Ralen or Amanda Coren in Ward 13's Warrens), dock bosses, or merchant caravans braving the Neither.
- **Physical / Scientific Explanation:** Adrenaline overdrive, hardened scar tissue, physiological pain suppression, and violent momentum. Their "Rage" is an adrenaline surge and reckless fury born of brutal work and brutal environments.

## 5. Multiclass Archetype (Dedication)
*Characters of other classes can gain training in the Brute (Barbarian) archetype [Player Core 2 p.219].*

- **Brute Dedication (Feat 2):** `[Player Core 2 p.219]`  
  - **Prerequisites:** Strength 14 (+2), Constitution 14 (+2).
  - **Benefits:** Trained in Athletics; trained in simple and martial weapons; trained in Brute class DC. Gain the Rage action (dealing +2 additional damage or Instinct bonus).
- **Basic Fury (Feat 4):** Gain a 1st- or 2nd-level Brute feat `[Player Core 2 p.219]`.
- **Brute's Resiliency (Feat 4):** Gain 3 additional HP for each Brute archetype feat taken `[Player Core 2 p.219]`.
- **Instinct Ability (Feat 6):** Gain the basic resistance and ability from your chosen Instinct `[Player Core 2 p.219]`.
- **Advanced Fury (Feat 6):** Gain a Brute feat up to half your character level `[Player Core 2 p.219]`.
