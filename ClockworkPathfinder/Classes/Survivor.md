# Survivor

- **Base Class:** Rogue `[Player Core p.168]`
- **Status:** **Modified** (1-to-1 baseline with Pathfinder 2e Remaster Rogue; flavored for street survivors, warrens dwellers, and underclass fixers)
- **Availability:** **Primary PC Class** (Full 1–20 progression)
- **Discipline / Trait:** Martial / Skill (Street Smarts & Cunning)

---

## 1. Setting Overview
*The hardened, adaptable survivors of the spire slums, Warrens, and low-circle alleyways.*

- **In-World Identity:** Born into the grinding poverty of the Lower and Upper Slums (Wards 4 & 5), the fungal trenches of the Labor Ward, or the lawless darkness of the Warrens (levels 1–13). They are the undisputed masters of urban stealth, second-story lockpicking, and social deception within the Great Spires and crowded city wards. They survive through razor-sharp situational awareness, slip through security checkpoints unnoticed, and know how to crack any municipal vault or mechanical tumbler.
- **Typical Social Class & Backing:** Laborer (Status 1), Serf / Debtor (Status –1), or Warrens "Nameless" (Status 0). While often hired by merchants, constables, university researchers, or nobles as guides, fixers, bodyguards, infiltration specialists, or thieves, they are strictly hired hands—never true members of the Merchant Houses, Noble families, or Academic Collegium.
- **Physical / Scientific Explanation:** Honed reflexes, environmental paranoia, street-fighting dirty tricks, opportunistic strikes, and extreme mastery of **Stealth, Thievery, and Deception**. They manipulate the vertical labyrinths of the Spires, blend into any crowd, crack precision locks, and strike with lethal precision from shadows using `[Clandestine]` and `[Professional]` tools.

## 4. Spells & Apparatus (If Applicable)
*Not applicable.* The Survivor relies entirely on mundane cunning, stealth, dirty fighting, and street-acquired survival skills. Any minor magical tricks (such as *Minor Magic* or *Trick Magic Item*) are reflavored as crude utility gadgets, pocket dusts, or match-head sparks.

## 5. Multiclass Archetype (Dedication)
*Characters of other classes can gain training in the Survivor (Rogue) archetype [Player Core p.221].*

- **Survivor Dedication (Feat 2):** `[Player Core p.221]`  
  - **Prerequisites:** Dexterity 14 (+2).
  - **Benefits:** Trained in light armor; trained in Stealth or Thievery; trained in one additional skill; trained in Survivor class DC. Gain the Surprise Attack class feature.
- **Basic Trickery (Feat 4):** Gain a 1st- or 2nd-level Survivor feat `[Player Core p.221]`.
- **Survivor's Resiliency (Feat 4):** Gain 3 additional HP for each Survivor archetype feat taken `[Player Core p.221]`.
- **Sneak Attacker (Feat 4):** Gain Sneak Attack dealing 1d6 precision damage `[Player Core p.221]`.
- **Advanced Trickery (Feat 6):** Gain a Survivor feat up to half your character level `[Player Core p.221]`.
- **Skill Mastery (Feat 8):** Increase one skill proficiency to Master and another to Expert `[Player Core p.221]`.
- **Evasive Reflexes (Feat 12):** Gain the Evasion class feature (Reflex to Master) `[Player Core p.221]`.
