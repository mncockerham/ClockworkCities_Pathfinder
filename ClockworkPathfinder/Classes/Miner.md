# Miner

- **Base Class:** Ranger `[Player Core p.152]`
- **Status:** **Modified** (1-to-1 baseline with Pathfinder 2e Remaster Ranger; tailored for subterranean tunnel excavators, deep-shaft miners, and Dwarven/Deep One sappers)
- **Availability:** **Primary PC Class** (Full 1–20 progression)
- **Discipline / Trait:** Martial / Skill (Subterranean Survival, Excavation & Tunnel Warfare)

---

## 1. Setting Overview
*The deep-shaft excavators, tunnel pioneers, and rock-heft veterans who brave the pitch-black subterranean depths.*

- **In-World Identity:** Deep miners excavating Neithercrete foundations beneath the Great Spires, Dwarven tunnel masters carving stone mines on the Isle of Man (100–5,000 ft underground), Deep One fungal foragers operating in the sweltering heat of The Deep (5,000–12,000 ft, >100°F), or municipal sappers shoring up collapsing Warrens tunnels and Tombs.
- **Typical Social Class & Backing:** Laborer (Status 1), Serf / Indentured Debtor (Status –1), Independent Miner / Claim-holder (Status 1–2), or Dwarven Guild Factor (Status 2–3).
- **Physical / Scientific Explanation:** Innate seismic awareness, reading stress lines in rock faces, heat and methane-gas tolerance, moving through cramped zero-visibility shafts, and wielding heavy picks, mattocks, sledgehammers, and shoring braces with mechanical precision.

## 4. Spells & Apparatus (If Applicable)
*Not applicable.* The Miner does not cast spells. Any warden spells or focus effects are reflavored as mechanical shoring wedges, controlled blasting caps, or acoustic signaling whistles.

## 5. Multiclass Archetype (Dedication)
*Characters of other classes can gain training in the Miner (Ranger) archetype.*

- **Miner Dedication (Feat 2):** `[Player Core p.220]`  
  - **Prerequisites:** Strength 14 (+2) or Constitution 14 (+2).
  - **Benefits:** Trained in Survival or Lore (Mining); trained in simple and martial weapons (picks and hammers); trained in Miner class DC. Gain Survey Quarry.
- **Basic Excavation (Feat 4):** Gain a 1st- or 2nd-level Miner feat.
- **Miner's Resiliency (Feat 4):** Gain 3 additional HP for each Miner archetype feat taken.
- **Miner's Edge (Feat 6):** Gain the benefits of your chosen Miner's Edge against your surveyed quarry.
- **Advanced Excavation (Feat 6):** Gain a Miner feat up to half your character level.
- **Subterranean Senses (Feat 12):** Perception increases to Master.
