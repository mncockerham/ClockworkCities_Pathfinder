# Clockwork Rifleman

- **Base Class:** Gunslinger `[Guns & Gears p.106]`
- **Status:** **Modified** (1-to-1 baseline with Pathfinder 2e Gunslinger; tailored to bridge both traditional gunpowder firearms and Neither-tech cartridge weapons)
- **Availability:** **Primary PC Class** (Full 1–20 progression)
- **Discipline / Trait:** Martial (Firearms, Cartridge Tech & Marksmanship)

---

## 1. Setting Overview
*The sharpshooters, wall snipers, and gun-artisans capable of firing any ballistic or cartridge weapon in the world.*

- **In-World Identity:** Wall snipers stationed on New Avalon's 150-foot inner battlements, royal marksmen from the King's Guard, High Elf rifle escorts visiting from the northern Aither spires, or trench gunners trained on the fragile 40-year-old cartridge weapons that revolutionized spire warfare.
- **Typical Social Class & Backing:** Military Officer / Sergeant (Status 3–4), King's Guard (Status 4), High Elf / Half-Elf trader escort (Status 2–4), or mercenary gun-for-hire (Status 2).
- **Universal Ballistic Mastery:** The Clockwork Rifleman is uniquely trained to operate, maintain, and fire **all gun types**:
  - **Neither-Tech & Cartridge Weapons:** Modern 40-year-old cartridge rifles, pneumatic pressure guns, spark-gap needle rifles, and Neither-stabilized kinetic ejectors that operate reliably in the damp, corrupting fog of the Neither.
  - **Traditional Gunpowder Weapons:** Imported Elven steel firearms, flintlocks, wheel-locks, and rifled muskets that function perfectly in the clean Aither (even with their inherent instability/explosion risk in the Neither).

## 4. Spells & Apparatus (If Applicable)
*Not applicable.* The Clockwork Rifleman does not cast spells; their capabilities stem from peerless ballistic marksmanship, trigger timing, and mechanical familiarity with all firearm actions.

---

## 5. Multiclass Archetype (Dedication)
*Characters of other classes can gain training in the Clockwork Rifleman (Gunslinger) archetype [Guns & Gears p.128].*

- **Clockwork Rifleman Dedication (Feat 2):** `[Guns & Gears p.128]`  
  - **Prerequisites:** Dexterity 14 (+2).
  - **Benefits:** Trained in simple and martial firearms and crossbows; trained in Clockwork Rifleman class DC. Gain a modified Slinger's Reload action.
- **Basic Shooting (Feat 4):** Gain a 1st- or 2nd-level Clockwork Rifleman feat `[Guns & Gears p.128]`.
- **Rifleman's Resiliency (Feat 4):** Gain 3 additional HP for each Clockwork Rifleman archetype feat taken `[Guns & Gears p.128]`.
- **Advanced Shooting (Feat 6):** Gain a Clockwork Rifleman feat up to half your character level `[Guns & Gears p.128]`.
- **Gunslinger's Senses (Feat 12):** Perception increases to Master `[Guns & Gears p.128]`.
