# Rake

- **Base Class:** Swashbuckler `[Player Core 2 p.176]`
- **Status:** **Modified** (1-to-1 baseline with Pathfinder 2e Remaster Swashbuckler; high-society duelists, salon swordsmen, and courtly rakes obsessed with honor and dueling codes)
- **Availability:** **Primary PC Class** (Full 1–20 progression)
- **Discipline / Trait:** Martial / Skill (Finesse & Social Dueling)

---

## 1. Setting Overview
*The sharp-tongued duelists, parlor fencing masters, and street swaggerers of the Spire.*

- **In-World Identity:** Defined by a fascination with dueling rituals, fencing aesthetics, codes of honor, and the outward projection of martial flair. A Rake can hail from **any social class**:
  - **High-Society Rakes (Nobles & Wall Merchants):** Younger children and wealthy dilettantes with coin to burn who turn high-society boredom into an art form. Proudly flaunting their lawful right to carry Fair Steel dueling rapiers in public wards where commoners are disarmed, they are known derogatorily among working citizens, constables, and weary elders as **Dandies**—vain, flamboyant duelists prowling salons looking for an insult to avenge.
  - **Middle-Class & Trade Rakes:** Ambitious guild apprentices, fencing academy students, or clerks who spend their hard-earned wages on sharp tailoring, private dueling club dues, and fencing practice within licensed club halls (fighting only with fists, boots, or heavy walking canes on public streets).
  - **Underworld & Warrens Rakes:** Flashy rookery rogues, gambling-hall bravos, and street brawlers who mimic high-class affectations and swagger in back alleys using fists, heavy bludgeons, and brutal unarmed footwork.
  - **The Strict 'Rotten Apple' Reality of Spire Weapons:** Under Spire Municipal Law, a commoner or street thug caught carrying an illegal sword or concealed blade poses an existential threat to everyone around them. Such a weapon invites immediate King's Guard intervention, risking collective punishment where an entire syndicate or ward circle is purged (*'throw away the whole barrel for one rotten apple'*). A street thug foolish enough to carry a sword cane will find their own crime boss turning them in to serve 15 to 20 years of forced labor in the sump-mines for unsolved crimes just to protect the syndicate's status.
- **Typical Social Class & Backing:** Any (Status 1 to 8). From street bravos (Status 1–3) to salon aristocrats (Status 5–8).
- **Physical & Combat Philosophy:** Lightning-fast footwork, precision thrusts, and psychological gamesmanship (feints, witty mockery, taunts) rather than heavy armor or crude brute force.

*Not applicable.* The Dandy is a non-magical martial combatant who relies on speed, fencing technique, charisma, and social poise.

## 5. Multiclass Archetype (Dedication)
*Characters of other classes can gain training in the Dandy (Swashbuckler) archetype [Player Core 2 p.220].*

- **Dandy Dedication (Feat 2):** `[Player Core 2 p.220]`  
  - **Prerequisites:** Dexterity 14 (+2), Charisma 14 (+2).
  - **Benefits:** Trained in Acrobatics and your chosen style skill; gain Panache class feature and access to basic Panache generation actions.
- **Basic Finisher (Feat 4):** Gain the Confident Finisher action `[Player Core 2 p.220]`.
- **Basic Flair (Feat 4):** Gain a 1st- or 2nd-level Rake feat `[Player Core 2 p.220]`.
- **Dandy's Resiliency (Feat 4):** Gain 3 additional HP for each Dandy archetype feat taken `[Player Core 2 p.220]`.
- **Advanced Flair (Feat 6):** Gain a Rake feat up to half your character level `[Player Core 2 p.220]`.
- **Vivacious Stepper (Feat 8):** Gain Vivacious Speed bonus (+5 ft, or +10 ft with Panache) `[Player Core 2 p.220]`.
- **Evasive Swashbuckler (Feat 12):** Gain the Evasion class feature (Reflex to Master) `[Player Core 2 p.220]`.
