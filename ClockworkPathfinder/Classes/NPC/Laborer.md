# Laborer (NPC Class)

- **Role:** Non-Player Character (Civilian / Working Class)
- **Location:** `ClockworkPathfinder/Classes/NPC/Laborer.md`
- **Typical Traits:** Human, Civilized (or unhardened outland laborer)
- **Status Tier:** Status –1 (Indentured Debtor, Serf) to Status 1 (Free Laborer, Guild Apprentice)
- **Design Intent:** Represents the physical workforce that keeps spires, foundries, docks, and fungal farms operating. They are not balanced against PC classes and have weak combat abilities, but possess solid physical stamina, heavy hauling capability, and access to bar-fight brawling feats.

---

## 1. Setting Overview & In-World Identity

Laborers form the demographic bedrock of civilization. They shovel coal and Neithercrete into municipal boilers, tend sweltering subterranean mushroom beds in the dark circles, man winch cables along vertical spire facades, and haul cargo crates across sky docks. 

- **Everyday Demeanor:** Hardened to exhaustion, repetitive strain, and industrial soot, but unaccustomed to tactical gunfights or lethal monster raids.
- **Tools as Implements:** Shovels, heavy wrenches, crowbars, sledgehammers, cargo hooks, and timber levers.
- **NeitherTeck Familiarity:** Low baseline. They operate heavy industrial tools via **Trained Trade Discount** (reducing AP costs by 1 point on tools directly related to their shift, minimum 1 AP).

---

## 2. Baseline Mechanics & Stat Block Profile

- **Hit Points:** 8 + Con modifier per level (or +2 flat HP/level if tagged `[Civilized]`).
- **Key Attributes:** Strength or Constitution (+2 or +3), Dexterity (+0 or +1), Mental Attributes (–1 to +1).
- **Perception:** Trained (rarely alert to ambushes; focused on immediate tasks).
- **Saving Throws:** 
  - **Fortitude:** Expert (conditioned by heavy lifting and industrial dust).
  - **Reflex:** Trained.
  - **Will:** Trained.
- **Armor & Defense:** Trained in Unarmored Defense and Padded/Heavy Leather aprons (equivalent to Light Armor). Untrained in Medium and Heavy armor.
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Trained in Simple Weapons and Unarmed Attacks. Untrained in Martial weapons and firearms.

---

## 3. Core NPC Features

### Heavy Hauler & Work Stamina
Laborers treat their Strength score as 2 higher for the purpose of carrying Bulk, and can perform 10 hours of heavy physical labor before requiring rest or rolling Fortitude saves against fatigue.

### Trade Tool Proficiency
When wielding a heavy work implement (sledgehammer, crowbar, shovel, cargo hook, or large wrench), the Laborer does not suffer improvised weapon penalties and treats the tool as a simple weapon dealing 1d6 bludgeoning or piercing damage.

### Familiar Tool AP Discount
When activating heavy municipal or industrial Neither-tools (such as pneumatic riveters, cable winches, or hydraulic jacks), the Laborer gains a **1-point AP discount (minimum 1 AP)** on tools matching their daily trade.

## 4. Optional "Bar Fight" & Flavor Feats

*GMs can give an individual Laborer 1 or 2 of these feats to represent street toughs, tavern brawlers, or foundry ringleaders.*

- **Bar-Room Brawler:** The Laborer gains a +1 circumstance bonus to attack rolls and damage with improvised weapons (bottles, bar stools, broken chair legs) and unarmed brawling strikes.
- **Foundry Grip ([one-action]):** The Laborer attempts an Athletics check to Grapple a foe. On a success, the foe takes 1d4 bludgeoning damage from callous hands in addition to the normal grapple effect.
- **Heave Cargo ([two-actions]):** The Laborer picks up a heavy object, barrel, or crate (Bulk 1 to 2) and hurls it up to 20 feet. Target creature must make a DC 14 Reflex save or take 2d6 bludgeoning damage and fall Prone.
- **Callous Skin:** Gain physical damage resistance 1 against bludgeoning and non-lethal damage from bare-knuckle strikes.
- **Union Call ([one-action], Auditory):** The Laborer shouts for aid from fellow workers. All allied laborers within 60 feet gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Will saves against fear and Intimidation for 1 minute.
