# Commoner / Burgher (NPC Class)

- **Role:** Non-Player Character (Civilian / Urban Commercial Baseline)
- **Location:** `ClockworkPathfinder/Classes/NPC/Commoner.md`
- **Typical Traits:** Human, Civilized
- **Status Tier:** Status 1 (Domestic Servant, Bartender) to Status 2 (Tavern Owner, Shopkeeper, Landlord)
- **Design Intent:** Represents the beating social heart of spire wards and market squares: innkeepers, shop proprietors, domestic staff, carriage drivers, and tavern masters. They lack military combat training, but possess acute social intuition, street-level gossip networks, and resourceful defensive instincts.

---

## 1. Setting Overview & In-World Identity

Commoners and Burghers keep urban society fed, sheltered, and entertained.
- **Tavern Keepers & Barmen:** Preside over neighborhood watering holes in the middle circles, listening to rumors from off-shift constables, dockers, and traveling scouts.
- **Shopkeepers & Stallholders:** Sell vegetables, processed Mela, cloth, candles, and brass household hardware in the Ward 11 markets.
- **Innkeepers & Landlords:** Rent cramped rooms and tenements, negotiate lease terms, and call the Ward Watch when brawls spill into the street.
- **Domestic Servants:** Cooks, scullery maids, valets, and footmen who serve noble estates and merchant manors, observing family secrets from the shadows.

---

## 2. Baseline Mechanics & Stat Block Profile

- **Hit Points:** 6 + Con modifier per level (+2 flat HP/level if tagged `[Civilized]`).
- **Key Attributes:** Wisdom or Charisma (+2 or +3), Dexterity (+1), Strength (+0), Intelligence (+0 or +1).
- **Perception:** Expert (keen sense of customer moods, pickpockets, and social cues).
- **Saving Throws:** 
  - **Fortitude:** Trained.
  - **Reflex:** Trained.
  - **Will:** Expert (unshakable stubbornness born of dealing with unruly patrons and demanding landlords).
- **Armor & Defense:** Trained in Unarmored Defense. Untrained in armor.
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Trained in Simple Weapons (clubs, daggers, rolling pins, kitchen knives) and Unarmed Attacks.

---

## 3. Core NPC Features

### Ward Rumor Network & Street Ear
Commoners and Burghers are the primary source of urban information. They gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Diplomacy checks to Gather Information or Recall Knowledge regarding neighborhood gossip, black-market connections, or merchant disputes within their home ward.

### Domestic Tool AP Discount
When activating commercial or domestic Neither-gear (such as radiant heating plates, refrigeration lockers, or wireless tavern lamps), the Commoner gains a **1-point AP discount (minimum 1 AP)**.

### Settle Down / Call the Watch ([one-action], Auditory)
The Commoner uses a mix of sharp reprimands or an emergency brass whistle:
- **Calm Crowd:** Attempt a Diplomacy check against the highest Will DC in an unruly room; on a success, non-lethal tavern arguments de-escalate.
- **Whistle for the Watch:** In an authorized ward, blowing their brass whistle alerts the local Ward Constabulary patrol, which arrives in 2d4 rounds.

---

## 4. Optional Tavern & Shop Talents

*GMs can give an individual Commoner 1 or 2 of these talents based on their establishment.*

- **Tavernkeeper's Bludgeon:** When wielding a heavy bottle, skillet, or club behind the counter, the Tavernkeeper deals 1d6 bludgeoning damage and gains a +1 circumstance bonus to Disarm or Shove drunk patrons.
- **Sharp Assayer:** The shopkeeper immediately detects counterfeit coin, forged credit notes, or shaved silver on a DC 15 Perception check.
- **Servant's Discretion:** The domestic servant can Hide or Sneak within their employer's manor with a +2 circumstance bonus, blending into the background as an invisible menial.
- **Behind the Counter Cover:** While standing behind an oak bar, merchant stall, or reinforced desk, the Commoner treats Standard Cover as Greater Cover (+4 AC / Reflex).
