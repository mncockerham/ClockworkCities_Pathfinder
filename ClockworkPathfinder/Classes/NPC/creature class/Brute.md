# Brute (Creature Class / Corruption Template)

- **Role:** Creature Class / Scalable Corruption Template for Animals, Beasts & Mutants
- **Location:** `ClockworkPathfinder/Classes/NPC/creature class/Brute.md`
- **Baseline Reference:** PF2e Barbarian `[Player Core 2 p.78–93]` / Legacy Brute Class
- **System Purpose:** A modular template that can be added to (or subtracted from) any baseline animal or creature stat block to represent differing degrees of **Neither corruption, adrenaline surge, feral muscle hypertrophy, and predatory rage**.

---

## 1. System Concept & Design Rules

In Clockwork Cities, animals and beasts exposed to the Neither do not simply acquire random magic; they undergo physiological hypertrophy, bone-calcification, cellular mutation, and neural degradation into frenzied predators.

By applying **Levels of the Brute Creature Class** to a base animal template (such as a standard Wolf, Boar, or Bear):
- **Base Animal (0 Levels of Brute):** Represents the pure, uncorrupted natural specimen found in high Aither summits or sterile sanctuaries.
- **+1 to +3 Levels of Brute (Fringe Exposure / Sump Mutants):** Represents animals dwelling along the edge of the Neither, Warren drains, or low valleys. Muscle mass increases, aggression surges, and hide thickens.
- **+4 to +6 Levels of Brute (Apex Corrupted / Dark Neither Stalkers):** Deep wasteland predators warped by long Neither saturation. They gain thick subcutaneous fat, dense bone plates, kinetic resistance, and devastating mauling attacks.
- **+7+ Levels of Brute (Storm Horrors / Deep Titans):** Creatures that have survived directly within Neither Storm surges or abyssal fissures. Unstoppable killing machines that can challenge high-level hero cadres.

## 3. Modular Brute Feat Menu (Choose at Indicated Levels)

Select feats from this menu based on the Barbarian/Brute progression `[Player Core 2 pp. 84–93]` to tailor the creature's feral style:

### 1st-Level Modular Feats:
- **Sudden Charge ([two-actions]):** The creature Strides twice and then makes a melee Strike.
- **Acute Scent:** The creature gains imprecise scent 60 feet (or precise scent 30 feet).
- **Raging Intimidation:** The creature gains Demoralize as a free action when rolling initiative or charging.
- **Furious Finish:** At the end of a round, the creature makes an all-out strike adding `+2 per die` to damage, then becomes Fatigued until the end of its next turn.

### 2nd-Level Modular Feats:
- **Bashing Charge ([two-actions]):** The creature charges through doors, barricades, or terrain, making an Athletics check to Force Open or Shove obstacles without slowing down.
- **Shake It Off ([one-action]):** The creature reduces Frightened, Sickened, or Stupified conditions by 1.
- **No Escape ([reaction]):** Trigger: An adjacent foe Strides away. The creature Strides up to its Speed, matching the escaping foe's movement.

### 4th-Level Modular Feats:
- **Sweeping Swipe ([two-actions]):** The creature makes a single melee Strike that applies its full attack and damage against two adjacent foes.
- **Knockback:** When the creature hits with a melee Strike, it can immediately make a free Shove check against the target.
- **Raging Athlete:** The creature gains a Climb and Swim speed equal to its base land speed and adds +10 feet to Leap distances.

### 6th-Level Modular Feats:
- **Cleave ([reaction]):** Trigger: The creature reduces a foe to 0 HP with a melee Strike. It immediately makes another melee Strike against an adjacent target.
- **Brutal Bully:** The creature adds its full Strength modifier as flat bludgeoning damage whenever it successfully Trips, Shoves, or Grapples a foe.
- **Apex Thrash ([one-action]):** Requirement: Creature has grabbed a foe. Deals automatic primary Strike damage to the grabbed victim without needing an attack roll.

### 8th-Level Modular Feats:
- **Terrifying Howl ([one-action]):** The creature emits a blood-curdling roar. All enemies within 30 feet must succeed on a Will save against the creature's Intimidation DC or become Frightened 2 (Frightened 3 on critical failure).
- **Renewed Vigor ([one-action]):** The creature catches its breath and shrugs off pain, regaining Temporary HP equal to `10 + (2 × Brute Level)`.
- **Bounding Pounce ([two-actions]):** The creature leaps forward up to its normal land Speed across low obstacles and makes a melee Strike at the end of the leap with full momentum.

### Feral Adrenaline Surge ([free-action] Trigger: Enters Combat)
The creature taps into explosive adrenaline and neither-fueled frenzy.
- Gains **Temporary Hit Points** equal to `10 + (2 × Brute Level)`.
- Gains a **+10 foot status bonus to Speed**.
- Suffers a **–1 penalty to AC** as it attacks with reckless ferocity, ignoring its own safety.
- Cannot use Concentrate actions other than Seek.
- Lasts for 1 minute or until combat ends.

### Relentless Momentum & Dense Bone
- **Massive Impact:** When the creature hits a foe smaller than itself with a primary Strike, it deals bonus bludgeoning force and forces the target to make a Fortitude save against the creature's Athletics DC or be knocked **Prone**.
- **Physical Hardness / Resistance:** Starting at Brute Level 3, the creature's thickened hide and bone plates absorb kinetic force, granting **Physical Resistance (bludgeoning, piercing, slashing)** as listed on the table (bypassed only by specialized energy, fire, or high-radiance weaponry).

### Brutal Maul ([two-actions] Requirements: Creature has grabbed a foe)
The creature violently shakes and crushes the grabbed victim.
- Deals its primary Strike damage plus `1d10` crushing bludgeoning damage.
- The target must succeed on a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + Creature Level + Str mod) or suffer broken bones: the victim is **Clumsy 1** and **Enfeebled 1** until medically treated.

## 4. Application Example: Scaling the Bear

To see how the Brute creature class modifies baseline animals:

### Step 1: Base Black Bear (Level 2 Animal — Uncorrupted Natural Baseline)
- **HP:** 30 | **AC:** 17 | **Attack:** +9 (Jaws `1d10+4`, Claws `1d6+4`) | **Fort:** +8
- Represents an unmutated black bear dwelling in pristine high Aither mountain forests.

### Step 2: Adding +3 Levels of Brute (Level 5 Sump Bear / Fringe Mutant)
- *Add Brute Level 3:* +45 HP, +3 Attack, +4 Damage, +4 Fort, Resistance 2.
- **HP:** 75 | **AC:** 16 (-1 reckless) | **Attack:** +12 (Jaws `1d10+8`, Claws `1d6+8`) | **Fort:** +12
- Gains *Feral Adrenaline Surge* (+16 Temp HP), *Sudden Charge*, and *Deny Advantage*.
- A dangerous woodland predator contaminated by surface Neither seepage.

### Step 3: Scaling to Grizzly Bear (+5 Levels of Brute — Level 7 Apex Threat)
- *Add Brute Level 5:* +80 HP, +5 Attack, +8 Damage, +6 Fort, Resistance 4.
- **HP:** 155 | **AC:** 25 (scaled base) | **Attack:** +18 (Jaws `2d12+9`, Claws `2d10+9`) | **Fort:** +19 | **Resistance 5**
- Fully matches the rescaled **Level 7 Grizzly Bear** requiring a full party of 5 5th-level heroes.

### Step 4: Neither-Grizzly (+7 Levels of Brute — Level 9 Storm Horror)
- *Add Brute Level 7:* +125 HP, +7 Attack, +12 Damage, +8 Fort, Resistance 6.
- **HP:** 180+ | **Attack:** +20 | **Fort:** +21 | **Resistance 8**
- Mutated by Lower Neither storm squalls; features electric-arc claws, Juggernaut saves, and devastating mauls that crush heavy knight plate.
