# Biochemical (Creature Class / Caustic & Chemical Template)

- **Role:** Creature Class / Scalable Caustic Acid & Chemical Gland Template for Animals & Beasts
- **Location:** `ClockworkPathfinder/Classes/NPC/creature class/Biochemical.md`
- **System Baseline:** Pathfinder 2e Remaster (ORC) Acid & Splash Rules `[Player Core 2 p. 44 / GM Core p. 77]`
- **Design Intent:** Represents biological adaptation to volatile mineral reactions, hyper-acidic digestive gizzards, combustible gas bladders, and caustic secretions. Natural mad science evolved into living predators—grounded in chemical biology without magic.

---

## 1. System Concept & Biological Realities

Animals feeding in Warren drainage basins, sulfuric subterranean hot springs, or Neithercrete waste dumps evolve internal bio-catalytic systems mirroring a **Mad Bomber’s** ordnance:
- **Hyper-Acidic Gastric Chyme:** Gizzards capable of dissolving scrap metal and stone produce pressurized jets of digestive acid (like a bombardier beetle scaled up to predator size).
- **Combustible Biogas Sacs:** Internal methane or sulfur bladders that can ignite upon exposure to air or through rapid glandular friction, producing thermal bursts.
- **Natural Chemical Spray (No Supernatural Flight or Phasing):** Attacks use pressurized physical vomiting, directional glandular expulsions (like a massive bombardier beetle or skunk spray), or caustic contact slime.

## 3. Modular Biochemical Feat Menu

### 1st-Level Feats:
- **Corrosive Saliva:** Primary melee bite attacks deal an extra `1d4` acid damage and corrode non-metallic gear.
- **Alchemical Insulation:** The creature is completely immune to Acid damage and takes half damage from fire.
- **Caustic Phlegm Spit ([one-action]):** (Ranged 30 ft) Fires a pressurized glob of bile. Deals listed ranged acid damage plus splash damage to adjacent creatures.

### 2nd-Level Feats:
- **Bombardier Jet ([reaction]):** Trigger: An attacker enters melee reach behind the creature. The creature expels a boiling jet of noxious liquid and steam from its abdominal glands in a 10-foot emanation behind it, dealing `2d6` fire/acid damage (DC Reflex save for half).
- **Acid Sizzle:** Whenever the creature scores a critical hit with an acid attack, the target's armor or shield is scorched, inflicting a -1 circumstance penalty to AC until repaired.
- **Sticky Chyme:** Creatures taking splash damage from the beast's acid suffer a -10 foot status penalty to Speed for 1 round as sticky digestive mucus congeals.

### 4th-Level Feats:
- **Caustic Vomit Cone ([two-actions]):** (Area Attack) The creature vomits a pressurized wave of boiling gastric bile in a **20-foot cone**. Deals `4d6` acid damage (DC Reflex save for half; failure inflicts `1d6` persistent acid damage). Recharges in `1d4` rounds.
- **Armor-Melting Slime:** Melee attackers striking the beast with unarmed strikes or non-reach weapons take `1d6` acid damage from caustic contact mucus.
- **Volatile Biogas Bladder:** The creature's gastric chyme can be ignited: the creature can choose whether its breath and ranged spit deal Acid or Fire damage.

### 6th-Level Feats:
- **Concentrated Dissolving Jet ([two-actions]):** The creature fires a focused, high-pressure stream of digestive enzymes at a single target within 40 feet. Deals `6d6` acid damage ignoring up to 5 points of object Hardness (DC Reflex save; critical failure destroys non-magical wooden or leather shields instantly).
- **Noxious Chemical Fumes (Aura 10 ft):** Boiling acid dripping from the beast's jaws releases choking vapors. Any creature entering or starting its turn in the aura must succeed on a Fortitude save against the creature's Chemical DC or become Sickened 1.

### 8th-Level Feats:
- **Catastrophic Detonation Rupture (Reaction on Death):** Trigger: The creature is reduced to 0 HP. Its pressurized gastric chambers and biogas bladders detonate violently in a **20-foot burst**. All creatures in the burst take `6d6` acid damage plus `6d6` fire damage (DC Reflex save for half). Creates a 20-foot pool of hazardous acid sludge that lasts for 3 rounds (`2d6` acid to anyone walking through it).
- **Caustic Surge Cone ([two-actions]):** Expands the creature's vomit cone to a **40-foot cone** dealing `8d6` acid damage.
