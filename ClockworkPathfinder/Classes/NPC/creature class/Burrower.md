# Burrower (Creature Class / Subterranean & Tunnel Template)

- **Role:** Creature Class / Scalable Subterranean & Ambush Template for Animals & Beasts
- **Location:** `ClockworkPathfinder/Classes/NPC/creature class/Burrower.md`
- **System Baseline:** Pathfinder 2e Remaster (ORC) Burrowing & Tremorsense Rules `[GM Core p. 77]`
- **Design Intent:** Represents specialized spade claws, thick snout plates, acoustic vibration whiskers, and sinkhole collapse ambushes evolved by badgers, moles, trapdoor vermin, and subterranean quarry lizards. Grounded in fossorial biology—no magical earth-phasing.

---

## 1. System Concept & Biological Realities

In the subterranean mines (**Layer 5: Lightless Neither**) and forgotten **Tombs** beneath Great Spires, creatures live entirely within loose bedrock, coal seams, and crumbly Neithercrete foundations:
- **Fossorial Claws & Gular Plates:** Thick, chisel-like keratin claws and reinforced skulls designed to displace tons of soil and cracked stone.
- **Vibration Sensing (Tremorsense):** Blind or near-blind eyes compensated by hair follicles and jaw sensors that detect footfalls and mechanical vibrations through the earth.
- **Physical Pitfall Ambushes (No Magic Teleportation):** The beast undermines soil beneath walking prey, creating natural sinkholes, or drags victims into shallow crawl-trenches.

## 3. Modular Burrower Feat Menu

### 1st-Level Feats:
- **Chisel Claws:** Primary claw strikes gain the Razing trait and deal double damage against stationary earthen walls, loose masonry, and wooden floorboards.
- **Blind Adaptability:** The creature is completely immune to the Blinded and Dazzled conditions from visual light flashes.
- **Tunnel Stability:** The creature cannot be knocked Prone or moved by Shove effects while standing on dirt, gravel, or fractured stone.

### 2nd-Level Feats:
- **Blinding Dirt Spray ([one-action]):** The creature's heavy rear claws violently kick gravel and dust in a **10-foot cone**. All creatures in the cone must succeed on a Reflex save against the creature's Sinkhole DC or be **Blinded for 1 round** (Dazzled on a success).
- **Subterranean Dig-In ([one-action]):** The creature burrows halfway into the ground, gaining standard cover (+2 circumstance to AC and Reflex saves) while stationary.
- **Vibration Echo:** Tremorsense allows the creature to pinpoint the location of creatures moving across the surface even through 5 feet of solid stone.

### 4th-Level Feats:
- **Burst From Below ([two-actions]):** The creature burrows up to its Burrow Speed beneath a surface target and erupts upward with a primary melee Strike. The target is caught **Flat-Footed** against the Strike; on a hit, the target must make a Fortitude save against the creature's Sinkhole DC or be knocked **Prone**.
- **Sinkhole Collapse ([two-actions]):** The creature undermines a 10-foot square of earth or loose foundation beneath foes. The ground collapses into difficult terrain (a 5-foot deep trench); creatures in the area must succeed on a Reflex save or fall into the pit, taking `2d6` bludgeoning damage and becoming Prone.
- **Reinforced Gular Plate:** Grants a +2 circumstance bonus to AC against ranged attacks while in a trench or hole.

### 6th-Level Feats:
- **Drag Under ([two-actions]):** Requirement: The creature has grabbed a foe smaller than or equal to its size. The creature Strides or Burrows up to half its Burrow Speed, dragging the victim into its crawlspace. The target remains grabbed and is **Suffocating / Immobilized** until it escapes.
- **Neithercrete Burrower:** The creature's reinforced claws can dig through soft bedrock and aged Neithercrete masonry at half its normal Burrow Speed.

### 8th-Level Feats:
- **Quake Slam ([two-actions]):** The creature thrashes against the underground bedrock, sending violent acoustic shockwaves through the surface in a **20-foot radius**. All surface creatures must succeed on a Reflex save against the creature's Sinkhole DC or take `4d8` bludgeoning damage and fall Prone.
- **Great Cavitation Collapse ([three-actions]):** The creature rapidly undermines structural foundations in a **20-foot burst**. Any masonry walls, stairs, or ceilings above the area collapse, dealing `6d8` bludgeoning damage to all creatures within (DC Reflex save for half; creates hazardous difficult terrain).
