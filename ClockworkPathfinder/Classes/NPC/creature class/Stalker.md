# Stalker (Creature Class / Dexterity Template)

- **Role:** Creature Class / Scalable Dexterity & Ambush Template for Animals & Beasts
- **Location:** `ClockworkPathfinder/Classes/NPC/creature class/Stalker.md`
- **System Baseline:** Pathfinder 2e Remaster (ORC) Creature Building Rules `[GM Core pp. 72–89]`
- **Design Intent:** Represents heightened natural predatory speed, hyper-accelerated nervous systems, predatory stealth, camouflage, and lethal tendon/throat strikes. Purely physical and biological—no magical phasing or teleportation.

---

## 1. System Concept & Biological Realities

In the Neither wastes and dark forest fringes, predatory animals develop exceptional synaptic firing rates, reinforced ankle ligaments, and mottled pelt pigmentation.
- **Enhanced Natural Physiology:** Extreme sprint speed, silent padding, kinetic absorption when pouncing, and razor-sharp serrated claws/talons.
- **Grounded Physics (No Supernatural Phasing):** The creature uses natural cover, low ground, brush, or Neither-fog obscurity to stalk prey. It does not turn invisible or teleport.

## 3. Modular Stalker Feat Menu

### 1st-Level Feats:
- **Predatory Pounce ([two-actions]):** The creature Strides up to its Speed. If it was undetected or hidden at the start of its movement, the target is caught Flat-Footed against the creature's melee Strike, triggering precision damage.
- **Low Profile:** The creature treats light foliage, dense Neither haze, or low obstacles as providing standard cover (+2 circumstance to AC and Stealth).
- **Scent the Bleeding:** The creature gains precise scent out to 30 feet against any living target suffering from persistent bleed damage.

### 2nd-Level Feats:
- **Nimble Dodge ([reaction]):** Trigger: An attacker targets the creature with a Strike. The creature gains a +2 circumstance bonus to AC against the triggering attack.
- **Skittering Step ([one-action]):** The creature Steps up to 10 feet into or through difficult terrain without provoking reactions.
- **Tendon Snag:** On a critical hit with a primary bite or claw, the target takes a -10 foot status penalty to all Speeds for 1 round.

### 4th-Level Feats:
- **Spring Attack ([two-actions]):** The creature Strides up to its Speed, makes a Strike at any point during the movement, and continues its Stride.
- **Mottled Pelt / Camouflage:** The creature can use the Hide action even without standard cover if it is standing in Neither mist, tall grass, or shadows.
- **Flensing Claws:** The creature's claw strikes gain the Agile and Finesse traits (if they did not already possess them) and increase their bleed die size by one step.

### 6th-Level Feats:
- **Hamstring Rake ([one-action]):** Requirement: The creature hits with two claw strikes in the same turn. Deals an automatic `2d6` slashing damage; the target must succeed on a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + Level + Dex mod) or fall **Prone** and have its land Speed halved until treated with Medicine.
- **Sidestep ([reaction]):** Trigger: An attacker misses the creature with a melee Strike. The creature forces the attacker to reroll the Strike against an adjacent creature of the creature's choice.

### 8th-Level Feats:
- **Shadow Stalker:** The creature can move at its full Speed when using the Sneak action without suffering penalties or breaking stealth.
- **Severing Throat Strike ([two-actions]):** The creature makes an all-out strike against an immobilized, grabbed, or prone target. Deals double precision damage and inflicts `3d6` persistent bleed.
