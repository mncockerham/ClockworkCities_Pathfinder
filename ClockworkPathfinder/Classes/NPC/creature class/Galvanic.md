# Galvanic (Creature Class / Electrical & Static Template)

- **Role:** Creature Class / Scalable Bio-Electrical & Dielectric Template for Animals & Beasts
- **Location:** `ClockworkPathfinder/Classes/NPC/creature class/Galvanic.md`
- **System Baseline:** Pathfinder 2e Remaster (ORC) Electricity Rules `[GM Core p. 77]`
- **Design Intent:** Represents biological electrocytes, dielectric scales/fur, atmospheric static retention, and bio-electric shocks evolved from living near the Zone or Neither lightning grounds. Grounded in organic electro-physiology (like electric eels and torpedo rays)—no magical spells.

---

## 1. System Concept & Biological Realities

Fauna inhabiting high mountain summits, outer Spire buttresses, or the turbulent lower rim of **The Zone (Layer 2)** evolve specialized cellular organs called **electrocytes**:
- **Atmospheric Static Harvesting:** Their coats, feathers, or scales contain dielectric quartz-like minerals and oily sheaths that trap ambient electrical charge from Neither squalls.
- **Natural Shock Discharge:** Striking the creature with conductive metal weapons completes a biological circuit, delivering painful retaliatory shocks.
- **Electrical Absorption (No Flight or Magic):** The beast feeds on electrical energy; taking lightning or electrical damage harmlessly charges its internal battery chambers rather than harming tissue.

## 3. Modular Galvanic Feat Menu

### 1st-Level Feats:
- **Dielectric Insulation:** The creature is completely immune to electrical damage.
- **Electrified Jaws / Talons:** Primary melee Strikes deal additional electricity damage as listed on the table.
- **Static Bristle ([one-action]):** The creature rubs its quills or flexes its fur, building a visible corona of static sparks; its next melee Strike within 1 minute deals an extra `1d6` electrical damage.

### 2nd-Level Feats:
- **Conductive Recoil ([reaction]):** Trigger: An attacker hits the creature in melee using a conductive metal weapon or unarmed strike. The attacker takes retaliatory electricity damage as listed on the table (metal armor wearers take double this damage).
- **Bio-Electric Spark ([one-action]):** (Ranged 20 ft) The creature discharges an arc of static from its horns, quills, or mouth, dealing listed ranged electrical damage.
- **Static Acceleration:** Whenever the creature is subjected to an electrical effect or takes electrical damage, it gains a +10 foot status bonus to Speed for 1 round.

### 4th-Level Feats:
- **Arc Discharge Cone ([two-actions]):** (Area Attack) The creature expels stored electrostatic charge from its crest or flanks in a **20-foot cone**. Deals listed cone damage (DC Reflex save for half; failure dazzles the target for 1 round). Recharges in `1d4` rounds.
- **Battery Glands (Cellular Absorption):** Whenever the creature would take electrical damage, it instead regains Hit Points equal to half the damage prevented.
- **Stunning Shock:** Whenever the creature scores a critical hit with an electrical Strike, the target must succeed on a Fortitude save against the creature's Shock DC or become **Stunned 1**.

### 6th-Level Feats:
- **Electromagnetic Clench ([reaction]):** Trigger: A foe misses the creature with a metal melee weapon. The creature creates a sudden electromagnetic surge across its hide, attempting a free Disarm check using its Shock DC against the weapon.
- **Cartridge Resonance Surge (Aura 15 ft):** Intense bio-static hums around the beast. Any firearm or radiant weapon utilizing Neither cartridges within the aura has a 20% chance per shot to misfire or suffer a temporary kinetic lock (requires 1 action to clear).

### 8th-Level Feats:
- **Blinding Arc Flash ([two-actions]):** The creature discharges a blinding sphere of pure white bio-static in a **30-foot emanation**. All creatures in the burst take `6d6` electrical damage and must make a Reflex save: failure inflicts **Blinded for 1 round** (Dazzled on success).
- **Catastrophic Death Surge (Reaction on Death):** Trigger: The creature is slain. Its swollen electrocyte capacitors violently rupture in a **25-foot burst**, dealing `8d6` electrical damage (DC Reflex save for half; knocks flying or mounted creatures Prone).
