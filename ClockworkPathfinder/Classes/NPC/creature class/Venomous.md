# Venomous (Creature Class / Poison Template)

- **Role:** Creature Class / Scalable Glandular Poison Template for Animals & Beasts
- **Location:** `ClockworkPathfinder/Classes/NPC/creature class/Venomous.md`
- **System Baseline:** Pathfinder 2e Remaster (ORC) Affliction & Poison Rules `[Player Core p. 446 / GM Core p. 77]`
- **Design Intent:** Represents specialized natural venom glands, grooved teeth/claws, barbed defensive spines, and pressurized skunk-style musk sprays. Grounded in biological toxins—no magical curses.

---

## 1. System Concept & Biological Realities

In Neither-saturated environments, snakes, spiders, sumpfowl, bats, and predatory mammals feed on toxic `Sycelium` fungal spores. Over generations, their salivary glands and subcutaneous scent sacs convert these toxic spores into potent neurotoxins and tissue-rotting venoms:
- **Natural Delivery Vectors:** Grooved canine fangs, barbed venomous spurs (like a platypus), scraping claws, or pressurized defensive scent glands (like a skunk or spitting cobra).
- **Realistic Biological Effects:** Neurotoxic paralytics (Clumsy, Slowed, Paralyzed), necrotizing hemotoxins (persistent bleed, Sickened, Enfeebled), or blinding glandular sprays.

## 3. Modular Venomous Feat Menu

### 1st-Level Feats:
- **Grooved Envenomed Fangs / Claws:** The creature coats its primary melee Strike with natural venom; on a hit, the target is exposed to the creature's poison affliction.
- **Toxin Resistance:** The creature is completely immune to its own venom and gains Resistance 5 to all environmental poisons and Neither-spores.
- **Caustic Saliva:** Melee bites inflict an additional `1d4` persistent acid damage as digestive enzymes break down tissue.

### 2nd-Level Feats:
- **Defensive Barbed Spines ([reaction]):** Trigger: An adjacent creature hits the beast with an unarmed Strike or attempts to Grapple it. The attacker takes `1d6` piercing damage and must succeed on a Fortitude save against the creature's Venom DC or be exposed to its poison.
- **Paralytic Hamstring:** Targets suffering from the creature's poison cannot use the Step action.
- **Noxious Hiss ([one-action]):** The creature rattles venomous quills or hisses, forcing all foes within 15 feet to make a Will save against its Venom DC or become Frightened 1.

### 4th-Level Feats:
- **Skunk Musk Spray ([two-actions]):** (Area Attack) The creature aims a specialized rear or mouth gland and sprays a **15-foot cone of oily, sulfurous musk**. All creatures in the area must succeed on a Fortitude save against the creature's Venom DC:
  - **Success:** Sickened 1.
  - **Failure:** **Blinded for 1 round**, Sickened 2, and exposed to Stage 1 poison. The stench lingers on their clothes/gear for 24 hours (-2 to Stealth and social checks).
  - **Critical Failure:** Blinded for `1d4` rounds, Sickened 3, and exposed to Stage 2 poison.
- **Blinding Spurt ([one-action]):** (Ranged 20 ft) The creature spits pressurized venom directly into a target's eyes. DC Fortitude save or the target is **Blinded for 1 round** (Dazzled on a success).
- **Virulent Strain:** The creature's poison requires 2 consecutive successful Fortitude saves to end rather than 1.

### 6th-Level Feats:
- **Pressurized Aerosol Cloud ([two-actions]):** The creature expels a pressurized mist in a **30-foot cone**. All breathing creatures must make a Fortitude save against the creature's Venom DC or suffer immediate respiratory spasms (Stage 1 poison + Slowed 1 for 1 round).
- **Necrotic Cell Breakdown:** Whenever a target fails a save against the creature's poison, their wound festers, dealing `2d6` persistent bleed damage that cannot be stopped until the poison is cured.

### 8th-Level Feats:
- **Apex Neurotoxin Paralysis:** Targets reaching Stage 2 of the creature's poison become **Paralyzed** for as long as they remain in Stage 2.
- **Death Gland Rupture (Reaction on Death):** Trigger: The creature is slain. Its swollen venom reservoirs rupture, spraying a 15-foot burst of noxious fluid. All adjacent creatures must succeed on a Fortitude save or take `4d8` poison damage and become Sickened 2.
