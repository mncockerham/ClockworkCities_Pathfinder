# Pack (Creature Class / Swarm & Coordination Template)

- **Role:** Creature Class / Scalable Pack Coordination & Swarm Synergy Template for Animals & Beasts
- **Location:** `ClockworkPathfinder/Classes/NPC/creature class/Pack.md`
- **System Baseline:** Pathfinder 2e Remaster (ORC) Pack & Swarm Rules `[GM Core p. 77]`
- **Design Intent:** Represents hyper-coordinated group instincts, sensory communication, synchronized takedowns, and numerical intimidation evolved by canines, rodents, raptors, and hyenas. Purely biological pack dynamics—no hive-mind magic.

---

## 1. System Concept & Biological Realities

In the Warren sumps, dark circles, and surface ruins, lone predators die quickly. Pack animals survive by hunting as an integrated biological machine:
- **Pheromonal & Vocal Signaling:** Micro-vocalizations, tail-flicks, and musks coordinate flanking moves without verbal delay.
- **Harassment & Distraction:** While one beast snaps at heels, another drives teeth into tendons, tearing prey down by mass and attrition.
- **Pack Morale:** Numerical presence stabilizes courage; animals fight with savage confidence when surrounded by brethren.

## 3. Modular Pack Feat Menu

### 1st-Level Feats:
- **Pack Tactics:** The creature treats any enemy engaged by at least one ally as **Off-Guard (Flat-Footed)** to all its melee Strikes, dealing its listed pack precision damage.
- **Pack Yip / Scent Mesh:** The creature gains imprecise scent out to 30 feet, sharing target locations automatically with all packmates within 60 feet.
- **Loyal Pack Morale:** Gains a +2 circumstance bonus to Will saves against Fear and Demoralize while within 30 feet of at least one conscious ally.

### 2nd-Level Feats:
- **Synchronized Harass ([reaction]):** Trigger: An adjacent ally successfully hits an enemy with a melee Strike. The creature immediately Steps up to 5 feet into an open flank around the target without provoking reactions.
- **Heel Snap:** When the creature hits an enemy from a flank, the target takes a -5 foot circumstance penalty to all Speeds for 1 round.
- **Snarl & Snap ([one-action]):** The creature snaps its jaws alongside an ally; both creatures gain a +1 circumstance bonus to AC against the target until the start of their next turn.

### 4th-Level Feats:
- **Coordinated Takedown ([reaction]):** Trigger: Both the creature and an ally hit the same target in the same round. The creature makes a free Athletics check to **Trip** the target.
- **Swarming Snap ([reaction]):** Trigger: An enemy adjacent to the creature falls Prone or is knocked down by an ally. The creature immediately makes a free melee Strike against the fallen victim.
- **Harrying Circle:** Foes threatened by at least two pack members cannot take the Step action and must take a Stride (provoking reactions if applicable).

### 6th-Level Feats:
- **Mob Pin ([two-actions]):** Requirement: Two or more pack members threaten the target. The pack members leap onto the victim; target must make a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + Level + Str mod) or become **Restrained** and pinned to the ground (target is immobilized and flat-footed; escapes via Athletics or Acrobatics check).
- **Incite the Pack ([one-action], Auditory):** The pack alpha lets out a hunting cry. All packmates within 60 feet gain a +10 foot status bonus to Speed and a +1 status bonus to melee damage for 1 round.

### 8th-Level Feats:
- **Feeding Frenzy ([reaction]):** Trigger: A packmate scores a critical hit against an enemy. The creature immediately uses its reaction to make a primary melee Strike against that target with a +2 circumstance bonus.
- **Unbreakable Alpha Bond:** The pack is completely immune to Frightened, Confused, and fleeing effects as long as the pack alpha remains conscious and fighting.
