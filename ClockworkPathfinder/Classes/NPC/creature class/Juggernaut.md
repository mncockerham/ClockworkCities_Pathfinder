# Juggernaut (Creature Class / Stamina Template)

- **Role:** Creature Class / Scalable Stamina & Unyielding Mass Template for Animals & Beasts
- **Location:** `ClockworkPathfinder/Classes/NPC/creature class/Juggernaut.md`
- **System Baseline:** Pathfinder 2e Remaster (ORC) Creature Building Rules `[GM Core pp. 72–89]`
- **Design Intent:** Represents immense physical bulk, dense subcutaneous fat layers, reinforced cartilaginous skeletons, massive lung stamina, and biological tissue recovery. Purely biological durability—no magical force fields.

---

## 1. System Concept & Biological Realities

In the harsh Neither wilderness, grazing mega-fauna, thick-hided pachyderms, and apex boars evolve massive biological survival traits:
- **Kinetic Shock Absorption:** Deep layers of blubber and fibrous connective tissue diffuse blunt trauma from cudgels and ballistic cartridges.
- **Continuous Cellular Clotting:** Accelerated natural blood clotting and rapid tissue granulating allow the beast to knit torn muscle and flesh over the course of a battle (Fast Healing).
- **Physical Bulk (No Magic Immunity):** The beast resists being stunned or knocked prone simply because shifting a 2,000-to-8,000 pound animal with normal weapons is physically impractical.

## 3. Modular Juggernaut Feat Menu

### 1st-Level Feats:
- **Diehard:** The creature's massive physical resilience makes it nearly impossible to fell with standard wounds; it adds +2 to all dying recovery checks.
- **Ponderous Charge ([two-actions]):** The creature Strides up to its Speed and makes a primary Strike. If it moved at least 20 feet in a straight line, it gains a +2 circumstance bonus to damage.
- **Subcutaneous Insulation:** Grants Resistance 5 against cold and environmental exposure in the Neither.

### 2nd-Level Feats:
- **Thick Fibrous Hide:** The creature's rubbery skin blunts blades; gains a +1 status bonus to AC against piercing and slashing attacks.
- **Shake It Off ([one-action]):** The creature violently flexes its massive frame, reducing its Frightened, Sickened, or Slowed condition value by 1.
- **Stubborn Anchor:** The creature gains a +4 circumstance bonus to its Fortitude DC against Shove, Trip, or Reposition attempts.

### 4th-Level Feats:
- **Trample ([three-actions]):** The creature Strides up to double its Speed, moving through the spaces of creatures at least one size smaller than itself. Each creature trampled takes primary Strike damage (DC Fortitude save for half; failure knocks the target **Prone**).
- **Dense Bone Plates:** The creature’s calcified bones and dense clotting agents make it completely **immune to persistent bleed damage**.
- **Heavy Headbutt / Shoulder Check:** Grants a secondary unarmed Strike (dealing bludgeoning damage equal to its primary attack die -1 step) with the Shove trait.

### 6th-Level Feats:
- **Barricade Breaker:** The creature deals double damage to stationary objects, wooden barricades, wagon carts, and palisade walls, ignoring up to 5 points of physical Hardness.
- **Unstoppable Mass:** The creature completely ignores non-magical difficult terrain (mud, thick brush, loose rubble, swamp water).
- **Adrenaline Surge of the Dying:** Trigger: The creature is reduced to below 25% HP. It immediately gains 20 Temporary HP and its Fast Healing increases by +5 for 3 rounds.

### 8th-Level Feats:
- **Ferocity ([reaction]):** Trigger: The creature would be reduced to 0 HP. It avoids being knocked unconscious and remains at 1 HP until the end of its next turn.
- **Living Fortress:** The creature’s shear tonnage and low center of gravity make it completely immune to the **Prone**, **Immobilized**, and **Stunned** conditions from physical attacks.
