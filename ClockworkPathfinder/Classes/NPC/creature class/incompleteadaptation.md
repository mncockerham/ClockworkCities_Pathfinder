# Incomplete Adaptation (Creature Flaw & Degeneration Template)

- **Role:** Creature Flaw / De-buff Template for Maladapted, SICK, or Displaced Fauna & Aberrants
- **Location:** `ClockworkPathfinder/Classes/NPC/creature class/incompleteadaptation.md`
- **System Baseline:** Pathfinder 2e Remaster (ORC) Condition Rules `[Player Core p. 442]`
- **Design Intent:** Represents animals, beasts, or aberrant humanoids struggling to adapt to a foreign Neither density (or suffering from Neither starvation/poisoning). Instead of complex granular debuffs, Storytellers can quickly apply stat/damage penalties and select thematic biological malfunctions.

---

## 1. System Concept & Biological Realities

Not every creature roaming the Spires, Warrens, or surface wastes is an apex predator in peak physical equilibrium:
- **Atmospheric Shock:** Animals migrating vertically across layers (e.g. mountain wolves trapped in the sweltering Lower Neither, or subterranean cave fauna driven to the sunlit upper slopes) suffer respiratory distress, cellular hypoxia, and ocular irritation.
- **Dietary Toxicity / Nutritional Deprivation:** Creatures adapted to Neither-corrupted fungi starve or develop neural tremors when fed clean Aither fare; conversely, pristine surface animals eating toxic raw Sycelium develop violent gut ulcers and cellular necrosis.
- **Why We Keep It Lean:** Standard baseline Pathfinder creatures are already relatively fragile against coordinated parties. Therefore, this template is designed as a **fast, modular toolkit of flavor and mechanical flaws** rather than a tedious multi-level deconstruction.

---

## 2. Quick Degeneration Adjustments (The Baseline Penalty)

When a creature is marked with **Incomplete Adaptation**, apply one or both of the following baseline reductions:

1. **Reduced Threat Rating (–1 to –2 Effective Level):**
   - **Damage Dice Step-Down:** Reduce all primary and secondary damage dice by one step (e.g., `d12` $\rightarrow$ `d10`, `d10` $\rightarrow$ `d8`, `d8` $\rightarrow$ `d6`, `d6` $\rightarrow$ `d4`).
   - **Stat / Attack Penalty:** Apply a flat **–2 status penalty** to all attack rolls, Perception checks, and saving throws.
   - **Hit Point Reduction:** Subtract **20% to 30% of its base Hit Points** (or simply reduce HP by `4 × Base Level`).

## 3. Modular Maladaptation Malfunctions (Choose 1 or 2)

To give maladapted creatures distinct tactical behaviors and narrative personality, select 1 or 2 flaws from this menu:

### A. Respiratory Wheeze & Hypoxia (Atmospheric Mismatch)
*The creature's lungs are blistering or struggling to extract oxygen through foreign Neither densities.*
- **Stamina Deficit:** The creature becomes **Fatigued** after 3 rounds of combat.
- **Violent Coughing Spasms:** Whenever the creature critically fails a physical check or takes a double Stride action, it must make a DC 15 Fortitude save or immediately become **Slowed 1** for 1 round as it hacks up bloody Neither-phlegm.

### B. Cellular Tremors & Neural Decay (Neither Poisoning / Starvation)
*Erratic electrochemical firings wrack the creature's nervous system.*
- **Muscle Tremors:** The creature suffers from **Clumsy 1** and takes a –2 circumstance penalty to all Athletics and Acrobatics checks.
- **Erratic Agression / Confusion:** At the start of its turn, if the creature is below half its maximum Hit Points, roll a flat DC 5 check. On a failure, the creature is **Confused** for that turn, snapping at nearby shadows, allies, or terrain obstacles instead of executing coordinated tactics.

### C. Photophobic Panic & Cataracts (Light Sensitivity)
*Subterranean or deep-trench fauna exposed to Spire radiant lighting or direct sunlight.*
- **Ocular Searing:** While in daylight or within 30 feet of a radiant streetlamp (~60W), the creature is permanently **Dazzled** and takes a –2 penalty to all visual Perception checks.
- **Frenzied Retreat:** Exposure to sudden bright light (such as a flare, torch, or alchemical bomb) forces the creature to make a Will save against the effect DC or spend its next turn using all actions to flee toward darkness.

### D. Festering Ulcers & Brittle Hide (Tissue Breakdown)
*Neither rot or dietary failure has caused the creature's hide to crack and slough away.*
- **Vulnerability to Physical Trauma:** The creature loses all physical damage resistance (if any).
- **Brittle Vessels (Lethal Bleed):** Any slashing or piercing Strike against the creature inflicts **`1d6` persistent bleed damage** on a standard hit (`2d6` on a critical hit).

### E. Erratic Bio-Chemistry (Failed Organ Glands)
*Glandular organs intended for poison, acid, or static discharge misfire or leak.*
- **Internal Burn / Backfire:** Whenever the creature uses an acid spit, breath weapon, or venom strike, roll a flat DC 5 check. On a failure, the glandular bladder leaks internally: the attack fails, and the creature takes `2d6` acid or poison damage to itself.
- **Diluted Potency:** All save DCs for the creature's natural poisons, venoms, or chemicals are reduced by 4.

## 4. Narrative Tactical Encounters (Storyteller Advice)

Maladapted creatures provide rich storytelling and dynamic, multi-stage combat:
- **Desperate Starving Predators:** A pack of wolves suffering from *Cellular Tremors* attacking a supply caravan out of sheer starvation rather than calculated tactical malice.
- **Escaped Warren Menageries:** A giant subterranean lizard forced into the middle Spire circles, thrashing blindly due to *Photophobic Panic* and knocking over market stalls.

### The "Act Like You Planned It" Rule (Rewarding Clever Play)
While you shouldn't hand out free victories every single time, **whenever players come up with a genuinely smart, logical plan, act like you had already considered that exact contingency all along**:
- *The Classic Flare Example:* A player says, *"Hey, this creature lives in the lightless mines and doesn't like bright light—I'm popping an alchemical signaling flare right in its face!"*
  - **Do NOT** scramble through notes or argue about whether flares deal damage.
  - **DO** smile knowingly, nod, and immediately weave it into the narration: *"The beast shudders violently at the sudden blinding glare, hissing in agony and squinting through milky cataracts. It takes a –2 circumstance penalty on all attack rolls as long as the flare persists."*
- **Why This Matters:** It makes the Storyteller look prepared, completely in control of the world's deep ecological logic, and most importantly: **it powerfully rewards players for thinking creatively and engaging with the setting instead of just passively rolling dice**.
