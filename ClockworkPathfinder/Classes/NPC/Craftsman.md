# Craftsman / Artisan Apprentice (NPC Class)

- **Role:** Non-Player Character (Civilian / Guild Trade Class)
- **Location:** `ClockworkPathfinder/Classes/NPC/Craftsman.md`
- **Typical Traits:** Human, Civilized
- **Status Tier:** Status 1 (Apprentice, Shop Hand) to Status 2 (Journeyman Smith, Tailor, Carpenter)
- **Design Intent:** Represents the millions of skilled guild laborers and shop assistants who forge horse shoes, tan leather hides, weave wool garments, and cut timber across Ward 11. Unlike the heroic, legendary **Artisan PC class** (who masters cutting-edge structural precision and high NeitherTeck resonance), the Craftsman is a solid, everyday producer who repairs broken gear, tends forge fires, and sells mundane wares.

---

## 1. Setting Overview & In-World Identity

Craftsmen make up the registered guild rank-and-file in Ward 11 (Craft Ward) and throughout peripheral trade towns.
- **Blacksmith Apprentices & Strikers:** Feed coke and coal into forge hearths, swing heavy tongs, and beat hot iron into plowshares and carriage axles.
- **Tannery Hands:** Work long shifts submerged in lime pits and acidic bark vats, conditioning hides for belts, harnesses, and leather aprons.
- **Carpenters & Joiners:** Build furniture, reinforce building timber frames, and construct shipping crates.
- **Masons & Bricklayers:** Lay Neithercrete blocks, mortar stone archways, and repair minor wall cracks.

---

## 2. Baseline Mechanics & Stat Block Profile

- **Hit Points:** 8 + Con modifier per level (+2 flat HP/level if tagged `[Civilized]`).
- **Key Attributes:** Strength or Dexterity (+2), Constitution (+1 or +2), Intelligence (+1 or +2), Wisdom (+0), Charisma (–1 to +0).
- **Perception:** Trained.
- **Saving Throws:** 
  - **Fortitude:** Expert (resilient against heat, fumes, and blisters).
  - **Reflex:** Trained.
  - **Will:** Trained.
- **Armor & Defense:** Trained in Unarmored Defense and Light Armor (heavy work leathers). Untrained in Medium and Heavy armor.
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Trained in Simple Weapons (hammers, chisels, shears, mallets, hatchets) and Unarmed Attacks. Untrained in Martial weapons and firearms.

---

## 3. Core NPC Features

### Guild Crafting & Bench Maintenance
The Craftsman is Trained (or Expert) in Crafting and one specific Trade Lore (e.g., *Blacksmithing Lore*, *Carpentry Lore*, *Masonry Lore*, *Leatherworking Lore*, or *Tailoring Lore*). They can repair mundane physical equipment (weapons, armor, tools) during downtime using standard tools without rolling critical failures on ordinary materials.

### Workshop Tool AP Discount
When using neither-conductive pneumatic workshop gear, forge blowers, or hydraulic presses related directly to their registered trade, the Craftsman gains a **1-point AP discount (minimum 1 AP)**.

### Sturdy Repose
Conditioned by hot furnaces and caustic tanning chemicals, the Craftsman gains physical resistance 1 against either fire, acid, or piercing damage (based on their specific trade).

## 4. Optional Workshop Talents

*GMs can give an individual Craftsman 1 or 2 of these talents based on their bench discipline.*

- **Forge Striker's Sledge:** When wielding a blacksmith hammer or heavy maul with both hands, the Craftsman deals 1d8 bludgeoning damage and gains a +1 circumstance bonus to damage against constructs or inanimate objects.
- **Tanner's Lye Splash ([one-action]):** The tanner flings a ladle of caustic tanning lye or lime slurry at an adjacent foe. Target must make a DC 14 Reflex save or take 1d6 acid damage and become *Dazzled* for 1 round.
- **Quick Patchwork:** During a 10-minute rest, the Craftsman can restore 5 HP to an ally's broken shield or patched armor using rivets and wire.
- **Stonemason's Chisel Strike:** When striking stone, brick, or Neithercrete, the Craftsman ignores 5 points of object Hardness.
