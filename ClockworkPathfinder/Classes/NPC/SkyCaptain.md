# Sky Captain & Shipmaster (NPC Class)

- **Role:** Specialized NPC Class (Vessel Commander, Aeronaut, Corsair & Sea Skipper)
- **Base Chassis:** Swashbuckler / Naval Commander `[Player Core 2 p.176]`
- **Availability:** **NPC Only** (Vessel hirelings, enemy corsairs, naval fleet admirals, and sky-caravan captains)
- **Discipline / Trait:** Nautical & Aerial Vessel Command

---

## 1. Setting & NPC Function
*The helmsmen, corsairs, fleet commanders, and steam-skippers who master sea lanes and the turbulent skies of the Zone.*

In Clockwork Cities, airships, zeppelins, steam cutters, and sea galleons represent massive capital investments owned by Merchant Houses, Crown Navies, or criminal corsair syndicates. Operating a vessel requires deep meteorological acumen, crew coordination, and specialized kinetic piloting. 

Because adventures frequently venture away from vessels into urban tenements, subterranean sumps, and outland Neither ruins, **Sky Captains exist primarily as specialized NPCs**. Players hire them to transport their expedition across the Zone, negotiate with them for passage, or battle them in boarding actions.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The Shipboard Conditioning Rule:**
> Sky Captains are master tacticians and acrobats **only while aboard a vessel or in immediate naval boarding operations**. Their entire suite of reactive maneuvers, panache, mobility, and command aura is conditioned by the deck beneath their boots. When removed from a ship or harbor, their abilities turn off, reducing them to standard mundane combatants on foot.

## 2. Core NPC Features & Ship-Bound Conditioning

### A. Ship-Bound Abilities (Active ONLY Aboard or Boarding a Vessel)
The following abilities function **strictly while the Sky Captain is aboard a ship, airship, boat, dockyard crane, or actively boarding an adjacent vessel**. When on foot in a city street, field, dungeon, or outland trek, these abilities are **completely inactive**:

1. **Deck Panache & Aerial Footwork:**
   - Gain Panache by performing daring shipboard maneuvers (swinging from rigging, halyards, catwalks, or issuing bold steering orders).
   - While holding Panache on a vessel, gain a **+10 to +30 ft status bonus to Speed** (applies equally to climbing ropes, masts, and ladders).
   - **Precise Strike:** Adds **+2d6 to +6d6 precision damage** on Finishers with cutlasses, sabers, rapiers, boarding axes, and pistols.
2. **Opportune Deck Riposte:**
   - While on deck, if an enemy critically fails a melee Strike against the Captain, the Captain can immediately Strike back as a Reaction.
3. **Cat Fall & Shunt Discharge (Airships Only):**
   - Automatically treats falls aboard an airship as 50 feet shorter.
   - Once per round as a Reaction, when the ship is struck by lightning or electric hazards, safely shunts the discharge through ground coils.
4. **Deck Command Aura (Allied Ship Buff):**
   - While the Captain stands on the bridge or helm, allies and crew aboard the vessel gain a **+1 circumstance bonus to Acrobatics/Athletics** against rolling decks or boarding, and a **+1 circumstance bonus on Will saves** against fear and demoralization.

---

### B. Dedicated Ship-Specific Skills & Actions
A Sky Captain possesses specialized actions that directly manipulate naval and aerial combat:

1. **Evasive Trim & Heel (2 Actions, Piloting):**
   - The Captain rolls an *Airship Lore* or *Sailing Lore* check against the opposing vessel's Maneuver DC.
   - On a Success, the ship gains **+2 circumstance bonus to AC and Reflex saves** against broadsides, ballistas, and ramming until the start of the Captain's next turn.
2. **Broadside Battery Cadence (1 Action, Command):**
   - The Captain coordinates the vessel's gunners and ballista crews.
   - All ship-mounted siege weapons fired before the end of the turn gain a **+2 status bonus to attack rolls** or bypass **5 Hardness** on enemy hull plating.
3. **Rigging Grapple & Boarding Draw (1 Action, Tactician):**
   - Directs the launching of pneumatic harpoons, magnetic clamps, or grappling hooks to lock onto an enemy vessel within 60 feet.
   - Draws the two ships 15 feet closer per round, creating gangplank bridges for boarding parties.
4. **Emergency Propulsion Surge (1/Encounter, Free Action):**
   - Bypasses engine governor valves or drops extra canvas, immediately increasing the vessel's tactical movement by **+15 feet** for 1 round.

## 3. The Four Vessel Command Specializations

Every NPC Sky Captain is designated with one of four primary masteries:

| Specialization | Vessel Types Commanded | Signature Command Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Tall Sail Master** | Surface Schooners, Canvas Cutters | **Wind-Reading Mastery:** Trims sails as 1 action instead of 2. Ignores wet/tilting difficult terrain. Crew cannot be swept overboard by wind. |
| **2. Geared Steam & Clockwork** | Paddleboats, Ironclad Steamers, Tenders | **Pressure Overcharge:** Can force a steam burst granting an extra ship maneuver action, taking 10-minute field repair checks to prevent boiler strain. |
| **3. Agile Zone Aeronaut** | Corsair Zeppelins, Scout Skyships, Cloud Skimmers | **Thermal Squall Surfing:** Operates at uniform Zone altitude (20–150 ft); grants +2 DC against enemy ramming and can execute slipstream banking turns. |
| **4. Spire Capital War Craft** | Dreadnought Air Galleons, Royal Navy Flagships | **Siege Battery Coordination:** Directs multi-deck batteries. Crew gains resistance to shipboard fires and morale collapse. |

## 4. NPC Statblock Profiles (Hiring & Encounter Tiers)

### Tier 1: Merchant Cargo Skipper (Level 3 NPC)
* **Role:** Standard merchant freighter pilot; hired for routine Spire-to-Spire cargo runs.
* **HP:** 38 (Deck) / 26 (On Foot) | **AC:** 19 (Deck) / 16 (On Foot)
* **Skills:** Airship Lore +10, Sailing Lore +10, Acrobatics +9, Diplomacy +7.
* **Weapons:** Cutlass (+9, 1d6+3 S, Agile), Flintlock/Cartridge Pistol (+9, 1d6+1 P, Concussive).
* **Hire Cost:** 10 to 25 shillings per flight leg + standard cargo insurance.

### Tier 2: Zone Corsair Captain (Level 7 NPC)
* **Role:** Ruthless aerial raider, pirate commander, or mercenary privateer holding a Crown *Letter of Marque*.
* **HP:** 92 (Deck) / 60 (On Foot) | **AC:** 25 (Deck) / 21 (On Foot)
* **Skills:** Airship Lore +16, Intimidation +15, Acrobatics +15, Athletics +13.
* **Weapons:** Master Dueling Saber (+17, 2d6+5 S, Deadly d8), Twin Heavy Pistols (+16, 2d6+3 P).
* **Tactics:** Aggressively grapples and boards, using *Broadside Battery Cadence* followed by leading the boarding charge with *Deck Panache*.

### Tier 3: Royal Sky Navy Commodore (Level 12 NPC)
* **Role:** Flag officer commanding Spire battle fleets, aerial blockades, or massive war dreadnoughts.
* **HP:** 180 (Deck) / 120 (On Foot) | **AC:** 32 (Deck) / 27 (On Foot)
* **Skills:** Military Warfare Lore +24, Airship Lore +24, Society +22, Diplomacy +22.
* **Command Power:** Directs massive battery barrages (3d10 siege artillery) and coordinates entire flotillas during naval blockade actions.

## 5. PC Aeronaut Dedication (Optional Archetype)
*For player characters (such as Officers, Scouts, or Engineers) who want to pilot ships without giving up their core class:*
- **Aeronaut Dedication (Feat 2):** Gain trained proficiency in *Airship Lore* or *Sailing Lore*. While aboard a vessel, gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Acrobatics and Athletics checks, and gain access to the *Evasive Trim* and *Broadside Cadence* ship actions.
