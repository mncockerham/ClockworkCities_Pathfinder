# Professional (NPC Class)

- **Role:** Non-Player Character (Educated Middle / Upper-Middle Class)
- **Location:** `ClockworkPathfinder/Classes/NPC/Professional.md`
- **Typical Traits:** Human, Civilized, Protected (often sheltered from physical violence)
- **Status Tier:** Status 3 (Senior Clerk, Apothecary, Professor) to Status 5 (Chief Barrister, University Dean, Diplomat)
- **Design Intent:** Represents the specialized non-combatant thinkers who run the legal, administrative, academic, and diplomatic apparatus of the spires. They crumble quickly in violent shootouts or trench warfare, but possess formidable institutional power, legal defenses, and bureaucratic influence.

---

## 1. Setting Overview & In-World Identity

Professionals inhabit the libraries, courtrooms, corporate chambers, bank vaults, and embassy salons of the higher circles.
- **Accountants & Actuaries:** Keep the ledgers of trade houses, track debt amortization, and calculate risk for airship voyages.
- **Barristers & Advocates:** Navigate the labyrinth of Ward Charters, aristocratic privileges, and municipal codes; defend clients in spire tribunals.
- **University Professors & Teachers:** Instruct the youth of the elite classes in history, stoichiometry, mathematics, and philosophy.
- **Diplomats & Envoys:** Manage fragile truces between Great Spires, negotiate food-tariff rates, and draft trade agreements.

---

## 2. Baseline Mechanics & Stat Block Profile

- **Hit Points:** 6 + Con modifier per level (or +1 flat HP/level if tagged `[Protected]`, +2 if `[Civilized]`).
- **Key Attributes:** Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma (+3 or +4), Dexterity (+0 or +1), Strength (–1 to +0), Constitution (–1 to +0).
- **Perception:** Expert (sharp eye for lies, forged signatures, and social subtext).
- **Saving Throws:** 
  - **Fortitude:** Trained (fragile constitution; sheltered indoor life).
  - **Reflex:** Trained.
  - **Will:** Expert (trained debate, mental discipline, and rhetoric).
- **Armor & Defense:** Trained in Unarmored Defense only. Untrained in all armor (wearing armor imposes Clumsy condition).
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Trained in Simple Daggers and Dueling Canes only. Untrained in all other weapons and firearms.

---

## 3. Core NPC Features

### Institutional Expertise & Lore Mastery
A Professional is trained (or Expert/Master depending on level) in two Lore skills related to their profession (e.g., *Legal Lore*, *Accounting Lore*, *University Lore*, *Heraldry Lore*, or *Diplomatic Lore*) and *Society*. They gain a +2 circumstance bonus when dealing with guild charters, court filings, or municipal contracts.

### Professional Instrument AP Discount
When activating diagnostic, calculation, or writing apparatuses (such as mechanical arithmometers, acoustic speaking tubes, or forensic magnification lenses), the Professional gains a **1-point AP discount (minimum 1 AP)**.

### Bureaucratic Shield ([one-action], Auditory, Mental)
The Professional cites legal precedent, municipal ordinances, or status authority at an aggressor. Target enemy within 30 feet who can understand them must succeed at a Will save against the Professional's Will DC or take a –1 circumstance penalty to attack rolls against the Professional for 1 round (or hesitate, choosing another target).

---

## 4. Optional Professional Specialty Talents

*GMs can give an individual Professional 1 or 2 of these talents based on their specific profession.*

- **Courtroom Advocate (Barrister):** Gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Diplomacy checks when presenting evidence or negotiating bail and fines. Once per day, they can delay an arrest warrant or legal seizure by 24 hours via judicial stay.
- **Forensic Auditor (Accountant):** In 10 minutes of examining financial records or cargo manifests, they automatically identify discrepancies, concealed embezzlement, or tax avoidance.
- **University Lecturer (Teacher):** By giving 1 minute of instruction, they grant an ally within earshot a +1 circumstance bonus to their next Recall Knowledge check within the next hour.
- **Diplomatic Immunity (Diplomat):** Striking or arresting this NPC within a civilized ward without approval from the High Council or Magistrate is a severe crime (Infamy +2), prompting immediate constabulary investigation.
- **Panic & Cower ([reaction]):** *Trigger:* An enemy attacks the Professional or fires a weapon within 30 feet. *Effect:* The Professional drops Prone and cowers behind the nearest furniture or ally, gaining Lesser Cover (+1 AC/Reflex) against ranged attacks.
