## 1. Setting Rationale & Selection Criteria

In the Clockwork Cities setting, supernatural magic does not exist. All traditional magical concepts have been removed or translated into physical mechanical apparatuses, Neither-harvesting technologies, or biochemical concoctions. Furthermore, societal survival in vertical spires and toxic Neither-filled lowlands places supreme emphasis on social standing, guild backed specialization, martial discipline, forensic investigation, and industrial/trade expertise.

To determine which Pathfinder 2e Remaster (and core legacy) archetypes are available, we apply the exact same criteria used for the 16 core setting classes:

1. **Non-Magical / Physical Trait:** Archetypes must rely on martial drill, athletic prowess, secular trade skills, forensic deduction, or mundane survival techniques.
2. **Magical Archetypes Removed:** Purely magical archetypes (*Wizard, Cleric, Druid, Bard, Sorcerer, Witch, Oracle, Eldritch Archer, Runesmith, Ritualist, Blessed One, Living Monolith*, etc.) are **Removed**.
3. **Setting Compatibility & Reflavoring:** Archetypes dealing with beasts, traps, dual weapons, acrobatics, armor, dueling, and criminal or detective trades fit seamlessly into spire life (skyship crews, arena pits, constable squads, aristocratic salons, deep mines).
4. **Multiclass Archetypes for Clockwork Classes:** Each of our 16 setting classes possesses its own multiclass archetype dedication per the rules in their respective class documents (e.g., *Soldier Dedication, Clockwork Engineer Dedication, Chirurgeon Dedication*).

### D. Explicitly Removed Archetypes

The following Pathfinder archetypes are **strictly prohibited** because they rely on supernatural spellcasting, divine deities, extraplanar spirits, or magical traditions:

- **All Spellcaster Multiclasses:** *Bard, Cleric, Druid, Oracle, Sorcerer, Witch, Wizard, Magus, Summoner, Psychic.*
- **Supernatural / Planar Archetypes:** *Blessed One, Eldritch Archer, Elementalist, Ghost, Ghost Hunter (G&G), Ghoul, Halcyon Speaker, Living Monolith, Mind Smith, Pactbinder, Ritualist, Runesmith, Shadowdancer, Soulforger, Undead Slayer.*
- **Magical Transformation / Aberrant:** Any archetype granting supernatural claws, planar teleportation, or divine healing hands.
