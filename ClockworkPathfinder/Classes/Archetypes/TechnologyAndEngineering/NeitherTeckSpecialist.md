# NeitherTeck Specialist Archetype

- **Base System:** Pathfinder 2e Remaster `[Player Core p.218–221]`
- **Location:** `ClockworkPathfinder/Classes/Archetypes/TechnologyAndEngineering/NeitherTeckSpecialist.md`
- **Precedence:** Rules in `AISummary/00_RulesFoundation.md`, `AISummary/10_Magic.md`, and `ClockworkPathfinder/Rules/NeitherTeck/NeitherTeckRules.md`.
- **Availability:** Any non-Type 4 class seeking to expand personal NeitherTeck tuning capacity (or Type 4 classes diversifying feats).

---

## 1. Overview & Conceptual Role

*Characters from all walks of life—line soldiers adapting scavenged capacitor carbines, street rogues wiring illegal pulse triggers, or airship deck officers balancing inductive receivers—can study the exacting principles of NeitherTeck calibration.*

This archetype allows any class to open up high-end NeitherTeck operations. 
- **Dedication at 4th Level:** Grants an immediate **+1 Activation Point (AP)** boost to your round-by-round capacity and opens access to specialized tuning feats.
- **Access to Higher Calibration Feats:** Grants access to the **8th, 12th, and 18th level** calibration feats shared with Artisans and Scholars.
- **Unique Purchase Constraint:** Feats sharing the same calibration tier (*NeitherTeck Advanced Calibration*, *Master Calibration*, *Supreme Calibration*) can **only be purchased once** across a character's entire build (taking it via your base class prevents taking it via this archetype, and vice versa).

---

## 2. Archetype Feats

### Level 4
#### NeitherTeck Specialist Dedication — Feat 4
- **Traits:** Archetype, Dedication
- **Prerequisites:** Intelligence +1 or trained in Crafting.
- **Benefit:** You master the operational balance of Neither-conductive mechanisms, pressure valves, and inductive coils. Your per-round Activation Point (AP) pool permanently increases by **+1 AP**. In addition, you become trained in Crafting (or another Lore skill if already trained in Crafting).
- **Special:** You cannot select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the NeitherTeck Specialist archetype.

---

### Level 8
#### NeitherTeck Advanced Calibration — Feat 8
- **Traits:** Archetype
- **Prerequisites:** *NeitherTeck Specialist Dedication* (or *NeitherTeck Tuning* from a base class).
- **Benefit:** You calibrate micro-escapements, thermal dissipation channels, and catalytic fluid balance with flawless precision. Your per-round Activation Point (AP) pool permanently increases by **+2 AP**.
- **Special:** You can only take *NeitherTeck Advanced Calibration* once across your character build.

---

### Level 12
#### NeitherTeck Master Calibration — Feat 12
- **Traits:** Archetype
- **Prerequisites:** *NeitherTeck Advanced Calibration*.
- **Benefit:** Your technical fine-tuning reaches master tolerances, effortlessly buffering back-EMF spikes and pressure surges during high-stress maneuvers. Your per-round Activation Point (AP) pool permanently increases by **+2 AP**.
- **Special:** You can only take *NeitherTeck Master Calibration* once across your character build.

---

### Level 18
#### NeitherTeck Supreme Calibration — Feat 18
- **Traits:** Archetype
- **Prerequisites:** *NeitherTeck Master Calibration*.
- **Benefit:** You achieve peak synchronization with resonant atmospheric fields, operating heavy inductive arrays and high-capacity capacitors with effortless cognitive flow. Your per-round Activation Point (AP) pool permanently increases by **+2 AP**.
- **Special:** You can only take *NeitherTeck Supreme Calibration* once across your character build.
