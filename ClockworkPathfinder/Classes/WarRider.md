# WarRider (Spire Cavalier)

- **Base Class:** Cavalier `[Player Core 2 p.200–201]`, Beastmaster `[Player Core 2 p.199]`, & Fighter/Ranger Mounted Chassis `[Player Core p.136, p.152, p.429–430]`
- **Status:** **New** (Dedicated **Tier 0 / Lowest AP** Mounted Combat & Strata-Steed Class)
- **Availability:** **Primary PC Class** (Full 1–20 progression)
- **Discipline / Trait:** Martial (Mounted Warfare, Cavalry Charges, Mobile Skirmishing & Bonded War-Mounts)
- **Active Surge AP Tier:** **Tier 0 (Dedicated Martials — Lowest AP: +0 Base AP at Lv 1, +1 AP at Levels 10 & 20)**

---

## 1. Setting Overview
*Known in the high plazas and royal regiments of New Avalon as the **Cavalier** or **Royal Dragoon**, and feared across the frontier badlands and Lower Neither wastes as the **WarRider**.*

- **Dual Cultural Identity (Spire Cavalier vs. Outland WarRider):**
  - **In the Great Spires — The Cavalier / Royal Dragoon:** Among the 25 Great Houses, Wall Merchant dynasties (Status 65–95), and the Crown Cavalry, maintaining a stable of purebred Spire Warhorses is one of the ultimate symbols of wealth and martial prestige (`[WorldSummary.md §7]`). Spire Cavaliers lead armored perimeter patrols, escort noble land-carriages between satellite shires, and charge down enemy infantry lines with lance and sabre.
  - **On the Frontier & In the Wilds — The Outrider / Pony Express Marshal:** Because surface communication between a Spire and a border fort 120 miles away relies on a grueling **6-day mounted relay** across hazardous roads (`[WorldSummary.md §6]`), frontier WarRiders are the lifeline of civilization—riding hardy Outland Mustangs and draft mules through Neither squalls with bow, crossbow, or carbine in hand.
  - **Among Savage Orc Warbands & Deep Clans — The Boar-Rider / Beast-Cavalry:** In the dense Lower Neither (Strata 4) and lightless caverns (Strata 5), where surface horses choke without breather-bags, Orc **WarRiders** thunder into battle astride massive **Neither-Hardened Dire Boars (Tuskers)** and **Wasteland Dire-Wolves**, while Dwarven tunnel-riders mount sure-footed **Subterranean Cave-Rams** and **Monitor Lizards**.
- **Physical / Scientific Explanation & The 0-AP Advantage:**
  - **100% Non-AP-Dependent (Tier 0 Lowest AP):** Like the **[Soldier](file:///c:/Users/Mark/OneDrive/Documents/Git/ClockworkCities_Pathfinder-1/ClockworkPathfinder/Classes/Soldier.md)** and **[Marksman](file:///c:/Users/Mark/OneDrive/Documents/Git/ClockworkCities_Pathfinder-1/ClockworkPathfinder/Classes/Marksman.md)**, the **WarRider** is a **Tier 0 Dedicated Martial** (`+0 Base AP` at Lv 1, `+1 AP` at Lv 10 & 20). Every WarRider class feature, mount command, trampling charge, and feat costs **0 AP** and requires zero clockwork technology.

### A. The Three Rider's Doctrines (Choose One at 1st Level)

#### 1. Doctrine of the Spire Cavalier (Heavy Lancer, Dragoon & Cuirassier)
*You are an armored shock-trooper of the Spire regiments, noble tournaments, or heavy vanguard—crushing lines with lance, sabre, and barded warhorse.*
- **Favored Weapons (+1 Proficiency Tier While Mounted):** Lances, spears, polearms, swords (sabres/broadswords), hammers, and flails.
- **1st Level — Heavy Barding, Shield Block & One-Handed Lance:**
  - You become **Trained in Heavy Armor** (scaling to Expert at Lv 11 and Master at Lv 17) and gain the **Shield Block** general feat. Your Bonded War-Mount is also trained in **Heavy Barding**.
  - **Saddle-Braced Polearms:** While mounted, you can wield any two-handed **Spear or Polearm** (such as a halberd, longspear, or pike) in **one hand**, and it gains the **Jousting d8** trait (adding **+1 circumstance bonus to damage per weapon damage die** when your mount moves at least 10 feet before your Strike; increases to **+2 per die** with a dedicated Lance!).
  - **Mounted Shield (`[PC2 p.201]`):** When you **Raise a Shield** while mounted, both you AND your War-Mount gain your shield's circumstance bonus to AC, and you can use *Shield Block* to prevent damage to your mount as well as yourself!
- **9th Level — Unstoppable Shock Charge:** When you use *Cavalcade Charge* and hit with a melee Strike, you can attempt a free **Shove** or **Trip** (using your Athletics modifier, ignoring free-hand requirements) against the target with no Multiple Attack Penalty.
- **15th Level — Cuirassier's Bastion:** While mounted, both you and your War-Mount gain **Physical Resistance equal to half your level** against ranged attacks and Reactive Strikes triggered by your movement.

#### 2. Doctrine of the Outland Outrider (Mounted Archer, Crossbowman & Carbineer)
*You are a lightning-fast frontier courier, border marshal, or Orc wolf-archer who unleashes dead-eye volleys from a galloping saddle.*
- **Favored Weapons (+1 Proficiency Tier While Mounted):** All Bows, Crossbows, Firearms/Carbines, and Thrown weapons.
- **1st Level — Gyro-Saddle Aim & Galloping Reload `[one-action]`:**
  - **Steady Stirrups:** While mounted, you ignore the **first range increment penalty** on all ranged weapons, and your ranged Strikes never take penalties from your mount's movement.
  - **Galloping Reload `[one-action]`:** In a single action, you **Command your War-Mount to Stride once** AND perform **1 Interact action to reload** a crossbow/firearm (or draw a thrown weapon / nock a trick arrow).
- **9th Level — Parthian Shot:** When you use *Cavalcade Charge* with a ranged weapon, your mount's movement **does not trigger Reactive Strikes** from the target of your shot, and on a hit, you deal an extra **1d6 precision damage** (2d6 on a crit).
- **15th Level — Wind-Runner Volley:** Whenever your mount moves at least 20 feet on your turn, you are **Quickened** for that turn and can use the extra action exclusively to make a **Ranged Strike** or **Interact to Reload**.

#### 3. Doctrine of the Warband Beast-Rider (Savage Boar/Wolf Raider & Pack Harrier)
*Favored by Savage Orc warbands, wasteland reivers, and subterranean tunnel-raiders whose mounts are ferocious predators in their own right.*
- **Favored Weapons (+1 Proficiency Tier While Mounted):** Axes, picks, clubs/mauls, brawling/unarmed attacks, and bows/thrown axes.
- **1st Level — Independent Jaws/Tusks & Saddle Flanking:**
  - **Uncoupled First Bite/Gore:** Normally in PF2e (`[PC1 p.429]`), a rider and mount share a Multiple Attack Penalty (MAP). Under this Doctrine, **your Bonded War-Mount's first Strike each round does NOT count toward your MAP, and your Strikes do not apply MAP to your mount's first Strike each round!**
  - **Mounted Pack Flanking:** Whenever you and your War-Mount are adjacent to an enemy, that enemy is treated as **Flanked (*Off-Guard*)** by both you and your mount—allowing you and your Dire-Boar or Dire-Wolf to tear apart isolated targets together!
- **9th Level — Gore & Cleave `[two-actions]`:** Command your mount to Stride and make a melee Strike (Tusk/Jaws/Horn), and you simultaneously make a melee Strike against the same target. Both Strikes use your current MAP (0 penalty on first attack!), and if both hit, combine their damage for overcoming resistances and inflict **1d6 persistent bleed**.
- **15th Level — Apex Predator Frenzy:** Whenever you or your War-Mount reduces an enemy to 0 HP or scores a critical hit, both you and your mount gain **Temporary Hit Points equal to your Level** and your mount can immediately **Stride up to half its Speed** as a free action.

> **Mount Hit Points, Morale, Healing & Replacement (`[mounts.md §2 & §8]`, `[Skills.md]`):**
> - **Simple Animal HP Pools & 0 HP Total Destruction:** All mounts (biological and clockwork) use **normal single Hit Point pools for animals** (no 3-Step Wound Thresholds) and wear barding for AC. At **`0 HP`**, a biological mount dies and a Clockwork Horse is **totally destroyed (reduced to unrepairable scrap)**; the rider makes a **0-action DC 15 Riding (or Reflex) check** to land on their feet in an adjacent square (falling **Prone** and taking `1d6` bludgeoning damage on a failure).
> - **Morale (Bolting vs. Fighting to the Death):** Once a **non-war-trained mount** takes **50% or more damage**, the rider must succeed at a **0-action DC 20 Riding check every round in combat** (never costs the PC an action) to prevent it from bolting. **All War-Trained mounts (including all Bonded War-Mounts) will not bolt and will fight to the death (0 HP).** **Clockwork Horses never bolt.**
> - **Unified Riding Rules:** Clockwork Horses work identically to normal mounts for all game rules governing riding, getting on/off, and special mounted maneuvers.
> - **Issued Mounts, Healing & Replacement:** Living mounts, Clockwork Horses, and all saddles/barding/modules have **no upkeep costs**—they are **issued and 100% covered by the issuing group**. Biological mounts heal using standard living creature rules (`[20_Healing.md]`), while damaged (`> 0 HP`) Clockwork Horses are repaired via Crafting (`DC 15` in an equipped shop for $\text{Roll} - 15$ HP; `1 hour, DC 20` in the field for up to `10 HP/hr`). If a Bonded War-Mount is slain or scrapped in battle, the WarRider is issued and bonds a replacement mount with **2 days of downtime** in any settlement, frontier camp, or warband corral.

## 3. Quick Reference: Mounted Combat Rules (`[Player Core p.429–430]`, `[Skills.md]`)

1. **Mounting & Dismounting (`[one-action]` Move):** Spend 1 Move action while adjacent to your mount to straddle the saddle, or 1 Move action to dismount into an adjacent square (*Quick Mount* feat compresses this to a free action or Mount + Command in 1 action).
2. **Shared Initiative & Space:** Your mount acts on your turn. You occupy all squares of your Large mount's **10×10 ft space** simultaneously, meaning your melee reach is measured from **any edge of your mount's space**!
3. **Commanding Your Mount (`[one-action]`):**
   - **With a WarRider's Bonded War-Mount:** Automatic success (no check needed). Spending **1 Action `[one-action]`** grants your mount **2 Actions** (3 Actions at Lv 19). If you spend **0 Actions** commanding your mount, **Instinctive Spur** still gives your mount **1 free Action** to Stride or Strike (from Lv 4; Stride/Step at Lv 1–3)!
   - **With an Ordinary Non-Companion Mount:** Controlled via the **Riding** skill (`[Skills.md]`), and you share a Multiple Attack Penalty. If the mount is **non-war-trained** and takes **50% or more damage**, you must succeed at a **DC 20 Riding check every round in combat** to prevent it from bolting.

## 5. Multiclass Archetype (WarRider / Cavalier Dedication)

*Allows any character (**Soldier**, **Marksman**, **Officer**, **Scout**, or **Noble**) to acquire a scaling Strata-Adapted War-Mount and Cavalier maneuvers (`[Player Core 2 p.200–201]`).*

- **WarRider / Cavalier Dedication — Feat 2 `[PC2 p.200]`:**
  - **Prerequisites:** Trained in *Riding*, *Aither Nature*, *Survival*, or *Athletics*.
  - **Benefits:** You become **Trained in Riding** (or **Expert in Riding** if you were already Trained), and you unlock the ability to advance **Riding up to Master** (at Level 7+; standard classes without this archetype cap at Expert in Riding). You also gain a **Young Bonded War-Mount** selected from Section 2.B (with the *Mount* trait). When you spend 1 Action `[one-action]` to Command your mount, it gains 2 actions.
- **Cavalcade Charge (Cavalier's Charge) — Feat 4 `[PC2 p.200]`:** Gain the *Cavalcade Charge* `[two-actions]` action (Mount Strides twice + you Strike with +1 to hit).
- **Impressive Mount — Feat 4 `[PC2 p.200]`:** Your War-Mount advances to a **Mature Animal Companion** (gaining 1 free Stride/Strike action even on turns you don't Command it).
- **Basic Rider's Maneuver — Feat 4:** Gain a 1st- or 2nd-level WarRider feat (*Quick Mount*, *Mounted Shield*, *Defend Mount*, etc.).
- **Incredible Mount — Feat 8 `[PC2 p.201]`:** Your War-Mount advances to a **Nimble** or **Savage** Animal Companion.
- **Specialized Mount — Feat 14 `[PC2 p.201]`:** Your War-Mount gains a **Specialization**.
