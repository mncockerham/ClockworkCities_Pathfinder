# Pugilist

- **Base Class:** Monk `[Player Core 2 p.140]`
- **Status:** **Modified** (1-to-1 baseline with Pathfinder 2e Remaster Monk; mystical ki magic removed; reflavored around prize-ring boxing, technical footwork, and street thug brutality)
- **Availability:** **Primary PC Class** (Full 1–20 progression)
- **Discipline / Trait:** Martial (Unarmed Brawling, Technical Boxing & Street Infighting)

---

## 1. Setting Overview
*The prize-ring boxers, underground champions, and ruthless street thugs of the spire underworld.*

- **In-World Identity:** Bare-knuckle prize-fighters in the illegal gambling rings of Ward 13's Warrens, dockside street thugs and extortion enforcers, tavern brawlers, or disciplined academy pugilists teaching the noble art of self-defense. They rely on hand-wraps, brass-weighted knuckles, bobbing-and-weaving footwork, and devastating kinetic punching rather than swords or firearms.
- **Typical Social Class & Backing:** Laborer (Status 1), Warrens resident / Street Thug (Status 0), Citizen (Status 1–3), or prizefighter sponsored by a gambling syndicate or wealthy noble patron.
- **Physical / Scientific Explanation:** Rigorous cardiovascular conditioning, conditioned bone density (calloused knuckles, hardened forearms), tactical leverage, rapid-combination muscle memory, and slipping blows at close quarters without relying on heavy armor.

### The Savage Combatant Feat Line (Levels 2, 4 & 8 — Replaces Standalone Brute Class)
*Shared with the **Soldier** class (`[Soldier.md]`). Represents pit fighters, Warrens street enforcers, and brutal bare-knuckle brawlers who trade technical poise and social grace for raw adrenaline fury.*

- **Savage Combatant — Feat 2 (Pugilist, Soldier):**
  - **Action:** 1 Action (Concentrate, Emotion, Mental). **Duration:** 1 minute, until no enemies remain, or until you fall unconscious (cooldown: 1 minute between uses).
  - **Adrenaline Surge (Lower-Impact Rage):**
    - Gain **temporary Hit Points equal to $\lfloor \text{Half your Level} \rfloor + \text{Constitution modifier}$** (minimum 1).
    - Gain a **+1 status bonus to melee weapon and unarmed damage rolls** (or **+2** with two-handed melee weapons).
    - Take a **–1 status penalty to AC**.
  - **Strict Ranged & Concentration Restrictions:**
    - **No Ranged Weapon Attacks:** While in a Savage state, **you cannot make Ranged Weapon Strikes** (bows, crossbows, firearms, or energy emitters; thrown melee items within 20 ft are permitted).
    - You cannot use actions with the **Concentrate** trait unless they are **Seek** or **Intimidate (Demoralize / Coerce)**.
  - **Severe Social Penalty (Except Intimidate):**
    - While in a Savage state—and for **10 minutes afterward** while your adrenaline subsides—you take a **–2 status penalty to all social interaction checks and Understanding checks** (*Diplomacy, Deception, Performance, Society, and calling upon polite Favors*), **except for Intimidation**, which gains a **+1 status bonus**.

- **Relentless Savagery — Feat 4 (Pugilist, Soldier):**
  - **Prerequisites:** *Savage Combatant*.
  - **Benefits:**
    - Your Savage Combatant melee/unarmed damage bonus increases to **+2** (or **+3** with two-handed melee weapons).
    - Gain a **+1 status bonus to Athletics checks** to **Grapple, Shove, Reposition, or Trip** while Savage.
    - When you enter *Savage Combatant* (1 Action), you may immediately **Stride** up to half your Speed or **Step** as a free action.

- **Savage Onslaught — Feat 8 (Pugilist, Soldier):**
  - **Prerequisites:** *Relentless Savagery*.
  - **Benefits:**
    - Your temporary Hit Points when entering *Savage Combatant* increase to **$\text{Your Level} + \text{Constitution modifier}$**.
    - While Savage, gain **physical Resistance equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum 1) against Bludgeoning damage and all Nonlethal damage**.
    - Your melee/unarmed damage bonus scales to **+3** (**+4** with two-handed melee weapons), and your status bonus to **Intimidation (Demoralize)** increases to **+2** while Savage.

## 4. Spells & Apparatus (If Applicable)
*Not applicable.* The Pugilist does not cast spells, use ki magic, or maintain clockwork/concoction slots. All techniques are purely physical manifestations of pugilistic discipline, conditioned reflexes, and kinetic impact.

## 5. Multiclass Archetype (Dedication)
*Characters of other classes can gain training in the Pugilist (Monk) archetype [Player Core 2 p.220].*

- **Pugilist Dedication (Feat 2):** `[Player Core 2 p.220]`  
  - **Prerequisites:** Strength 14 (+2) or Dexterity 14 (+2).
  - **Benefits:** Trained in unarmed attacks and unarmored defense; trained in Athletics or Acrobatics; trained in Pugilist class DC. Gain Powerful Fist (1d6 unarmed strike damage).
- **Basic Brawling (Feat 4):** Gain a 1st- or 2nd-level Pugilist feat `[Player Core 2 p.220]`.
- **Pugilist's Resiliency (Feat 4):** Gain 3 additional HP for each Pugilist archetype feat taken `[Player Core 2 p.220]`.
- **Monk's Flurry / Pugilist's Flurry (Feat 10):** Gain the Flurry of Blows action `[Player Core 2 p.220]`.
- **Advanced Brawling (Feat 6):** Gain a Pugilist feat up to half your character level `[Player Core 2 p.220]`.
- **Perfection of Movement (Feat 8):** Gain Incredible Movement bonus (+5 ft, scaling to +10 ft) `[Player Core 2 p.220]`.
