# Artisan (Craftsman)

- **Base Class:** Modified Hybrid (Investigator / Rogue chassis re-engineered for Material Expertise, Trade Tools, and Guild Mastery) `[Player Core p.166, Player Core 2 p.40]`
- **Status:** **New / Replaced**
- **Availability:** **Primary PC Class** (Full 1–20 progression)
- **Discipline / Trait:** Skill / Material Crafting / Trade Guild

---

## 1. Setting Overview
*The true industrial spine of the Clockwork Cities: master blacksmiths, leather tanners, structural masons, weavers, carpenters, and tailors whose disciplined labor turns Neither-hardened raw materials into the garments, armor, tools, and structures that keep civilization alive.*

- **In-World Identity:** Blacksmiths of the Ward 11 Great Foundries, Master Tanners curing beast-hides in the chemical vat-houses, Ward 13 Master Tailors weaving reinforced layered fabrics, stonecutters shaping Neithercrete foundations, and guild journeymen undertaking trade-caravan contracts.
- **Typical Social Class & Backing:** Guild Members & Artisans (Ward 11 & Ward 13, Status 2–5). Supported by guild halls, legal guild defense, access to wholesale guild material exchanges, and formal voting representation.
- **Physical / Scientific Explanation:** Artisans operate through exhaustive material knowledge, physical stamina honed by years over anvils, looms, and tanning vats, structural flaw exploitation, and rapid equipment conditioning. They do not invent high-concept experimental clockwork devices (the domain of the Clockwork Engineer); instead, they master the physical properties, tolerances, and structural integrity of leather, cloth, steel, bronze, and stone.

> [!TIP]
> **Player Advisory (Combat Utility & NPC Suitability):**
> While fully supported with a complete 1–20 PC progression, the Artisan's primary mechanical strengths lie in downtime fabrication, material conditioning, and equipment maintenance. In active combat, their abilities focus on supporting party gear and improvised tool strikes; players expecting high-mobility martial bursts or dynamic tactical agency may find this playstyle subdued or frustrating in dungeon-crawl and combat-heavy adventures. Consequently, the Artisan frequently serves best as a **key NPC archetype** (master guild smiths, armory factors, and municipal contractors) or for players pursuing industrial/economic campaigns.

### Hit Points & Proficiencies
- **Hit Points:** 10 + Constitution modifier per level. (Rugged manual labor builds resilient constitutions).
- **Perception:** Trained (improves to Expert at 9th level, Master at 17th level).
- **Saving Throws:**
  - Fortitude: Expert (improves to Master at 7th level).
  - Reflex: Trained (improves to Expert at 11th level).
  - Will: Expert (improves to Master at 15th level).
- **Skills:** Trained in Crafting, your Trade Lore, plus 4 + Intelligence modifier additional skills. Gains a Skill Increase and a Skill Feat at **every level** (2nd through 20th), reflecting specialized trade development.
- **Attacks:** Trained in simple weapons, martial hammers, axes, flails, picks, and knives/daggers (the primary tools of tradecraft). Improves to Expert at 5th level, Master at 17th level.
- **Defenses:** Trained in light and medium armor, and unarmored defense. Improves to Expert at 13th level.

### 1st Level Features

#### Artisan's Trade & Specialty
Choose one primary trade tradition. This grants you a signature Lore skill, specialized equipment knowledge, and a passive mechanical bonus:
- **Blacksmith (Forge & Anvil):** Trained in Metallurgy / Blacksmithing Lore. Gain proficiency in heavy armor. Your bludgeoning and piercing weapon strikes gain the *Shove* trait, and metal armor you wear or maintain has its Check Penalty reduced by 1.
- **Tanner & Leatherworker (Vat & Hide):** Trained in Tanning / Leather Lore. Gain resistance equal to half your level (minimum 1) against acid and chemical damage from tanning liquors. You can treat hides and light armors to grant acid/corrosion resistance to allies.
- **Tailor & Weaver (Loom & Needle):** Trained in Textiles / Tailoring Lore. You excel in concealed pockets, ballistics-weave linothorax, and social finery. Garments tailored by you grant wearers a +1 circumstance bonus to Deception (to conceal small items) and Diplomacy checks in high-society circles.
- **Mason & Stonecutter (Chisel & Mortar):** Trained in Masonry / Architecture Lore. You gain the *Hefty Hauler* feat and a +2 circumstance bonus to Recall Knowledge regarding structural integrity, cave-ins, Neithercrete fortification, and stone traps.
- **Carpenter & Wheelwright (Saw & Joinery):** Trained in Carpentry / Carriage Lore. You can repair wooden structures, carts, wagons, and ship timbers in half the standard time, and your strikes against wooden objects or barriers ignore 5 points of Hardness.

#### Material Mastery
You possess an intuitive understanding of physical stresses, shear points, and metallurgical or organic grain. Once per round as a single action ([one-action]), you can assess an enemy's manufactured gear, armor, or natural shell (similar to *Devise a Stratagem* [Player Core 2 p.42]):
- Make a Crafting check against the target's Fortitude DC.
- **Success:** Until the start of your next turn, you know the weakest joint in their defenses. Your next strike deals an additional **1d6 precision damage** (increasing to 2d6 at 5th, 3d6 at 11th, and 4d6 at 17th level), and ignores 2 points of their physical resistance or object Hardness.
- **Critical Success:** As success, but your attack ignores 5 points of resistance/Hardness, and on a hit, the target's armor or primary weapon gains the *Clumsy 1* or *Enfeebled 1* condition until repaired.

#### Tool as Weapon
You have swung sledgehammers, drawn scrapers, shears, and mallets for countless hours. When wielding an implement of your trade (heavy hammers, hatchets, skinning cleavers, picks, shears, chisels, or heavy tongs), it counts as a martial weapon with which you are proficient, gains the *Versatile* (Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing as appropriate) trait, and does not suffer improvised weapon penalties.

#### Reinforced Workmanship
Beginning at 3rd level, during daily preparations, you can condition and tune up to three suits of armor, shields, or weapons for yourself or your party members:
- **Conditioned Armor:** Increases the item bonus to AC by +1 (or increases the maximum Dexterity bonus by +1). (Improves to +2 at 7th level, +3 at 13th level).
- **Conditioned Weapon:** Grants a +1 circumstance bonus to damage rolls, and its bulk is treated as 1 lower (minimum 0).
- **Reinforced Shield:** Increases shield Hardness by +2 and shield HP by +10.

## 3. Artisan Class Feats

### 1st Level
- **Eye for Quality ([one-action]):** You immediately determine the market value, material composition, and structural flaws of an item or barrier upon inspection.
- **Quick Repair [Player Core p.247]:** Gain the *Quick Repair* skill feat. In combat, you can repair an item or fix a jammed Neither firearm/mechanism with 3 actions instead of 1 minute.
- **Durable Gear:** Your personal clothing and armor are reinforced with heavy leather aprons or padded linings. Gain a +1 circumstance bonus to AC against physical critical hits.

### 2nd Level
- **Rapid Fabrication:** You can craft mundane ammunition, nails, bindings, pitons, bandages, or trade tools in a 10-minute exploration activity using scrap materials.
- **Flaw Exploit ([two-actions]):** Make a weapon strike using your trade tool. On a hit, if the target is wearing armor or holding a shield, reduce the AC bonus of that gear by 1 until the end of combat or until repaired.

### 4th Level
- **NeitherTeck Tuning (Artisan):** You master the harmonic calibration of inductors and catalytic seals. Your per-round Activation Point (AP) pool permanently increases by **+2 AP**.
- **Jury-Rigged Barricade ([three-actions]):** Using nearby salvage, rubble, barrels, or timber, you construct a hasty waist-high barricade providing Standard Cover (+2 AC / Reflex) for up to two Medium creatures in a 10-foot line.
- **Treated Protective Linings:** Allies wearing armor conditioned by your *Reinforced Workmanship* gain resistance 3 against fire, acid, or piercing damage (choose one when conditioning during daily preparations).

### 6th Level
- **Breakthrough Strike ([two-actions]):** Strike an obstacle, door, lock, or construct. Double your damage roll before applying Hardness. If the strike destroys the barrier, the sound dazes adjacent foes (DC Fortitude save against your class DC or become Stunned 1).
- **Master's Overhaul:** During a 10-minute rest, you can completely restore all HP to a broken shield or repair damaged equipment for up to four allies simultaneously.

### 8th Level
- **NeitherTeck Advanced Calibration (Artisan):** Prerequisites: *NeitherTeck Tuning*. You calibrate micro-escapements and thermal dissipation channels with flawless precision. Your per-round Activation Point (AP) pool permanently increases by **+2 AP**.
- **Hardened Habit:** Long years of exposure to forge-heat, tanning fumes, sawdust, and stone dust grant you permanent resistance 5 against poison, acid, and heat hazards, and a +2 circumstance bonus to saving throws against inhaled toxins.
- **Armor Sunder ([two-actions]):** Deliver a crushing blow to an opponent's armor. On a hit, the armor becomes broken. If the target is not wearing armor (natural armor or hide), they suffer the *Off-Guard* condition and *Clumsy 1* for 1 minute.

### 10th Level
- **Guild Wholesale Access:** Your guild status allows you to purchase raw materials, industrial Neither-tech components, and standard supplies at a 20% discount across all allied spires and trade circles.
- **Unwavering Discipline:** When subject to mental fatigue or fear effects, you can focus on the rhythmic cadence of manual labor. Once per encounter, you can roll a Crafting check in place of a Will save against mental or emotional effects.

### 12th Level
- **NeitherTeck Master Calibration (Artisan):** Prerequisites: *NeitherTeck Advanced Calibration*. Your mechanical fine-tuning reaches master guild tolerances. Your per-round Activation Point (AP) pool permanently increases by **+2 AP**.

### 14th Level
- **Masterwork Modification:** You permanently upgrade any mundane weapon or armor suit with specialized ergonomics, adding one weapon trait (such as *Agile*, *Deadly d8*, *Trip*, or *Backswing*) or reducing armor Bulk by 1.
- **Indestructible Reinforcement:** Once per day, when an ally within 15 feet would suffer a critical hit while wearing gear conditioned by your *Reinforced Workmanship*, you can use a reaction ([reaction]) to turn the critical hit into a normal hit by having the conditioned gear absorb the structural shock.

### 18th Level
- **NeitherTeck Supreme Calibration (Artisan):** Prerequisites: *NeitherTeck Master Calibration*. You achieve perfect resonance synchronization with municipal and outpost dynamos. Your per-round Activation Point (AP) pool permanently increases by **+2 AP**.
- **Industrial Foreman ([two-actions]):** Direct your allies with the commanding authority of a factory overseer. Up to three allies within 30 feet can immediately use a reaction to Step, Stride, or Interact (such as reloading or drawing an item).
- **Shatter Construct / Armor ([three-actions]):** Strike an object, vehicle, construct, or armored enemy. If you hit, deal triple weapon damage. If the target is a construct or wearing heavy armor, it must succeed on a Fortitude save against your class DC or immediately be destroyed or knocked unconscious.

### 20th Level
- **Magnum Opus:** You construct your lifetime masterpiece: a unique, bespoke suit of armor, weapon, or trade apparatus that defies standard limitations. The item gains legendary properties, including +3 circumstance bonuses to checks, Hardness 20, and grants the wielder an extra reaction every round to use on physical actions.

## 4. Multiclass Artisan Archetype

Characters from other backgrounds (such as a Soldier with blacksmithing training or a Survivor who learned tanning) can take the Artisan Dedication:
- **Artisan Dedication (Feat 2):** Requires Strength or Intelligence +2, Trained in Crafting. You gain trained proficiency in your chosen Trade Specialty Lore, the *Tool as Weapon* feature, and can condition 1 piece of equipment during daily prep (+1 circumstance bonus to damage or AC).
- **Basic Trade Craft (Feat 4):** Gain a 1st- or 2nd-level Artisan feat.
- **Material Insight (Feat 6):** Gain the *Material Mastery* precision strike ability (scales to 1d6 precision damage).
- **Advanced Trade Craft (Feat 8):** Gain an Artisan feat of up to half your character level.
