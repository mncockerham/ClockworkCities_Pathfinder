# Merchant

- **Base Class:** Bard `[Player Core p.94]` (Spellcasting removed; supplemented with non-combat, non-stealth skill progression from Rogue `[Player Core p.168]`)
- **Status:** **Replaced** (Magic completely removed; re-engineered into a social, logistical, and mercantile powerhouse balancing skill mastery, financial incentives, and commercial directives)
- **Availability:** **Primary PC Class** (Full 1–20 progression)
- **Discipline / Trait:** Skill / Social (Commerce, Negotiation, Leadership & Logistics)

---

## 1. Setting Overview
*The financiers, trade factors, caravan masters, and Wall Merchant scions whose coin and contracts move the world.*

- **In-World Identity:** Accredited factors of the Merchant Guilds, independent overland traders running Peasant Path routes, bank underwriters, or scions of the Wall Merchant dynasties owning airship fleets above the Aither line. They wield social leverage, institutional backing, contractual authority, and economic incentives rather than brute steel or volatile tech.
- **Typical Social Class & Backing:** Trader / Lesser Merchant (Status 3–4), Merchant (Status 4), Merchant House (Status 6), or Wall Merchant elite (Status 8). They command substantial liquid wealth, credit lines, cargo manifests, and legal protection backed by the merchant federations.
- **Physical / Scientific Explanation:** Encyclopedic knowledge of trade law, currency valuation, logistics, human psychology, contract enforcement, and motivational leadership. In combat, their "directives" represent tactical coordination, cash incentives, and decisive commands that rally allies and demoralize foes.

## 4. Spells & Apparatus (If Applicable)
*Not applicable.* The Merchant possesses **no magical abilities, no spell slots, and no apparatus**. All directives and abilities are manifestations of social clout, contractual authority, economic leverage, and professional expertise.

---

## 5. Multiclass Archetype (Dedication)
*Characters of other classes can gain training in the Merchant archetype.*

- **Merchant Dedication (Feat 2):**  
  - **Prerequisites:** Charisma 14 (+2) or Intelligence 14 (+2).
  - **Benefits:** Trained in Diplomacy and Society (or another skill if already trained); trained in Merchant class DC. Gain access to the *Lucrative Incentive* Commercial Directive and basic Line of Credit.
- **Basic Directives (Feat 4):** Gain a 1st- or 2nd-level Merchant feat.
- **Merchant's Resiliency (Feat 4):** Gain 3 additional HP for each Merchant archetype feat taken.
- **Skill Expansion (Feat 6):** Gain an additional skill increase and skill feat.
- **Advanced Directives (Feat 6):** Gain a Merchant feat up to half your character level.
- **Expert Negotiator (Feat 12):** Increase proficiency in Diplomacy or Society to Master, and Merchant class DC to Expert.
