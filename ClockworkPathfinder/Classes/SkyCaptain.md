# Sky Captain

- **Base Class:** Swashbuckler `[Player Core 2 p.176]`
- **Status:** **Modified** (1-to-1 baseline with Pathfinder 2e Remaster Swashbuckler; tailored for airship officers, sky privateers, and helmsmen riding the Zone)
- **Availability:** **Primary PC Class** (Full 1–20 progression)
- **Discipline / Trait:** Martial / Skill (Aerial Dueling, Rigging Mobility & Skyship Command)

---

## 1. Setting Overview
*The daring airship commanders, helmsmen, and boarding officers who navigate the lightning storms of the Zone.*

- **In-World Identity:** Commanders of long-range trading airships, sky-fleet naval officers of the Arclean Crown, blimp captains ferry-cargoing across the highlands of Karfcana, or daring privateers operating out of the Sky Docks (800 ft above New Avalon, past the Zone line). They thrive on moving decks, high-altitude rigging, and aerial broadsides.
- **Typical Social Class & Backing:** Wall Merchant ship commander (Status 4–6), Royal Sky Navy Officer (Status 4), Merchant House captain (Status 4), or freelance skyship privateer (Status 2–4).
- **Physical / Scientific Explanation:** Exceptional balance on pitching deck-plates, mastery of airship winches and grapnels, reading high-voltage Zone thermal winds, and executing high-velocity boarding actions with saber, cutlass, and pistol.

## 5. Multiclass Archetype (Dedication)
*Characters of other classes can gain training in the Sky Captain archetype.*

- **Sky Captain Dedication (Feat 2):** `[Player Core 2 p.220]`  
  - **Prerequisites:** Dexterity 14 (+2) or Strength 14 (+2), Charisma 14 (+2).
  - **Benefits:** Trained in Lore (Airships) or Lore (Zone Navigation); trained in Acrobatics or Athletics; trained in Sky Captain class DC. Gain Aerial Panache.
- **Basic Aeronaut (Feat 4):** Gain a 1st- or 2nd-level Sky Captain feat.
- **Sky Captain's Resiliency (Feat 4):** Gain 3 additional HP for each Sky Captain archetype feat taken.
- **Boarding Finisher (Feat 4):** Gain the Confident Finisher action.
- **Advanced Aeronaut (Feat 6):** Gain a Sky Captain feat up to half your character level.
