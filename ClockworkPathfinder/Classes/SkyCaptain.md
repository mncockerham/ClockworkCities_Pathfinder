# Captain (Shipmaster & Aeronaut)

- **Base Class:** Swashbuckler `[Player Core 2 p.176]`
- **Status:** **Modified** (1-to-1 baseline with Pathfinder 2e Remaster Swashbuckler; expanded for maritime sea captains, steam/clockwork skippers, and Zone airship commanders)
- **Availability:** **Primary PC Class** (Full 1–20 progression)
- **Discipline / Trait:** Martial / Skill (Naval & Aerial Dueling, Rigging Mobility & Vessel Command)

---

## 1. Setting Overview
*The bold shipmasters, helmsmen, corsairs, and naval commanders who sail the sea lanes and ride the lightning storms of the Zone.*

- **In-World Identity:** Commanders of Wall Merchant cargo zeppelins, Royal Navy sea captains sailing the Inner Sea out of the New Avalon Docks (Ward 15), privateer corsairs raiding coastal routes, steam-paddleboat skippers, and high-altitude blockade runners. Whether commanding wooden sea galleons or metal-framed airships, they live and die by deck discipline, naval artillery, and swift boarding actions.
- **Typical Social Class & Backing:** Wall Merchant fleet captain (Status 4–6), Royal Navy / Sky Fleet Commissioned Officer (Status 4), Merchant House cargo skipper (Status 4), or independent sea/sky privateer (Status 2–4).
- **Physical / Scientific Explanation:** Flawless balance on rolling deck-plates, reading tide swells and Zone thermal squalls, coordinating multi-crew gun batteries, and leading close-quarters boarding clashes with saber, cutlass, boarding axe, and pistol.
- **The Tactical Nature of Vessels in Clockwork Cities:** 
  - **Zone Airships:** Bound to the uniform buoyancy plane of the Zone (20–150 ft), all airships naturally float at the exact same altitude in any given location. Cloud swells over distance are gentler than ocean waves, preventing vertical "bombing from above" and locking aerial warfare into tactical **2-dimensional naval maneuvering**.
  - **Sea Vessels:** Traverse the Neither-contaminated surface waters between spires and satellite islands, relying on tall canvas sails, clockwork-wound paddle wheels, or closed-cycle boilers where Neither conditions permit.
  - **From 1860s Trade Lanes to the Golden Age of Piracy (1650–1730) & Sudden Blockades:** Within sight of the Great Spires and along major patrolled routes, sea and Zone shipping operates under **1860s Victorian maritime order** (customs cutters, naval escorts, and regulated convoys). Once out of sight of the Spires and patrolled lanes, however, both the seas and the Zone shift directly into the **Golden Age of Piracy (1650–1730)**—complete with pirate havens, broadside duels, and **rival kingdoms funding privateers (*Letters of Marque*) who operate openly among legitimate merchants** before seizing rival vessels in open skies or waters. Furthermore, **militaries from different kingdoms periodically erupt into open warfare**, and a Captain arriving at a distant Spire to trade may **suddenly discover it under a full naval and aerial blockade**—whether enforced by their own kingdom's fleet or a rival foreign armada.

### The Four Vessel Specialties

All Captains are trained to helm and fight aboard any vessel. However, at 1st level, you select one primary discipline where you hold master certification and tactical insight:

1. **Sail Vessels (Tall Ships, Cutters & Surface Schooners):**
   - *Mastery:* Wind reading, traditional rigging, neither-sea tides, and heavy canvas sail trim.
   - *Specialty Benefit:* You gain trained proficiency in Sailing Lore and Nature (Weather). When aboard a sailing vessel, you ignore difficult terrain caused by wet or tilting decks, and you can trim sails or adjust rigging with a single Interact action instead of two. Your allies aboard your vessel gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Acrobatics checks to avoid slipping or falling.

2. **Clockwork & Steam Vessels (Paddleboats, Ironclads & Tender Craft):**
   - *Mastery:* Pressure boilers, geared spring-wound paddlewheels, and industrial closed-cycle engines.
   - *Specialty Benefit:* You gain trained proficiency in Engineering Lore and Crafting. You can perform emergency field repairs to propulsion shafts and steam linkages during combat in half the standard time. Once per encounter as a free action, you can order an **Emergency Surge**, increasing your vessel's tactical speed by 10 feet for 1 round without damaging the engine linkages.

3. **Agile Airships (Corsair Skyships, Scout Zeppelins & Zone Cutters):**
   - *Mastery:* Thermal wind riding, lightning-rod grounding, high-speed banking, and winch-assisted boarding.
   - *Specialty Benefit:* You gain trained proficiency in Airship Lore and Acrobatics. When piloting or commanding a light or agile airship in the Zone, your vessel gains a +2 circumstance bonus to DC against ramming and boarding attempts, and you gain the *Cat Fall* feat while aboard an airship. Once per round, you can use a reaction ([reaction]) when your ship is struck by lightning or electric hazards to safely shunt the discharge into the exhaust coils.

4. **Large War Crafts (Ironclad Spire Dreadnoughts & Capital Air Galleons):**
   - *Mastery:* Multi-deck gun coordination, armor-plate angling, broadside discipline, and crew logistics.
   - *Specialty Benefit:* You gain trained proficiency in Military Warfare Lore and Society (Naval Regulations). When directing ship-mounted arc cannons or deck ballistas, gun crews under your direct command gain a +1 circumstance bonus on attack rolls. In personal combat, while standing on the command deck or bridge of a large vessel, you and all allies within 30 feet gain a +1 circumstance bonus on Will saves against fear and demoralize effects.

## 5. Multiclass Archetype (Dedication)
*Characters of other classes can gain training in the Sky Captain archetype.*

- **Sky Captain Dedication (Feat 2):** `[Player Core 2 p.220]`  
  - **Prerequisites:** Dexterity 14 (+2) or Strength 14 (+2), Charisma 14 (+2).
  - **Benefits:** Trained in Lore (Airships) or Lore (Zone Navigation); trained in Acrobatics or Athletics; trained in Sky Captain class DC. Gain Aerial Panache.
- **Basic Aeronaut (Feat 4):** Gain a 1st- or 2nd-level Sky Captain feat.
- **Sky Captain's Resiliency (Feat 4):** Gain 3 additional HP for each Sky Captain archetype feat taken.
- **Boarding Finisher (Feat 4):** Gain the Confident Finisher action.
- **Advanced Aeronaut (Feat 6):** Gain a Sky Captain feat up to half your character level.
