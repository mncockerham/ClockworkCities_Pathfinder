# Officer

- **Base Class:** Champion `[Player Core 2 p.18]`
- **Status:** **Modified** (1-to-1 baseline with Pathfinder 2e Remaster Champion; religious deity, holy/unholy sanctification, and divine magic replaced with secular institutional commissions: Military Officers, Constabulary Chiefs, and the King's Guard)
- **Availability:** **Primary PC Class** (Full 1–20 progression)
- **Discipline / Trait:** Martial (Heavy Armor Mastery, Defensive Reactions & Tactical Authority)

---

## 1. Setting Overview
*The high-ranking commanders, constabulary leadership, and elite King's Guardsmen of the Realm.*

- **In-World Identity:** Commissioned field captains and generals of the Spire Garrison, senior commanders and precinct chiefs of the Ward Constabulary, or elite members of the King's Guard (≤1,000 royal officers with supreme jurisdiction across all wards who answer only to King Macelus Wilheart). They lead through tactical discipline, heavy plate defense, and supreme legal authority.
- **Typical Social Class & Backing:** Military Officer / Captain (Status 4), General (Status 5), Constable Chief (Status 4), or King's Guard (Status 4–5). They hold the legal power to command enlisted ranks, direct constables, and deputize citizens on the spot.
- **Physical / Scientific Explanation:** Peerless heavy armor drill, defensive shield formations, battlefield triage, and psychological authority. Their defensive reactions are split-second tactical interventions, shield interceptions, and commanding orders that snap allies out of shock.

## 4. Spells & Apparatus (If Applicable)
*Not applicable.* The Officer has no divine spellcasting or magical focus pool. Any focus abilities like *Lay on Hands* are reflavored as **Battlefield Triage / Officer's Rally** (restoring **regular Damage HP only** per `AISummary/20_Healing.md`).

## 5. Multiclass Archetype (Dedication)
*Characters of other classes can gain training in the Officer (Champion) archetype [Player Core 2 p.219].*

- **Officer Dedication (Feat 2):** `[Player Core 2 p.219]`  
  - **Prerequisites:** Strength 14 (+2) or Dexterity 14 (+2), Constitution 14 (+2).
  - **Benefits:** Trained in light, medium, and heavy armor; trained in Society or Warfare Lore; trained in Officer class DC. Gain your chosen Command Mandate.
- **Officer's Reaction (Feat 4):** Gain your Command Mandate's Officer's Reaction `[Player Core 2 p.219]`.
- **Officer's Resiliency (Feat 4):** Gain 3 additional HP for each Officer archetype feat taken `[Player Core 2 p.219]`.
- **Basic Tactics (Feat 4):** Gain a 1st- or 2nd-level Officer feat `[Player Core 2 p.219]`.
- **Advanced Tactics (Feat 6):** Gain an Officer feat up to half your character level `[Player Core 2 p.219]`.
- **Diverse Armor Expert (Feat 14):** Heavy armor proficiency increases to Expert `[Player Core 2 p.219]`.
