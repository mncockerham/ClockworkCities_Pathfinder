# Social Class Equipment: Warrens, Slums & Outcasts (Status –1 to 0)

- **Social Standing:** Fungal farmers, indentured debtors, Warrens dwellers, day scavengers, and syndicate toughs.
- **Backing / Source of Supply:** Informal patronage from local Warrens bosses (e.g., Johnny Ralen, Amanda Coren), black market fences, pawnshops, or scavenged scrap. Gear is frequently battered, secondhand, or improvised.
- **Reference Catalog:** `ClockworkPathfinder/Equipment/EquipmentCatalog.md`
